# The Sermon: Embracing Strategic Blindness to Preserve Trust in Cooperation

Created at 2025/12/22 4:33 PM

### 🧩 **The Sermon as Anti-Feedback-Loop Architecture**

***

### ∴ Core Idea Unit

Cooperation does not fail because people are insufficiently virtuous.It fails when **visibility, monitoring, and internal accounting** convert relational action into a feedback loop that collapses trust.

Ancient injunctions against self-observation function as **systems design**, not moral mysticism.

**Mental shift provoked:**From *“We need better incentives and transparency”* → *“Some cooperation only survives if it cannot be audited.”*

***

### ▲ Identity Play & Roles

This meme reassigns the practitioner from manager to guardian of opacity:

- **Moral accountant → Blind steward**
- **Transparent leader → Boundary keeper**
- **Optimizer → Gardener of conditions**

The self is repositioned as a **protector of relational dark matter**, not its overseer.
