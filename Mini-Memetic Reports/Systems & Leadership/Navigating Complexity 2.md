### ◈ Mini-Memetic Profile
### ∴ Core Idea Unit

Organizations crave the illusion of clarity. They valorize decisiveness and polished decks, mistaking tidiness for truth. The real work—reflection, sense-making, adaptive learning—gets sacrificed at the altar of deliverables.
### ▲ Identity Play & Roles

The Navigator — rejects the “heroic problem-solver” myth and embraces uncertainty as terrain. Acts as a field guide through ambiguity rather than a commander seeking closure.
### ≈ Emotional Triggers

😬 Anxiety at not knowing · 🧠 Curiosity re-emerging · 😔 Frustration at institutional inertia · 🤯 Liberation through reflective awareness
