### ◈ Mini-Memetic Profile

***

**🧠 Title:***“Low-Frequency People” — The Energetic Othering Mechanism*

***

### ∴ Core Idea Unit

- Categorizes certain individuals as **intrinsically misaligned** or harmful based on their “frequency,” a loosely defined proxy for energy, mindset, or emotional state.
- Positions them as **toxic, stagnant, or spiritually regressive**, warranting exclusion or avoidance—not for specific actions, but for who they are energetically.
- Core meme: *“Low-frequency people drain you just by existing in your field.”*
- Collapses relational ethics into **vibe assessment**, using pseudo-scientific metaphors to **morally disengage** without conflict.

***

### ▲ Identity Play & Roles

- **Vibrational Ascendant:** Sees themselves as elevated, awakened, or energetically refined.
- **Boundary Enforcer:** Keeps a psychic perimeter against “low vibes,” reframed as a spiritual hygiene practice.
- **Energetic Purist:** Avoids complexity or ambiguity; upholds binary split between “high” and “low.”
- **Wounded Mystic:** May frame their discernment as a result of trauma or empathic exhaustion.

***

### ≈ Emotional Triggers

- **Fear of Contamination:** Framing others as “low frequency” activates avoidance, revulsion, self-protection.
- **Empowerment via Disconnection:** Cutting people off becomes a righteous act of self-preservation.
- **Validation of Discernment:** Encourages intuitive sorting of people based on internal sensation rather than evidence.
- **Superiority & Distancing:** Evokes subtle pride in being energetically “beyond” others.

***

### 𐂷 Spread Mechanics

- **Distribution Vectors:**
    - Common in spiritual influencer content, empath communities, trauma recovery spaces, #WitchTok, astrology meme pages, and manifestation coaching platforms.
    - Shared via quotes, story reposts, aesthetic reels, and soft-lit affirmations.
- **Propagation Style:**
    - **Binary judgment phrasing:** “Surround yourself with high-vibe souls only.”
    - **Affirmation + threat hybrid:** “Protect your energy from low-frequency people who feed on your light.”
    - **Visual metaphors:** Foggy or shadowed figures, aura breaches, faded or grey energy signatures.

***

### ⛨ Defense Reflexes

- **Ontological Essentialism:** Criticism reframed as low-vibration behavior, thus invalid.
- **Spiritualized Othering:** Reduces disagreement or discomfort to energetic misalignment, bypassing empathy or accountability.
- **Non-responsiveness Armor:** Refusal to engage is rebranded as vibrational wisdom.
- **Diagnostic Flexibility:** “Low-frequency” can mean anything: negativity, trauma, messiness, criticism, or failure to resonate.
