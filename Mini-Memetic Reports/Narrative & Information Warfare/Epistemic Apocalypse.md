**Title:***Epistemic Apocalypse — The Meme of Certainty Collapse*

### ∴ Core Idea Unit

- Belief: Humanity faces not only crises of politics and environment but of **knowledge itself** — the breakdown of shared truths, stable maps, and credible authorities.
- Encodes a frame of **cognitive disintegration**: when epistemic frameworks collapse, all narratives compete in a chaotic marketplace of doubt.

***

### ▲ Identity Play & Roles

- User as the **seer of collapse**, warning of the unraveling.
- Others cast as **denialists** (clinging to false certainty) or **lost souls** (adrift in the chaos).
- Insider positioning as **navigator of ruins** or **cartographer of the post-truth landscape.**

***

- **Fear & dread** (loss of certainty, chaos of meaning).
- **Awe** (the scale of collapse, mythic resonance of “apocalypse”).
- **Alienation** (isolation from shared reality).
- **Empowerment** (those who can re-map gain power).
