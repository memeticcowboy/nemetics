# Sunset Bay Trail Run 2024 Training Plan

Created at 2024/05/21 10:25 AM

### Sunset Bay Trail Run 2024 Training Plan

#### Training Schedule
- **Observe**
  - Collect and organize data: Track running metrics such as distance, pace, heart rate, and elevation gain.
  - Base decisions on current information: Regularly review training data to understand progress and identify areas for improvement.
- **Orient**
  - Analyze data and brainstorm solutions: Evaluate training data to identify patterns and potential issues.
  - Foster a bridge builder mentality: Connect with other runners or join a running group to share insights and gain new perspectives on training.
- **Decide**
  - Choose the most effective course of action: Based on analysis, decide on specific training adjustments.
  - Craft compelling narratives: Develop a motivational story or goal that keeps focused and committed to the training plan.
- **Act**
  - Implement the chosen action: Execute the revised training plan with discipline and consistency.
  - Track metrics: Continuously monitor performance metrics to ensure adjustments are effective.

#### Nutrition
- Maintain a balanced diet with a focus on carbohydrates, proteins, and healthy fats.
- Stay hydrated before, during, and after training sessions.
- Consider consulting a nutritionist to optimize dietary intake for training and race day.

#### Gear
- Invest in proper trail running shoes with good traction and support.
- Dress in moisture-wicking and breathable clothing suitable for trail running.
- Use a hydration pack or belt for longer training runs to stay hydrated.

#### Mental Preparation
- Cultivate empathy: Understand your body's signals and avoid overtraining.
- Explore global impact: Consider how training and participation in events like the Sunset Bay Trail Run can inspire others and contribute to the running community.
- Anticipate roadblocks: Prepare for potential challenges such as injuries or weather conditions and develop strategies to overcome them.
