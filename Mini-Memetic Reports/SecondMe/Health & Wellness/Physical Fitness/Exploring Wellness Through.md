# Six Dimensions of Wellness through lens of Bloom’s Taxonomy

Here are questions aligned with Bloom's Taxonomy for each of the wellness categories: physical, intellectual, occupational, spiritual, social, and emotional. These questions will help you explore and address each category systematically.

### Physical Wellness

1. **Remembering:** What are the key components of physical wellness?

2. **Understanding:** Why is maintaining physical wellness important for overall health?

3. **Applying:** How can you incorporate regular exercise into your daily routine?

4. **Analyzing:** What are the benefits and challenges of different types of physical activities?

5. **Evaluating:** How effective is your current physical wellness routine?

6. **Creating:** Develop a personalized fitness plan that includes various types of exercises.

### Intellectual Wellness

1. **Remembering:** What activities stimulate intellectual growth?

2. **Understanding:** Why is continuous learning important for intellectual wellness?

3. **Applying:** How can you engage in activities that challenge your mind?

4. **Analyzing:** What are the pros and cons of different learning methods (e.g., reading, online courses)?

5. **Evaluating:** How well do your current activities contribute to your intellectual wellness?

6. **Creating:** Design a learning plan that includes diverse intellectual activities.

### Occupational Wellness

1. **Remembering:** What are the key factors that contribute to occupational wellness?

2. **Understanding:** Why is job satisfaction important for overall well-being?

3. **Applying:** How can you improve your work-life balance?

4. **Analyzing:** What are the strengths and weaknesses of your current job role?

5. **Evaluating:** How fulfilling is your current career path?

6. **Creating:** Develop a career development plan that aligns with your personal and professional goals.

### Spiritual Wellness

1. **Remembering:** What practices contribute to spiritual wellness?

2. **Understanding:** Why is spiritual wellness important for mental and emotional health?

3. **Applying:** How can you incorporate mindfulness or meditation into your daily routine?

4. **Analyzing:** What are the benefits and challenges of different spiritual practices?

5. **Evaluating:** How aligned are your current spiritual practices with your beliefs and values?

6. **Creating:** Create a spiritual wellness plan that includes regular practices and reflections.

### Social Wellness

1. **Remembering:** What are the key elements of social wellness?

2. **Understanding:** Why are healthy relationships important for social wellness?

3. **Applying:** How can you build and maintain strong social connections?

4. **Analyzing:** What are the positive and negative aspects of your current social interactions?

5. **Evaluating:** How satisfied are you with your current social life?

6. **Creating:** Develop a plan to enhance your social network and relationships.

### Emotional Wellness

1. **Remembering:** What are the signs of good emotional wellness?

2. **Understanding:** Why is emotional wellness crucial for overall health?

3. **Applying:** How can you manage stress and emotions effectively?

4. **Analyzing:** What are the triggers and coping mechanisms for your emotional responses?

5. **Evaluating:** How well do you handle emotional challenges and stress?

6. **Creating:** Create an emotional wellness plan that includes stress management techniques and emotional regulation strategies.

By using these questions, you can explore each wellness category in depth, ensuring a holistic approach to your well-being.
