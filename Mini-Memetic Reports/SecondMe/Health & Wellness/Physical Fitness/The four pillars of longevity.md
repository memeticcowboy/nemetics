The four pillars of longevity discussed in the video by Dr. Bob Hariri in [this video](https://youtu.be/OY36GKQQ9p8?si=kssikxEOK3B_R2WY) are:



 * High performance mobility: This refers to the ability to maintain muscle strength and joint function, which is essential for overall physical health and quality of life.

 * High performance cognition: This refers to the ability to maintain brain function and cognitive abilities, such as memory, learning, and problem-solving.

 * High performance immunity: This refers to the ability of the immune system to function effectively in protecting the body from disease and promoting recovery.

 * Youthful aesthetics: This refers to maintaining a youthful appearance, which can have a positive impact on mental and emotional well-being.



Dr. Hariri emphasizes that all four pillars are important for achieving longevity and that neglecting any one of them can have negative consequences.
