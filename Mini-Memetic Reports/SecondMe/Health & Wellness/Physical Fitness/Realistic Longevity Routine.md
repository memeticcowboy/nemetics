# Realistic Longevity Routine: Simple Steps to Enhance Health and Longevity

Created at 2025/05/07 1:01 PM

Here are concise study notes based on the lecture [“Realistic Longevity Routine for Normal People - Be Healthier Than 95% of People.”](https://youtu.be/LCnRP2iHzmw?si=uJJq4jtVzy0Z5W28)
Main Ideas
	•	Healthy Living Misconceptions:
	•	You don’t need complicated routines or expensive diets to be healthy.
	•	Simple changes can lead to significant health improvements.
	•	Exercise Recommendations:
	•	Sedentary to Active: Moving from no exercise to 90 minutes/week reduces overall mortality risk by 14% and adds 3 years to life expectancy.
	•	Daily Exercise Impact: Every additional 15 minutes of exercise reduces mortality risk by 4%.
	•	Walking Benefits: Increasing daily steps from 2,000 to 8,700 lowers all-cause risk by 60%.
Weekly Exercise Plan (Less than 2 hours/week):
	•	Monday: Resistance training (pulling muscles) - 30 mins
	•	Tuesday: Rest
	•	Wednesday: Resistance training (leg muscles) - 30 mins
	•	Thursday: Rest
	•	Friday: Resistance training (pushing muscles) - 30 mins
	•	Saturday: High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) - 30 mins
	•	Sunday: Rest
	•	Technical Failure: In resistance training, perform exercises until form breaks down (8-12 reps).
	•	Daily Walking: Aim for at least 8,700 steps daily.
	•	Auxiliary Wellness Devices: Discussed benefits of red light therapy for skin, energy, and overall wellness.
Nutrition Guidelines
	•	Moderation is Key:
	•	Eat everything in moderation with a focus on whole, minimally processed foods (fruits, vegetables, fish, nuts).
	•	Reduce ultra-processed foods (donuts, chips) but some are acceptable in small amounts.
	•	Dietary Focus:
	•	Body composition and blood work are crucial indicators of health (lean vs. overweight, blood sugar levels).
	•	Meal Timing:
	•	No strict schedule necessary; eat whenever, but consider stopping a few hours before bed for better sleep.
Supplement Recommendations
	•	Key Supplements:
	•	Creatine Monohydrate: 100 mg/kg/day for muscle strength and brain aging.
	•	Magnesium: 200-400 mg/day for general health.
	•	Glycine: 5-15 g/day for collagen synthesis and sleep support.
	•	Collagen Peptides: 10 g/day for skin and joint health.
	•	Omega-3: 1,000-3,000 mg/day for heart health; target an omega-3 index of 8-12%.
	•	Taurine: 3-6 g/day for metabolism and exercise performance.
Sleep Guidelines
	•	Importance of Sleep:
	•	Aim for 7-8 hours of quality sleep to avoid premature aging.
	•	Go to bed around the same time each night.
	•	Use sleep masks, dim lights before bed, and consider blue-blocking glasses.
Auxiliary Routines
	•	Social Connections: Spend time with friends and family; relationships significantly impact happiness and longevity.
	•	Light Exposure: Get bright light exposure (natural sunlight or SAD lamp) within 30 minutes of waking up.
	•	Reduce Plastic Exposure: Minimize use of plastic containers and products to lower microplastic consumption.
Health Testing
	•	Important Health Metrics:
	•	Measure waist circumference, blood pressure, blood sugar, LP little A, HSRP, and grip strength.
	•	Lower values are generally favorable for longevity.
Summary of Longevity Routine
	•	Exercise: 3 resistance training sessions and 1 HIIT session per week.
	•	Nutrition: 90% whole foods, minimal ultra-processed foods.
	•	Sleep: 7-8 hours of sleep with consistent bedtime.
	•	Social Life: Nurture relationships for better overall health.
	•	Supplements: Focus on key supplements for foundational health benefits.
This routine can help you become healthier than 95% of the population.
