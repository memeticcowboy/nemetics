*   The hint mentions meditation turning into a nap, which could be a sign that the body is still in recovery mode. During illness, the body requires more rest to repair and rebuild. Meditation can be a useful tool for relaxation, but if fatigue sets in, allowing yourself to nap can be beneficial for recovery.

*   Working out while getting over a cold can be a double-edged sword. Moderate exercise can sometimes alleviate symptoms by opening up nasal passages and boosting the immune system temporarily. However, intense exercise can suppress the immune system, potentially prolonging the illness. It's generally recommended to reduce the intensity and duration of workouts and to avoid exercise if you have a fever, body aches, or a productive cough.
