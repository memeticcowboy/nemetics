# Improving Fast and Slow Twitch Muscles Through Running

Created at 2024/05/18 9:28 AM

## Hill Run and Muscle Types
- Hill run of 2 miles up and 2 miles down
- Hill running enhances both endurance and strength

## Incorporating Sprinting
- Interval Training: Sprint for 30 seconds, then recover
- Fartlek Training: Vary pace throughout the run
- Dedicated Sprint Sessions: Short sprints with ample rest
- Hill Sprints: Sprint up the hill and walk down for recovery

By incorporating sprinting techniques like interval training, fartlek training, dedicated sprint sessions, and hill sprints, one can effectively train both fast and slow twitch muscles while enhancing overall fitness and performance.
