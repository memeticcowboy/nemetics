## Insight


* The ingredient list indicates a strong focus on enhancing both physical performance and cognitive function. Ingredients like **Beta Alanine** and **L-Citrulline** suggest that the formulation aims to minimize fatigue and improve blood flow, which is crucial for athletic performance and recovery.

* Inclusion of **Taurine** and **Alpha GPC** highlights the supplement's approach to balancing stimulants and hydration management, mitigating potential side effects commonly associated with high-stimulant pre-workout supplements. This can benefit users who may be sensitive to intense stimulants.

* The presence of **Neurofactor** (derived from coffee fruit) and **Huperzine A** suggests a cognitive enhancement focus. These ingredients can support memory and mental alertness, essential aspects for athletes needing both physical and mental readiness during workouts.

* The use of **Yohimbine** points to an increase in thermogenesis, which can aid in fat loss and energy levels. This aligns with trends in fitness supplements where metabolic boost is often sought after by individuals looking to improve overall body composition.

* Additionally, the structured serving size (16 grams) and clear usage instructions demonstrate a user-centric design, encouraging optimal absorption and effectiveness, thus promoting adherence to supplementation routines among fitness enthusiasts.
