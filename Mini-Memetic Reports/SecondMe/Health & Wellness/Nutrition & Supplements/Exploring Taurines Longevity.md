Potential Longevity Benefits of Taurine

 * Extended lifespan in mice: Studies have shown that taurine supplementation can extend the lifespan of mice by up to 25%.

 * Improved health markers: Taurine has been associated with various health benefits in mice, including increased bone density, reduced fat mass, and better cognitive function.

 * Reduced inflammation: Taurine supplementation has been shown to reduce markers of inflammation in both mice and humans.

Human Clinical Trials on Taurine

 * Metabolic health benefits: Taurine supplementation has been shown to improve metabolic health markers in people with chronic diseases such as diabetes and obesity. This includes reducing blood pressure, cholesterol, triglycerides, and fasting blood sugar.

 * Exercise performance: Taurine supplementation has been shown to increase exercise performance and endurance in both healthy individuals and athletes.

Dosage and Sources of Taurine

 * Effective dosage: The effective dosage of taurine for improving metabolic health and exercise performance is 0.5 to 6 grams per day.

 * Dietary sources: Taurine can be obtained from various dietary sources, including seafood, meat, and dairy products.

 * Supplementation: If you are unable to meet your daily taurine needs through your diet, you can consider taking a taurine supplement.
