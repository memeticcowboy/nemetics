Alpha Lipoic Acid (ALA) is an antioxidant that plays an essential role in energy production in cells. It helps protect cells from oxidative stress, which is linked to aging and various health issues. ALA has several potential health benefits:

	1.	Blood Sugar Regulation: It may help lower blood sugar levels, which is especially beneficial for individuals with type 2 diabetes.
	2.	Antioxidant Properties: ALA works as both a fat- and water-soluble antioxidant, helping protect various parts of cells from damage.
	3.	Nerve Health: It may be useful in alleviating symptoms of nerve pain, particularly in conditions like diabetic neuropathy.
	4.	Skin Health: ALA is also believed to improve skin texture and reduce signs of aging, due to its role in neutralizing free radicals.

Biotin (vitamin B7), added as an “optimizer” in this formula, is essential for healthy skin, hair, and nails, and it also helps in metabolizing carbohydrates, fats, and proteins. Biotin is often paired with ALA because high doses of ALA can deplete biotin levels, so the addition helps maintain a proper balance.
