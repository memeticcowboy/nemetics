# Synergistic Benefits of HMB, TMG, Magnesium Glycinate, and Melatonin for Recovery and Sleep

Created at 2025/04/09 5:42 PM

Taking HMB (β-Hydroxy β-Methylbutyrate), TMG (Trimethylglycine), Magnesium Glycinate, and Melatonin before bed can offer synergistic benefits for muscle recovery, sleep quality, methylation support, and overall relaxation. Here’s a breakdown of each and their combined potential:


**1. HMB (β-Hydroxy β-Methylbutyrate)**

- Supports muscle preservation during sleep, especially during calorie deficits or intense training.
- May reduce muscle protein breakdown and promote muscle repair overnight.
- Helps enhance recovery without requiring large protein intake at night.

**2. TMG (Trimethylglycine)**

- Supports methylation processes, which are essential for DNA repair, detoxification, and neurotransmitter production.
- May lower homocysteine levels, improving cardiovascular health.
- Combined with HMB, it may support cellular energy and metabolic repair overnight.

**3. Magnesium Glycinate**

- Highly bioavailable form of magnesium with calming effects due to glycine.
- Helps regulate GABA activity, promoting relaxation and deeper sleep.
- Supports muscle relaxation, reducing cramping and restlessness.
- Aids in neurotransmitter balance and stress reduction.

**4. Melatonin**

- Regulates the sleep–wake cycle and improves sleep onset and quality.
- May enhance REM sleep and overall sleep efficiency.
- Antioxidant properties may support brain health and immune repair overnight.


**Synergistic Benefits of the Stack Before Bed**

- Improved Sleep Quality: Melatonin + Magnesium Glycinate promote deep, restorative sleep.
- Muscle Recovery & Preservation: HMB supports protein retention; Magnesium aids muscle relaxation.
- Cellular Repair & Detox: TMG enhances methylation; Melatonin adds antioxidant support.
- Stress Reduction: Magnesium + glycine reduce cortisol and anxiety; Melatonin eases nervous system tension.


**Possible Use Case Scenarios**

- Athletes or fitness-focused individuals optimizing recovery and muscle retention.
- People under chronic stress or with poor sleep hygiene.
- Individuals supporting methylation or detox pathways, especially if using nootropics or higher protein diets.
