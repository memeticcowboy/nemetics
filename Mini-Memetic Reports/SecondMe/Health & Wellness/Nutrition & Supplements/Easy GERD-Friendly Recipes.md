# Easy GERD-Friendly Recipes

Created at 2025/10/29 4:26 PM

## Oatmeal with Bananas and Pears
- Cook rolled oats in water or low-fat milk.
- Top with sliced banana and diced pear.
- Add a sprinkle of cinnamon (omit if sensitive).  
- This dish is soothing, filling, and fiber-rich.

## Grilled Salmon with Steamed Broccoli and Brown Rice
- Season salmon with olive oil, salt, and turmeric.
- Grill or bake until cooked through.
- Serve with steamed broccoli and a side of brown rice.
- An anti-inflammatory and easy-to-digest meal.

## Chicken and Quinoa Bowl
- Grill skinless chicken breast with olive oil and ginger.
- Serve over cooked quinoa with steamed spinach.
- Drizzle with kefir or plain yogurt (if tolerated).
- A protein-packed and gut-friendly bowl.

## Carrot Ginger Bisque
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1 lb carrots, peeled and chopped
  - 1 small potato, peeled and diced
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and sliced
  - 1 small leek or onion
  - 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  - Salt to taste (optional)
  - Optional: a dollop of plain yogurt or kefir for serving
  
- **Instructions:**
  1. Heat olive oil over medium-low, add leek/onion and ginger, and sauté until soft.
  2. Add carrots and potato, stir for a minute.
  3. Pour in broth, bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until tender (about 20 minutes).
  4. Blend until smooth (use immersion blender or transfer to a blender).
  5. Adjust salt and serve warm, with yogurt/kefir if desired.  
- This soup is gentle, warming, and supports gut health, with ginger for digestion.
