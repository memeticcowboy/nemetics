# Revolutionary Noninvasive BCI Empowers Paralyzed Patients

Created at 2025/03/07 6:07 PM

## Summary
Researchers from Carnegie Mellon University and the University of Minnesota developed a noninvasive brain-computer interface (BCI) that allows users to control a robotic arm using EEG and machine learning, achieving continuous and smooth tracking of a computer cursor, a breakthrough beneficial for paralyzed patients and those with movement disorders.

  - Unlike previous invasive methods, this noninvasive approach minimizes risks associated with surgical procedures, making it more accessible for patients.
