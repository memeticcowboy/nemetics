# Methylene Blue Benefits for ME/CFS and Fibromyalgia: Dosage, Safety, and Conclusion

Created at 2024/07/10 10:07 AM

### Methylene Blue for Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS) and Fibromyalgia

#### Mechanism of Action
Methylene blue enhances mitochondrial respiration and cellular oxygen consumption, boosting energy and cognitive function. It also has neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory properties, beneficial for neurodegenerative diseases and chronic pain conditions【1】【2】.

#### Benefits for ME/CFS and Fibromyalgia
1. **Energy and Cognition**: Methylene blue can improve energy levels, memory, and mental clarity in ME/CFS patients by enhancing tissue oxygenation and mitochondrial protection【3】.
2. **Pain and Inflammation**: Its anti-inflammatory properties may reduce fibromyalgia pain. Neuroprotective abilities can help manage the condition【4】.
3. **Symptom Relief**: Low doses have shown symptom reduction in fibromyalgia, with significant relief over weeks【3】【4】.

#### Dosage and Safety
1. **Dosage**: Typical dosages range from 0.5-4 mg/kg per day, starting low and gradually increasing【1】【2】.
2. **Safety**: Avoid use with SSRIs due to serotonin syndrome risk. Common side effects include blue or green urine and mild gastrointestinal discomfort【1】【3】.

### Conclusion
Methylene blue shows promise as an adjunct therapy for ME/CFS and fibromyalgia, but consultation with healthcare providers and further research is essential.
