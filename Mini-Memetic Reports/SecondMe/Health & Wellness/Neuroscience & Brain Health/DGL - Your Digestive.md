# DGL: Your Digestive Superhero!

Created at 2024/09/30 7:45 PM

What is DGL?

	•	DGL (Deglycyrrhizinated Licorice) is a form of licorice root extract that has had the compound glycyrrhizin removed. Glycyrrhizin can lead to side effects like increased blood pressure, which is why removing it makes DGL safer for long-term use.
	•	Licorice root has been used traditionally to help with gastric and digestive issues, including indigestion, heartburn, and stomach ulcers.

Potential Benefits:

	1.	Digestive Health:
	•	Ulcer Relief: DGL is known to help soothe the mucous membranes of the digestive tract, promoting healing in cases of stomach ulcers. It stimulates mucus production, which helps protect the lining of the stomach from acid.
	•	Heartburn and Acid Reflux: DGL may help alleviate acid reflux and heartburn by coating and protecting the esophagus and stomach lining, reducing irritation and discomfort.
	2.	Anti-Inflammatory Properties:
	•	Licorice root has anti-inflammatory properties, which can be beneficial for calming the digestive tract in conditions such as gastritis or irritable bowel syndrome (IBS).
	3.	Aloe Vera Addition:
	•	Aloe Vera is well-known for its soothing and healing properties. In this supplement, it helps reduce inflammation in the digestive system and provides additional relief for stomach irritation.
	•	Aloe Vera can also support regularity and healthy bowel function.

How and When to Take It:

	•	Dosage: The product contains 400 mg of DGL per capsule. A common dosage is 1-2 capsules, 20 minutes before meals, to help prepare the stomach lining and reduce the chances of heartburn or acid reflux.
	•	Best Time: DGL is typically taken before meals, especially those that might trigger digestive discomfort. Taking it before meals helps coat and protect the stomach lining.
	•	With or Without Food: Since DGL is meant to protect the digestive lining, it’s best taken before eating for maximum effect.

Who Might Benefit from DGL with Aloe Vera?:

	•	Individuals with Acid Reflux or Heartburn: Those who experience frequent heartburn or GERD (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease) may find relief by using DGL before meals.
