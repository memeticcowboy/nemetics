# Ketone Bodies: A Breakthrough in Neurodegeneration

Created at 2024/12/10 1:16 PM

### Summary
Recent research reveals that **ketone bodies**, particularly β-hydroxybutyrate, can directly bind to misfolded proteins in the brain, enhancing their clearance through autophagy. This interaction alters the solubility of damaged proteins, facilitating their removal and reducing pathological aggregation. Studies in mouse models of Alzheimer's disease and aging demonstrated that treatment with ketone esters decreased insoluble protein accumulation and improved brain health. These findings suggest a novel metabolic link to aging and neurodegeneration, offering potential therapeutic avenues.
