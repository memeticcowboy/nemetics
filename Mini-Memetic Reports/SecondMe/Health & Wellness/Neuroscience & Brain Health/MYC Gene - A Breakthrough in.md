# MYC Gene: A Breakthrough in Muscle Growth

Created at 2024/12/08 12:13 PM

### Summary
Recent research indicates that controlled activation of the **MYC gene** can stimulate muscle growth akin to the effects of physical exercise. In studies, transient increases in MYC levels led to enhanced muscle mass and fiber size without physical activity. However, due to MYC's association with cancer, its therapeutic application requires careful consideration.
