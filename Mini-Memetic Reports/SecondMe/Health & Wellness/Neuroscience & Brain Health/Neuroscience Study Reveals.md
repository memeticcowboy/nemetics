# Neuroscience Study Reveals Brain's Adaptation to Visual Distractions

Created at 2025/04/17 8:17 AM

## Summary
The human brain can adapt to filter out repeated visual distractions through experience, as shown by an EEG study. Participants learned to ignore frequent distractions in specific locations, enhancing their focus and reducing early visual processing of these stimuli.
