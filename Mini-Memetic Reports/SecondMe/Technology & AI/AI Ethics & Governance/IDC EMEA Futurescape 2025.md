## Insight
Hey there! The IDC EMEA Futurescape event was all about exploring the transformative potential of AI in business and IT sectors. The main objective was to present seven impactful predictions for 2025, based on insights from CIOs and technology executives. The discussion highlighted AI's role in redefining IT spending, driving sustainable value, and the need for a cohesive AI strategy. Key outcomes included the rapid growth of AI markets, with a notable 40% growth in EMEA, and the challenges organizations face in scaling AI use cases. The session emphasized the importance of a value-driven, data-centric approach, and the need for continuous learning to adapt to AI-driven changes. Looking ahead, the focus is on responsible AI, balancing economic and environmental sustainability, and preparing for a new geopolitical order. The event wrapped up with practical tips on prioritizing change management to enhance productivity. Stay tuned for more detailed insights and future trends!

**📈 Economic and Technological Insights**
- **CEO and Technology Leadership Perspectives**: Despite economic and political challenges, CEO sentiment remains positive with a focus on growth. Technology leadership is more divided, emphasizing transformation and the integration of AI into digital products and services.[_TIMESTAMP_]('107-155')
- **IT Growth vs. GDP**: IT is growing five times faster than GDP, driven by software and a resurgence in hardware sectors like PCs and mobile phones. Infrastructure, particularly server and storage, is also experiencing rapid growth.[_TIMESTAMP_]('227-276')

**🌐 AI Implementation and Challenges**
- **AI Market Growth and Challenges**: AI is a rapidly growing market in EMEA, with a 50 billion market size and close to 40% growth. However, only 10% of AI use cases make it to production, highlighting challenges in cost, skills, and C-suite coordination.[_TIMESTAMP_]('279-450')
- **Scaling AI Use Cases**: Organizations are moving from siloed to cross-functional AI use cases, aiming to rethink business processes for greater value. Examples include improving customer experience by integrating insights across departments.[_TIMESTAMP_]('474-802')

**🔧 Building an AI-Driven Business Model**
- **Value-Driven AI Strategy**: A value-driven AI strategy is essential, combining bottom-up innovation with top-down strategic alignment. Carlsberg's dual approach is highlighted as a best practice.[_TIMESTAMP_]('611-676')
- **Platform and Data-Driven Approach**: Organizations are adopting a platform approach, integrating infrastructure, data, and AI strategies. This holistic approach is exemplified by Novo Nordisk, aiming for scalability and differentiation.[_TIMESTAMP_]('816-970')

**☁️ Cloud Infrastructure Trends**
- **European vs. Meta Organizations' Cloud Preferences**: European organizations are more optimistic about using public cloud environments compared to their meta peers, who are more conservative due to digital sovereignty and governance concerns, leading them to prefer private infrastructure. This trend is expected to evolve as organizations balance security, cost, and innovation needs.[_TIMESTAMP_]('970-1006')
- **Data's Role in Infrastructure**: Data is fundamental to building infrastructure platforms, with organizations investing in data planes and considering data control and synthesis planes to improve data quality and accessibility. Novo Nordisk is highlighted for its strong unified governance strategy.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1010-1048')

**🔮 Predictions on AI and Data**
- **Synthetic Data Usage in AI**: By 2025, 30% of large EMEA organizations will use synthetic data to train AI models, accelerating the transition from proof of concept to production. Synthetic data offers quick access to AI tools, reduces costs, and mitigates risks related to personal data and compliance.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1055-1115')
- **Trust and Governance in AI**: By 2028, 20% of organizations will adopt an auditable trust score for data hygiene to communicate externally and drive AI adoption. This score will measure transparency, ethics, privacy, and security, influenced by the regulatory environment in EMEA.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1140-1216')

**🤖 AI's Impact on Workforce**
- **AI Agents in the Workplace**: By 2028, 40% of business professionals in top 500 companies will rely on AI agents for reporting and data workflows, enhancing data access and solving business challenges faster. The role of humans will shift towards critical thinking and innovation.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1393-1455')
- **AI-Driven Job Changes**: By 2030, 70% of new job roles in EMEA will be directly enabled by AI, with a focus on continuous learning to support role changes. Companies prioritizing this will outperform competitors in employee productivity by 60%.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1514-1559')

**🌐 Future Trends and Strategies**
- **Upcoming Digital Regulations**: 2024 will be the year of digital regulations, with DORA impacting financial services. The geopolitical order will influence macroeconomic and technology perspectives, with politics and tech becoming more intertwined.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1727-1761')
- **Quantum Computing and AI**: Quantum computing is seen as the next computing paradigm for AI, enabling sophisticated algorithms and models. This advancement will support new industries and extend existing ones, particularly in space technology.[_TIMESTAMP_]('1794-1803')
