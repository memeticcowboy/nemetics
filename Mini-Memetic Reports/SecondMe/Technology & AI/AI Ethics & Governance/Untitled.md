### The Intelligence Age

* The previous ages were named after the driving force of progress (industry, information).

* This new age will likely be called the "Intelligence Age".

* The beginning of this age is debated, with possibilities like Google's 2017 paper *"Attention is All You Need"* or the 2022 release of ChatGPT.

* Altman has a history of making bold predictions about AI, some of which have come true.

### Sam Altman's Predictions

* In 2016, Altman predicted the creation of artificial general intelligence (AGI).

* In 2021, he predicted that AI would be able to read legal documents and give medical advice within five years.

* He also discussed the possibility of an "accelerating revolution" as AI helps create more advanced AI.

### The Stages of Truth

* Altman references a concept where new ideas go through three stages: ridicule, violent opposition, and acceptance.

* AI is currently in the second stage, with many people being resistant to the idea of machines having human-like understanding.

### Altman's Vision of the Future

* Altman believes that AI will revolutionize many aspects of society, including education, healthcare, and software development.

* He envisions a future where everyone's lives can be significantly improved through AI.

* He compares the creation of AI to humanity's greatest achievement, similar to discovering fire or the wheel.

### Deep Learning and Its Potential

* Deep learning has been a key breakthrough in AI development.

* It allows machines to learn from data and discover underlying patterns.

* Deep learning has the potential to solve complex problems in various fields.

### Concerns and Considerations

* While Altman is optimistic about the future of AI, he also acknowledges the potential risks.

* These risks include job displacement, misuse of AI, and existential threats.

* Many experts are working on AI safety to mitigate these risks.
