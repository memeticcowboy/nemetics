# Observations on OpenAI and Meta's Vision for AI

Created at 2025/08/05 6:55 PM

## OpenAI's Accelerated Timeline
- OpenAI is significantly speeding up their timeline for portable AGI.
- Focus is on reasoning capabilities rather than just computational skills.
- Aims to create AI that thinks more like humans.

## Meta's Vision for Personal Superintelligence
- Meta is positioning itself with a vision for “personal superintelligence” through AR glasses.
- There is a competitive dynamic between OpenAI and Meta regarding the future of AI interfaces.
- The concept of “personal superintelligence” raises questions about the nature of human cognition augmentation.

## Implications of the Competition
- The race is on to define the next interface for human cognition.
- OpenAI's approach may lead to context-aware, reasoning AI in personal devices.
- Meta's approach focuses on an immersive AR layer that could change how we interact with technology.
- Consideration of whether one approach fosters genuine augmentation or deeper dependency on technology.
