# Ontologies: Enhancing Explainable AI

Created at 2024/11/05 3:52 PM

**Summary**:

This paper explores the versatile role of ontologies in enhancing Explainable AI (XAI), describing how structured knowledge representations help clarify AI's reasoning. Ontologies serve three key roles: as reference models that establish a common vocabulary, as tools for enabling common-sense reasoning, and as frameworks for tailoring explanations to users of varying expertise. The hierarchical nature of ontologies allows for adaptive, layered explanations, making complex AI decisions more accessible.
