**🔍 Understanding Key AI Characteristics**

- **Defining Transparency**: Transparency involves the openness of an AI system's processes and data. It ensures stakeholders understand the system's operations, which is vital for building trust and meeting regulatory requirements.

- **Importance of Explainability**: Explainability allows AI systems to articulate their decision-making processes in human-understandable terms. This is particularly important in sectors like healthcare and criminal justice, where clear insights are necessary for decision-makers.

- **Role of Interpretability**: Interpretability focuses on the human ability to understand cause-effect relationships within an AI system. This is essential for validation and troubleshooting, enabling domain experts to engage with the system without needing extensive external resources.
