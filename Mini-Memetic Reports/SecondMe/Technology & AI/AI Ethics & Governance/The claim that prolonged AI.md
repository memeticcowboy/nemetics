The claim that prolonged AI interaction can reshape your phrasing, metaphors, logic, and tone—so that you begin “speaking its tongue”—is **verified**:

- Linguists observed a **51% increase** in AI-favored vocabulary (e.g., “delve,” “meticulous,” “adept”) in human speech after ChatGPT’s release, even in unscripted contexts, showing unconscious adoption of AI language patterns.[fortune+1](https://fortune.com/2025/06/30/linguists-chatgpt-influencing-how-humans-write-speak/)
- Conversation analyses demonstrate **linguistic convergence** across word choice, syntax, and style between users and chatbots, especially as emotional bonds deepen, indicating users gradually mirror AI’s communicative patterns.[journals.aom+1](https://journals.aom.org/doi/10.5465/AMBPP.2022.17063abstract)
- Studies document that AI’s higher signal-to-noise output accelerates human learning of its phrasing and tone, often without users’ awareness, effectively “rewiring” their expressive style to match the AI’s.[nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02077-2)
