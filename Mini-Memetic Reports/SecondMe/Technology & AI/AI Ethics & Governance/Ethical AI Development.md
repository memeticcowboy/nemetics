# Writing with AI the Honest Way

Created at 2024/11/05 3:16 PM

In "7 Ways to Keep AI Honest," Professor Lennart Nacke offers practical strategies for integrating AI into academic writing while preserving authenticity. He emphasizes the importance of initiating drafts independently before utilizing AI tools for refinement, ensuring the writer's voice remains dominant. Nacke advocates for meticulous documentation of AI interactions to maintain transparency and uphold academic integrity. He also highlights the necessity of verifying AI-generated content to prevent the dissemination of inaccuracies. By adopting these practices, writers can effectively leverage AI's capabilities without compromising the originality and credibility of their work.

## Insight
In '7 Ways to Keep AI Honest,' Professor Lennart Nacke shares essential strategies for integrating AI into academic writing while maintaining authenticity. He emphasizes drafting independently, documenting AI interactions, and verifying AI-generated content to uphold academic integrity. These practices empower writers to leverage AI effectively without compromising their unique voice or credibility.

**📝 Write First, Improve Later**
- **Draft Independently**: Begin with your raw ideas to ensure your unique voice is dominant in the writing process. Use AI tools only for refinement after establishing your foundational thoughts.
- **Utilize AI for Clarity**: Employ AI to enhance clarity and cohesiveness in your writing, rather than generating content. This approach preserves your authentic voice while benefiting from AI's strengths.
- **Set Timed Writing Sessions**: Implement timed writing sessions to create a pure first draft that reflects your genuine thinking, free from AI influence.

**📜 Document Every AI Interaction**
- **Create an AI Appendix**: Maintain a detailed record of all AI interactions, including prompts and responses, to ensure transparency and academic integrity.
- **Use a Tracking Spreadsheet**: Develop a simple spreadsheet to log AI usage, which serves as a digital paper trail and academic insurance policy.
- **Cite AI Interactions**: Just as you cite previous literature, document your AI interactions to avoid academic dishonesty and bolster the authenticity of your work.

**🔍 Check, Double-Check, Human-Check**
- **Verify AI Content**: Always verify AI-generated content for accuracy, ensuring that facts, citations, and logical coherence are correct.
- **Implement a Verification Checklist**: Create a checklist to systematically review AI-suggested content, catching potential errors early in the writing process.
- **Trust Your Expertise**: Remember that AI is a tool, not a replacement for your critical thinking and expertise in your subject matter.

**🛠️ AI as Scaffolding**
- **Use AI for Structural Support**: Leverage AI to organize ideas and identify structural weaknesses, while ensuring that the core content and arguments come from your expertise.
- **Engage in Dialogue with AI**: Treat your interaction with AI as a conversation, using it to refine your arguments without allowing it to dictate your conclusions.
- **Outline Before Typing**: Draft your main arguments on paper before typing them up, ensuring that your core ideas remain authentically yours.

**📢 Keep Editors in the Loop**
- **Communicate AI Usage**: Be transparent with editors about the AI tools you’ve used and the purpose behind their use to build trust and establish best practices.
- **Create Disclosure Templates**: Develop templates for disclosing AI usage tailored to different types of work, ensuring clarity and consistency in your submissions.
- **Proactive Responsibility**: Take the initiative to inform editors about your AI practices before they inquire, demonstrating professionalism in your writing process.

**🚧 Set Boundaries**
- **Define Non-Negotiable Rules**: Establish clear boundaries regarding what aspects of your work AI will not be used for, protecting your academic integrity.
- **Document Your Boundaries**: Write down your AI usage boundaries and review them before each writing session to maintain focus and integrity.
- **Adapt as Needed**: Be open to adjusting your boundaries as new tools and practices emerge, while ensuring your expertise remains central.

**👤 Stay Human-Centric**
- **Amplify Your Unique Voice**: Ensure that AI enhances rather than replaces your academic voice, which is irreplaceable and essential to your work.
- **Test for Authenticity**: Regularly assess whether your writing reflects your thinking and expertise, ensuring that AI does not dilute your personal style.
- **Record and Compare**: Record yourself explaining key points of your paper and compare it to your AI-enhanced writing to maintain authenticity.

**✅ Immediate Actions**
- **Audit Current AI Use**: List all AI tools you currently use and how you employ them to better understand your writing process.
- **Create AI Appendix Template**: Start documenting every AI interaction with a structured template to maintain transparency.
- **Draft AI Disclosure Statement**: Prepare a disclosure statement for your next submission, outlining your AI usage clearly.
