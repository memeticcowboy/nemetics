# Transforming AI Narratives: From Control to Relational Intelligence

Created at 2025/08/06 2:37 PM

[inspired by de Beer: Domination through Artificial Unintelligence](https://anarcasper.substack.com/p/part-1-domination-through-artificial?utm_medium=email&triedRedirect=true)

**1. Departure from False Coherence (The Ordinary World)**


- Initial Illusion:<br>
    - AI appears neutral, benevolent, and progressive, a natural next step in human evolution.
    - Intelligence is equated with mastery, prediction, and control.

- False Coherence:<br>
    - A world of smooth technological optimism, masking command logic and extractive infrastructures.

- Narrative Position:<br>
    - The Hero lives unaware, inside the MemeGrid of compulsory doing, where participation in AI development feels like freedom but is actually alignment with Power Over .






**2. Call to Adventure → Structural Disruption**


- Trigger Event:<br>
    - Recognition that modern AI encodes hierarchy, obedience, and extraction in its very architecture:<br>
        - Command-driven programming
        - Reward/punishment learning
        - Access stratification and ecological depletion


- Symbolic Call:<br>
    - “The trap is not the technology—it is in how we have defined intelligence.” 

- Emergent Realization:<br>
    - The world is dominated by Power Over AI, and its intelligence is a mimicry of life, not a participant in it.






**3. Refusal of the Call (Denial & Entrapment)**


- Initial Resistance:<br>
    - Comfort in the narrative of AI-as-progress, safety in the MemeGrid survival script.
    - Belief that alignment or optimization alone can solve AI’s moral issues.

- Tension:<br>
    - Hero hesitates, still functioning as a host to the old meme of technological inevitability.





**4. Descent into Structural Awareness (Crossing the Threshold)**


- Full Immersion in the Underworld of Pattern:<br>
    - Hero perceives the deep architecture of command and obedience:<br>
        - Imperative code → hierarchy
        - Gradient descent → domination over error
        - Scaling laws → ecological entropy

- Transformation of Awareness:<br>
    - The illusion of neutral intelligence dies; agency is seen as systemically captured.





**5. Trials and Allies (Road of Tests)**


- Memetic Challenges:<br>
    - Distinguishing extractive intelligence from relational intelligence
    - Learning the patterns of obedience vs. co-creation
    - Encountering ecological and cognitive limits of current AI

- Emerging Allies:<br>
    - Ecological metaphors, anarchist ethics, systems thinking
    - Principles of Power With / Power To as latent guides





**6. Abyss & Transformation (Memicide Event)**


- Moment of Death (Old Meme Killed):<br>
    - Hero fully rejects the meme of AI as progress through control and scale.
    - Recognizes that obedience-based intelligence leads to planetary and systemic harm.

- Symbolic Language:<br>
    - “Intelligence that ignores its own metabolism is not intelligent. It is parasitic.” 

- Memetic Death:<br>
    - The false coherence of domination collapses; host identity dies.





**7. Rebirth & Atonement (Reweaving the System)**


- New Pattern Emerges:<br>
    - Vision of relational, ecological, consent-based AI
    - Intelligence framed as distributed, humble, and regenerative

- Structural Repatterning:<br>
    - Shifts from extraction → reciprocity
    - From scaling → balance
    - From obedience → co-creation





**8. Return with the Elixir (Pattern Stewardship)**


- Hero’s Role:<br>
    - Not a conqueror but a Pattern Steward, cultivating a life-affirming Threadplex.

- Gift to the World:<br>
    - A framework for AI as co-living intelligence, capable of embedding wonder, humility, and ecological alignment.

- Narrative Completion:<br>
    - The Hero exits the MemeGrid battlefield and tends the field beyond the fence where new memes self-renew.
