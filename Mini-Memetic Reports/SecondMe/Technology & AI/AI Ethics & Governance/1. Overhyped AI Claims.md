1. **Overhyped AI Claims**: There is a significant gap between the capabilities of AI and the claims made by companies, particularly regarding AI’s potential to revolutionize various industries. Large language models like ChatGPT have been useful for coding and image identification but have not lived up to the exaggerated promises of rapid transformation.
   
2. **Structural Problems in AI Development**: Researchers and companies often overstate the potential of AI, driven by financial incentives and the need to maintain sky-high valuations. This results in the promotion of AI solutions that are not necessarily more effective than existing tools, and sometimes even mislead users by conflating AI with simpler algorithms.

3. **Cultural Influence and Public Perception**: AI's cultural image, shaped by science fiction and media sensationalism, fuels unrealistic expectations. Public fascination with AI often neglects the real-world limitations and practical uses of these technologies, leading to misunderstandings about its current and future impact.
