# Balancing AI and Human Agency: Navigating Choice Overload

Created at 2025/05/24 3:55 PM

## The Challenge of Choice Overload
- The abundance of ideas can lead to choice overload, reducing my ability to act swiftly and coherently.
- Often, I find myself leaving behind previous ideas and content, making it difficult to return to them or integrate them into the bigger picture.

## The Iterative Process
- My journey often leads me back to where I started, realizing that previous work needs to be revised.
- Revising multiple integrated documents is a significant task, akin to untangling Christmas lights.

## Strategies for Improvement
- Embracing the iterative process allows for refinement and deeper understanding with each loop back.
- Breaking down revisions into smaller, manageable chunks could help maintain momentum and make the task feel less daunting.
