## Insight
In this episode of the D-Suite, hosts Rita Sallam and Afraz Jaffri delve into the complexities of implementing generative AI responsibly. They are joined by Lydia Clougherty Jones, a Gartner expert in data ethics, and Giles Pavey, Global Data Science Director at Unilever. The discussion centers around the ethical considerations, workforce implications, and best practices for responsible AI implementation. Key outcomes include the importance of developing ethical guidelines, the necessity of cross-functional collaboration, and the implementation of AI assurance programs. The episode emphasizes the need for organizations to think about AI ethics not just as a compliance issue but as a core business value. Actionable insights include starting with ethical dilemmas, involving diverse stakeholders, and continuously monitoring and updating AI practices to align with evolving regulations and societal expectations.
