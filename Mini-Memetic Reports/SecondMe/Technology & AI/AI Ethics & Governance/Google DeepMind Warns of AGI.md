- **⚠️ Existential Risks of AGI**: The study categorizes the risks associated with AGI into four major areas: misuse, misalignment, mistakes, and structural risks. - **🛡️ Need for Preventive Measures**: The research advocates for proactive strategies to mitigate risks, particularly focusing on misuse prevention. This involves establishing guidelines and protocols to ensure that AGI is developed and utilized safely, minimizing the potential for harm to humanity.

**🕊️ Global Oversight and Regulation**

- **🔍 Monitoring Unsafe Projects**: The suggested oversight framework would include mechanisms similar to the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) to monitor and regulate potentially dangerous AGI projects. - **🤝 Multi-Country Involvement**: The proposed organization would involve multiple countries, allowing for diverse input on the ethical use and deployment of AGI technologies. This collaborative approach aims to create a balanced and comprehensive regulatory environment.
