Methods for Persona Management<br>- Researchers at Anthropic have developed a method to extract vectors corresponding to different character archetypes, allowing them to see which parts of the model's them away from it can make them more willing to adopt alternative identities, and Anthropic has developed a "light touch intervention" called activation capping to limit the range of activations and
