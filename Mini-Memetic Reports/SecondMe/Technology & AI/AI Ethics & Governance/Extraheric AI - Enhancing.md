# Extraheric AI: Enhancing Higher-Order Thinking

This paper introduces "extraheric AI," a conceptual framework aimed at fostering higher-order thinking skills such as creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving through human-AI interaction. Unlike typical AI designs that replace or augment cognitive functions, extraheric AI encourages cognitive engagement by prompting users with questions and alternative perspectives instead of providing direct solutions. It aims to mitigate the risks of over-reliance on AI, such as deskilling and diminished critical thinking.

## Insight
This paper presents the concept of 'extraheric AI,' a framework designed to enhance higher-order thinking skills through human-AI interaction. By encouraging cognitive engagement rather than providing direct answers, extraheric AI aims to mitigate the risks of over-reliance on AI, fostering creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving. The findings suggest that this approach can lead to improved cognitive skills and a balanced partnership between humans and AI. For those looking to integrate AI into their workflows, consider how extraheric AI can stimulate your thinking processes rather than just providing solutions.

**🧠 Fostering Higher-Order Thinking**
- **Encouragement of Cognitive Engagement**: Extraheric AI promotes higher-order thinking by asking questions and presenting alternative perspectives, which stimulates users' cognitive activities.
- **Mitigation of Deskilling Risks**: By engaging users in critical thinking, extraheric AI helps prevent deskilling that can arise from over-reliance on AI technologies.
- **Support for Creativity and Problem-Solving**: The framework is designed to enhance creativity and problem-solving skills, essential for effective task completion.

**🤝 Human-AI Interaction Design**
- **Interactive Tutoring Systems (ITS)**: Extraheric AI can be integrated into various domains, including ITS, to provide personalized learning experiences that foster higher-order thinking.
- **Effortful User Interfaces**: These interfaces intentionally increase cognitive load to encourage deeper learning and engagement with the material.
- **Diverse Interaction Strategies**: The framework includes various strategies, such as questioning and scaffolding, to enhance user engagement and cognitive development.

**⚠️ Risks of Over-Reliance on AI**
- **Acceptance of Misinformation**: Over-reliance on AI can lead users to accept information without critical examination, increasing the risk of misinformation.
- **Cognitive Load Management**: The paper discusses the importance of managing cognitive load to ensure that users remain engaged and do not become overly dependent on AI.
- **Need for Balanced AI Integration**: A balanced approach to AI integration is necessary to maintain human cognitive skills while leveraging AI capabilities.

**📊 Evaluation Methods**
- **Alignment with Cognitive Load Theory**: Evaluation methods are designed to align with cognitive load theory, ensuring that users can effectively engage with the material.
- **Utilization of Bloom’s Taxonomy**: The framework incorporates Bloom’s taxonomy to assess users' cognitive development and higher-order thinking skills.
- **Performance-Based Assessments**: Future research should explore performance-based assessments to evaluate the effectiveness of extraheric AI in promoting higher-order thinking.

**🔍 Questioning Techniques**
- **Stimulating Critical Thinking**: Extraheric AI employs questioning techniques to encourage users to think critically about the content they engage with.
- **Encouraging Perspective-Taking**: By asking users to consider different viewpoints, extraheric AI fosters a deeper understanding of complex issues.
- **Learning by Teaching**: This approach allows users to engage actively with the material, enhancing their learning experience.

**🛠️ Scaffolding Strategies**
- **Customized Support for Learning**: Extraheric AI can provide tailored support to help users learn new concepts while gradually reducing assistance as they gain competence.
- **Facilitating Task Completion**: By taking on parts of a task, extraheric AI allows users to focus on higher-order thinking without overwhelming them.
- **Promoting Self-Efficacy**: As users develop their skills, they may become more confident and less reliant on AI support.

**📈 Future Research Directions**
