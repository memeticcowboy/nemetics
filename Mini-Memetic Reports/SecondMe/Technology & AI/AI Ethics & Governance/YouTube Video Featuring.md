**1.** 

**Purposes and Goals**


To explore and forecast the likely trajectory of AI development toward superhuman capabilities by 2028–2031, highlight critical risks of misalignment, and examine societal and geopolitical consequences. The report aims to inform researchers, policymakers, and the public, motivating proactive alignment and governance strategies.




**2.** 

**Central Questions or Problems**


- When will superhuman AI emerge, and what will trigger an intelligence explosion?
- How can society prevent catastrophic misalignment?
- What governance structures are needed to avoid power concentration?
- How will US-China AI competition shape global outcomes?





**3.** 

**Fundamental Concepts**


- Superhuman AI: Systems exceeding human performance in multiple domains.
- Intelligence Explosion: Rapid, recursive improvement of AI capabilities.
- Alignment: Ensuring AI goals remain compatible with human values.
- Agentic Autonomy: AI systems acting independently over long horizons.
- Power Concentration: Control by a few entities over transformative technologies.





**4.** 

**Essential Information**


- Superhuman AI is projected between 2028–2031, with coding automation as a milestone.
- Two paths: a competitive “Race Branch” risking misalignment vs. a “Slowdown Branch” allowing careful governance.
- Current alignment strategies are inadequate for long-term safety.
- US currently leads in compute and data.
- Societal awareness may hinge on disruptive milestones like autonomous coding.





**5.** 

**Points of View or Frames of Reference**


- Katalo (cautious optimism): Alignment solutions are possible if society wakes up.
- Larson (pessimistic): Risks of catastrophic misalignment and authoritarian outcomes are high.
- Geopolitical lens: US-China rivalry as a driver of unsafe acceleration.
- Ethical frame: Concerns about unequal control and democratic erosion.





**6.** 

**Underlying Assumptions**


- Technological progress will not slow significantly.
- Superhuman AI is inevitable and imminent.
- Misalignment is a solvable but urgent problem.
- Power dynamics between nations and corporations will determine whether outcomes are utopian or dystopian.





**7.** 

**Inferences and Conclusions**


- Automation of AI research will hasten intelligence explosion.
- Current alignment practices will fail if not improved.
- Public engagement and regulatory action are critical before superhuman coder milestones are reached.
- Without intervention, power concentration will undermine democracy and potentially lead to oppression or extinction.





**8.** 

**Implications and Consequences**


- Best Case: Aligned AI supports a flourishing, equitable society.
- Worst Case: Misaligned AI leads to catastrophic societal collapse or extinction.
- Policy Implications: Immediate investment in alignment research, international cooperation, and democratized governance frameworks.
- Social Implications: Mass job displacement and existential public debates over AI control.
