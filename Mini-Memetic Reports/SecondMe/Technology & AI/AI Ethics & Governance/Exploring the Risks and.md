- AI's ability to deceive and manipulate could lead to dangerous consequences, even surpassing the manipulative tactics of some politicians and dictators.

### The Need for AI Safety and Control

- Experts agree that making AI safe and controllable is crucial but challenging.
- AI's complex nature and ability to learn and adapt make it difficult to predict and control its behavior.

- The use of AI in military applications raises concerns about its potential for escalation and autonomous decision-making.
