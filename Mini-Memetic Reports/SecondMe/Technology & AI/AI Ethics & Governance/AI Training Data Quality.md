# AI Training Data Quality Crisis

Created at 2024/08/04 8:07 PM

Researchers at the University of Cambridge found that training AI models on AI-generated content leads to degraded output quality. This “cannibalistic” approach quickly causes models to produce nonsensical results, similar to the effects of species inbreeding. High-quality, human-created data is essential to prevent this degradation, increasing the cost and complexity of AI development as AI-generated content proliferates online.

## Insight
Researchers at the University of Cambridge have discovered that training AI models on AI-generated content leads to significant degradation in output quality. This 'cannibalistic' approach results in models producing nonsensical results, akin to species inbreeding. High-quality, human-created data is essential to prevent this degradation, which increases the cost and complexity of AI development. If you're working with AI, prioritize sourcing high-quality, human-created data to maintain model integrity. Now, let's delve deeper into the findings and implications of this study.

**🔍 AI Training Data Quality**
- **High-quality data is essential**: Human-created data is crucial to maintain the integrity and performance of AI models.
- **AI-generated content degrades models**: Using AI-generated content for training leads to rapid degradation in model output quality.
- **Mathematical analysis confirms issues**: Researchers used mathematical analysis to demonstrate the problem affecting large-language models and AI image generators.

**⚠️ Model Degradation**
- **Cannibalistic training approach**: Training new model iterations on data generated by previous versions leads to nonsensical outputs.
- **Example of degradation**: An AI model included irrelevant details about jackrabbits in an article about English church towers.
- **Speed of degradation**: Researchers were surprised by how quickly the quality of the models deteriorated when using AI-generated content.

**💸 Increased AI Development Costs**
- **Rising cost of quality data**: The need for high-quality, human-created data will increase the cost of building AI models.
- **Pollution of open web data**: As AI-generated content proliferates online, it will pollute the data sources AI companies rely on.
- **Impact on AI companies**: AI companies will need to rethink their data sourcing strategies due to the increasing presence of AI-generated content.

**🔄 Inbreeding Analogy**
- **Comparison to species inbreeding**: The degradation of AI models is compared to the collapse of species due to inbreeding.
- **Lack of data diversity**: Just as inbreeding reduces genetic diversity, using AI-generated content reduces data diversity, leading to poor model performance.

**📊 Study and Findings**
- **Published in Nature**: The study was published in the journal Nature, highlighting the significance of the findings.
- **Impact on various AI models**: The problem affects both large-language models like ChatGPT and AI image generators like Midjourney and DALL-E.

**🖼️ Visual Evidence**
- **Distorted image series**: A series of images showed increasing distortion when an AI image model was trained on data from its predecessor.
- **Visual comparison**: A photo comparison illustrated the degradation from a realistic portrait to an abstract, nonsensical image.

**🗣️ Expert Opinions**
- **Zakhar Shumaylov's warning**: AI researcher Zakhar Shumaylov emphasized the importance of careful data selection to avoid model degradation.
- **Hany Farid's analogy**: Computer scientist Hany Farid compared the issue to species inbreeding, stressing the need for diverse data.

**🎨 Copyright and Ethical Concerns**
- **Artists' and creators' reactions**: Content creators are concerned about the use of their work to build AI models without proper compensation.
- **Ethical implications**: The proliferation of AI-generated content raises ethical questions about data usage and intellectual property.
