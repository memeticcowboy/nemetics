- **Current Technology Trends**: The current trend in technology development is focused on labor-automating approaches, driven by the choices of those in power rather than inevitability. **📚 Insights from "Power and Progress"**

- **Technology Revolution Insights**: The book "Power and Progress" by MIT economists Jeroen Ossimoglu and Simon Johnson discusses how technology revolutions have historically taken a labor-automating approach, not due to inevitability, but because of deliberate choices by those in power. [_TIMESTAMP_](44-60)
- **Decision-Making in Technology Design**: The design of technology is influenced by the desire to sell to executives, promoting the idea of reducing costs by replacing human labor with AI. This reflects a strategic choice rather than a technological necessity. [_TIMESTAMP_](60-69)

**🔄 The Case for Labor-Assistive Technologies**

- **Benefits of Labor-Assistive AI**: Studies suggest that developing AI tools to assist rather than replace professionals, such as doctors and teachers, can lead to better outcomes in healthcare and education. This approach enhances the capabilities of professionals rather than rendering them obsolete. [_TIMESTAMP_](76-100)
- **Improved Outcomes with AI Assistance**: AI tools that assist professionals can lead to improved cancer diagnoses and better educational outcomes for students, demonstrating the potential benefits of labor-assistive technologies. [_TIMESTAMP_](81-103)
