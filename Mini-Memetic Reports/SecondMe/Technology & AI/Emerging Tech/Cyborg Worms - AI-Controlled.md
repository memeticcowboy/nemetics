**🧠 Neuroengineering**
- **Neural activity manipulation through optogenetics**: The worms were genetically engineered to respond to light, allowing researchers to control specific neurons. This manipulation of neural activity is a key aspect of neuroengineering, showcasing how external stimuli can influence behavior.
- **Varied responses based on genetic lines**: The study tested six genetic lines of worms, each with different numbers of light-sensitive neurons. The varying responses to light stimulation highlight the complexity of neural control and its implications for neuroengineering.
