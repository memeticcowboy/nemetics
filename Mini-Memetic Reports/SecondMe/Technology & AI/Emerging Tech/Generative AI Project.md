## Insight
Gartner forecasts that by the end of 2025, 30% of generative AI projects will be abandoned post-proof of concept due to challenges like poor data quality and unclear business value. This report highlights the importance of understanding the financial implications and strategic planning necessary for successful generative AI implementation. If you're involved in such projects, consider focusing on data quality and clear ROI metrics to enhance your chances of success. Let's delve deeper into the key findings and recommendations.

**📉 Project Abandonment Risks**
- **30% Abandonment Rate**: Gartner predicts that 30% of generative AI projects will be abandoned after the proof of concept phase by 2025, primarily due to issues like poor data quality and inadequate risk controls.
- **Financial Burden**: Organizations are increasingly feeling the financial strain of developing and deploying generative AI models, with costs ranging from $5 million to $20 million.
- **Impatience for Returns**: Executives are becoming impatient for returns on generative AI investments, leading to increased scrutiny and pressure on project viability.

**💰 Financial Justification Challenges**
- **Difficulty in Justifying Investments**: Organizations struggle to justify the substantial investments in generative AI, as the productivity enhancements are hard to translate into immediate financial benefits.
- **Higher Tolerance for Indirect Value**: Generative AI requires a higher tolerance for indirect, future financial investment criteria, which many CFOs find challenging.
- **Skewed Investment Allocation**: This reluctance can lead to skewed investment allocations favoring tactical outcomes over strategic ones.

**📊 Realizing Business Value**
- **Reported Business Improvements**: Early adopters report an average of 15.8% revenue increase, 15.2% cost savings, and 22.6% productivity improvement, highlighting the potential benefits of generative AI.
- **Use Case Variability**: The benefits derived from generative AI vary significantly by use case, job type, and worker skill level, making it essential to tailor approaches.
- **Delayed Impact Realization**: The impact of generative AI may not be immediately evident, often materializing over time, which organizations need to account for.

**📈 Calculating Business Impact**
- **Establishing Direct ROI**: Organizations can calculate the direct ROI and future value impact of generative AI by analyzing business value against total costs.
- **Opportunity for Expansion**: If business outcomes meet or exceed expectations, there is an opportunity to expand investments and scale generative AI usage.
- **Exploring Alternatives**: Conversely, if outcomes fall short, organizations may need to explore alternative innovation scenarios to optimize resource allocation.

**🔍 Strategic Resource Allocation**
- **Informed Investment Decisions**: Understanding the business value and costs associated with generative AI helps organizations make informed investment decisions.
- **Scaling Across User Base**: Successful projects can lead to scaling generative AI innovations across a broader user base or additional business divisions.
- **Effective Path Forward**: Insights from project outcomes guide organizations in determining the most effective path forward for future investments.
