- **Practical Vision Solutions**: The primary goal of IXI's autofocus glasses is to address vision problems rather than simply adding smart features. **🔍 Eye-Tracking Technology Explained**
- **Real-Time Focus Adjustment**: The glasses employ a low-power sensor that tracks eye movements by emitting light pulses and measuring reflections. This allows the glasses to determine whether the wearer is looking at something near or far, adjusting the lens focus accordingly with a response time of approximately 0.2 seconds.

- **Liquid Crystal Lens Mechanism**: The lenses consist of a thin layer of liquid crystal sandwiched between two plastic layers. When an electric field is applied, the liquid crystals change their structure, bending light differently to achieve the desired focus. This innovative design enables dynamic vision correction without the bulk of traditional eyewear.

- **Potential for Full Field of Vision**: IXI's autofocus glasses aim to provide a full field of vision across the entire lens, overcoming the limitations of traditional bifocal and progressive lenses, which often have restricted usable zones and distortions at the edges.
