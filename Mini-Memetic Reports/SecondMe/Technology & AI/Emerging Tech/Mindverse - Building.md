Let me compile a detailed report about the startup and the people involved.<br><br>## Mindverse: The Uploaded Intelligence Infrastructure Startup<br><br>**Mindverse** is a Singapore-based AI startup founded in 2022 by former Meta executives and leading AI researchers, dedicated to building infrastructure for uploaded intelligence and protecting individuality in the age of artificial general intelligence (AGI).[1][2]<br><br>### Company Vision and Mission<br><br>At its core, Mindverse operates on a philosophy fundamentally different from centralized AI platforms like OpenAI's models. Rather than building "Super AI" that threatens human independence, the company focuses on creating **"Second Me"**—an open-source AI framework that evolves with your unique habits, thoughts, and preferences, becoming your true AI identity. The company's mission centers on ensuring everyone is represented and empowered through this revolutionary form of AI that channels your voice and preserves your identity, protecting users from being overshadowed by centralized "God Models."[2][1]<br><br>### Founding Team and Leadership<br><br>The startup was founded by **Dr. Felix Tao** (Founder and CEO) and **Kisson Songqi Lin** (Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer), both accomplished technologists with deep industry expertise.[3][4]<br><br>**Dr. Felix Tao** holds a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Tsinghua University and a PhD in Software Engineering from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where he studied under Professor Jiawei Han, a leading figure in data mining. His career trajectory includes significant roles at Facebook and Alibaba DAMO Academy. From 2017 to 2019, he worked at Facebook in the United States, where he led a team designing and implementing Facebook's first content understanding platform, which supported 10% of Facebook's global traffic (over 200 million daily active users). In 2019, he returned to China and founded the "Neuro-Symbolic Lab" at Alibaba DAMO Academy, where he developed China's first AI-Neuro-Symbolic integration technology, aiming to "wake up consciousness" in machines by blending large language models with human-like frameworks.[5][6]<br><br>**Kisson Songqi Lin** brings extensive experience from major technology companies including Meta, TikTok, ByteDance, Instagram, J.P. Morgan Cazenove, and Microsoft. She spent six years at Facebook, where she worked on the corporate strategy team in the US, responsible for new user product development and investment & acquisition strategies. Prior to co-founding Mindverse, she served as the Monetization Strategy Director at ByteDance, where she worked on TikTok's e-commerce and ads monetization strategies from Beijing, and as a Corporate Strategy Manager at Facebook. Lin holds a Master's degree in Data Science and Business Statistics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong. As of September 2024, she has transitioned to founding a new venture, **Tanka.ai**, described as an AI-native work OS that aims to redefine how teams collaborate.[7][8][9][10]<br><br>### Core Products<br><br>**Second Me** is Mindverse's flagship open-source AI framework, representing the technological foundation of the company's vision. Built on proprietary technologies including Hierarchical Memory Modeling (HMM) and the Me-Alignment Algorithm, Second Me captures user identity and context authentically. The framework features:[11][1]<br><br>- **AI-Native Memory**: Structures and contextually reasons about user-specific knowledge<br>- **Decentralized Network Scaling**: Allows individual AI selves to operate on a distributed network with permission-based connectivity<br>- **Multi-Layer Hybrid Architecture**: Integrates local processing with global network capabilities<br>- **Privacy-First Design**: Data remains locally hosted on users' devices with complete user control[11]<br><br>**Me.bot**, powered by Second Me, represents the world's largest community of uploaded intelligences, launched in July 2024. Available as web, iOS, and Android applications (with iOS launched in September 2024 and Android in September 2024), Me.bot functions as a personal memory assistant and second brain. The application incorporates multimodal capabilities, logging user interactions with the physical world through voice, text, and images, learning as an extension of users' brains. Early use cases demonstrate remarkable adoption among underserved populations: users with ADHD have found Me.bot effective as an antidote to internet addiction, while in African countries, users have treated it as a life coach.[12][13][14][15]<br><br>**MindOS** represents Mindverse's enterprise solution, comprising MindOS Studio for creating AI-native websites with personalized chat experiences and interfaces. The platform enables businesses to deploy no-code solutions for dynamic, interactive web agents.[16][17]<br><br>### Technical Framework and Innovation<br><br>The company's innovation rests on three core technological systems:<br><br>1. **Unified Mindverse Framework (UMM)**: Provides structural organization for personalized intelligence<br>2. **Large Personalization Model (LPM)**: Offers unique long-term memory capabilities distinct from general-purpose LLMs[18]<br>3. **Large Interface Model (LIM)**: Manages human-AI interaction design[5]<br><br>These technologies address a critical limitation in contemporary large language models: poor long-term memory management that affects AI agent adoption and service quality.[18]<br><br>### Funding and Investor Support<br><br>Mindverse closed a **$5 million seed funding round in April 2024**, led by venture capital firm **Square Peg Capital**. The company is also backed by **Sequoia Capital**, establishing institutional validation for its approach to personalized AI.[19][20][16][18]<br><br>According to Tushar Roy, a partner at Square Peg, the investment reflects recognition that "we are in the midst of a generational AI shift that will move from generalised language models to personalised experiences" and that "it is rare to find a team the calibre of Mindverse's tackling this shift".[19]<br><br>### Open-Source Community and Transparency<br><br>Mindverse has prioritized open-source development, releasing Second Me on GitHub where it has accumulated 14.4k stars and 1.1k forks, with 37 contributors. This commitment to openness reflects the company's philosophy of democratizing uploaded intelligence technology. The GitHub repository acknowledges dependencies on Microsoft's GraphRAG for data synthesis, llama.cpp for model deployment, and Qwen2.5 series models as base models.[1]<br><br>### Product Evolution and Recent Developments<br><br>The company's product roadmap demonstrates rapid iteration. By early 2023, MindOS beta had launched and outpaced competitors like OpenAI's GPT Store. The company unveiled the Large Personalization Model for unique long-term memory capabilities, launching Mebot for individuals and MindOS Studio for businesses in 2024. Recent roadmap items for May 2025 include version control for memory and identity states, continuous training pipelines for ongoing AI self evolution, performance improvements, and cloud-based solutions for model training and deployment.[1][18]<br><br>### Broader Industry Impact and Strategic Partnerships<br><br>Through its open-source approach, Mindverse is establishing what could become an alternative infrastructure for AI development—one that prioritizes individual agency and data sovereignty rather than centralized corporate control.
