# Summary of Key Points from "The Singleton Paradox"

Created at 2025/02/03 8:12 PM

**Part 1**

**[Source: Ben Zweibelson](https://www.usmcu.edu/Outreach/Marine-Corps-University-Press/MCU-Journal/JAMS-vol-14-no-1/The-Singleton-Paradox/)**

#### **1. Technological Evolution and Warfare:**

- **Historical Context:** Technology has historically advanced military strategy—from the wheel to satellites.
- **AI's Unique Disruption:** Unlike past military technologies, AI could redefine the very nature of warfare, not just improve existing practices.
- **Human-Machine Teaming:** Evolving from simple analog tools to complex systems where AI may dominate decision-making.

#### **2. The Concept of the Singleton:**

- **Definition:** Originating from Nick Bostrom, a singleton is an entity (possibly an AI) that holds total, centralized decision-making power over human civilization.
- **The Paradox:** A singleton could either:
    - **End War:** By making conflict obsolete through perfect coordination and control.
    - **Trigger New Conflicts:** As nations race to achieve singleton status, potentially leading to catastrophic wars.

#### **3. The Singleton in Security Affairs:**

- **Beyond Tactical AI:** Current military focus is on narrow AI applications (targeting, surveillance). The singleton concept demands rethinking strategy, ethics, and decision-making frameworks entirely.
- **Potential Forms:** Could be an AI, a genetically modified human, or a cybernetically enhanced being with intelligence vastly beyond human capabilities.

#### **4. Implications for Military Strategy:**

- **Break from Tradition:** Conventional military doctrines are rooted in human-centered decision-making. A singleton could develop strategic concepts beyond human comprehension.
- **Ethical Dilemmas:** Who controls the singleton? How do we ensure it aligns with human values? What if rival powers develop competing singletons?

***

### **Key Questions Raised:**

1. **What is the ethical framework for developing an AI singleton?**
2. **How would human autonomy be preserved if strategic decisions are made by a superintelligent AI?**
3. **Could human operators meaningfully participate in decision-making with a singleton in control?**
4. **Is humanity prepared for the societal shifts that an AI singleton could trigger?**
5. **Could competing singleton entities lead to global instability rather than peace?**

***
