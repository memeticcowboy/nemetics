# AI as Cognitive Infrastructure: Rethinking Human-Machine Interaction

**Emerging interdisciplinary framing: “AI as cognitive infrastructure”**<br>A 2025 paper proposes a new domain (Cognitive Infrastructure Studies). It reframes AI not merely as a tool, but as a pre‑conscious cognitive substrate that conditions relevance judgments and epistemic agency at scale. The framework aims to integrate individual cognition, distributed public reasoning, and cultural knowledge flows — a conceptual leap linking attention ecology, epistemic trust biases, and social epistemologies.

## Insight


**🧠 AI as Cognitive Infrastructure: A New Paradigm in Understanding Human Cognition**

- **💡 Introduction to Cognitive Infrastructure Studies**: The paper proposes the establishment of Cognitive Infrastructure Studies (CIS) as a novel interdisciplinary domain focusing on AI's role as a foundational cognitive infrastructure. This reframing views AI not merely as a functional tool, but as a subtle cognitive substrate that influences relevance judgments and epistemic agency unconsciously within digital societies.

- **🌐 Redefining AI's Role in Knowledge**: The authors highlight that contemporary research often neglects how AI systems shape human cognition at a pre-conscious level. This crucial oversight needs addressing as it limits the understanding of distributed cognition, emphasizing that AI has a significant role in conditioning knowledge and actionable insights.

- **🔍 Understanding the Mechanics of Cognitive Infrastructures**: AI systems function as semantic infrastructures that enable the transmission of meaning and automate relevance judgments. This shift transforms the locus of epistemic agency, where individuals may increasingly rely on non-human systems for evaluating what is knowable, effectively reshaping public reasoning processes.

**📊 The Impact on Individual Cognition**

- **🚶 Cognitive Dependency**: The study discusses how AI creates cognitive dependencies at the individual level. Individuals start to rely on AI for decision-making, altering their cognitive processes regarding information evaluation and relevance judgment.

- **⚖️ Implications for Democratic Deliberation**: The influence of cognitive infrastructures extends to collective cognition, impacting how democratic deliberations occur. AI's way of presenting information can sway public opinion and the democratic process significantly.

- **🌆 Societal Effects on Governance**: At a societal scale, these infrastructures play a critical role in governance and policy-making, determining what information becomes actionable and how societal knowledge flows.

**🔗 Methodological Innovations for Analysis**

- **🔍 Infrastructure Breakdown Methodologies**: The paper introduces innovative experimental approaches to study the invisible influences of AI preprocessing on cognition. These methodologies help in identifying cognitive dependencies by methodically removing AI influences after periods of adaptation.

- **📉 Gaps Across Disciplines**: CIS also aims to bridge crucial gaps in existing disciplines. Cognitive science is lacking broad preprocessing analysis, while digital sociology struggles with individual cognitive mechanisms, and computational methods often miss cultural dynamics in knowledge transmission.

- **🎓 Interdisciplinary Collaboration Needed**: To effectively study these dynamics, there is a necessity for unprecedented integration of diverse disciplinary methods, fostering collaboration between cognitive science, sociology, and computational fields.

**🌍 Scenarios Exploring Cognitive Infrastructure Effects**

- **🔄 Individual Scenarios**: The paper provides narrative scenarios that illustrate varying impacts across individual, collective, and societal levels regarding how cognitive infrastructures reshape cognitive dependencies.

- **🏛️ Collective Scenarios**: It highlights scenarios pertaining to community knowledge flows, how communities engage with information, and the influence of AI on group cohesion and decision-making.

- **📚 Cultural Scenarios**: In cultural contexts, the implications are explored, detailing how AI affects cultural narratives, memory, and the transmission of knowledge across generations.

**🧪 Conclusion and Future Research Directions**

- **📈 Expanding CIS Research**: The introduction of CIS encourages the necessity for further research into the pre-conscious influence of AI systems on cognition, stressing the importance of aligning technological advancement with human cognitive processes.

- **⚙️ Policy Implications**: The findings raise important policy considerations regarding the ethical implications of automated relevance judgments made by AI systems, calling for careful regulation of AI's role in shaping public knowledge.

- **💪 Call to Action for Researchers**: Finally, the paper invites researchers to adopt the suggested methodologies in their studies to better understand cognitive infrastructures and their pervasive influence across various domains of human cognition.
