# Unlocking Gesture Control in Dynamic Environments

Created at 2025/11/21 8:06 PM

Engineers at the University of California San Diego have developed a wearable system that enables people to control machines using everyday gestures, even in dynamic environments.


The system combines stretchable electronics with artificial intelligence to reliably recognize gesture signals, overcoming a long-standing challenge in wearable technology.


Potential uses for the technology include enabling patients in rehabilitation, industrial workers, and first responders to control robotic aids and tools with natural gestures, even in high-motion or hazardous environments.
