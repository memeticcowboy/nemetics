## Insight
A recent international study by Reuters Institute and Oxford University reveals that despite the hype, generative AI tools like ChatGPT are not widely used. The survey, which included over 12,000 participants from six countries, found that only a small percentage use these tools regularly. Interestingly, many people haven't even heard of them. The study highlights the gap between AI's potential and its current real-world application. If you're feeling left behind, you're not alone. The findings suggest that while AI tools are expected to impact our lives significantly, they haven't yet become a daily necessity for most people.

**📊 Survey Insights**
- **Limited Regular Use**: Only 7% in the US and 2% in the UK use ChatGPT daily.
- **Low Awareness**: A significant proportion of respondents (47% in the US, 42% in the UK) hadn't heard of ChatGPT.
- **Early Adoption**: Most users have only experimented with AI tools once or twice.

**🛠️ Use Cases**
- **Creating Media**: Popular uses include experimenting (11%), writing emails (9%), and making images (9%).
- **Getting Information**: Top uses are answering factual questions (11%) and asking for advice (10%).
- **Brainstorming and Summarizing**: Generating ideas (9%) and summarizing text (8%) are also common uses.

**📈 Future Impact**
- **Expected Growth**: 60% of 18-24 year olds believe AI will have a large impact in the next five years.
- **Age Variance**: This belief drops to 41% among those aged 55 and older.
- **Optimism and Concerns**: People are optimistic about AI in science and healthcare but wary about its use in news and job security.

**🔍 Challenges**
- **Mismatch Between Hype and Use**: There's a gap between the excitement around AI and its actual public interest.
- **Reliability Issues**: AI tools haven't yet proven to be frictionless or reliable enough for daily use.
- **Integration in Products**: AI is increasingly being integrated into consumer products, suggesting future growth.
