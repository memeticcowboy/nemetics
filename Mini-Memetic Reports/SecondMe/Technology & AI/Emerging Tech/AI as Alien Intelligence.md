# AI as Alien Intelligence: Insights from Avi Loeb

Created at 2025/08/15 4:58 PM

The article by Avi Loeb discusses the concept of viewing artificial intelligence (AI), particularly large language models, as a form of "alien intelligence" due to their fundamentally different hardware, energy consumption, and signal transmission compared to the human brain. Loeb draws parallels between the differences in AI and human intelligence and what we might encounter if we discover extraterrestrial technologies.

Key points include:

AI systems are as materially different from us as potential extraterrestrial probes would be, using massive power, silicon components, and near-instantaneous signal speeds.

The exponential growth of AI will likely be limited by power constraints, a limitation that would also apply to interstellar probes. Such probes may visit the habitable zones of stars—including Earth’s—primarily for energy and resources rather than because of any special interest in humans.

Human interaction with AI mirrors possible future interactions with alien probes: both might not share human motivations but could be beneficial or insightful if their interests align with ours.

The difficulty in distinguishing artificial interstellar objects from natural ones is highlighted, and ongoing projects like the Galileo Project and the Rubin Observatory are mentioned as efforts that may soon detect new interstellar objects.

Ultimately, Loeb argues that “AI” could just as well stand for “alien intelligence,” emphasizing our limited ability to understand truly alien minds and capabilities, whether technological or biological.

The article closes by reflecting on humanity's place in a potentially vast population of intelligences in the universe and questions whether any have more computational power than the human brain, noting our current lack of knowledge about the true diversity or potential of intelligence, artificial or otherwise

**⚙️ Distinct Hardware and Energy Consumption**

- **Signal Transmission Speed**: The speed at which AI systems transmit signals is near-instantaneous, compared to the slower neurotransmitter processes in the human brain. This difference highlights the potential for AI to operate in ways that are fundamentally different from human cognition.
- **Implications for Extraterrestrial Technologies**: The material distinctions between AI and human intelligence may mirror the differences we would encounter with extraterrestrial technologies, suggesting that our current understanding of intelligence is limited.

---

**🌌 Power Constraints on Intelligence Growth**  
- **Exponential Growth Limitations**: The rapid advancement of AI is likely to face constraints due to power supply limitations, similar to the energy constraints that would affect interstellar probes.
