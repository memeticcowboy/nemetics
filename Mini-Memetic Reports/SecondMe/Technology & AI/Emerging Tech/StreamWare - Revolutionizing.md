## Insight
StreamWare, a new software developed by researchers from Harvard, NJIT, and USC, aims to revolutionize live data analytics by enabling real-time decision-making. This innovative approach, supported by major tech companies and a National Science Foundation grant, promises to transform various fields, from e-commerce to military planning. **🔍Reimagining Data Science**
- **Objective of StreamWare**: StreamWare aims to analyze multiple sources of live data in real-time, moving away from traditional data collection and processing methods.
- **Key Researchers**: The project is led by David Bader (NJIT), David Brooks (Harvard), and Viktor Prasanna (USC).
- **Support and Collaboration**: The project has garnered support from AMD, Google, Intel, Microsoft, and Xilinx, with plans to involve more companies like IBM and Qualcomm.

**💡Innovative Approach**
- **Streaming Analytics**: StreamWare focuses on creating streaming analytics capable of making decisions on the fly, rather than waiting to collect and process data centrally.
- **Interdisciplinary Team**: The project involves a vertically integrated system covering applications, algorithms, software, and architecture.
- **Planning Grant**: Supported by a $250,000 National Science Foundation planning grant to study improvements in high-transaction online applications and underlying hardware.

**🔬Research and Development**
- **Testing and Prototyping**: The team will test new combinations of open-source algorithms and hardware accelerators on data sets from astrophysics, smart grids, and network science.
- **Prototype Timeline**: Prototypes are expected to be ready for demonstration within 3-6 months.
- **Impact on Applications**: The project aims to positively influence a wide range of scientific and engineering applications.

**📚Educational Impact**
- **Curriculum Development**: The project results will influence course curricula in data science and related fields, starting with Harvard.
- **Involvement of Students**: The project promotes scientific education by involving high school and university students, especially female and underrepresented minority students.
- **Future Research Directions**: Outcomes will include proposals for future research activities, publications, and curriculum development for graduate and undergraduate courses.

**🔮Future Prospects**
- **Predictive Analytics**: The ultimate goal is to advance towards predictive analytics using machine learning and artificial intelligence.
- **Big Data Research**: The developed data science kernel catalogue will help the big data research community prioritize performance improvements.
- **Long-term Vision**: StreamWare aims to set new standards in streaming data science, making real-time analytics more sophisticated and scalable.
