# New AI Model Outperforms ChatGPT in Reasoning

Created at 2025/11/19 7:20 PM

Scientists have developed a new AI model called Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM) that outperforms large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT in reasoning tasks.


HRM is inspired by the human brain's hierarchical and multi-timescale processing and achieved impressive results in the ARC-AGI benchmark, scoring higher than models like o3-mini-high and Claude 3.7.


HRM uses a different approach to reasoning, executing sequential tasks in a single forward pass without explicit supervision, and has achieved near-perfect performance in tasks like complex Sudoku puzzles and optimal path-finding in mazes.

## Insight


**🧠 Introducing the Hierarchical Reasoning Model: A New AI Frontier**  
Scientists have unveiled the Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM), an advanced AI model that surpasses traditional large language models like ChatGPT in reasoning tasks. This innovative model mimics the human brain's processing capability, achieving remarkable results on benchmark tests.

---

**📈 Performance on Benchmark Testing**  
- **Superior Performance in ARC-AGI Benchmark**: HRM scored 40.3% on the ARC-AGI-1 test, outperforming models like OpenAI's o3-mini-high and Anthropic's Claude 3.7. This demonstrates HRM's capability in handling complex reasoning tasks that challenge existing models.
- **Consistent Success in Harder Tests**: In the more rigorous ARC-AGI-2 benchmark, HRM still led with a score of 5%, showing a steady advantage over competitors, such as o3-mini-high (3%), indicating a reliable performance across different levels of difficulty.
- **Effective Utilization of Parameters**: While traditional models possess billions or trillions of parameters, HRM operates efficiently with only 27 million parameters, achieving high accuracy with fewer training samples.

---

**⚙️ Innovative Reasoning Processes**  
- **Single Forward Pass Execution**: Unlike traditional chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning, HRM processes sequential tasks in a single forward pass, allowing for efficiency and speed without requiring explicit guidance on intermediate steps.
- **Dual Module Architecture**: HRM employs a two-module system: a high-level module for abstract planning and a low-level module for quick computations, mirroring the multi-timescale processing of the human brain.
- **Iterative Refinement Techniques**: This model continuously refines its answers through short bursts of computation, which enhances the precision of its final outputs.

---

**🧩 Success in Challenging Tasks**  
- **Near-Perfect Sudoku Performance**: HRM demonstrated exceptional ability in solving complex Sudoku puzzles, tasks that commonly challenge other AI models, proving its advanced reasoning capacity.
- **Optimal Path-Finding Capabilities**: HRM excelled at solving maze navigation tasks, indicating that its reasoning components are well-suited for spatial and sequential problem-solving.
- **Real-World Application Potential**: The ability to accomplish these tasks efficiently suggests that HRM could be applied in various fields, from robotics to logistics, leveraging its advanced reasoning for practical solutions.
