# Google Achieves Quantum Breakthrough with Echoes Algorithm

Created at 2026/01/31 5:34 AM

<br><br>Concise summary<br><br>- Google's Quantum AI team, led by Hartmut Neven, has achieved a "verifiable quantum advantage" using their Quantum Echoes algorithm, demonstrating a significant step towards real-world applications of quantum computing.<br>- The team used a controlled rewind technique, called Quantum Echoes, to expose details that normally vanish in quantum systems, and measured an out-of-time-order correlator (OTOC) to capture the sensitivity of the system.<br>- This achievement exceeds the capabilities of classical supercomputers, with a 65-qubit data point taking approximately 2.1 hours on Google's quantum hardware, compared to an estimated 3.2 years on the Frontier supercomputer.<br><br><br>
