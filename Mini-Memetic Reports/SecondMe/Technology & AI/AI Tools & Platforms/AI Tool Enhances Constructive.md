**🤖 Enhancing Constructive Disagreement through AI in Education**

- **AI-Based Teaching Tool: Sway**: Sway is an innovative chat platform designed to facilitate constructive disagreement among students. It connects pairs of students with opposing views on topics chosen by their instructors, using AI to guide discussions and promote respectful dialogue. This tool aims to create a safe environment for students to express their opinions on sensitive issues, such as the Israel-Palestine conflict and abortion, which many students find challenging to discuss openly.

- **Discussion Guidance Mechanism**: The AI Guide integrated into Sway plays a crucial role in moderating conversations. It helps de-escalate tensions, ensures clarity in communication, and encourages students to articulate their arguments more effectively. By posing challenging questions and prompting for clarification, the Guide enhances students' reasoning skills and fosters a deeper understanding of differing perspectives.

- **Charitable Rephrasing Feature**: Sway includes a feature that suggests more constructive ways to express potentially unhelpful messages. This encourages students to communicate respectfully while maintaining the essence of their original thoughts. The immediate feedback provided by this feature helps students develop better communication habits, which are essential for engaging in meaningful discussions.

**📚 The Role of Behavioral Science in Education**

- **Scaffolded Learning Environment**: The development of Sway is grounded in behavioral science principles, emphasizing the importance of scaffolded learning. By providing structured support, Sway enables students to practice and refine their disagreement skills in a low-stakes environment, ultimately preparing them for more challenging discussions in real-life settings.

- **Empirical Evidence of Effectiveness**: Initial studies indicate that AI-facilitated discussions can significantly improve students' ability to engage constructively across political and moral divides. Feedback from students who participated in Sway has been overwhelmingly positive, with many reporting that their discussions were respectful and enlightening.

- **Integration with Traditional Learning**: Sway can be used as a preparatory tool for in-person discussions, enhancing the quality of classroom interactions. Instructors have noted that students who engage with Sway come to class better prepared, leading to more thoughtful and productive discussions.

**🧑‍🏫 Implementation Strategies for Educators**
