# Brilliant Labs Launches Halo Smart Glasses with AI Assistant

Created at 2025/08/10 5:17 PM

**Summary:**  

Brilliant Labs unveiled the Halo smart glasses, featuring a 0.2-inch color microOLED display, bone conduction speakers, and the AI assistant Noa. Designed for all-day wear with up to 14 hours battery life, they offer real-time AI interaction, memory assistance, and customizable voice-coded apps at a $300 price point[5][1][4].

## Insight


**👓 Brilliant Labs Launches Halo Smart Glasses: A New Era of Wearable Technology**  
Brilliant Labs has introduced the Halo smart glasses, a cutting-edge device designed for everyday use, featuring advanced AI capabilities and a lightweight design, all at an accessible price point.

---

**🧠 Advanced AI Integration**  
- **Real-Time Interaction**: The Halo smart glasses come equipped with the AI assistant Noa, which allows users to engage in real-time conversations and receive memory support. This feature enhances user experience by providing immediate assistance and information.
- **Memory Assistance**: Noa is designed to remember what users see and experience, making it a valuable tool for those who need help recalling information or tasks throughout the day.
- **Subscription Tiers**: Noa will be available in two versions: a free Basic version with limited features and a Plus subscription that offers full conversational capabilities, catering to different user needs.

---

**💡 Lightweight and Consumer-Friendly Design**  
- **Comfortable for All-Day Wear**: Weighing just over 40 grams, the Halo glasses are designed for extended use without discomfort, making them suitable for daily activities.
- **Vision Correction Support**: The glasses feature adjustable optics that accommodate various levels of vision correction, ensuring that users can enjoy both functionality and clarity.
- **Battery Life**: With a battery life of up to 14 hours, users can rely on the Halo glasses for a full day of use without needing to recharge frequently.

---

**🔧 Open-Source Customization**  
- **Developer-Friendly**: Brilliant Labs has made the Halo glasses open-source, allowing developers to access design files and source code on GitHub. This encourages innovation and customization within the tech community.
- **Personalized User Experience**: Users can modify and experiment with the software, tailoring the glasses to their specific needs and preferences, which enhances user engagement and satisfaction.
- **Community Collaboration**: By inviting developers to contribute, Brilliant Labs fosters a collaborative environment that can lead to new features and improvements over time.

---

**📱 Innovative Features and Specifications**  
- **MicroOLED Display**: The glasses feature a 0.2-inch color microOLED display, providing vibrant visuals for notifications and interactions without being intrusive.
- **Bone Conduction Audio**: Equipped with bone conduction speakers, the Halo glasses allow users to listen to audio without blocking their ears, maintaining awareness of their surroundings.
- **Advanced Sensors**: The integration of a low-power optical sensor and a six-axis IMU enables gesture recognition and tap detection, enhancing the interactivity of the device.

---

**💰 Affordable Pricing and Availability**  
- **Competitive Price Point**: Priced at $300, the Halo smart glasses offer a cost-effective solution for consumers looking for advanced wearable technology without breaking the bank.
- **Pre-Order Launch**: Pre-orders are currently available, with shipping expected to begin in Q4 2025, allowing early adopters to secure their devices ahead of the official release.
- **First Come, First Served**: The shipping process will be based on a first-come, first-served basis, encouraging prompt pre-orders to ensure timely delivery.

---

**🔊 Enhanced Audio Experience**  
- **Dual Microphones**: The inclusion of two microphones allows for clear audio input, facilitating effective communication with the AI assistant Noa.
- **Audio Activity Detection**: This feature ensures that the glasses can respond accurately to voice commands, improving the overall user experience.
- **Bluetooth Connectivity**: The Halo glasses utilize Bluetooth 5.3 for seamless connectivity with other devices, enhancing their functionality as a smart accessory.
