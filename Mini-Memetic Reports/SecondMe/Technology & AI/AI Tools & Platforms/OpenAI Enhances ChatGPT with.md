# OpenAI Enhances ChatGPT with Internal Data Integration

Created at 2025/03/29 2:23 PM

## Summary
OpenAI has enhanced ChatGPT by allowing users of the ChatGPT Team to reference internal databases, improving the context and relevance of the chatbot's responses. This feature supports integration with Google Drive and plans to include other internal knowledge sources like data analytics platforms and CRMs.
