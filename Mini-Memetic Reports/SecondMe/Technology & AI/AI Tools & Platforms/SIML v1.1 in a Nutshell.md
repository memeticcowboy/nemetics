SIML v1.1 in a Nutshell

SIML (Social Intelligence Meta Language) is a precise, atomic grammar for dissecting and mapping entangled social, cognitive, memetic, and institutional dynamics. It breaks phenomena into **Objects** (e.g., Actors, Narratives, Environments), **Relations** (e.g., Flow, Resonance, Conflict), and **Verbs** (NEMA for sensemaking, NEME for governance), forming analyzable graphs.

The v1.1 **MetaTaxonomy Overlay** adds optional "coordinates" (ontology, epistemics, time, qualia, etc.) to nodes for richer, transdisciplinary depth—without bloating the core. It enforces **no synonym drift**, binds qualia to relations, and integrates Sphere ecology diagnostics via strict question-translation rules, turning perceptual habitats into structured questions for Ω-permeability (openness to alterity).

Result: Clarity under complexity—maps ideology to ecology, critique to play, without prescriptive leakage. Operational for diagnosis, not doctrine.
