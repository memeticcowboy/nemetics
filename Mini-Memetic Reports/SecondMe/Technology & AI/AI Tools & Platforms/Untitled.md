# "Choosing Your Future AI Decisioning Platform: 5 Key Questions for Decision-Makers" by Nellie Aboudaram Maufras

Created at 2024/09/08 1:47 PM

The article discusses the importance of selecting the right AI Decisioning Platform (AIDP) to improve organizational decision-making. AIDPs combine technologies like business rules and machine learning to automate business decisions, while maintaining human oversight. The article presents five key questions decision-makers should ask: (1) What are the business needs and goals? (2) Is the platform flexible and scalable? (3) Does it provide transparency and interpretability? (4) What level of control and governance is offered? (5) What is the level of support and expertise provided?

The article also highlights insights from Forrester's Q2 2023 report, emphasizing the need for no-code tools, industry-specific accelerators, and rapid learning loops. Key AIDP providers include SAS, FICO, IBM, CRIF, Pegasystems, and others.

**Top three themes**:

1. Selecting the right AIDP based on business goals and platform scalability.
2. Importance of transparency, governance, and human control in AI decision-making.

## The Key Providers in Q2 2023

According to [The Forrester Wave™ last report](https://reprints2.forrester.com/#/assets/2/202/RES178488/report), when evaluating AIDP providers, companies should consider the following:

- Keep human business experts in control: AIDPs should have no-code tools that allow business experts to author, change, test, and deploy decision models easily.
- Have industry-specific solution accelerators: AIDP vendors may offer solution accelerators, such as training materials, sample code, or pre-built configurable modules, to provide a head start in implementing horizontal and vertical use cases.
- Support rapid learning loops: AIDPs should offer capabilities to monitor and measure the effectiveness of AI decisions, allowing for iteration and refinement based on real-world business outcomes.
