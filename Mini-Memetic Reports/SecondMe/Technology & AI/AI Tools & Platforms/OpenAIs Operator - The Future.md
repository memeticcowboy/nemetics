# OpenAI's Operator: The Future of AI Agents

Created at 2024/11/17 10:55 AM

OpenAI plans to launch “Operator,” an autonomous AI agent, in early 2025. Operator aims to revolutionize task management and process automation by performing functions like coding and travel reservations. CEO Sam Altman emphasizes that such agents represent significant progress in AI, offering natural interactions through browser-based execution. OpenAI will provide an API for developers to integrate Operator into various applications. This development intensifies competition among tech giants like Microsoft and Google, who are also advancing in AI assistant technologies.
