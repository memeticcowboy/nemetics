**📊 Key Features of the New AI Models**
- **Multimodal Capabilities**: The new "Qwen2.5-Omni-7B" model can process various types of inputs, including text, images, audio, and video. This versatility enables it to generate real-time text and natural speech responses, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.

- **Deployment on Edge Devices**: The model is designed for deployment on edge devices, such as mobile phones, ensuring high efficiency without compromising performance. This feature allows for the development of cost-effective AI agents that can operate in real-time environments.

- **Real-World Applications**: The AI model can be utilized in practical scenarios, such as assisting visually impaired individuals by providing real-time audio descriptions of their surroundings. This highlights the potential for AI to deliver tangible benefits to users in everyday situations.
