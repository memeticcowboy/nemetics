## Bloom's Category: Understand

### Mechanistic Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Explain how mechanistic thinking can be applied to understand different natural phenomena or machine operations.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Interpret diagrams and models that showcase mechanistic systems, such as flowcharts or blueprints.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Summarize studies or examples where mechanistic thinking has solved complex problems.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Discuss practical scenarios where mechanistic thinking might be applied, understanding the direct implications and outcomes.

### Living Systems Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Explain the principles of living systems thinking and how they differ from mechanistic views, particularly in understanding biological or ecological systems.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Interpret complex ecological data or organizational patterns through the lens of living systems theory.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Summarize the impact of living systems thinking on modern environmental management practices.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Discuss real-world applications of living systems thinking in business or sustainability projects, understanding their effectiveness and areas for improvement.

### Journey Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Explain the concept of journey thinking and how it can be used to frame personal growth or business development strategies.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Interpret narratives or case studies that utilize journey thinking, identifying key stages and transformations.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Summarize the benefits and challenges of using journey thinking in long-term planning and decision making.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Discuss potential future paths in personal or professional contexts using journey thinking, understanding its guidance for step-by-step development.
