**🚀 ElevenLabs Launches Flash**
- **Introduction of Flash Model**: ElevenLabs has unveiled Flash, a new text-to-speech model that boasts an impressive speed of converting text to speech in just 75 milliseconds, making it one of the fastest AI voice models available.
- **Focus on Real-Time Applications**: The Flash model is specifically designed for real-time applications, particularly enhancing the performance of conversational AI agents where quick response times are crucial.
- **Trade-offs in Expressiveness**: While Flash prioritizes speed, it sacrifices some expressiveness compared to slower models like Turbo. However, it is believed that most users will not notice this difference in real-time scenarios.

- **Speed and Efficiency**: Flash represents a significant advancement in text-to-speech technology, focusing on ultra-fast performance which is essential for modern AI applications.

**🌍 Multilingual Support in AI Models**
- **Two Variants of Flash**: Flash is available in two versions: v2, which supports only English, and v2.5, which accommodates 32 different languages, thus broadening its usability for a global audience.
- **Access and Integration**: Users can access both versions through ElevenLabs' Conversational AI platform or via API, making it easy to integrate into various applications.

**💡 Pricing Structure**
- **Cost Efficiency**: Both versions of Flash share the same pricing model, charging one credit for every two characters of text processed, which is competitive in the market.
