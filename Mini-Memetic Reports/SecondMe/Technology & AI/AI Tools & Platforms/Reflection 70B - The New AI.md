- **Real-time error correction**: Reflection 70B employs a unique reflection tuning technique that allows it to identify and correct its own mistakes during text generation, enhancing the accuracy of its outputs.
- **Improved reliability**: By integrating self-correction capabilities, the model significantly reduces the occurrence of hallucinations, a common issue in traditional LLMs.
