Alright dorks, buckle in. We have the timeline (mostly) figured out. 

2025 The Year of the Agent -- This is all but locked in. Agent frameworks and cognitive architectures are on the rise, and y'all are about to experience the "automation cliff" -- the automation doesn't do anything until you turn it on, at which point it does everything all at once. Keep an eye on desktop-using agents (remember my rule: anything you do at a computer can be automated). 

2026 The Year of the Robot -- Figure has started shipping to their first customers THIS WEEK, with Unitree and Boston Dynamics and everyone else not far behind. Given some lag time, plus mid 2026 is when NVIDIA says they'll have a "robot world model solved" (don't bet against NVIDIA kiddos) we'll be looking forward to mass rollout. The most capable robots will be expensive for a while (looking like about $80k) but light utility droids will probably settle around $2k to $10k. 

2027 The Year of Superintelligence* -- This one needs an asterisk because it won't be superintelligence proper. However, this is the year that I'm predicting that we'll start to see unsupervised recursive self improvement. The reason is because we need one more generation of frontier models to get to the tipping point of enough intelligence to take over their own research. We also need agents, but that will be figured out by then.
