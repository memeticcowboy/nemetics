# Meta's Breakthroughs in Brain-AI Language Decoding

## Summary

Meta's brain-ai research focuses on developing a system to decode brain signals into text, using a magnetic scanner and deep neural networks. The system can determine typed letters with high accuracy, offering insights into language production in the brain and potential applications in AI design.
