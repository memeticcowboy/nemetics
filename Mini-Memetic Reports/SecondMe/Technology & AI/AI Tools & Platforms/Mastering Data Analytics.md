## Insight
David Pidsley is dropping some serious knowledge on data analytics and decision making! Let's dive into his insights and see how we can make the most out of tools like Tableau, Power BI, and Google Analytics.

- David emphasizes not to start with a short list of technologies, especially when comparing different tools. It's like comparing apples to oranges! Instead, align your vision with your business strategy and consider both internal and external drivers of change. This ensures that your choice of tools is well-rounded and strategic.
- Understanding your stakeholders' objectives is crucial. Dive deep into target user personas and use cases. This means knowing who will use the data and how they will use it. By doing so, you can better weigh the critical capabilities you need from your data analytics tools.
- David suggests considering the overall ability to execute and the completeness of the vendors' vision. This means looking at how well a tool can be implemented and whether it aligns with your long-term goals. It's not just about the features but also about the tool's potential to grow with your business.
- He also recommends doing a proof of concept with real data. This is like a test drive for your data analytics tools. By assessing the value and getting end-user feedback, you can make a more informed decision. This step helps in understanding the practical implications and benefits of the tool in a real-world scenario.
- Maximizing the reversibility of your decision is another key point. This means ensuring that if things don't go as planned, you can easily switch to another tool without significant losses. It's about being flexible and prepared for any changes in your data strategy.
