# Analyzing Mechanistic, Living Systems, and Journey Thinking

Created at 2024/07/06 1:25 PM

## Bloom's Category: Analyze

### Mechanistic Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Differentiate between mechanistic thinking and other models in understanding physical systems, examining the benefits and limitations of each.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Break down the components of a typical mechanistic model, analyzing how each part contributes to the overall function.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Analyze case studies where mechanistic thinking has led to significant breakthroughs or failures, identifying key factors that influenced the outcomes.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Apply mechanistic thinking to a new or unfamiliar situation, dissecting the process to understand how different elements interact.

### Living Systems Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Compare and contrast living systems thinking with mechanistic approaches, focusing on how each interprets interdependencies within biological ecosystems.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Analyze the structure of a living system, such as a coral reef or a human social organization, to understand the roles and interactions of various components.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Examine specific examples where living systems thinking has been applied to solve complex problems, identifying the methodologies and their effectiveness.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Implement living systems thinking in a practical project, analyzing the outcomes to understand how the theory translates into practice.

### Journey Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Contrast journey thinking with goal-based planning approaches, analyzing the strengths and weaknesses of focusing on the journey versus the destination.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Dissect a narrative or historical account using journey thinking, identifying the key transitions and decisions that shaped the outcomes.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Analyze personal or professional development plans through the lens of journey thinking, evaluating the progression and pivotal moments.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Utilize journey thinking to review a completed project or personal experience, analyzing the sequence of events and their impact on the final result.
