Created at 2024/08/31 11:12 PM

AI models risk "cannibalization" by training on data generated by other AI, leading to degradation in quality over time. This self-referential training loop could cause models to collapse, highlighting the need for diverse, high-quality data sources to maintain AI reliability.

**🤖 AI Cannibalization**
- **AI relies on AI-generated data**: The current trend shows that AI models are increasingly consuming data produced by other AI, creating a feedback loop that can degrade the quality of outputs.
- **Human-generated data is diminishing**: As AI technology advances, the volume of human-created data is not keeping pace, leading to a reliance on synthetic data for training.
- **Potential for content saturation**: The internet risks becoming saturated with AI-generated content, which may further exacerbate the quality issues in AI training.

**💥 Model Collapse**
- **Feedback loops can lead to inaccuracies**: When AI models are trained on their own outputs, the results can drift away from reality, resulting in a phenomenon known as model collapse.
- **Dysfunctional outputs persist**: Despite advancements, many generative AI tools still produce outputs filled with biases and inaccuracies, which can carry over to subsequent models.
- **Need for intervention**: Experts suggest that without intervention, the reliance on AI-generated content could lead to a significant decline in the overall quality of AI models.

**📊 Data Quality**
- **Importance of diverse data sources**: To maintain AI reliability, it is crucial to incorporate a variety of high-quality data sources rather than relying solely on synthetic data.
- **Research on filtering synthetic data**: Ongoing research aims to develop methods for filtering synthetic data to improve the quality of AI training datasets.
- **Synthetic data can be beneficial**: In certain contexts, such as training smaller models with verified outputs, synthetic data can enhance learning and performance.
