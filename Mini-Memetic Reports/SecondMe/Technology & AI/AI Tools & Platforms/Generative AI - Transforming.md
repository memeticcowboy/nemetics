## Insight
In this episode of the D-Suite, Gartner experts present their forward-looking predictions for data, analytics, and AI for 2024 and beyond, with a special focus on the transformative impact of generative AI (GenAI). The episode features insights from Rickard Wieselfors, Ericsson’s head of AI and automation, on how GenAI is driving digital transformation within the company. Key predictions include the potential assimilation of Chief Data and Analytics Officers (CDAOs) into technology functions if they fail to demonstrate measurable impact, the rise of natural language interfaces in data management, and the shift towards AI-enabled content creation. The discussion emphasizes the need for CDAOs to elevate their roles to true C-level leadership positions, the importance of data management leaders sticking to the basics while enabling GenAI, and the critical balance between accelerated development and trust in GenAI outcomes. The episode concludes with actionable insights and recommendations for data and analytics leaders to navigate these transformative changes effectively.
