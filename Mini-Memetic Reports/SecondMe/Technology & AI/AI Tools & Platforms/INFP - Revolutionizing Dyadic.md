**🎤 INFP Framework Overview**
- **Introduction of INFP**: INFP is an audio-driven interactive head generation framework designed for dyadic conversations, enabling dynamic synthesis of verbal and non-verbal interactions using dual-track audio and a single portrait image.
- **Key Features**: The framework is lightweight and powerful, making it suitable for instant communication scenarios like video conferencing, and it emphasizes interactivity, naturalness, flash performance, and person-generic capabilities.

**🗣️ Dynamic Interaction Model**
- **Dual-Stage Process**: INFP operates through two main stages: Motion-Based Head Imitation, which learns facial behaviors from real conversations, and Audio-Guided Motion Generation, which maps audio inputs to motion codes for head generation.
- **Seamless Role Adaptation**: Unlike previous models that require manual role assignment, INFP automatically alternates between speaking and listening states based on audio input, enhancing the natural flow of conversation.

**📊 Dataset Contribution**
- **Introduction of DyConv Dataset**: The research introduces DyConv, a large-scale dataset of dyadic conversations collected from the Internet, which supports the development and testing of the INFP framework.
- **Rich Data for Training**: DyConv provides a diverse range of conversational data, facilitating the learning of realistic facial communicative behaviors essential for effective head generation.

**🎥 Video Generation Capabilities**
- **Motion Diversity**: INFP can generate varied motion synthesis results for the same reference image based on different audio inputs, showcasing its adaptability.
- **Support for Non-Human Images**: The framework can also handle non-human realistic and side-face images, broadening its application scope.

**⚡ Real-Time Communication**
- **High Inference Speed**: INFP achieves over 40 frames per second on Nvidia Tesla A10, enabling real-time communication between agents and between humans and agents.
- **Versatile Interaction**: The framework supports both agent-agent and human-agent interactions, making it versatile for various communication scenarios.

**🔄 Comparison with Existing Methods**
- **Dynamic Role Adaptation**: INFP's ability to dynamically adapt to conversational roles leads to smoother and more natural interactions compared to existing methods that require manual role switching.
- **High Fidelity Generation**: The framework excels in generating high lip-sync accuracy, expressive facial expressions, and rhythmic head movements, enhancing the realism of interactions.

**🎶 Multi-Language Support**
- **Talking Head Generation**: INFP generates talking head outputs with high fidelity, supporting singing and various languages, which expands its usability in multilingual contexts.
- **Listening Head Generation**: The framework also produces listening head outputs with natural facial behaviors and diverse head motions, ensuring a comprehensive interaction experience.
