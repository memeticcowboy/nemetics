[The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/articles/how-are-companies-using-ai-agents-heres-a-look-at-five-early-users-of-the-bots-26f87845) recently published an article highlighting how companies are using AI agents. The article mentioned that companies are optimistic about the potential of AI agents to improve efficiency and productivity.

The article provided five examples of how companies are using AI agents:

- **Johnson & Johnson** is using AI agents to optimize drug discovery. The agents are able to analyze data from experiments and extrapolate it to arrive at an optimal method for drug manufacturing.
- **Moody's** has developed a team of AI agents to research public company filings and perform industry comparisons. Each agent has its own set of instructions, personality, and data access.
- **eBay** is using AI agents to write code, create marketing campaigns, and help buyers find items. The company built its own agent framework that can take advantage of several different LLMs.
- **Deutsche Telekom** is using AI agents to answer employee questions about internal policies and benefits. The company is also exploring how to allow agents to execute requests on behalf of employees.
- **Constantino** is using AI agents to fill in gaps for their customer service staff. The agents are expected to have basic skills but receive training when they begin work.

The article concludes by saying that 2025 is going to be a huge year for AI agent pilots.

A recent white paper from Google suggests that companies should think about AI agents as a fundamental shift in the way organizations operate. The paper highlights two key capabilities of AI agents:

- **Access to other systems:** AI agents can access databases, real-time data feeds, and other systems to inform their decision-making.
- **Ability to reason through multi-step tasks:** AI agents can use chain of thought or tree of thoughts to manage uncertainty and complexity.

The speaker in the video also mentions that they are working on an AI agent readiness audit to help companies understand how they can best use AI agents.
