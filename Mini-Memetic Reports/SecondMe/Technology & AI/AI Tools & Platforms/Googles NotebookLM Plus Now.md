# Google's NotebookLM Plus Now for Individual Users

Created at 2025/02/12 7:58 AM

## Summary

Google has expanded its AI-powered note-taking tool, NotebookLM Plus, from enterprises to individual users through the Google One AI Premium plan. This premium version offers increased usage limits, customizable AI personalities, and advanced analytics, with a 50% discount for eligible students. Google plans to enhance language support and develop a mobile app.

**📝 Google Expands NotebookLM Plus to Individual Users: Key Features and Future Plans**

- **🌍 Expansion and Accessibility**: Google has made NotebookLM Plus available to individual users through the Google One AI Premium plan, which was previously limited to enterprises. This move aims to cater to a broader audience, particularly students, who have shown significant interest in the tool.

- **💡 Enhanced Features and Capabilities**: The premium version of NotebookLM Plus offers substantial improvements over the free version, including five times more usage capacity, customizable AI personalities, and advanced analytics. Users can now manage up to 500 notebooks and 300 sources per notebook, along with 500 chat queries and 20 audio generations daily.

- **📱 Future Development and Integration**: Google is actively working on enhancing language support and developing a mobile app for NotebookLM. This will allow users to access the tool on-the-go while ensuring a seamless experience that mirrors the desktop version.

- **🎓 Student Discounts**: To encourage adoption among students, Google has introduced a 50% discount for eligible students over 18 in the U.S., making the Google One AI Premium plan more affordable at $9.99 per month.

- **🔊 Audio Overviews Feature**: The service includes an innovative Audio Overviews feature that generates podcast-like audio conversations based on user-uploaded content. This feature is set to be enhanced with support for multiple languages, making it more accessible to a global audience.

- **🔍 Continuous Improvement**: Google is committed to maintaining a high-quality experience for both free and paid users of NotebookLM. The company plans to introduce more features for Plus users without compromising the experience for those using the free version.

- **📈 User Engagement and Growth**: Although Google has not disclosed specific user numbers for NotebookLM, market intelligence suggests that the AI assistant has garnered significant attention, with over 28 million visits in the last three months, indicating a growing user base.

- **🤖 Integration with Gemini AI Models**: NotebookLM utilizes the latest Gemini AI models for its functionalities. Google is continuously experimenting with different variants to ensure optimal performance for various tasks, enhancing the overall user experience.

This expansion of NotebookLM Plus signifies Google's commitment to making advanced AI tools accessible to a wider audience, particularly students and individual users, while continuously improving the features and capabilities of the platform.
