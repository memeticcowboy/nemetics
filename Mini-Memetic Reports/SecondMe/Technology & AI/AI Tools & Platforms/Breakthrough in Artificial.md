# Breakthrough in Artificial Neurons at UMass Amherst

Created at 2025/11/19 7:27 PM

Engineers at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, led by Jun Yao and including graduate student Shuai Fu, have created the first artificial neurons that can directly interface with living cells.


These artificial neurons have electrical functions similar to biological ones, operating at a low voltage of 0.1 volts, making them potentially useful for building more efficient computers.


The discovery could lead to a range of applications, including bio-inspired computers, wearable electronic sensing systems, and electronic devices that can communicate directly with the human body.
