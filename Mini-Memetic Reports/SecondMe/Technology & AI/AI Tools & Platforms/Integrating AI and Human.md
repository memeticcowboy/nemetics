# Decision RADAR for UNITs

Created at 2025/08/15 2:45 PM

Here’s a backronym for **RADAR**, evolving the Excitant six-step decision process to incorporate AI agents collaborating with human teams, emphasizing **decision rule execution** and keeping humans in the loop:

**R**ecognize

- **AI Layer:** Continuous scanning of data streams, anomalies, signals.
- **Human Layer:** Contextualize alerts, filter noise, identify which signals are decision-worthy.
- **Economics Layer:** Tag work type (*planned, ad hoc, curiosity ask*). Assign **attention cost**.

**A**ssess

- **AI Layer:** Aggregate data, surface patterns, predict outcomes.
- **Human Layer:** Frame the decision scope, define criteria, add domain insight.
- **Economics Layer:** Log **opportunity cost** (what gets delayed by this decision?). Start **ROI pre-check**.

**D**esign & Decide

- **AI Layer:** Generate scenarios, recommend rules, simulate tradeoffs.
- **Human Layer:** Apply judgment, ethics, and strategy. Assign decision rights: *AI vs. human execution*.
- **Economics Layer:** Record projected costs, benefits, and risks. Explicitly capture *“why this decision, not another.”*

**A**ctivate

- **AI Layer:** Automate workflows, monitor implementation, scale execution.
- **Human Layer:** Execute tasks requiring nuance, lead stakeholder communications.
- **Economics Layer:** Log actual costs (time, resources). Capture **decision ID** for traceability.

**R**eview & Reflect

- **AI Layer:** Track KPIs, detect drift, suggest rule or model updates.
- **Human Layer:** Reflect on outcomes, validate fairness/ethics, share learnings across UNITs.
- **Economics Layer:** Update ROI calculator: **value delivered vs. cost incurred**. Surface hidden overheads (e.g., unplanned meetings).

This **RADAR** process ensures that both AI agents and human teams co-create, supervise, and refine decision-making, maximizing speed, adaptability, and trust in **rule-driven decision execution**.
