**📊 Performance and Capabilities**

- **State-of-the-Art Performance**: Manus has achieved a new state-of-the-art (SOTA) performance benchmark in the GAIA test, which evaluates general AI assistant capabilities. This accomplishment underscores its effectiveness in various complex tasks.

- **Task Execution**: The AI agent can autonomously execute tasks from planning to completion, as demonstrated in a four-minute showcase where it screened candidates for a job position. This level of independence is a significant leap from traditional AI tools.

- **Multisig Approach**: Manus employs a multi-signature approach powered by multiple independent models, enhancing its reliability and performance in executing tasks.
