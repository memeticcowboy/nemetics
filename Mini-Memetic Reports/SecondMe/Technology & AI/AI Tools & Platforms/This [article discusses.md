This [article discusses DARPA's In the Moment (ITM) program](https://drexel.edu/news/archive/2023/October/DARPA-ITM-CCI-decision-making?t), which aims to develop AI algorithms that can mimic human decision-making in complex and rapidly changing scenarios. Some interesting points from the article include:

1) The ITM program will focus on quantifying the alignment of AI algorithms with human decision-makers to help in situations where there are no agreed-upon answers.

2) The goal is to create AI systems that can assume decision-making responsibilities when human decision-makers disagree or face uncertainty, resource limitations, or time constraints.

3) Understanding the key characteristics of expert human decision-making and representing them in algorithmic decision-makers is crucial for ensuring that algorithms make trustworthy choices.

4) The ITM program includes developing decision-maker characterization techniques and creating a quantitative alignment score to measure the level of alignment between AI algorithms and human decision-makers.

Overall, the ITM program highlights the importance of building AI systems that can work effectively with humans in complex and dynamic environments.
