## The Great Liberation from Mundane Work
- **Core Narrative Structure:**  
  - Problem: People are trapped in repetitive, unfulfilling tasks.  
  - Resolution: AI automates the boring stuff, freeing humans for creative, meaningful work.  
- **Key Symbols/Phrases/Anchors:**  
  - “Liberation,” “mundane work,” “creative and strategic endeavors,” “job satisfaction”  
- **Emotional Hooks/Cognitive Levers:**  
  - Relief from drudgery, hope for fulfillment, pride in meaningful contribution  
- **Ideological Frame/Position:**  
  - Pro-innovation, humanistic, anti-drudgery  
- **Target Identity Roles:**  
  - Knowledge workers, creatives, future-oriented professionals  
- **Transmission Vectors:**  
  - Workplace training, motivational talks, HR campaigns, LinkedIn posts
