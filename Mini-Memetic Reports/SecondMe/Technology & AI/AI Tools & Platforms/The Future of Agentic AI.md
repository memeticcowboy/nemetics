# The Future of Agentic AI: Enhancing User Experience through Agents, Sims, and Assistants

Created at 2025/01/16 11:55 AM

𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬 𝐀𝐫𝐞 𝐍𝐨𝐭 𝐄𝐧𝐨𝐮𝐠𝐡 - 𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐅𝐮𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐨𝐟 𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐜 𝐀𝐈<br>[via Stuart Winter on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/stuart-winter-tear_agents-are-not-enough-activity-7279548579881336833-LN5V?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop)

<br>“Agents, by definition, remove agency from a user in order to do things on the user’s behalf and save them time and effort. However, such a trade-off may be brought into question if relinquishing that control does not generate sufficient user value”.<br><br>To make Agents successful they need: personalization, privacy, user agency, value generation, trustworthiness, standardisation, and widespread acceptance.<br><br>They see a tripartite ecosystem of: Agents, Sims, and Assistants.<br><br>𝐀𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬 - Specialised, autonomous modules designed to perform specific tasks. While narrow in focus, agents collaborate by interfacing with other agents to handle more complex workflows.<br><br>𝐒𝐢𝐦𝐬 - Digital representations of users, built from user profiles, preferences, and behaviors. Sims can manage privacy settings and act independently by interacting with agents to perform tasks on behalf of the user, reducing the need for constant input.<br><br>𝐀𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐧𝐭𝐬 - Programs that interact directly with users, deeply understanding their needs and preferences. Assistants coordinate tasks by calling Sims and Agents, proactively or reactively managing workflows to simplify user experiences.<br><br>Assistants work alongside Sims to reflect different aspects of the user’s life, creating a seamless, personalised ecosystem. This layered collaboration ensures that tasks are handled efficiently, enhancing precision and user satisfaction.<br><br>The authors envision an Agent Store, similar to an app store with vetted agents available for Assistants to interact with and accomplish various tasks.<br><br>“Agents may be the centerpiece of this ecosystem, but it is the availability of Sims, Assistants, and the set of protocols that connects them that can really make this next evolution of agentic AI successful.”
