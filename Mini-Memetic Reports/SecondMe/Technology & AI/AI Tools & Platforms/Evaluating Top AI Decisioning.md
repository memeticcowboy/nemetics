# Evaluating Top AI Decisioning Platforms

Created at 2024/09/08 1:55 PM

This report evaluates the top 13 AI decisioning platform vendors, helping technology professionals choose the right platform for their needs. AI decisioning platforms combine decision intelligence technologies (business rules, machine learning, mathematical models) to automate business decision logic. The report stresses the importance of keeping human business experts in control while using these platforms. It also highlights the growing need for industry-specific solution accelerators and tools that support rapid learning from real-world business outcomes.  The article also highlights insights from Forrester's Q2 2023 report, emphasizing the need for no-code tools, industry-specific accelerators, and rapid learning loops. Key AIDP providers include SAS, FICO, IBM, CRIF, Pegasystems, and others.

## Insight
This report evaluates the leading AI decisioning platforms, assisting technology professionals in selecting the most suitable vendor. It highlights the necessity of maintaining human oversight while leveraging AI technologies and emphasizes the demand for industry-specific tools and rapid learning capabilities. The findings suggest that platforms should offer no-code solutions and support continuous improvement based on real-world outcomes. For those exploring AI decisioning platforms, consider the importance of user-friendly tools and industry experience when making your choice.

**🧠 AI Decisioning Importance**
- **Human Oversight is Crucial**: AI decisioning platforms must empower human experts to maintain control over automated decisions, ensuring that technology complements human judgment rather than replacing it.
- **Integration of Decision Intelligence Technologies**: AIDPs combine various technologies, including business rules and machine learning, to automate complex decision-making processes effectively.
- **Need for Industry-Specific Solutions**: Vendors should provide tailored accelerators that cater to specific industry needs, allowing enterprises to implement solutions without starting from scratch.

**🔧 No-Code Tools**
- **User-Friendly Authoring Tools**: Platforms should feature no-code tools that enable business experts to easily create and modify decision models, enhancing accessibility and usability.
- **Empowerment of Business Experts**: The ability for non-technical users to author decision logic is essential for rapid deployment and adaptation to changing business needs.
- **Focus on Ease of Use**: When evaluating vendors, prioritize those that demonstrate intuitive interfaces and robust support for business users.

**📈 Rapid Learning Loops**
- **Continuous Improvement of AI Decisions**: AIDPs should facilitate the monitoring and evaluation of decision outcomes, allowing teams to refine models based on real-world performance.
- **Adaptation to Market Changes**: The ability to iterate decision models in response to evolving business strategies and market conditions is vital for maintaining competitive advantage.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making**: Vendors must provide tools that enable organizations to learn from past decisions and adjust strategies accordingly.

**🏆 Vendor Evaluation Criteria**
- **Current Offering Assessment**: The evaluation considers the strength of each vendor's current offerings, including features, tools, and security measures.
- **Strategic Vision and Market Approach**: Vendors are assessed on their product vision, market strategy, and planned enhancements to ensure they align with future needs.
- **Market Presence and Customer Base**: The size and engagement of a vendor's customer base are critical indicators of their reliability and effectiveness in the market.

**📊 Forrester Wave Evaluation**
- **Categorization of Vendors**: Vendors are classified into Leaders, Strong Performers, Contenders, and Challengers based on their evaluation scores and market presence.
- **Key Players Identified**: Top vendors include SAS, FICO, IBM, and Pegasystems, each demonstrating unique strengths in decisioning capabilities.
- **Evaluation as a Starting Point**: The report encourages users to adapt evaluation criteria based on their specific needs and to utilize the provided comparison tools.

**🔍 Insights from Forrester's Report**
- **Emphasis on No-Code Solutions**: The report highlights the growing demand for no-code tools that allow business users to engage directly with decision-making processes.
- **Industry-Specific Accelerators**: Vendors are encouraged to develop accelerators tailored to specific industries, enhancing the relevance and effectiveness of their solutions.
- **Learning from Real-World Outcomes**: The ability to learn from actual business outcomes is essential for refining decision models and improving overall effectiveness.

**📅 Future Trends in AI Decisioning**
- **Increased Demand for Automation**: As businesses seek to enhance efficiency, the demand for AI decisioning platforms that automate complex processes will continue to grow.
- **Integration of Advanced Technologies**: Future platforms will likely incorporate more advanced machine learning techniques and data analytics capabilities.
- **Focus on User Experience**: Vendors must prioritize user experience to ensure that their platforms are accessible and effective for a wide range of users.

**💡 Recommendations for Enterprises**
- **Evaluate Vendor Capabilities**: Enterprises should thoroughly assess vendor capabilities, focusing on ease of use, industry experience, and support for rapid learning.
- **Prioritize Human-Centric Solutions**: Select platforms that emphasize human oversight and control in decision-making processes to ensure better outcomes.
- **Leverage Industry-Specific Tools**: Consider vendors that offer tailored solutions and accelerators that align with your specific industry needs.
