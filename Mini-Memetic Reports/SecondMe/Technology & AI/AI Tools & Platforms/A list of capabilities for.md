A list of capabilities for **Decision Intelligence (DI) vendors**, ordered from most common to least common:

1. **AI-driven decision automation:** Leveraging artificial intelligence to automate decision-making processes across various business functions.
2. **Predictive analytics:** Using historical data and machine learning algorithms to forecast future trends and outcomes.
3. **Real-time data analysis:** Processing and analyzing data in real-time to support immediate decision-making.
4. **Data integration and management:** Combining data from multiple sources to create a comprehensive view for decision-making.
5. **Risk management and compliance:** Assessing and mitigating risks while ensuring regulatory compliance.
6. **Supply chain optimization:** Enhancing efficiency and responsiveness in supply chain operations through data-driven decisions.
7. **Customer experience optimization:** Personalizing interactions and improving customer satisfaction through intelligent decision-making.
8. **Visualization and reporting:** Presenting complex data and insights in easily digestible formats for decision-makers.
9. **Natural language processing:** Interpreting and generating human language to support decision-making processes.
10. **Collaboration tools:** Facilitating team-based decision-making and knowledge sharing.
11. **Machine learning model deployment:** Integrating and managing ML models within decision-making workflows.
12. **Scenario planning and simulation:** Running "what-if" analyses to explore potential outcomes of decisions.
13. **Explainable AI (XAI):** Providing transparency and interpretability in AI-driven decision-making processes.
14. **Edge computing support:** Enabling decision-making at the network edge for faster response times.
15. **IoT integration:** Incorporating data from Internet of Things devices into decision-making processes.
16. **Cognitive computing:** Using AI to mimic human thought processes in complex decision-making scenarios.
17. **Blockchain integration:** Leveraging distributed ledger technology for secure and transparent decision-making.
