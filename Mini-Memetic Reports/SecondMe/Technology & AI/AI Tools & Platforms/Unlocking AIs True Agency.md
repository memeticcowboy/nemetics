# Unlocking AI's True Agency with Large Action Models

Created at 2024/12/04 9:17 AM

**Summary:** Large Action Models (LAMs) enhance AI capabilities by combining language comprehension with the ability to perform tasks autonomously. Integrating neural networks with knowledge graphs, LAMs enable AI assistants and agents to understand context, infer user goals, and execute actions such as interacting with web interfaces and APIs. This advancement moves beyond traditional large language models, paving the way for AI systems with true agency.
