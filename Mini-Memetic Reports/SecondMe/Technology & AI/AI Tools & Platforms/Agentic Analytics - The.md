# Agentic Analytics: The Future of AI-Driven Decision Making

Created at 2024/10/15 10:20 AM

Agentic analytics, as defined by Gartner, refers to the use of AI agents to automate and support tasks in the data-to-insight workflow, enabling better decision-making, operational efficiency, and risk management. It involves semi-autonomous or autonomous orchestration of analytics, from data preparation to insight generation, optimizing processes across the business.

**Top 3 Themes**  
1. **Enhanced Decision-Making**: AI agents process vast datasets, generating timely insights for proactive business strategies.
2. **Operational Efficiency**: Automating repetitive tasks allows human workers to focus on higher-value activities.
3. **Risks and Challenges**: Potential pitfalls include LLM hallucination, limited explainability, and over-reliance on AI outputs.
