# Google DeepMind Unveils Gemini 2.0 AI Model

Created at 2025/02/05 3:48 PM

## Summary

Google DeepMind has introduced Gemini 2.0, its most capable AI model yet, designed for the agentic era. This model enhances multimodality, native tool use, and real-time streaming, enabling more helpful AI agents that can understand and act on user instructions.

- **Advancements in Multimodality**: Enhanced capabilities in handling multiple forms of input and output, including text, images, audio, and video.

- **Agentic Capabilities**: AI models that can use memory, reasoning, and planning to complete tasks under user supervision.

- **Developer and User Integration**: Availability of the model for developers and users through various platforms, including Vertex AI and the Gemini app.

This shift towards agentic AI represents a major leap in how AI can assist users in everyday tasks.

- **🔧 Native Tool Use**: Gemini 2.0 incorporates native tool use, allowing AI agents to interact with various applications and tools seamlessly.
