This trend highlights the necessity for businesses to adapt to AI advancements to remain competitive.

- **🔄 Job Role Transformations**: The emergence of the generative AI management consultant role signifies a transformation in job requirements across various industries. This shift suggests that businesses are prioritizing strategic implementation of AI over mere development.
