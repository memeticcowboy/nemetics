# Enhancing Human Thinking in a Humans + AI World

Created at 2024/10/10 10:40 AM

In shaping a Humans + AI world the most important application of AI is to enhance human thinking. There are a multitude of cognitive and action domains for implementing AI-augmented thinking. Each requires distinct strategies and approaches.

This visual shows 8 key domains where AI can enhance human thinking, if used well, and a small selection of the specific applications.

For example, generative AI can assist in assimilating information into knowledge and useful mental models using a range of techniques, from search distillation and concept visualization through to semantic space mapping and cognitive debiasing. 

Complex problem-solving can be supported by problem decomposition, context reframing, applying analytic frameworks, and bringing in diverse stakeholder perspectives, among other tools.

Using AI to support effective human decision-making is at the center of my work. Just a handful of the many useful techniques in this area are shown in this diagram.

Some tasks and decisions we can begin to delegate to AI, with appropriate human involvement and oversight.

However the most important activities will and should remain human-first, amplified and augmented by AI to achieve superior intelligence and outcomes.

## Insight
In a world where AI and humans team up, the possibilities are as exciting as a rollercoaster ride through a tech wonderland! Let’s dive into how this dynamic duo can enhance our thinking and decision-making.

- AI's role in assimilating information is like having a supercharged librarian at your fingertips. Techniques like search distillation and cognitive debiasing help us sift through the noise, turning chaos into clarity. Imagine trying to find a needle in a haystack, but instead, you have a magnet! This not only saves time but also enriches our mental models, making us smarter and more efficient.
- When it comes to complex problem-solving, AI acts as a trusty sidekick, helping us break down challenges into manageable pieces. By applying diverse analytic frameworks and stakeholder perspectives, we can tackle issues from multiple angles. It’s like having a brainstorming session with a room full of experts, minus the awkward silences! This collaborative approach leads to innovative solutions that might have otherwise slipped through the cracks.
- Effective decision-making is at the heart of our daily lives, and AI can be our guiding star. With tools for scenario analysis and option generation, we can weigh our choices more thoughtfully. Think of it as having a wise old sage whispering insights in your ear while you navigate life’s tricky crossroads. This partnership ensures that while we delegate some tasks to AI, the final call remains a human touch.
- The creativity and ideation domain showcases how AI can spark our imagination. By utilizing systems mapping and combinatorial ideation, we can explore new ideas and connections we might not have considered. It’s like having a brainstorming buddy who never runs out of energy or enthusiasm! This synergy not only enhances our creative processes but also leads to groundbreaking innovations.
- Framing is crucial in how we perceive challenges, and AI can help us reframe contexts effectively. By applying contextual reframing techniques, we can shift our perspectives and uncover new opportunities. It’s akin to putting on a new pair of glasses that reveal hidden details in a familiar landscape. This fresh outlook can be the key to unlocking solutions that were previously obscured.
