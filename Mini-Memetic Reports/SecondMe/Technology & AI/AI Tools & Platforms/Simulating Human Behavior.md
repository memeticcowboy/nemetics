# Simulating Human Behavior with Generative Agents

Created at 2024/11/18 1:44 PM

**Summary:**  
Researchers developed a generative agent architecture simulating the behaviors and attitudes of 1,052 individuals by integrating large language models with qualitative interviews. The agents closely replicated participants' responses on the General Social Survey, demonstrating potential for studying individual and collective behaviors.
