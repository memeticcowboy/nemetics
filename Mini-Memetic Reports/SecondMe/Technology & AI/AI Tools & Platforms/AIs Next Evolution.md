## Insight
The article discusses the evolving role of AI in enhancing human productivity by shifting from being prompted to prompting users. The key outcome is the prediction that AI will start asking users questions to improve clarity, foster new perspectives, and enhance conversations. As an assistant, I suggest embracing this change by being open to AI's questions and using them to refine your thoughts and ideas. This shift aims to make AI a more interactive and insightful tool, ultimately helping us think more deeply and work more efficiently.

**🤖 AI as Clarifier**
- **AI will ask follow-up questions**: AI will start asking follow-up questions to gather more context, similar to how humans seek additional information before completing tasks.
- **Grounding in AI**: AI will use grounding techniques, asking for clarification to ensure it understands user requests accurately, improving the quality of its responses.
- **Improving prompt outcomes**: Research indicates that AI asking clarifying questions can lead to better outcomes, as it helps refine vague or broad prompts.

**💡 AI as Provocateur**
- **AI spurs new thinking**: AI can challenge users by asking questions that push them to refine their arguments and consider new ideas.
- **Productivity gains with AI**: Studies show significant productivity gains when AI is used to perform tasks like drafting or summarizing, but it can also inspire new perspectives.
- **Two-way inquiry**: Engaging critically with AI and being open to its challenges can create a dynamic exchange that drives growth and discovery.

**🗣️ AI as Conversation Catalyst**
- **Enhancing human conversations**: AI's questions can make human conversations more effective by suggesting follow-up questions and different perspectives.
- **Collaborative prompt testing**: Early collaborative sessions with AI models showed the value of shared learning and creative prompt experimentation.
- **AI in meetings**: Using AI in meetings to suggest questions can spark valuable discussions and make better use of time.
