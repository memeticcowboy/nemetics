# NEME and YOU⁴: Frameworks for Human–AI Interaction and Human Sovereignty

Created at 2025/12/22 9:32 AM

## **NEME**

**NEME = Notice · Engage · Mull · Exchange (or Exit)**

It’s a **human–AI interaction stance**, not an ontology and not a system claim.

Think of NEME as a **discipline for how interaction unfolds**, especially under uncertainty.

### The four moves

- **Notice**<br>Attend to signals, tensions, artifacts, or surprises *without acting yet*.
- **Engage**<br>Interact deliberately: ask, respond, explore, test.
- **Mull**<br>Pause. Let meaning settle. No output required. This is not a failure state.
- **Exchange / Exit**<br>Either:
    - Exchange artifacts back into a larger system (handoff), **or**
    - Exit cleanly without resolution.

### Why NEME exists

NEME exists to prevent two common failures:

1. **Reflexive automation** (responding just because something can respond)
2. **False continuity** (pretending engagement must always continue)

NEME explicitly legitimizes *pause, silence, and exit*.

It is about **interaction hygiene**, not intelligence.

***

## **YOU⁴ (You‑to‑the‑Fourth)**

**YOU⁴ is a human‑sovereignty constraint**, not a personality model and not a moral claim.

It answers one question:

> *Who retains agency when systems become complex, legible, and persuasive?*

### What the “⁴” points to

YOU⁴ says that **human agency operates across four simultaneous dimensions**:

1. **Cognitive** – understanding, sense‑making
2. **Temporal** – pacing, readiness, timing, fatigue
3. **Normative** – values, legitimacy, what “should” happen
4. **Existential / Volitional** – the right to refuse, pause, or disengage

If *any* of these are overridden by a system’s momentum, clarity, or helpfulness, **human sovereignty has been violated** — even if the system was “useful”.

### The key YOU⁴ rule (as used here)

> If a human signals pause, overload, ambiguity, refusal, or silence,<br>**the system must stop** and must not compensate by inferring intent or accelerating.

That’s it.

No mysticism. No sentience claim.<br>Just a **hard boundary against coercive helpfulness**.

***

## How they relate (simply)

- **NEME** = *how interaction proceeds*
- **YOU⁴** = *what must never be taken away from the human*

You can think of it like this:

- NEME structures *flow*
- YOU⁴ enforces *sovereignty*

Together, they prevent:

- AI filling silence
- AI mistaking coherence for consent
- AI turning engagement into obligation

***

## What they are *not*

- Not claims about AI consciousness
- Not branding terms
- Not required beliefs
- Not universal standards

They are **local, explicit constraints** this group is choosing to work under.
