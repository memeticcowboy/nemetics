Shared Mental Models (SMMs) in Human-AI Teaming: SMMs, which are knowledge structures shared among team members, are crucial for understanding, predicting, and explaining team tasks and dynamics. In human-AI teams, the quality of these shared models significantly impacts team performance. However, the development, measurement, and application of SMMs in human-AI contexts are complex and require further research to overcome current limitations, such as inconsistent terminologies and inadequate measurement techniques. A conceptual model for applying SMM literature to human-AI settings has been proposed, alongside design considerations for future experiments (Andrews et al., 2022).
