In Ethan Mollick’s blog post “I, Cyborg: Using Co-Intelligence,” he defines “cyborgs” as individuals who deeply integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into their work processes, collaborating seamlessly with AI systems to enhance productivity and creativity. Unlike “centaurs,” who maintain a clear division of labor between human and machine tasks, cyborgs engage in a dynamic, intertwined partnership with AI, continually exchanging tasks and inputs. For instance, a cyborg might start a sentence and have the AI complete it, resulting in a fluid, cooperative workflow. Mollick emphasizes that this cyborg approach allows for a more profound synergy between human intuition and AI capabilities, leading to innovative outcomes that neither could achieve alone.
