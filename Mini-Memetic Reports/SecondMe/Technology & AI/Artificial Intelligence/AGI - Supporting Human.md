## Insight
This article delves into the potential and challenges of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), highlighting its aim to achieve human-level cognition. Professor Nicu Sebe from the University of Trento and Humans.ai discusses the need for a clear definition and the steps required to make AGI a reality. Key outcomes include the importance of societal readiness and the potential for AGI to support rather than replace human intelligence. For those interested in AI, it's crucial to stay informed about ethical considerations and technological advancements to prepare for AGI's transformative impact.

**🚀 AGI Becoming a Reality**
- **Human-level cognition**: AGI aims to solve a broad spectrum of tasks like humans.
- **Unfamiliar tasks**: AGI should autonomously tackle new tasks, mirroring human cognitive skills.
- **Ethical dilemmas**: Without clear boundaries, AGI could lead to unintended consequences.

**🤖 AGI as a Support for Human Intelligence**
- **Societal readiness**: Preparation in technological, ethical, and educational dimensions is crucial.
- **Symbiotic relationship**: AGI should support human intelligence, not replace it.
- **Equitable access**: Ensuring everyone benefits from AI advancements is essential.

**🌟 AGI Holds the Potential to Change Everything**
- **Revolutionize industries**: AGI can create new opportunities and enhance human capabilities.
- **Collective effort**: Realizing AGI's potential requires collective foresight and effort.
- **Harmonious coexistence**: Embracing a future where AGI and humanity coexist can unlock unprecedented possibilities.
