Another way we can define Energy is by looking at the Movement Qualities. Movement Qualities are energy released during various time spans to portray distinct qualities. There are six dynamic Movement Qualities:<br>Sustained (slow, smooth, continuous)<br>Percussive (sharp, choppy, jagged)<br>Swinging (swaying, to and fro, pendulum-like)<br>Suspended (a moment of stillness, the high point, a balance)<br>Collapsed (fall, release, relax)<br>Vibratory (shake, wiggle, tremble)<br>
