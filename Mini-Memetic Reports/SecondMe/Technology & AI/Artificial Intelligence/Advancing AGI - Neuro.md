**🧠 Limitations of LLMs**
- **Need for Explainability**: Current LLMs lack the ability to explain their reasoning, which is essential for human-like decision-making. A System2 approach must incorporate explainability to communicate decisions effectively.
- **Selective Forgetting Capability**: LLMs currently blend all content into a single interpretation, making it difficult to reassess the validity of information. The ability to forget selectively would allow LLMs to discard unworthy knowledge, similar to human cognitive processes.
- **Continuous Learning Requirement**: LLMs are limited by their inability to learn continuously, relying instead on expensive training periods. This limitation hinders their adaptability in fast-moving domains, necessitating a shift towards continuous learning mechanisms.

**🔗 Neuro-symbolic AI**
- **Integration of Intuitive and Rational Reasoning**: Neuro-symbolic AI combines the rapid, intuitive reasoning of System1 with the rational, explainable reasoning of System2, aligning AI decision-making with human cognitive processes.
- **Causality Link's Approach**: Causality Link leverages symbolic AI to enhance LLMs by extracting readable patterns from texts, creating a more interpretable and effective AI system that can reason about reasoning.
- **Dynamic Knowledge Graphs**: The use of mini knowledge graphs allows for better organization and understanding of entities and their relationships, facilitating improved reasoning and decision-making capabilities.

**🧩 System 1 and System 2 Thinking**
- **Human Decision-Making Framework**: The dual-system theory, as described by Kahneman, illustrates how humans use both intuitive (System1) and rational (System2) thinking in decision-making, which AI systems should emulate.
- **Coherence Between Systems**: Establishing coherence between System1 and System2 in AI is crucial for effective reasoning. This involves creating a bridge using language and structured ontologies to facilitate communication between the two systems.
- **Back-Testing for Trust Validation**: The ability to back-test decisions made by AI systems against historical data is essential for validating their effectiveness and building trust in their recommendations.

**📈 Four Essentials for LLMs**
- **Explainability as a Foundation**: For LLMs to be effective, they must possess the ability to explain their reasoning, which is critical for user trust and understanding.
- **Dynamic Forgetting Mechanism**: Implementing a selective forgetting mechanism allows LLMs to adapt and improve by discarding outdated or irrelevant information, enhancing their decision-making capabilities.
- **Real-Time Learning and Adaptation**: Continuous learning capabilities enable LLMs to update their knowledge base in real-time, making them more responsive to new information and trends.

**🔍 Causality Link's Data Model**
- **Pattern Extraction from Texts**: Causality Link's approach focuses on extracting readable patterns from texts, which are stored in a data lake, allowing for better analysis and understanding of complex data.
- **Entity Recognition and Taxonomy**: The system utilizes a comprehensive ontology for entity recognition, enabling the identification and categorization of various types of data, which enhances the AI's reasoning capabilities.
- **Dynamic Querying and Analysis**: The architecture allows for dynamic querying of the data lake, facilitating real-time analysis and the ability to filter and manipulate data as needed.

**⚙️ Real-Time Learning in Action**
