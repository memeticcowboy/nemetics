## Insight
This IDC MarketScape report evaluates vendors in the global decision intelligence (DI) platforms market, highlighting their role in automating decision-making processes. The findings underscore the increasing necessity for DI platforms as organizations face growing data complexity and decision frequency. Key recommendations for technology buyers include understanding use cases and ensuring data security. This summary prepares you for a deeper exploration of vendor evaluations and market insights.

**📊 Decision Intelligence Overview**
- **Definition of Decision Intelligence**: Decision intelligence (DI) is a discipline that automates decision-making processes, integrating aspects of business intelligence (BI) and AI/ML to enhance scalability and efficiency.
- **Growing Demand for DI Platforms**: As organizations encounter more complex decisions and increased data volume, the need for DI platforms is becoming critical for effective governance and orchestration.
- **Distinction from BI and AI/ML**: DI differs from BI and AI/ML by focusing on automating decision-making processes while still involving human oversight when necessary.

**🛠️ Vendor Evaluation Criteria**
- **Inclusion Criteria for Vendors**: Vendors must support decision design, engineering, and orchestration, have AI/ML capabilities, and be in a production environment for at least six months.
- **Exclusion of Broad Portfolio Vendors**: Vendors with extensive software portfolios that do not offer a cohesive decision intelligence solution are excluded from evaluation.
- **Market Presence Requirement**: Vendors must demonstrate market presence and momentum based on IDC inquiry volume to be considered in the assessment.

**🔍 Key Findings**
- **Diverse Deployment Options**: Vendors offer various deployment options, including on-premises and cloud solutions, to meet data residency and sovereignty requirements.
- **Focus on Generative AI**: Many vendors are incorporating generative AI functionalities tailored to specific roles or use cases, enhancing decision-making capabilities.
- **Ease of Use and Support Needs**: Ease of use is a priority, with many customers requesting additional support and training as vendors expand their decision-making capabilities.

**💡 Advice for Technology Buyers**
- **Clarify Use Cases**: Organizations should clearly define the use cases for decision intelligence, understanding which aspects of decision-making can be automated and which require human input.
- **Assess Data Security Needs**: Evaluate the data sources required for the solution, ensuring compliance with security, privacy, and residency regulations.
- **Consider Integration and Scalability**: During vendor evaluation, focus on integration capabilities, scalability, and the solution's adaptability to changing market dynamics.

**🏆 Vendor Profiles**

- **Challenges Faced by Aera**: Aera currently supports fewer languages and needs to expand its deployment options to cater to more regulated industries.

**📈 Market Trends**
- **Rising Need for Complex Decisioning**: The current economic climate is driving the demand for complex decision-making across various business lines, presenting opportunities for vendors.
- **Focus on Professional Services Partnerships**: Vendors with strong partner ecosystems are better positioned to scale adoption and meet organizational requirements.
- **Future of Decision Intelligence**: The integration of advanced AI/ML capabilities, including generative AI, will be crucial for the evolution of decision intelligence platforms.
