# Incorporating Dignity into Presentations

Created at 2024/12/16 8:01 AM

## Aligning Content with Core Values
- Ground the message in what truly matters.
- Ensure passion and beliefs are evident in every slide and word.

## Storytelling as a Tool
- Weave in personal insights and experiences.
- Make the presentation a genuine expression of self.

## Creating Connection
- Invite the audience to connect on a deeper level.
- Share not just information, but a part of yourself.

## Impactful Presentation
- Presenting with dignity makes the presentation more impactful.
- Focus on authenticity to captivate the audience.
