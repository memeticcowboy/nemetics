# Prompts for Enhancing Content

Created at 2024/11/17 1:46 PM

## Conversational Tone
- Review content to rewrite in a friendly, human voice.
- Use short sentences and simple words.
- Remove academic language and corporate jargon.

## Personal Stories
- Analyze content to suggest 3-5 places for personal stories or anecdotes.
- Focus on authentic experiences that build trust.
- Ask for specific stories rather than inventing them.

## Flow Improvement
- Review content to improve flow between paragraphs and sections.
- Remove obvious transition words.
- Connect ideas naturally for a casual, flowing style.

## AI Writing Patterns
- Check content for common AI writing patterns.
- Rework repeated sentence structures and overused transitions.
- Suggest specific rewrites to sound more human.

## Adding Personality
- Review content to add personality through word choice and casual asides.
- Make subtle, infrequent touches for a more personal feel.
- Explain changes made and the reasoning behind them.
