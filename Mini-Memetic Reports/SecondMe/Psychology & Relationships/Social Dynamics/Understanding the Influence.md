# Understanding the Influence Matrix in Psychology

Created at 2024/11/25 5:08 PM

[Link to thread on X](https://x.com/JSpicerVet9/status/1861204402393973067)

The Influence Matrix is a framework within the Unified Theory of Psychology that maps human relational dynamics. It emphasizes two core socio-motivational processes: relational value—the need to feel known and valued by others—and social influence—the capacity to affect others in line with one's goals. The matrix outlines three dimensions: Power (dominance vs. submission), Love (affiliation vs. hostility), and Freedom (autonomy vs. dependency). Balancing these dimensions is crucial for relational health, with emotions serving as signals to adjust behaviors within social contexts.
