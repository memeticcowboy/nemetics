# Supporting Others Without Overshadowing Them

Created at 2025/05/14 1:55 PM

## Listening More
- Engage in deep listening and ask questions that allow others to explore their own thoughts.

## Empowering, Not Rescuing
- Encourage others to find their own solutions while being available to brainstorm or offer perspective when asked.

## Checking Intentions
- Reflect on whether the desire to help is for the benefit of others or for personal validation.

## Being a Mirror, Not a Spotlight
- Reflect back what you hear instead of projecting your own ideas, allowing others to see their thoughts from different angles.

## Celebrating Wins
- Act as a hype person for others, genuinely celebrating their successes without making it about yourself.
