# Unconscious Psychological Games in Relationships and Professional Life

Created at 2024/07/07 2:45 PM

1. **Pseudo-Small Talk**: Develops from normal small talk but becomes unhealthy, like coworkers muttering insults within earshot to provoke a reaction[2].
2. **Couples Games**: Unhealthy patterns in relationships, such as one partner treating the other poorly to "prove" negative beliefs[2].
3. **"Now I've Got You, You SOB!"**: Setting someone up to fail and then expressing anger, often played by parents with children[1].
4. **"Kick Me"**: Repeatedly putting oneself in situations where they'll be rejected or mistreated[1].
5. **Career Games**: Long-term manipulative behaviors in professional life, like office politics[2].
6. **Emotional Manipulation**: Using negative emotions to manipulate others[4].
7. **Social Roles**: Playing certain roles in social situations for psychological payoffs[1][4].
8. **Gaslighting**: Psychological manipulation making someone question their own perceptions[3].
9. **Silent Treatment**: Using silence and withdrawal as punishment or control[3].
10. **Moving the Goalposts**: Changing criteria for success, making it impossible to meet expectations[3].
11. **Triangulation**: Involving a third party in a conflict to manipulate relationship dynamics[3].

These games are often played unconsciously, with players unaware of their motivations. Understanding them can help individuals recognize unhealthy patterns and lead to more fulfilling interactions.
