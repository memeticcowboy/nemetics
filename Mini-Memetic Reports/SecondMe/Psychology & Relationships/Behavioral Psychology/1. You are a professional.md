1. You are a professional sleeper. (Mindset) 

2. Final meal at least two hours before bed. Try even 3, and 5 hours before.

3. Consistency - in bed within 30 minutes of bedtime everyday. 

4. Screens off 1 hour before bed. Blue light is bad for sleep. Red light is good. 

5. Wind down routine - 30min-1 hour before bed, screens off. Read, go for a walk, talk to a friend, breathe work, mediate, take a bath. Calm your body and mind for sleep. Journal, writing down the ideas swirling in your brain so by the time your head hits the pillow you’ve done first line reconciliation with your thoughts. 

6. Avoid stimulants such as caffeine at least 6 hours if not 12 hours before bed. One cup of coffee six hours before bed can leave half the caffeine in your body when you go to sleep. 

The most powerful predictor of sleep quality is resting heart rate before sleep. All the above is designed to lower your resting heart rate, calm your nervous system and ready your body for sleep.

Sleep is the most power drug in existence for human well-being and performance and warrants being your top life priority.
