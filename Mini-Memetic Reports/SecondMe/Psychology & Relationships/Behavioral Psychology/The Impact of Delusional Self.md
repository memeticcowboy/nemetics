# Delusional Power - Selfplex in-SANITY

Created at 2025/06/24 5:52 AM

Study Notes: [The Power of Delusional Self Belief - YouTube](https://youtu.be/V_Z8XoPVDBg?si=HkFm6G4fwxJ3QfEd)

Main Ideas

	•	Delusional Self-Belief: The idea that believing you can achieve something, despite evidence to the contrary, can drive success.

	•	Breaking Barriers: Roger Banister broke the 4-minute mile barrier, showing how belief can change what is perceived as possible.

	•	Success and Luck: While luck plays a role in success, hard work and perseverance are crucial.

	•	Persistence: Many successful individuals had long periods of struggle before achieving success, exemplifying the need for persistence and belief in oneself.

Key Concepts

	•	Self-Belief: Confidence in one’s abilities can propel individuals toward their goals, even when others doubt them.

	•	Exponential Growth: Success often comes after a period of persistence, leading to significant breakthroughs or recognition.

	•	Delusional Thinking: A positive form of self-delusion can protect individuals as they develop their skills and talents.

Definitions

	•	Delusional Self-Belief: A state where an individual maintains belief in their potential despite significant challenges or setbacks.

	•	Exponential Success: A concept where success builds over time and can lead to breakthroughs that seem sudden.

Important Examples

	•	Roger Banister:

	•	First person to run a mile in under 4 minutes on May 6, 1954.

	•	After his achievement, over 22 others also broke the record, demonstrating that belief can change limits.

	•	Bill Gates:

	•	Developed Microsoft after dropping out of college, emphasizing hard work (18-hour workdays, no weekends) and passion for programming.

	•	Microsoft initially lost money for the first five years, but breakthrough came with a deal with IBM.

	•	Quentin Tarantino:

	•	Faced eight years of failure before his first feature film success. His persistence and delusional belief in his talent eventually led to success.

Summary of Complex Concepts

	•	The Role of Time in Success: Success is often not linear; it can take years of hard work before any recognition comes. The belief in eventual success is crucial in maintaining motivation during tough times.

	•	Luck vs. Hard Work: While factors like luck can play a role in success, it is the combination of hard work, belief, and persistence that ultimately leads to achieving extraordinary outcomes.

Additional Notes

	•	Mindset Shift: Individuals are often conditioned to lower their ambitions as they grow older; however, those who retain a “delusional” belief in their dreams can achieve great things.

	•	Life’s Uncertainty: Success can happen unexpectedly; persistence is key, as opportunities can arise at any moment.
