# Gibbs meets BOPFLAX via Deepseek R1

Created at 2025/01/24 9:09 AM

Integrating the **BOPFLAX process** (Blockages, Overwhelm, Procrastination, Fears, Love, Achievements, eXpansion) into **Gibbs’ Reflective Cycle** (1988) can deepen reflection by addressing emotional, behavioral, and aspirational dimensions. Here’s how they align:

---

### **1. Description**  

- **BOPFLAX elements**: **Blockages** (what hindered progress), **Procrastination** (actions delayed or avoided).  

- *Gibbs’ Stage*: Objectively outline the experience.  

  - Example: *"During the project, I faced creative blockages and procrastinated on critical tasks due to unclear priorities."*

---

### **2. Feelings**  

- **BOPFLAX elements**: **Overwhelm**, **Fears**, **Love** (emotional responses).  

- *Gibbs’ Stage*: Reflect on emotions during the experience.  

  - Example: *"I felt overwhelmed by deadlines, feared failure, but loved collaborating with my team."*

---

### **3. Evaluation**  

- **BOPFLAX elements**: **Achievements** (successes), **Blockages/Procrastination** (challenges).  

- *Gibbs’ Stage*: Weigh what worked well vs. poorly.  

  - Example: *"My achievements included completing tasks under pressure, but blockages in communication worsened procrastination."*

---

### **4. Analysis**  

- **BOPFLAX elements**: Root causes of **Blockages**, **Overwhelm**, **Fears**, **Procrastination**.  

- *Gibbs’ Stage*: Dig deeper into "why" these occurred.  

  - Example: *"Fears stemmed from perfectionism; procrastination arose from unclear goals."*

---

### **5. Conclusion**  

- **BOPFLAX elements**: **Love** (motivators), **Achievements** (strengths), **eXpansion** (growth insights).  

- *Gibbs’ Stage*: Synthesize lessons.  

  - Example: *"Love for teamwork and past achievements can counteract fears. Expansion requires addressing blockages proactively."*

---

### **6. Action Plan**  

- **BOPFLAX elements**: Strategies for **eXpansion**, managing **Overwhelm/Fears**, leveraging **Love/Achievements**.  

- *Gibbs’ Stage*: Plan future steps.  

  - Example: *"I’ll use time-blocking to reduce procrastination, celebrate small achievements, and seek mentorship for expansion."*

---

### **Why This Integration Works**  

- BOPFLAX adds specificity to Gibbs’ model by naming emotional and behavioral patterns (e.g., fears, procrastination) that often underpin experiences.  

- It emphasizes **growth** (eXpansion) and **positives** (Love, Achievements), balancing Gibbs’ focus on challenges.
