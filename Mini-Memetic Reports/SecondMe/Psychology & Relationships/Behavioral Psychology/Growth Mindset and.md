# Growth Mindset and Metacognition

Created at 2024/05/30 9:51 AM

💭 Our ability to think and get better at thinking is not fixed from birth, contrary to the dominant view that we're either born smart or not. This fixed mindset can be challenged.
🌱 Having a growth mindset, based on Carol Dweck's research, means believing you can improve at things through dedication, motivation, and purposeful practice.
🧠 To improve at anything, you first have to be able to think differently about it. Thinking drives change by allowing you to see new possibilities.
🏋️ Character traits like discipline, tenacity, and grit are key to putting in the consistent effort required to truly get better at something over time.
🔍 Metacognition and self-awareness are important for noticing your own thought patterns and mental models so you can examine and change stubborn beliefs. 'I don't believe in beliefs, so you know whenever I have a belief, I don't. One belief I have is not to have beliefs because beliefs are just really stubborn mental models. Be agnostic to beliefs. You have mental models for the time that you have them and you're willing to change them. You never dip your toe into the same river twice' - Derek Cabrera
🌿 Ethics and interconnectedness are fundamental patterns found in nature. Symbiosis is the norm, not the exception. We rely on each other.
🌉 DSRP is a bridge between mind and nature, or mental models and reality. The goal is to align how we group, distinguish, relate, and take perspectives on things with how those things actually exist.
