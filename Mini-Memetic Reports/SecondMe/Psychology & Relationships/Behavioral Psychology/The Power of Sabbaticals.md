## Insight
Jen Hitze explores the concept of sabbaticals as a remedy for workplace fatigue and burnout. Highlighting Stefan Sagmeister's practice of taking year-long sabbaticals every seven years, the text delves into the historical and religious roots of sabbaticals and their importance for personal growth, creativity, and finding one's calling. If you're feeling burnt out or stuck in a rut, consider taking a break to recharge and explore new interests. This summary will guide you through the key points and benefits of incorporating sabbaticals into your life.

**🌟 Reasons for Job Departure**
- **Variety of Reasons**: People leave their jobs due to burnout, seeking a calling, or needing a break from monotony.
- **Workplace Fatigue Solution**: Taking a sabbatical is presented as a solution to combat workplace fatigue and burnout.

**🛠️ Stefan Sagmeister's Practice**
- **Year-Long Sabbaticals**: Every seven years, Sagmeister's design studio takes a 365-day hiatus for experimentation.
- **Activities During Sabbaticals**: The team engages in creative projects like crafting furniture, producing portraits, and filming movies.

**📅 Structuring Sabbaticals**
- **Challenges of Unstructured Time**: Without structure, people may feel anxious or fabricate trivial tasks.
- **Tips for Effective Sabbaticals**: Turn off notifications, inform others of your unavailability, and focus on experimentation.

**📜 Historical and Religious Roots**
- **Biblical Origins**: The concept of sabbaticals is rooted in the Bible, where the land rests every seventh year.
- **Etymology**: The word 'sabbatical' comes from 'sabbath,' meaning 'day of rest.'

**🏛️ Ancient Practices**
- **Roman Concept of Otium**: In ancient Rome, otium referred to leisure time for self-realization activities among the elite.
- **Modern Need for Leisure**: Many people today are exhausted and need leisure to reconnect with their priorities.

**💡 Benefits of Sabbaticals**
- **Rejuvenation and Clarity**: Sabbaticals offer rejuvenation, clarity, and creativity upon returning to work.
- **Personal Connections**: They allow for prioritizing personal connections and spending more time with loved ones.

**🔍 Finding Your Calling**
- **Seeking Fulfillment**: People should seek a calling rather than just a job or career.
- **Quotes on Vocation**: Inspirational quotes from Phil Knight, Viktor Frankl, and James Allen emphasize the importance of following one's calling.

**📢 Advocacy for Sabbaticals**
- **Jen Hitze's Perspective**: Jen Hitze advocates for sabbaticals as a means to find meaningful work and avoid mediocrity.
- **Tim Kreider's Insight**: Tim Kreider criticizes the American work culture and encourages people to engage in worthwhile pursuits.
