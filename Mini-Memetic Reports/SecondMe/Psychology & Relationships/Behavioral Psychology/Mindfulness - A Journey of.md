## Insight
These quotes remind us that mindfulness is a journey, not a destination, and it's okay to stumble along the way.

- Carol S. Dweck's quote about becoming versus being highlights the importance of growth in mindfulness. It’s a gentle nudge that perfection isn’t the goal; showing up with intention is what truly matters. Remember those times we tried to meditate and ended up just thinking about lunch? That’s part of the process!
- G.K. Chesterton’s wisdom on gratitude is a beautiful reminder to appreciate the little things. Practicing gratitude can shift our focus from what’s lacking to what’s abundant in our lives. Think about those moments we shared, laughing over a cup of coffee, and how they filled our hearts with joy.
- Queen Elizabeth II’s insight on balancing action and reflection resonates deeply in our fast-paced lives. It’s so easy to get caught up in the hustle and forget to pause and breathe. Remember our spontaneous breaks during study sessions? Those moments of reflection were often the most enlightening!
