**📊 Research and Impact**
- **Positive Effects in Low-Income Communities**: Research shows that school-based mindfulness programs are particularly beneficial in low-income areas where students face high stress and trauma.
- **Improved Emotional Regulation**: Teachers and administrators have observed noticeable improvements in students' ability to manage their emotions.

**🌍 Community Support**
- **GreenLight Fund Atlanta's Role**: This organization helps Georgia school systems fund mindfulness programs, ensuring they are accessible and relevant to communities of color.
