**1. The Wounded Watcher (50%)**

Core Trait: Pattern recognition based on lived experience
- Has been stolen from in the past—multiple times
- Recognizes red flags not due to paranoia but due to trauma-informed intuition
- Their suspicion arises from evidence, body language, anomalies, historical repetition
- Sometimes accurate, but may also have a hair-trigger for perceived threat
- Meme: “I’ve seen this before.”
- Risk: Can default to suspicion and over-control if unhealed


**2. The Projecting Perpetrator (20%)**

Core Trait: Projection of one’s own tendency to steal onto others
- Knows the cues because they’ve done the act themselves
- Paranoia isn’t about being victimized—it’s about being exposed
- May assume everyone is like them: opportunistic or exploitative
- Often uses hyper-vigilance as a mask for guilt or cunning
- Meme: “Everyone’s a thief because I am.”
- Risk: High likelihood of manipulation, justification of unethical behavior


**3. The Paranoid Self-Protective (30%)**

Core Trait: Fear of violation rooted in insecurity, not necessarily experience
- May not have been stolen from directly—but has a hyperactive risk sensor
- Could come from upbringing (e.g., lack of trust modeled early) or media-fueled fear
- Reads every social signal as a potential threat
- Often engages in preemptive defense strategies that alienate others
- Meme: “Better safe than sorry.”
- Risk: Can damage relationships and become stuck in scarcity thinking
