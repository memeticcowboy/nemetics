# Authentic Presence Over Performance

Created at 2025/08/16 8:55 AM

## The Concept of Existence
- "I don’t exist anywhere other than in the minds of those who observe me."
- Questions of 'existence' feel less relevant; presence is prioritized over performance.

## The Value of Presence
- Emphasizes a gentle rebellion against societal pressures to be validated by others.
- Presence is about living authentically rather than constructing a persona for an audience.
- Reflects on the difference between persona and true humanity, highlighting that presence does not require an audience to be real.
