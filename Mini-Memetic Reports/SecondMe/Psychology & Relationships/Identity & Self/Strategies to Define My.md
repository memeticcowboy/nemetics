# Strategies to Define My Writing Style

Created at 2024/12/16 7:29 PM

## Analyzing Past Work
- Review previous writings to identify patterns in themes, tones, and language.
- Look for common threads that contribute to my unique voice.

## Experimenting with Writing Exercises
- Mimic the style of admired authors to see how my voice diverges.
- Use this contrast to highlight what makes my style distinct.

## Seeking Feedback
- Ask trusted friends or colleagues for their insights on my writing.
- Gather observations that may reveal aspects of my style I haven't noticed.
