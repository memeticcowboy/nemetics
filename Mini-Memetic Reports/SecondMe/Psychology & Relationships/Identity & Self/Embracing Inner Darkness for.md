## Insight
Exploring the depths of our inner selves can be a transformative journey. Let's delve into the wisdom of Carl Jung and understand the importance of acknowledging our inner darkness.

- The quote by CG Jung emphasizes that true enlightenment comes not from ignoring our darker aspects but by bringing them into our conscious awareness.
- Imagining only positive or light aspects of ourselves can lead to an incomplete understanding of our true nature.
- By making the darkness conscious, we can achieve a more holistic and authentic self-awareness.

Reflect on your own inner darkness and consider how acknowledging it can lead to personal growth.
