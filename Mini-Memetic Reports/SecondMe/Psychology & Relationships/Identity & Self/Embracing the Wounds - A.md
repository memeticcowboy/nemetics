# Embracing the Wounds: A Jungian Journey to Self-Actualization

Created at 2025/01/22 8:15 PM

1. **Summary:** Jungian psychology views psychological issues, dreams, and symptoms as pathways to self-discovery, emphasizing individuation and the integration of the unconscious to find purpose and meaning.

2. **Overarching Themes:**

- **Individuation Process:** The journey toward becoming one's true self by integrating conscious and unconscious aspects.

- **Symbolism in Dreams and Symptoms:** Interpreting dreams and symptoms as messages from the unconscious that guide personal growth.

- **Holistic Approach to Psychotherapy:** Addressing the mind, soul, and spirit to achieve self-actualization and authenticity.
