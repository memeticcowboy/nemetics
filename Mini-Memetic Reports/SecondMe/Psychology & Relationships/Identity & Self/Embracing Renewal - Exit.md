## 🧠 **Core Idea Unit**

This text functions as a **psychological refusal of ossification**: a way to stop past choices, identities, or habits from hardening into destiny.<br>Here, the “Grid” is internalized—consistency pressure, optimization guilt, the demand to remain legible to one’s former self and to others.<br>The mental shift is from *“I chose this, so I must continue”* to *“I chose this once, and I can choose again.”*
