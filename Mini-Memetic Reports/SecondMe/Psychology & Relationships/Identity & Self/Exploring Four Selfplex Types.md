# Exploring Four Selfplex Types: Autonomy, Collective Liberation, Neuro-Determinism, and Spiritual Autonomy

### 🧠 Selfplex Type: *Sovereign Individualists*

- **Core Narrative:** *“The world is trying to control you; only total personal autonomy can save you.”*<br>Based on a hero's journey archetype where the individual escapes collective delusion or tyranny to realize true agency.
- **Memetic Immune Triggers:**
    - Phrases suggesting structural determinism (e.g. “You didn’t build that”)
    - Collectivist rhetoric or appeals to systemic thinking
    - Scientific critiques of consciousness or free will
- **“They heard…” Biases:**
    - “We are shaped by society” → *“You’re saying I’m a victim or not in control.”*
    - “Freedom requires limits” → *“You’re justifying oppression.”*
- **Symbolic Markers:**
    - Black-and-red aesthetics, chains breaking, lone wolves, “red pill” symbolism
    - Language of *sovereignty*, *self-ownership*, *voluntarism*, *the Matrix*
- **Epistemic Style:**
    - Personal introspection, counter-cultural revelations, libertarian rationalism
- **Vulnerabilities:**
    - Ignores unconscious influences, neurobiological constraints
    - Can veer into conspiracy or radical solipsism
- **Persuasion Entry Points:**
    - Frames around “mastery” or “voluntary cooperation”
    - Philosophical compatibilism (i.e., determinism *with* agency)

***

### 🧠 Selfplex Type: *Collective Liberators*

- **Core Narrative:** *“No one is free until we all are.”*<br>Centers on social justice mythos where systemic liberation overrides personal volition as the ultimate moral imperative.
- **Memetic Immune Triggers:**
    - Appeals to meritocracy, bootstrapping, or hyper-individualism
    - Language framing “free will” as equally distributed or universal
    - Abstract philosophical discourse that evades material context
- **“They heard…” Biases:**
    - “Everyone has free will” → *“You’re blaming the oppressed.”*
    - “We are already free” → *“You’re erasing lived injustice.”*
- **Symbolic Markers:**
    - Raised fists, intersectional flags, slogans like “freedom is collective”
    - Terms like *systems of oppression*, *lived experience*, *unearned privilege*
- **Epistemic Style:**
    - Lived experience, collective consensus, post-structural critique
- **Vulnerabilities:**
    - Prone to epistemic closure and purity spirals
    - Can de-emphasize individual moral agency entirely
- **Persuasion Entry Points:**
    - Moral narratives that integrate *personal liberation through systemic change*
    - Emphasizing the empowerment dimension of constrained choice

***

### 🧠 Selfplex Type: *Neuro-Determinists*

- **Core Narrative:** *“You are a biological machine running on predictable patterns.”*<br>Draws on a reductionist science myth where neuroscience and AI define the limits of consciousness and behavior.
- **Memetic Immune Triggers:**
    - Appeals to metaphysical freedom, “soul,” or divine agency
    - Legal or moral frameworks implying free will as real
    - Folk psychology and introspective testimony
- **“They heard…” Biases:**
    - “You chose this” → *“You’re ignoring causality and brain chemistry.”*
    - “You’re responsible” → *“That’s pseudoscientific moralism.”*
- **Symbolic Markers:**
    - Brain scans, neural pathways, computational metaphors
    - Language of *bias*, *neural correlates*, *predictive modeling*
- **Epistemic Style:**
    - Empirical reductionism, experimental validation, cognitive modeling
- **Vulnerabilities:**
    - Underestimates subjective experience and ethical nuance
    - Easily challenged by anomalies in behavioral variance
- **Persuasion Entry Points:**
    - Compatibilist arguments that preserve accountability within constraints
    - Emotional narratives about meaningful decision-making under probabilistic models

***

### 🧠 Selfplex Type: *Spiritual Autonomists*

- **Core Narrative:** *“You are a soul navigating karmic or divine freedom; true will transcends material constraint.”*<br>Based in mystical or esoteric traditions, framing freedom as a spiritual journey and will as a divine gift.
- **Memetic Immune Triggers:**
    - Materialist reduction, scientism, state control
    - Any argument denying inner sovereignty or sacred volition
- **“They heard…” Biases:**
    - “You’re influenced by biology” → *“You’re denying the spirit.”*
    - “Freedom is a political construct” → *“You don’t understand true liberation.”*
- **Symbolic Markers:**
    - Chakras, light imagery, lotus symbols, sacred geometry
    - Language of *awakening*, *alignment*, *divine will*, *vibration*
- **Epistemic Style:**
    - Revelation, inner knowing, spiritual transmission
- **Vulnerabilities:**
    - Confirmation bias, spiritual bypassing, detachment from material reality
- **Persuasion Entry Points:**
    - Dialogues around integrated embodiment, soul + system harmonization
    - Mythic storytelling that acknowledges both inner and outer liberation
