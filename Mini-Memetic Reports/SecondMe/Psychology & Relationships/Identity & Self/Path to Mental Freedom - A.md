## Insight
Let's explore the concept of freedom through a structured approach to mental well-being.

- Part I focuses on self-awareness: observing your mind, understanding that you are not your mind, and achieving the first step to freedom.
- Part II emphasizes rebuilding and nurturing your mind through sleep, diet, exercise, relationships, and consistent habits to achieve the second step to freedom.

Consider integrating these steps into your daily routine for a holistic approach to mental freedom.
