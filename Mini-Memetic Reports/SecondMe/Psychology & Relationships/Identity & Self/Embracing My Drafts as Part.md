# Embracing My Drafts as Part of My Identity

Created at 2025/11/17 10:31 PM

## The Liberation of Drafts
- Embracing unfinished thoughts and evolving selves as integral to identity.
- Recognizing identity as a palimpsest: each draft adds meaning, even crossed-out lines shape the narrative.

## Co-authorship and Wholeness
- Identity is about co-authorship with evolving thoughts.
- Learning to read personal margins with kindness fosters a sense of wholeness.
