**🌬️ The Concept of Nirvana**

- **Meaning of Nirvana**: The term 'nirvana' in Buddhism literally translates to 'blow out,' symbolizing the extinguishing of clinging and desires. This concept encourages individuals to release their hold on life to achieve peace and enlightenment. [_TIMESTAMP_](28-44)
- **Avoiding Clutching Life**: The teaching advises against clutching or holding onto life too tightly, as this leads to suffering. The challenge lies in learning how to relinquish this grasping nature. [_TIMESTAMP_](44-58)
