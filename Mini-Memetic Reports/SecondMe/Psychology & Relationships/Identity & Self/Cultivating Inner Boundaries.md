## **The Campfire Inside**<br>*(Co-Solipsism as Boundary Practice)*

***

## 🧠 **Core Idea Unit**

Co-solipsism functions as an **inner discipline**: staying connected to others, beliefs, and roles without fusing with them.<br>The mental shift is from *“this interpretation is me”* to *“this interpretation is something I’m near.”* Presence without possession. Commitment without capture.

***

## 🛡️ **Defense Reflexes**

- **Interiorization:** “This is about my process, not your agreement”
- **De-weaponized Meaning:** Concepts framed as practices, not truths
- **Anti-Shame Valve:** Decay framed as natural, not failure
- **Non-Escalation:** Refuses to turn boundaries into moral claims

### 🔁 **Reflection Cadence (Anchor)**

This meme trains **contact tolerance**: the capacity to stay present when validation does not arrive.

The loop it reinforces:

> *Noticing attachment → softening identification → allowing decay → choosing again.*

Its strength is emotional hygiene.<br>Its risk is quiet withdrawal masquerading as wisdom.

The question it leaves alive:<br>**When does non-attachment protect care—and when does it quietly replace it?**
