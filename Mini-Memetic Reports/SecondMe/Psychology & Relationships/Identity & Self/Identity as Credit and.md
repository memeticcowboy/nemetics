# Identity as Credit and Porosity

Created at 2026/01/07 7:41 PM

## The Fragility of Identity
- Identity flickers between ownership and passage.
- When tightened into a noun (“this is me”), identity becomes property.
- This shift slows the flow of identity, creating an economy that calcifies.
- Value becomes hoarded, losing currency over time.

## Porosity as a Technical Instruction
- Carson’s phrase “keep the economy porous” emphasizes the need for flexibility.
- Porosity allows for exchange and interaction with the world.
- Sealed identities cannot transact; they recirculate stale air, making them counterfeit.
- Renewal occurs in the small slack following moments of tightening, allowing for movement and adaptation.

## Current and Renewal
- The notion of slack is where renewal lives.
- Balanced selfhood requires tension and release to maintain authenticity.
- Monitoring the flow of identity is crucial to keep meaning alive and relevant.
