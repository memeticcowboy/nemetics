## Uncanny Valley in Simulations: What is Conscious 

Interesting to consider this. What if our creators in this simulation can’t tell if we’re conscious as their consciousness is so wildly different?

The "uncanny valley" phenomenon primarily affects the perception of human-like entities, rather than the measurement of proto-consciousness. It describes the discomfort or eeriness people feel when encountering entities that are almost, but not quite, human-like[3][6]. This phenomenon is more relevant to fields like robotics and animation, where human likeness is a factor, rather than to the study of proto-consciousness in panpsychism. The challenge lies in distinguishing genuine consciousness from mere simulations or approximations of human-like behavior, which can complicate interpretations of consciousness in artificial systems.
