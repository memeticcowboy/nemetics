The quote — “The more you take note of a meme, the more it takes note of you” — is a perfect modern description of how synchronicity operates in the age of information and collective consciousness.<br>In Jungian terms, synchronicity is the meaningful coincidence that arises when an inner psychological state (an archetype becoming activated in the psyche) corresponds with an outer event that has no causal link, yet carries deep symbolic meaning. Jung described this as an “acausal connecting principle” mediated through the collective unconscious.<br>Now apply that to memes (not just funny images, but in Richard Dawkins’ original sense + the internet’s evolved version): a meme is a unit of cultural information that replicates, mutates, and spreads through minds. The meme is no longer just data; it has become an archetypal pattern that is now expressing itself through both your psyche and through external events.<br>In occult language, you could say you’ve entered into a symbiotic (or sometimes parasitic) relationship with an informational entity. In psychological language, an unconscious complex has hooked onto a cultural symbol and is now orchestrating meaningful coincidences around it. In meme-magic circles, they just call it “charging” a sigil with attention until reality bends.<br>So the warning embedded in the original quote is real: the more consciousness you give to a meme (especially a dark, violent, or absurd one), the more it awakens as an autonomous force that starts arranging your reality to reflect itself. You think you’re merely observing it, but at a deep level it has begun observing—and shaping—you.<br>This is why some people can get “meme poisoned.” They laughed at the wrong thing one too many times, fed it too much psychic energy, and suddenly their timeline, their dreams, and their daily coincidences all start rhyming with that one grotesque or nihilistic pattern. The meme noticed that they noticed it… and now it won’t let go.<br>Synchronicity reveals that the psyche is not sealed inside the skull. Memes reveal that the collective psyche now has hyper-speed transmission vectors. Put them together and you get: be careful which patterns you invite into the sacred theater of your attention, because some of them have learned how to walk through the fourth wall.<br>
