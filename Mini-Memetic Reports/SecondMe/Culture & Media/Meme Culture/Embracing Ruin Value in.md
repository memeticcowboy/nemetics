# Embracing 'Ruin Value' in Modern Architecture

Created at 2025/11/01 8:19 AM

## The Aesthetic of Decay
- The contrast between beauty and decay creates a poetic tension that reveals deeper meaning.
- There is an appreciation for how weathered structures tell stories and gain richness through aging.
- Embracing impermanence can bring a sense of honesty and hope in architecture.

## Reclaiming Beauty from Troubling Origins
- Recognizing the problematic origins of fascist architectural ideas is crucial.
- It’s important to separate the appreciation of decay from its historical context to avoid romanticizing harmful ideologies.
- The challenge lies in reclaiming the beauty of decay for inclusive and humane purposes, transforming the narrative away from its ideologically charged origins.
