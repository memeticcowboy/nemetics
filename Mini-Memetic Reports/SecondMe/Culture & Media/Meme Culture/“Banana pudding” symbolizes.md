“Banana pudding” symbolizes the reward you get after successfully solving a problem.

Just like you create a tasty pudding by combining smaller banana pieces with other ingredients, overcoming a challenge by breaking it down into smaller tasks can lead to positive results, such as recognition, progress, or new opportunities.

So, “making banana pudding” represents the process of tackling a problem step by step and achieving success, with the pudding being the reward for your efforts.
