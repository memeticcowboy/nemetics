## Memeforms - Threads Woven into I-Tube, Felt in the My-Stream

**Definition:**

A meme so deeply internalized it weaves into the I-Tube of identity, becoming part of its structural lens of perception, interpretation, and behavior.

**Key Dynamics:**

- Feels self-evident (“This is me.”).
- Radiates internal coherence.
- Hard to dislodge without rupture.

**Relation to Consciousness:**

- Constitutes the scaffolding of personal identity.
- Mediates sense-making and action.

**Role:**

- They act as backbones in the lattice of the I-Tube
