Blended Visual Learning: Implications Wheel 

Joel A. Barker’s blended visual learning “implications wheel diagrams” for assessing impacts and (possible, probable, preferred) implications might facilitate “integrated thinking” pathways

Author Joel A. Barker's blended visual learning approach, specifically the "implications wheel diagrams," can facilitate integrated thinking pathways by assessing impacts and implications for sustainable alternative futures scenarios. These diagrams help individuals explore the potential consequences of decisions or trends by visually mapping out the ripple effects across various categories like social, technological, economic, ecological, and political (STEEP). By using this tool, individuals can identify pathways to beneficial possibilities, erect barriers to negative outcomes, and pinpoint crucial information needs for informed decision-making. 

The implications wheel method encourages a comprehensive analysis of both positive and negative consequences, promoting thoughtful and powerful decision-making processes. Through this visual representation, stakeholders can avoid immediate benefits that may lead to long-term negative consequences and capitalize on positive implications before they occur. 

Overall, Barker's implications wheel diagrams support integrated thinking by providing a structured approach to considering the multifaceted impacts of decisions and trends on sustainable future scenarios.
