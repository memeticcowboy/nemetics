**Types of Power: Technofeudalism**

Here's a breakdown of the different types of power described in the video, with specific attention to the provided context:

**Brute Force**

Definition: The use of physical force, violence, or the threat of violence to establish dominance and control.


**Soft Power**

Definition: The ability to influence others through persuasion, cultural appeal, or the shaping of preferences, as opposed to force or coercion.


Manifestations in the Video:


**The Techno Structure**: Corporate entities using advertising and behavior modification to control consumer choices and social values.


**Cloud Capital**: Digital platforms subtly controlling attention and influencing behavior through algorithms and network effects.


**Political Power (or Agenda-Setting Power)**

Definition: The ability to control who is represented and what issues are discussed or decided in forums where collective decisions are made.


Traditional Examples: Landowners and monarchs in feudal times, factory owners during early capitalism.


**Modern Evolution**:


Techno Structure: The rise of corporate influence in shaping what social goals are deemed important.


Cloud Capital: Potential for online platforms to manipulate what information is seen, which voices are amplified, and what societal values dominate.


**Capitalist Power**

Definition: Control over the means of production, used to extract profits by employing labor. Power stemming from owning the tools and resources required for others to make a living.


Example: Factory owners using their control of the production process to exert power over workers.


Important Notes from the Video:

These forms of power are not mutually exclusive and can be wielded in tandem.


The rise of Cloud Capital introduces new dynamics of power, particularly around control of networks and access to information.


Understanding these power types is key to analyzing the potential shift from capitalism to technofeudalism.
