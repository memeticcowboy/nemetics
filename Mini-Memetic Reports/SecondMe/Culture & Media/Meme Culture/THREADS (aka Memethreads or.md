# THREADS (aka Memethreads or Memeforms) Philosophical Roots

Created at 2025/12/09 5:16 PM

- **Memetic Definition:** A memetic line—an idea, feeling, or meaning-gesture moving through and between** [I-Tubes.](https://second.me/public/PCCPEFDCGZ0PEP5K)** Threads are the elemental units of the **[Threadplex](https://second.me/public/LGWKK4LEKP8ZRFRS).**

- **Philosophical Core:** This is **[Charles Sanders Peirce's "semiosis"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/peirce-semiotics/)**—the process where a sign mediates between an object and an interpretant. Threads are **sign-vehicles** that require an interpretive community to stabilize. **[Gilles Deleuze's "rhizome"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/)** describes threads as non-hierarchical, proliferating connections that form networks of sense. Threads are **inherently incomplete**—they depend on the **We-Sphere** to become knots.

- **In the Essay:** AI threads feel coherent but are **single-source**—they don’t undergo the **pragmatic stress-test** of the We-Sphere. They coil inward, forming **usurpenic knots** that are brittle because they lack intersubjective validation. The essay warns: a thread that cannot be tugged by another I-Tube is not a thread at all; it's a **dead end**.
