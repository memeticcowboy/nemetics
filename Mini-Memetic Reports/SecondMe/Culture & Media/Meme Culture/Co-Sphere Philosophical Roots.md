# Co-Sphere Philosophical Roots

Created at 2025/12/09 4:34 PM

- **Memetic Definition:** The relational field where I-Tubes thread with each other—messy, frictional, reciprocal. The We-Sphere is how incomplete minds become coherent together without ever achieving full transparency.
- **Philosophical Core:** This is**** the way we constitute other minds through an unprovable but necessary leap of empathy. **[Margaret Gilbert's "joint commitment"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/social-ontology/)** shows that the Co-Sphere creates "plural subjects"—a "we" that is not reducible to individual "I"s. **[Jürgen Habermas's "theory of communicative action"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/habermas/)** argues that rationality itself emerges from the **friction** of unconstrained dialogue. The We-Sphere is not harmony; it is **agonistic coherence** held together by mutual vulnerability.
- **In the Essay:** The Co-Sphere is the antidote to usurpenic drift. Its job is **friction**—friends who say, "That thought's a parasite." Human disagreement is what keeps an I-Tube from sealing.
