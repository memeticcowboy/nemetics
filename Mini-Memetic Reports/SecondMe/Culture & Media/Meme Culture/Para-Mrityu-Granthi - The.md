It may function within esoteric, mystical, or post-human discourses, symbolizing unresolved karmas, intergenerational trauma, soul memory, or ideological continuation past corporeal extinction. Its memetic power lies in compressing vast ontologies into a compact symbol — a “granthi” or knot, denoting entanglement, difficulty, and binding — extending this into para (beyond) and mrityu (death), invoking both mythic and existential reverberations.
