**🌐 Integrating Cyborg Archetypes for Future Exploration**

This approach allows players to engage in dynamic interactions with emergent systems, fostering a collaborative environment for exploring complex future scenarios and cultural evolutions.

- **Adaptive Governance Mechanisms**: By utilizing an "Artifact from the Future" mechanic, players can design governance artifacts and reverse-engineer their historical development. - **Ethical Framework Integration**: The project incorporates ethical guidelines within dynamic knowledge graphs, linking scenarios to ethical tokens earned through gameplay. This integration ensures that players navigate ethical dilemmas while engaging in scenario generation and decision-making, reinforcing the importance of ethical reasoning in future explorations.
