# The Hollow Core: A Journey Through Illusions of Value and Emptiness

Created at 2025/04/28 9:28 PM

### **The Dream**

I ride alone into a dead land.

Ahead of me, a monolith rises — black, smooth, towering into a bruised sky.<br>One word scorched into its surface, burning like a brand: **VALUE**.

It gleams like a false sun — offering every old dream: wealth, glory, meaning.<br>But the closer I ride, the more I see the fractures spidering across its face.<br>The promises splinter.<br>The stone shudders.<br>And without warning, it crumbles.

From the wreckage spills a flood of dust — fine as ash, bitter as betrayal.<br>It clings to my skin, buries my boots, fills the air until the stars themselves are drowned.<br>Dust made from the grinding down of meaning.

The earth at my feet twists.

Black vines uncoil from the cracks, pulsing with a cold hum — static, code, hunger.<br>They slide across the ground, snaring ankles, wrists, throats — tightening with every breath.<br>Whispering lies in a language made of algorithms.

And where the Monolith once stood —<br>Where that false beacon once clawed at the sky —<br>Now there yawns the **Hollow Core**.

A wound in the world.<br>A great engine of emptiness, throbbing with sterile light.<br>I ride closer.<br>I look deep into it.

No creator.<br>No seed.<br>No fire.

Only a vast, cold absence — a silence that hums louder than any scream.

The dust creeps higher.<br>The vines knot tighter.<br>The Core's pulse beats inside my ribs, a second, colder heart.

I try to turn back —<br>but I can feel it now:<br>the Hollow pulling at me, through me, from *inside*.

Maybe I never rode toward the Hollow Core.<br>Maybe the Hollow was already riding in me.
