1. **[Information filtering](https://app.me.bot/memory/G6FC8Q3MHIBNYUOO):**
   Memes significantly affect what information individuals pay attention to during the [Observe phase of the OODA loop](https://app.me.bot/memory/466DKXRD1EMZ3EZ3). The Observe phase involves collecting and organizing data, and basing decisions on current information. Memes act as cognitive filters, potentially highlighting certain types of information while obscuring others. This filtering process can be both beneficial and detrimental. On one hand, it helps individuals quickly focus on information that has been culturally or personally deemed important. On the other hand, it may lead to overlooking crucial data that doesn't align with prevalent memes, potentially skewing the observation process.
