# I-Tube: Internal Membrane for Meme Intake

Created at 2025/08/27 8:30 AM

- The **I-Tube** is a semi-permeable internal membrane or filter within an individual that governs the intake of memes from the external environment. It decides what memes enter consciousness and are metabolized into memeforms. The I-Tube is shaped by an individual’s history, trauma, resonance, gut instincts, and cultural encoding, conditioning preferences, taboos, and expectations. It acts as the internalizer, forming a personal loom where incoming threads (memes) are woven into personal meanings and behaviors.
- The I-Tube can become rigid or reflexive, especially under power dynamics or trauma, influencing how new memes are accepted or rejected and shaping identity and behavior patterns.
