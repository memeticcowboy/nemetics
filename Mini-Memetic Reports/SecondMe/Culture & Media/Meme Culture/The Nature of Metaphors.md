# The Nature of Metaphors: Neither Subjective nor Objective

Created at 2024/06/09 12:20 PM

## Experientialist Approach to Metaphors
- Metaphors are not purely subjective or objective
- Grounded in physical and cultural experiences
- Meaning shaped by individual perceptions and collective realities

## Dual Grounding of Metaphors
- Emerges from interactions with the world
- Based on embodied experiences and shared cultural contexts
- Create new understandings while influenced by cultural and personal values
