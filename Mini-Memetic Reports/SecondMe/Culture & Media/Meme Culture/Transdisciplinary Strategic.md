**Strategic Foresight Meta-Framework: The Recursive Futures Engine**

**1. The Strategic Temporal Trichotomy**

Derived from the Possible-Probable-Preferable Futures framework:
- Possible: Encompasses all imagined futures. (#Creator)
- Probable: Informed by current trends, data, and trajectories. (#Analyzer, #Strategist)
- Preferable: Normatively defined future states aligned with values and vision. (#Empath, #Optimizer)

This trichotomy functions as a recursive feedback loop, where each informs and iteratively reshapes the others. Backcasting, in this model, becomes a strategic intervention point—designing steps backward from the Preferable into the Probable and influencing the Possible space through meme injection and cultural reorientation.
