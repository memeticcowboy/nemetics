## Insight
Let's explore how Bloom's Taxonomy can be applied to the study of memes using the NEME framework (Notice, Engage, Mull, Exchange). This approach provides a structured method for understanding and creating memes.

- Notice: Students first encounter and recognize memes. They recall basic information and explain what makes a meme a meme.
- Engage: Students interact deeply with memes by using them in relevant contexts and deconstructing their components.
- Mull: Students critically evaluate memes, assessing their effectiveness and appropriateness, and create new, original memes.
- Exchange: Students share and discuss their memes, collaborating to enhance and refine them through group activities.

Consider using this framework to create a meme-based project in your class to enhance engagement and critical thinking.
