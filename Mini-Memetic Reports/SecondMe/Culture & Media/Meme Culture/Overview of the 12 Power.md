# 12 Power Types

Created at 2025/07/12 6:55 AM

Here is a clean list of the 12 Power Types as sourced from [Pieter de Beer | Substack](https://substack.com/@powercoord) with their definitions, as used in the Memetic Ecology Power Typology Analysis:

**Power Types & Definitions**

1. Power To<br>The capacity to initiate, shape, or enact transformation.<br>Expressed through volitional coherence or creative emergence.
2. Power For<br>Ethical or relational service to others.<br>Devotion to healing, care, or justice through action.
3. Power Against<br>Structural resistance or systemic refusal.<br>Often embodied in rupture, protest, or withdrawal from dominant norms.
4. Power From<br>Groundedness in ancestral, cultural, or ecological lineage.<br>Carries intergenerational memory or deep coherence.
5. Power Beneath<br>Subversive or hidden power expressed from marginal positions.<br>Emerges through dissidence, liminality, or poetic refusal.
6. Power As<br>Memetic or symbolic embodiment of a role, archetype, or identity.<br>Can be empowering or hollow, depending on coherence.
7. Power Over<br>Dominance through control, coercion, or narrative closure.<br>Often imposed via institutions or normalized scripts.
8. Power With<br>Co-created coherence through mutual resonance, trust, and collective authorship.<br>Often visible in rituals, sync, and co-regulation.
9. Power Through<br>Emergent systemic coherence.<br>Power flowing through distributed actors, fields, or feedback loops—found in networks and swarm logic.
10. Power Within<br>Structural sovereignty and internal coherence.<br>The ability to re-author belief, action, and pattern from the inside out.
11. Pseudo Power<br>Simulated or performative power that lacks authentic traction.<br>Memeforms of false coherence or borrowed authority.
12. Null Power<br>Total collapse of volition, structure, or meaning.<br>May appear as apathy, fragmentation, or semantic exhaustion.
