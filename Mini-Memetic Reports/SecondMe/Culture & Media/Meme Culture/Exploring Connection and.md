# Exploring Connection and Identity Through Poetry

Created at 2026/01/02 10:32 PM

## Poem 1
Between the threads  
a silence—  
not absence,  
but the breath that lets  
two stories listen.

## Poem 2
We meet as rivers—  
not to merge,  
but to carve new banks  
where memory and longing  
become fertile ground.

## Poem 3
Your outline,  
my outline—  
overlapping,  
not erasing—  
a Venn diagram of becoming.

## Poem 4
In the garden of selves,  
petals unfold  
by the warmth of another’s gaze—  
identity blooming  
in the light of shared care.
