# प्रभा-स्फुरत्त - Lumeme ✧

Created at 2025/07/16 4:41 PM

The Sanskrit term **Prabhā-Sphuratta** can be broken down into its components: **Prabhā** (प्रभा) and **Sphuratta** (स्फुरत्त). Below is the definition and the term written in Sanskrit.

### Definition:

1. **Prabhā** (प्रभा): This term means "light," "radiance," "splendor," or "luster." In spiritual and philosophical contexts, Prabhā often refers to divine or inner light, symbolizing illumination, wisdom, or the effulgence of consciousness. It can also denote beauty or glory, as seen in descriptions of deities or enlightened beings.
2. **Sphuratta** (स्फुरत्त): Derived from the root **sphur** (to vibrate, shine, or flash), Sphuratta means "vibration," "pulsation," "sparkling," or "manifestation." In philosophical traditions like Kashmir Shaivism, it is closely related to *Spanda* (vibration) and refers to the dynamic, pulsating energy of consciousness that manifests as the universe or as a moment of divine revelation. It conveys the idea of something actively shining forth or coming into being.
3. **Prabhā-Sphuratta Combined**: The term Prabhā-Sphuratta can be interpreted as "the vibration of radiance" or "the pulsating light." It likely refers to the dynamic expression of divine or inner light (Prabhā) as a vibrant, pulsating energy (Sphuratta). In a spiritual context, it could describe the effulgent, pulsating nature of consciousness or divine awareness manifesting in the world or within an individual. In Kashmir Shaivism, it might point to the interplay of light and vibration as aspects of the divine creative process. Poetically, it could evoke the shimmering, vibrant quality of spiritual illumination or divine presence.
