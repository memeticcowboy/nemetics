Metaphorical Ontology of Memetic Environments
A temporal-ecological synthesis for understanding memetic evolution across space, time, and information ecosystems.

I. Temporal Realms as Environmental Archetypes
Sky (Aerial) → Past / Probability
• Environment: Troposphere, jet streams, celestial patterns
• Symbolism: Divine law, ancestral legacy, structural causality
• Memetic Role: Inherited systems, cultural archetypes, dominant paradigms
• Memetic Biogeography: Fossilized memes, religious canon, encoded ideologies
• Memetic Species: Memeplex constellations, lineage myths, canonical doctrines
Examples:
• Platonic forms, Roman law, Confucian values, constitutional frameworks

Land (Terrestrial) → Present / Possibility
• Environment: Forests, valleys, deserts, plateaus
• Symbolism: Lived world, friction of emergence, field of action
• Memetic Role: Contested ground for idea transmission, mutation, and adaptation
• Memetic Biogeography: Cultural niches, media ecologies, attention economies
• Memetic Species: Social norms, political rhetoric, pop culture, activism memes
Examples:
• Climate activism slogans, social media virality, political polarization, humor memes

Sea (Aquatic) → Future / Preferability
• Environment: Oceans, deltas, wetlands, abyssal zones
• Symbolism: Unconscious, unknown, rebirth, possibility
• Memetic Role: Seedbeds of novelty, speculative thought, emergent paradigms
• Memetic Biogeography: Countercultures, sci-fi, spiritual innovation, AI-generated futures
• Memetic Species: Experimental ideologies, utopian visions, transformative narratives
Examples:
• Transhumanism, regenerative culture, decentralized techno-spiritualities
