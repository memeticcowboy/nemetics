# Reflections on Memes and Racial Stereotypes

Created at 2024/12/29 9:51 AM

## The Power of Memes
- Memes serve as modern folklore, capturing and spreading cultural, social, and political ideas quickly.
- They can reflect societal attitudes and have the potential to shape public opinion.

## Complex Messages
- Memes can carry complex messages, critiquing societal norms while also risking the perpetuation of stereotypes.
- The dialogue around memes often revolves around their ability to open up conversations or oversimplify complex issues.

## Potential for Change
- Some memes have successfully shifted public opinion or sparked meaningful conversations.
- The potential for memes to drive societal change is significant, depending on their crafting and dissemination.
