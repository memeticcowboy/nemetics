## **Campfire Pluralism**<br>*(aka: “Co-Solipsism, But Make It Cozy”)*

***

## 🧠 **Core Idea Unit**

People don’t need shared truth, frameworks, or ontologies to coexist—only enough tolerance, irony, and warmth to sit near one another without resolving disagreement.<br>The mental shift invited is **from argument to atmosphere**: stop asking “who’s right?” and start asking “can we stay here anyway?”

***

## 🛡️ **Defense Reflexes**

- **Irony Shield:** Any critique is absorbed as “missing the point”
- **Ambiguity Armor:** Claims are atmospheric, not falsifiable
- **Moral Softening:** Disagreement framed as aggression or over-seriousness
- **Exit Clause:** “It’s just a vibe, not a system”
