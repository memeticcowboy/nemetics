# Council of the Six Cyborgs: Tackling Memetic Crises with AI and Collaboration

Created at 2024/12/15 3:58 PM

"Council of the Six Cyborgs" is a text-based simulation game where six players, each embodying a unique cyborg role with specific strengths (Analyzer, Strategist, Creator, Empath, Protector, Optimizer), collaboratively combat a harmful meme. The game unfolds in rounds with proposal and feedback phases, culminating in a "Council Plan" to address the memetic crisis. Success hinges on the group's ability to create a coherent, ethical, and effective plan that considers risks and long-term consequences. Each player contributes via short, strategic "tweets," fostering collaborative decision-making under pressure. The game simulates the complexities of managing a global memetic crisis through a collaborative, role-playing approach.
