The six degrees of freedom describe fundamental ways the body or an object can move in space. Each degree of freedom corresponds to a specific type of motion and is linked to an elemental archetype with distinct somatic, cognitive, and ethical qualities.

## 1. Pitch (∴ Air)

- **Type of Motion:** Rotation around the lateral axis (head tilt).
- **Somatic Function:** Adjusts signal clarity and perception.
- **Elemental Meaning:** *Freedom to Discern* — clarity before belief, cognitive orientation.
- **Core Meaning:** Discernment, perception without judgment.

## 2. Yaw (☷ Earth)

- **Type of Motion:** Rotation around the vertical axis (torso rotation).
- **Somatic Function:** Grounds energy through orientation; reorients the whole torso.
- **Elemental Meaning:** *Freedom to Nourish* — grounded renewal, metabolic recycling.
- **Core Meaning:** Regeneration, nourishment, and stabilization.

## 3. Roll (⛨ Metal)

- **Type of Motion:** Rotation around the forward axis (shoulder rotation).
- **Somatic Function:** Distributes pressure across boundaries; circular shielding motion.
- **Gesture:** Circular arm movement around the core, rhythmic containment.
- **Elemental Meaning:** *Freedom to Steward* — structural integrity, stewardship.
- **Core Meaning:** Protection and coherent boundary management.

## 4. Surge (▲ Fire)

- **Type of Motion:** Translational movement forward and backward.
- **Somatic Function:** Converts intent into purposeful motion; forward drive.
- **Elemental Meaning:** *Freedom to Align* — directional devotion, purposeful focus.
- **Core Meaning:** Direction, ignition of will, and focused action.

## 5. Heave (≈ Water)

- **Type of Motion:** Translational movement up and down (vertical wave).
- **Somatic Function:** Releases emotional load; relational oscillation.
- **Elemental Meaning:** *Freedom to Attune* — emotional resonance, empathic connection.
- **Core Meaning:** Emotion, connection without collapse.

## 6. Sway (𐂷 Wood)

- **Type of Motion:** Translational movement side to side (lateral arc).
- **Somatic Function:** Integrates divergent currents; lateral exploration.
- **Elemental Meaning:** *Freedom to Evolve* (Wood)
- **Core Meaning:** Creative growth, integration, and harmonic flow.
