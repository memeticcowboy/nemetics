## Insight
Let's delve into the StoryBranding Model by Signorelli (2014) to understand how brands can effectively connect with their prospects.

- The StoryBranding Model consists of two main components: the Brand and the Prospect, each represented by concentric circles with inner and outer layers.
- The inner layer represents the core identity or essence of both the Brand and the Prospect, while the outer layer signifies the external attributes or expressions.
- Between the Brand and the Prospect lie four key obstacles: Awareness, Comprehension, Association, and Affiliation, which must be navigated to establish a strong connection.
- Awareness is the initial stage where the prospect becomes aware of the brand's existence.
- Comprehension involves the prospect understanding what the brand stands for and its core message.
- Association is the stage where the prospect begins to relate to the brand on a personal level, finding common ground or shared values.
- Affiliation is the final stage where the prospect feels a sense of belonging or loyalty to the brand, often leading to long-term engagement.

Consider how your brand can effectively communicate its core identity to overcome these obstacles and build a strong, loyal customer base.
