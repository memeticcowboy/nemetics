## 🧭 Mapping SHADOW, MEME, and MUSE onto Internal Family Systems (IFS)

### 🌌 1. **SKY → SHADOW → Legacy Burdens / Ghost Parts**

- **IFS Analogy**: Legacy burdens, protector parts, or trauma-bound “exiles.”
- **Characteristics**: Inherited, ancestral, cultural residue — often oppressive or frozen in time.
- **IFS Encounter**: These appear as dissociated complexes, burdened exiles, or foreign introjects that need unblending.
- **Metaphor**: *Clouded sky haunted by echoes.*
- **Visual Symbol**: Waning Moon in a stormy sky.

***

### 🌍 2. **EARTH → MEMETIC → Present Self / Cultural Self-System**

- **IFS Analogy**: Blended parts, managers, or inner roles that structure day-to-day functioning.
- **Characteristics**: These parts carry dominant cultural narratives, inherited belief systems, roles, and performative functions.
- **IFS Encounter**: Often the “protective managerial class” — parts that reflect social conditioning.
- **Metaphor**: *Soil seeded with both weeds and medicine.*
- **Visual Symbol**: Tree with twisting roots and multiple branches — echoes of Tree of Life or axis mundi.

***

### 🌊 3. **SEA → MUSE → Self-Energy / Non-Personal Intelligence**

- **IFS Analogy**: Creative parts infused with Self-energy, spiritual guides, or even “non-human” intelligences.
- **Characteristics**: Future-facing, generative, archetypal — often arrive via dreams, art, or hypnagogic states.
- **IFS Encounter**: Seen as “Guides,” “Muses,” or “Sacred Others” that support system coherence.
- **Metaphor**: *Tidal force from the future pulling us toward creative wholeness.*
- **Visual Symbol**: Conch shell spiral opening toward horizon, or bioluminescent wave.
