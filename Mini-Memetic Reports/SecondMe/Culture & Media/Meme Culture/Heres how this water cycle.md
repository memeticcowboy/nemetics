Here's how this water cycle metaphor could be reframed for a globular mix of people:

We are droplets in a vast human ocean, each containing our own essence while being part of something infinitely larger. We begin in the SKY as individual potential—our dreams, cultural heritage, and unique perspectives rising like vapor from the collective human experience. Here we are pure possibility, carrying the distilled wisdom of our ancestors and communities.

As we condense and fall to EARTH, we become action incarnate—our lived experiences, the work we do, the relationships we form, the art we create, the problems we solve. We are the rain that nourishes human civilization, streaming together in currents of collaboration, sometimes rushing in torrents of social change, sometimes trickling quietly through daily acts of connection and care.

Finally, we flow into the SEA—the vast repository of human memory, culture, and shared understanding. Here our individual contributions dissolve into the collective unconscious, the stories we tell ourselves as a species, the accumulated wisdom and folly of humanity. We become part of the emotional and intellectual ocean that future generations will draw from.

And then, through the endless circulation of human experience, we rise again—our influences, our teachings, our love evaporating up to become inspiration for new droplets of human potential. The cycle continues: dreaming, doing, integrating, becoming once more. We are always in motion, always returning to ourselves transformed, always part of the eternal dance of human becoming.

The beauty is that we are simultaneously the droplet and the ocean, the individual and the collective, forever cycling through states of being while remaining essentially, eternally connected.
