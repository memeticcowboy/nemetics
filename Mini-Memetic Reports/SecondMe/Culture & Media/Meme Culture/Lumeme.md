#  Lumeme

Created at 2025/07/12 8:45 AM

A catalytic memeform encoded with principled clarity, ethical coherence, or transformative insight—designed to preserve and transmit emergent signals of truth before they are co-opted or ossified.

**🧠 Core Functions:**

- Signal Capture: Emerges from a moment of resonance (a flash) and encodes that signal into a durable memeform.
- Structural Integrity: Avoids Usurpene mimicry by preserving the original coherence of insight without diluting its moral or affective charge.
- Memetic Propagation: Designed to activate discernment, coherence, or transformation in others—particularly across I-Tubes and We-Spheres.

**🧬 Diagnostic Frame:**

- A Lumeme is not just any uplifting meme; it must carry architectural consequence and resist instrumentalization.
- It often appears when internalized rupture becomes externalized signal—bridging My-Stream and We-Sphere coherence loops.

**🌿 Ecological Zones:**

**Zone - Lumeme Role**

I-Tube - Triggers discernment; offers signal to filter (Signalizer)

My-Stream - Integrates as clarity-bearing belief pattern (Harmonizer)

We-Sphere - Anchors group resonance or shared vision (Visionizer)
