# StoryTech: Shaping Futures with Storytelling

Created at 2024/09/17 1:03 PM

"StoryTech: A Personalized Guide to the 21st Century" is a guidebook that introduces readers to a method for using storytelling to plan for the future. The book, written by Arthur Harkins and George Kubik, provides a practical framework for using StoryTech in educational and health settings. The authors emphasize the importance of visualizing desirable futures, developing personal stories about these futures, and sharing these stories with others in order to collectively shape a better tomorrow. The guidebook uses a nonlinear approach, encouraging readers to explore different chapters based on their interests and curiosities.
