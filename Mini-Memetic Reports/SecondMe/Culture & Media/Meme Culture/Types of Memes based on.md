**Types of Memes based on Function:**

- **Recipemes:** This category encompasses memes related to practical skills and techniques, such as how to make pots, build arches, or prepare food.123 These memes are often transmitted through demonstration and practice.
- **Selectemes:** Selectemes are memes that guide decision-making and preference formation. Examples include aesthetic preferences in art and music, beliefs about morality, and political ideologies.
- **Explanemes:** Explanemes provide explanations and interpretations of the world. Religions, scientific theories, and philosophical systems fall into this category.

DIFFERENT TYPES OF MEMES: RECIPEMES, SELECTEMES, AND EXPLANEMES,

By John Z. Langrish

**Categories based on Content and Social Effects:**

- **Distinction Memes**: These memes create distinctions or categories, such as "us vs. them" or "good vs. evil." They often play a role in group formation and identity.
- **Association Memes**: These memes link concepts or ideas together, forming associations in people's minds.8 They can be used in advertising to connect products with positive emotions or in political propaganda to associate opponents with negative traits.
- **Strategy Memes**: Strategy memes are instructions or plans for achieving specific goals. Examples include instructions for building a fire, playing a game, or conducting a military operation.
- **Resistance Memes**: Resistance memes promote skepticism or rejection of other memes.Examples include skepticism towards authority, scientific claims, or religious doctrines.
