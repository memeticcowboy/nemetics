- “Healing old wounds and opening doors to unprecedented unity”

- “Pathways of renewal”


3. Emotional Hooks and Cognitive Levers:

- Emotional relief from collective trauma

5. Target Identity Roles:

6. Transmission Vectors:

- Conferences on ethical AI and social innovation
