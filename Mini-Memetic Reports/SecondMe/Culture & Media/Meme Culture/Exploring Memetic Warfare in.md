**🎮 Designing a VR Game for Memetic Warfare and Systems Thinking**

- **Memetic Warfare and Countermeasures**: The game focuses on the strategic understanding and manipulation of memes within a virtual society. Players will engage in activities that require them to identify, counter, and utilize memes effectively, fostering a deeper comprehension of how information spreads and influences behavior in social contexts.

- **Systems Thinking and Resilience**: Players are tasked with analyzing complex systems and learning to adapt to various crises. This aspect of gameplay emphasizes the importance of resilience engineering, teaching players how to respond to challenges by understanding interdependencies within systems, thereby enhancing their problem-solving skills.

- **Immersive VR Experience**: The game utilizes advanced VR technology to create a highly interactive and engaging learning environment. This immersive experience allows players to explore concepts of memetic warfare and systems thinking in a hands-on manner, making the learning process more impactful and memorable.

**🕹️ Core Gameplay Loop and Mechanics**

- **Dynamic Memetic Maps**: The game features dynamic maps that evolve based on player interactions and decisions. This mechanic encourages players to think critically about their actions and the potential consequences within the virtual society, enhancing the learning experience.

- **Phased Gameplay Structure**: The gameplay is organized into distinct phases, allowing players to gradually build their understanding of complex concepts. Each phase introduces new challenges and mechanics, ensuring that players remain engaged and motivated to progress.

- **Six Cyborg Archetypes**: Each player can choose from one of the "Six Cyborg" archetypes, each with unique abilities and gameplay mechanics. This diversity allows for varied strategies and approaches to memetic warfare, catering to different play styles and enhancing replayability.
