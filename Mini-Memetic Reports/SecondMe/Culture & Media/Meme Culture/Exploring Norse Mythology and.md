Key Concepts & Definitions

	•	Yggdrasil: In Norse mythology, the world tree that connects the realms of existence.

	•	Woden/Odin: A deity representing chaos and the pursuit to alter destiny; symbolic of the fight against inevitable outcomes.

	•	Cognitive Dissonance: Psychological state where conflicting beliefs lead to discomfort, often resulting in rationalization to maintain social harmony.

	•	TikTok Religiosity: A new form of belief and cultural identity emerging through social media, diverging from traditional religious structures.

	•	Protestantism: Historical movement that emphasizes individual interpretation of faith and has led to various sects, reflecting a personal relationship with spirituality.
