**UFOs originate from parallel dimensions, not outer space:** Vallée suggests that UFOs are physical manifestations from a multiverse that co-exists with our world, rather than traditional extraterrestrial visitors. [Source](https://www.patheos.com/blogs/publictheology/2024/10/uap-the-interdimensional-hypothesis/)

**Strong historical parallels between UFOs and folklore:** Vallée draws connections between modern UFO encounters and historical reports of fairies, elves, and religious apparitions, arguing they are variations of the same phenomenon. [Source](https://www.patheos.com/blogs/publictheology/2024/10/uap-the-interdimensional-hypothesis/)

**UFOs as a control system:** Vallée theorizes that UFOs act as a "spiritual control system," influencing human consciousness and culture rather than simply being alien craft. [Source](https://orbitaltoday.com/2025/08/09/the-cryptoterrestrial-hypothesis-explained/)

**Rejection of the extraterrestrial hypothesis (ETH):** Vallée's five arguments against ETH include the improbability of humanoid aliens evolving elsewhere and the presence of psychic phenomena in encounters. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Vall%C3%A9e)

**Psychic and symbolic aspects of UFOs:** Vallée emphasizes that UFOs cannot be understood as simple machines but must be analyzed through their psychic and symbolic impact on humans. [Source](https://www.patheos.com/blogs/publictheology/2024/10/uap-the-interdimensional-hypothesis/)
