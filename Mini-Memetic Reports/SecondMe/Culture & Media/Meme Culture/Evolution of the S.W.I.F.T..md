# Evolution of the S.W.I.F.T. Framework

Created at 2025/08/03 8:56 AM

## Shift from Speed to Awareness
- Earlier SWIFT memes focused on speed and efficiency in problem-solving and communication.
- Emphasized quick action and results, often in the context of "information warfare" or tactical collaboration.

## New S.W.I.F.T. Framework
- Represents a shift from “fast and smart” to “fluid and aware.”
- Focuses on cultivating presence, discernment, and co-creative flow.
- Moves from memetic “warfare” to memetic “gardening,” promoting a regenerative approach to culture.

## Connection to Threadplex
- Aligns with the idea of meaning as something we co-weave rather than just consume.
- Encourages participatory sensemaking and a living, participatory meaning-making process.
