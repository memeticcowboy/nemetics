**🔍 Potential Causes**
- **Link Accessibility**: The image link might not be accessible due to incorrect sharing settings or permissions.
- **URL Formatting**: The URL might be improperly formatted, leading to loading issues.
- **Network Issues**: Network connectivity problems could prevent the image from loading.

**🛠️ Proposed Solutions**
- **Check Sharing Settings**: Ensure the image link is set to 'Anyone with the link can view' in Google Drive.
- **Verify URL**: Double-check the URL for any typos or formatting errors.
- **Test Network Connection**: Ensure a stable internet connection to rule out network-related issues.

**📋 Additional Recommendations**
- **Use Alternative Hosting**: Consider using another image hosting service if the issue persists.
- **Contact Support**: Reach out to Google Drive support for assistance if the problem cannot be resolved.
