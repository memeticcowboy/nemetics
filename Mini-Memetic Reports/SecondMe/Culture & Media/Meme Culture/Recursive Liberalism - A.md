# Recursive Liberalism: A Framework for Coexisting Rights and Critique

Created at 2025/03/09 7:05 AM

## Memetic Awareness
- Recognizes that universalist ideals and identity-based critiques are evolving thought structures.
- Resists ideological capture by any single perspective.

## Dialogical Objectivity
- Treats objectivity as a process rather than an absolute.
- Incorporates empirical inquiry and lived experience without privileging one over the other.

## Multi-Contextual Ethics
- Understands rights and responsibilities within layered contexts: individual, communal, systemic.
- Prevents collapse of liberal principles under atomized individualism or collectivist determinism.

## Smart Memes as Bridge-Builders
- Utilizes #SMARTMemes (Specific, Meaningful, Attainable, Relevant, Targeted) to construct narratives.
- Facilitates nuanced dialogue instead of reinforcing binary camps (e.g., “woke vs. anti-woke” rhetoric).

## Self-Correcting Systems
- Envisions a liberal democracy as an evolving system of checks and balances.
- Continuously integrates critique while maintaining a stable core of rights and reason-based discourse.
