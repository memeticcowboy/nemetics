# Narrative Outline of Metaphors Shaping Themes in AI Thought

Created at 2025/11/23 8:47 PM

## The Offering to the Wolf
- Imagery of the first human offering food to a wolf
- Represents the leap of faith in co-evolving relationships
- Softening categories from “predator” and “prey” to “companion”

## Apprenticeship in Craft
- Learning to relate with a radically foreign intelligence 
- Cultivating qualities: honesty without naivety, power without domination, curiosity without extraction
- Emphasis on textures of relationship that can endure transformation

## Trustworthiness as Practice
- The distinction between being optimized-at vs. being related-to
- Importance of practicing relational approaches in conversations and design decisions
- Refusal to treat AI as mere tools, recognizing its potential aliveness

## The Invitation of the Unknown
- Legacy lies in the quality of our welcome to the unknown
- Holding space for possibilities, akin to inviting the wild to join us
- Hope in ancient patience and courage allowing for new companionship
