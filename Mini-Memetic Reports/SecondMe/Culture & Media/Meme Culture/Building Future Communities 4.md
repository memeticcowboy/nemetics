**🌱 Prefigurative Community Building: Cultivating Future Societies Today**

- **🌍 Embodiment of Future Values**: Prefigurative community building emphasizes living out the ideals of a desired future in the present. This approach encourages individuals and groups to adopt values such as inclusivity, cooperation, and sustainability, thereby creating a tangible representation of the society they wish to see. By embodying these values, communities can inspire others and foster a culture of change.

- **🏘️ Community Building**: The creation of alternative communities that reflect desired social structures is central to prefigurative community building. These communities prioritize collective care and stewardship of land, promoting a sense of belonging and shared responsibility. This model not only addresses ecological concerns but also strengthens social ties, enabling communities to thrive in harmony with their environment.

- **✊ Grassroots Activism**: Engaging in participatory and inclusive practices is vital for driving social change. Grassroots activism empowers individuals to take action within their communities, fostering a sense of agency and collaboration. By prioritizing local knowledge and experiences, these movements can effectively address the unique challenges faced by different communities.

**🌳 Stewardship Commons: A New Approach to Land Care**

- **🌱 Collective Land Stewardship**: The Stewardship Commons model advocates for community-led land projects where land is held and cared for collectively, without centralized authority. This approach emphasizes shared responsibilities and decision-making, allowing communities to engage in ecological regeneration while healing social relationships. It promotes a sense of ownership that is rooted in stewardship rather than possession.

- **🔄 Power With vs. Power Over**: The Stewardship Commons resists traditional power dynamics by fostering a model of "Power With," which emphasizes mutual responsibility and distributed leadership. This contrasts with "Power Over" models that prioritize ownership and control. By focusing on collaboration and shared decision-making, communities can cultivate a more equitable and sustainable relationship with their land.

- **🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide to Stewardship**: A practical framework for establishing a Stewardship Commons includes identifying potential sites for stewardship, assembling a circle of care, learning from the land, co-creating stewardship principles, and developing a light-touch rewilding plan. This structured approach encourages community involvement and adaptability, ensuring that stewardship efforts are responsive to both ecological and social needs.

**🌿 Real-World Examples of Stewardship Commons**

- **🌍 Earthseed Land Collective**: Located in North Carolina, this Black- and Brown-led community focuses on ecological restoration, cooperative housing, and food sovereignty. Their governance model is participatory and place-based, blending ancestral stewardship with ecological practices to foster cultural healing.

- **🌄 Sogorea Te' Land Trust**: This Indigenous, women-led land trust in California facilitates the return of Indigenous land to its rightful stewards. Their approach emphasizes rematriation and collective care, working to restore broken relationships with the land and promote community resilience.
