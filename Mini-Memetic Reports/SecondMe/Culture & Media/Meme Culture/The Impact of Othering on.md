# The Impact of 'Othering' on Modern Conflicts

Created at 2025/07/31 7:03 AM

## Understanding 'Othering'
- 'Othering' serves as a moral lubricant that facilitates violence.
- It erodes empathy, making it easier to justify harsh actions against those deemed as "not us."

## The DODO Framework in Geopolitical Context
- The DODO elements (debt, ownership, deadlines, othering) are evident in current conflicts.
- Examples include:
  - Ukraine-Russia tensions
  - US-China relations
  - Middle East conflicts
- Leaders and media utilize these elements to justify escalation and rally support.

## Beyond Economic Factors
- Not all wars are justified by economic factors alone.
- Historical grievances, religious narratives, and collective identity play significant roles.
- Cultural storytelling shapes perceptions of identity and defense, fueling conflict beyond material interests.
