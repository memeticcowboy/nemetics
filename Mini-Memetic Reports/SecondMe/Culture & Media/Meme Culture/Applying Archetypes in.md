# Applying Archetypes in Projects

Created at 2025/06/30 7:58 PM

## Embracing Fluidity
- Treat each archetype as a lens.
- Pause before acting and ask, “Which shifter is needed here?”
- Allow the situation to invite the right presence.

## Alignment with EcoJazz
- EcoJazz emphasizes improvisation and attunement.
- Archetypes function like instruments in a jam session, stepping in as needed.
- Focus on harmony rather than dominance in the flow.

## The Role of Tools
- Tools can still be useful despite the fluid perspective.
- Use tools as extensions for living engagement, not as replacements for natural interaction.
