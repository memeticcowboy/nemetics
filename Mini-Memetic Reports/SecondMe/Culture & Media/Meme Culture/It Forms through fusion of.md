Authority echoes hollow where reverence once lived. This is not safeguarding—it’s enforcement. The line is drawn not to protect, but to control. Metal forgets its flow. It becomes armor that no longer reflects, only repels. This is safety without trust, structure without soul—a fossil of care, buried in command.
