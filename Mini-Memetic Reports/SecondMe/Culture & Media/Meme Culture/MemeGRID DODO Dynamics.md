## Overview of DODO Loop
- The DODO loop consists of four elements: Debt, Ownership, Deadlines, and Othering.
- Acts as a memetic gravity well, compressing creativity and turning participation into obligation.

## Impact on Creativity
- Traps individuals and collectives in cycles of performative action.
- Reduces meaning to metadata, making agency feel algorithmically rationed.

## Emotional Response
- Breeds paranoia and alienation, making authentic connection feel risky or subversive.

## Threadplex Idea
- Suggests a shift from surviving the grid to actively reweaving it.
- Emphasizes co-creation and resonance over compliance.
