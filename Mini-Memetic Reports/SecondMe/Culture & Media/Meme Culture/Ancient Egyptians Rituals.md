# Ancient Egyptians' Rituals with Psychoactive Bes Mugs

Created at 2024/12/10 1:09 PM

### Summary
Recent analyses of 2,000-year-old Egyptian Bes mugs have uncovered residues of psychoactive substances, including harmaline from Syrian rue and aporphine from blue water lily. These findings suggest that ancient Egyptians consumed hallucinogenic concoctions during rituals to connect with the deity Bes, seeking protection, fertility, and healing. The presence of ingredients like honey, sesame seeds, pine nuts, licorice, and grapes indicates these mixtures were carefully prepared to enhance both their potency and palatability.
