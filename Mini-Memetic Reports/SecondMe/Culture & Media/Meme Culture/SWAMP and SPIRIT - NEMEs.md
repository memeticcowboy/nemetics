**🧠 What is** **SWAMP** **now?**


Think of it as:

- The mental mangrove, where contradictory frameworks tangle
- A testing ground for models that don’t neatly resolve
- The site of cognitive overgrowth and conceptual friction
- A processing layer for “too complex for SMART, too ungrounded for SWIFT”


🌀 SWAMP = where thinking gets thick

It’s where epistemic knots form — and either clarify through struggle, or become quicksand.


**🕊️ What is** **SPIRIT** **now?**

- Imagination, myth, soulfulness
- Affect-laden intuition and symbolic processing
- Dream logic, archetypal patterning, visionary framing


This restores soul-space as its own mode of sensemaking.
