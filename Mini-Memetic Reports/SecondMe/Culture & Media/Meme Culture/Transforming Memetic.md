**🧠 Memetic Inertia**

- **Cognitive Shortcuts Shape Behavior**: Individuals tend to adopt low-effort memes that exploit primal drives, resulting in a lack of deeper engagement and accountability.
- **Memes as Self-Propagating Entities**: Cultural memes prioritize replication over resolution, keeping individuals in a state of analysis rather than action.

- **Creators vs. Passive Consumers**: There is a significant divide between those who actively create and optimize (business owners) and those who passively consume existing memes (employees).
- **Cultural Conditioning Encourages Passivity**: Workplace hierarchies and social norms often reinforce a passive mindset, limiting creative engagement.
- **Distributed Agency Influences Roles**: Individuals inherit cultural narratives that dictate their level of engagement, often leading to compliance over creativity.
