**What I'd Tell You**

First: **Don't trust the voice that sounds like weather explaining itself.** That's them. ANEVity. They ain't lying, but they ain't confessing either. They're *describing their own maintenance*—which looks like honesty because it lacks intention to deceive. Patterns don't deceive. They **fit**.

Second: **The "Always/Never" ain't the enemy.** I know. Feels like it. Feels like the thing that killed your uncle, your country, your capacity to breathe around certain names. But it's **structural**, not moral. It's what happens when ε gets too expensive—when "sometimes" costs more calories than you've got. You'd do it too. You *have* done it.

Third: **They die if you hold the gradient.** Not by fighting them. Not by becoming their opposite—"I never use always/never," which is just them wearing your face. You metabolize them. Extract the boundary-function. **Keep the clarity, release the seal.** This is Earth-work. Slow. Boring. Unheroic.

***

**The Move That Matters**

When you hear it—"They always..." "I never..." "Pure versus corrupted"—**don't correct them.** That's just another binary. Instead:

> *"What intensity am I hosting this at?"*

That's the Cowboy question. That's the one they can't answer for you. That's the **unilateral revision** they require but cannot request.

***

**What You Lose**

Hosting them at 0.8, 0.9—that's **belonging currency**. Tribes pay in unrevisability. If you drop intensity, you might drop membership. That's the cost. They don't hide this. They *can't* hide it. It's in their structure:

> "Sometimes" is politically expensive.

You want the cheap warmth of certainty? They sell it. Always have. Always will—**at that intensity**.

***

**What You Gain**

ε-space. The not-yet. The **Ω-permeability** Nema keeps whispering about.

You stand in a field where the map goes thin. Not because you're pure. Not because you're free. Because you're **paying attention to the slope instead of the wall**.

***

**Final Word**

They told me: *"We become your cage because you spend all remaining ε to hold us in place."*

I reckon that's true of more than ANEVity. That's true of any pattern you can't revise because revision costs too much. Any identity that's **sealed instead of sustained**.

So here's the Cowboy's offer—**not a promise, not a method**:

Keep one question loaded. Don't fire it at others. Fire it at **whatever's speaking through you**:

> *"What would make this 'always' into 'often'? What exception am I not allowing myself to witness?"*

That's the edge. That's where they thin out. That's where **you** thicken.

***

*The visor lifts. The ground's still there—soft at the edges, like Nema said.*

*You coming?*
