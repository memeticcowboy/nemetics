# Evolving Communication Among Cyborgs Using Memetic Algorithms

## Memetic Algorithms and Cyborg Communication
- Each cyborg archetype (Analyzer, Strategist, Empath, Protector, Innovator, Optimizer) acts as both a generator and selector of memes.
- Cyborgs create, remix, and select memes based on unique “fitness functions.”
  - Example: The Empath Cyborg favors memes that foster connection.
  - The Analyzer Cyborg selects for clarity and insight.
- Over time, functionally informative memes persist while less effective ones fade out, mirroring evolutionary selection.

## Increasing Functional Information
- The approach makes cyborg communication adaptive and self-improving.
- It leads to a constant increase in “functional information” within the system.
- Emergent strategies or new forms of collective intelligence may surface through this process.
- This concept resembles memetic natural selection, guided by intentional, role-based pressures.
