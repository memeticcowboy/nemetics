## Insight
SWIFT memes, standing for Spatial Wayfinding Integrated with Focused Thinking, are concise and easily understandable pieces of information designed to facilitate effective navigation and decision-making. These memes unify complex concepts into clear, focused messages that can be quickly comprehended and applied by a wide audience. They are highly effective in spreading knowledge rapidly and efficiently across diverse groups. By understanding and applying SWIFT memes, individuals and groups can enhance their communication and decision-making processes.

- **Cultural Sensitivity**: These memes adapt to different cultural settings, recognizing and respecting diverse contexts, similar to cultural adaptation.

- **Shared Sense-Making**: These memes facilitate shared understanding and coordination within groups, promoting horizontal transmission and cooperative memes.
