**🌱 Prefigurative Community Building: A Path to Future Societies**

- **🌍 Embodiment of Future Values**: Prefigurative community building emphasizes the importance of enacting the values of a desired future society in present-day practices. This approach encourages communities to adopt principles such as mutual aid, collective decision-making, and social justice, creating a living model of the society they aspire to achieve.

- **🏘️ Community Building**: The focus on creating and sustaining communities through prefigurative politics fosters a sense of belonging and shared purpose. By prioritizing grassroots activism, these communities can effectively address local issues and work towards broader social change, reinforcing the idea that collective action is essential for transformation.

- **✊ Grassroots Activism**: Engaging in local activism is crucial for effecting meaningful change. Grassroots movements empower individuals to take ownership of their narratives and advocate for their communities, ensuring that the voices of marginalized groups are heard and valued in the pursuit of social justice.

**📖 Power With Approach to Storytelling**

- **🗣️ Community-Centric Storytelling**: The Power With model shifts the narrative control from outsiders to the community itself. By allowing community members to share their stories, this approach fosters cultural continuity and collective meaning-making, reinforcing the idea that everyone has a voice and a history worth sharing.

- **🤝 Intergenerational Connections**: Encouraging storytelling across generations strengthens community bonds. By pairing elders with youth, communities can facilitate relationship-building and knowledge transfer, ensuring that cultural heritage is preserved and celebrated.

- **📜 Consent-Based Agreements**: Establishing shared agreements on how stories are shared and archived is vital for maintaining trust within the community. Ongoing consent ensures that storytelling respects individual privacy and emotional safety, allowing for a more authentic and respectful exchange of narratives.

**🛠️ Steps to Launch a Power With Oral History Project**

- **👥 Core Group Formation**: Initiating a storytelling project begins with gathering a diverse core group through relational invitations. This step emphasizes connection over recruitment, inviting community members to share their stories and experiences in a supportive environment.

- **📅 Low-Tech Tools for Accessibility**: Utilizing accessible, low-tech tools for storytelling ensures that the focus remains on connection and dignity rather than media quality. This approach democratizes the storytelling process, making it available to all community members regardless of technological access.

- **🏛️ Community Sharing Spaces**: Sharing stories in local spaces rather than institutional archives fosters a sense of ownership and pride within the community. By organizing exhibitions in familiar settings, storytelling becomes a public and local celebration of shared histories.

**🌟 Examples of Existing Power With Storytelling Projects**

- **📚 The Black Memory Workers Project**: This initiative, led by Black women and queer individuals, focuses on reclaiming historical records and oral histories for community empowerment. By prioritizing consentful practices and memory justice, the project exemplifies non-extractive storytelling.

- **🌵 Desert Survivance**: A grassroots initiative in Tucson, AZ, this project is run by and for Tohono O’odham and Pascua Yaqui youth and elders. It emphasizes co-creating stories through various mediums while respecting the wishes of storytellers regarding digitization and sharing.

**💡 Conclusion: Memory as Power and Healing**

- **🔄 Transformative Storytelling**: Storytelling serves as a powerful tool for healing and transformation. By fostering an environment where stories are shared with care and respect, communities can reclaim their narratives and histories, countering the effects of erasure and trauma.

- **🌐 Local and Relational Focus**: The emphasis on keeping storytelling local and relational encourages communities to cultivate their own storytelling sites. This approach nurtures a living memory that is shared and celebrated, rather than commodified.

- **🌱 Seeding Capacity Across Communities**: The goal of prefigurative community building is not to scale up but to seed wide, allowing every community to develop its own storytelling practices. This decentralized approach empowers individuals and groups to take ownership of their narratives and histories.
