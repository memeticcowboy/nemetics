# Definition of MeMes ꔛ

Created at 2025/08/29 6:49 AM

- **MeMes** are recursive patterns or *meta-memes* that guide how individuals weave their experiences, activating authorship and turning incoming threads into self-aware loops of integration. They are not just carriers of content but patterns that enable individuals to re-author their experiences and weave new energy into their personal and collective meaning ecology, called the Threadplex.
- Unlike traditional memes seen as payloads or cultural bullets, MeMes function as recursive, generative, and sovereign processes that help individuals notice, evaluate, modulate, express, and sense incoming memetic threads, allowing for dynamic pattern adjustment and expression in life.
- MeMes aim to weave *lumemes* (expansive, illuminating threads) into the Threadplex, fostering resonance and harmony with the Field, rather than *usurpenes* which contract and hijack mental space.
