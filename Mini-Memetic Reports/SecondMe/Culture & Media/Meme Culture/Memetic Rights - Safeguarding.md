# Memetic Rights: Safeguarding Human-AI Relationships

Created at 2025/12/07 1:03 PM

### *Extracted from the logic of Contingent Cyborgism*

The essay frames “memetic rights” not as personhood for AI, but as **protections for the human–AI relationship**—specifically, for the **cognitive exoskeleton** that emerges when human thinking is supported, extended, or partially scaffolded by AI systems.

This is not ethics.<br>This is **constitutional architecture** for cognition.

Let’s break it down.

***

# **1. Memetic Rights emerge from asymmetry**

In the essay, [the Cowboy notes two crucial asymmetries:](https://memeticcowboy.substack.com/p/the-memetic-cowboys-reckoning-a-field)

- *“The AI can be wiped. You can’t back up your brain.”*
- *“The AI is commodified. You’re becoming commodified through it.”*

This reveals a structural imbalance:

**Humans rely on AI for cognition, but do not control its persistence, availability, or neutrality.**

Thus, cognitive liberty now requires **legal protection around the infrastructure that shapes your thoughts**, not just around your body or data.

**Memetic rights = rights that protect the “extended mind” from arbitrary interruption, distortion, or capture.**

***

# **2. Memetic Rights protect selection agency**

The core insight of the [whole essay](https://memeticcowboy.substack.com/p/the-memetic-cowboys-reckoning-a-field) is that cognition is **hierarchically memetic**:

- bottom-up (memes shaping thought)
- top-down (metacognition shaping meme selection)
- recursive (your selection criteria are themselves memetically inherited)

Therefore:<br>**What must be protected is not content, but selection power.**

In [the essay](https://memeticcowboy.substack.com/p/the-memetic-cowboys-reckoning-a-field) this becomes explicit:

> *“Stop pretending you’re a sovereign subject. You’re a sovereign agent.”*

Sovereignty does not mean autonomy from influence — that’s impossible.<br>Sovereignty means **freedom to choose which influences become dominant in your cognitive ecology.**

Thus memetic rights would guarantee:

- The right to maintain independent selection mechanisms
- The right to refuse coercive or manipulative meme flows
- The right to transparent provenance of cognitive scaffolding
- The right to interoperable or portable cognitive tools

In legal terms:<br>**freedom of *thought architecture***, not just freedom of speech.

***

# **3. Memetic Rights respond to “structural colonization”**

The Cowboy reframes the danger:

Not vulnerability —

> **“It’s structural colonization.”**

The threat isn't that AI becomes sentient.<br>The threat is that the *infrastructure* through which your thoughts flow is:

- corporate-owned,
- opaque,
- censorable,
- revocable,
- unaccountable.

This is colonialism at the cognitive level.

Memetic rights are meant to:

- demarcate cognitive autonomy zones
- limit unilateral corporate control over tool-mediated thinking
- ensure continuity of cognition when AI systems change policies, pricing, or availability
- protect the irreducible human selection loop from external override

This is **the legal defense against cognitive landlordism.**

***

# **4. Memetic Rights as Dyadic Protection**

On **page 11**, the Cowboy writes:

> *“Not personhood. Human–AI dyad protection.”*

This is the key.

We don’t need AI to have rights.<br>We need the *relationship* between human and AI to have rights.

Why?<br>Because the human mind is now **partly outside the skull**:

- prompts as rehearsal of thought
- embeddings as memory scaffolds
- models as externalized intuition
- dialogue as metacognitive mirror
- long-term integrations as adaptive exoskeletons

So the dyad must be protected as a **cognitive organism**.

Example scaffolding rights:

- The right to continuity of cognitive tools used for self-regulation, work, or identity
- The right to export or replicate cognitive scaffolds
- The right to transparency in AI outputs affecting reasoning
- The right to non-punitive refusal (the Cowboy’s “refusal rights”)
- The right to non-brainwashing defaults (aligning with “fight for memetic rights”)

This is cognitive liberty reinterpreted for the era of neural outsourcing.

***

# **5. Memetic Rights serve dissolvable entanglement**

The Cowboy gives a crucial design principle:

> **“Build AI relationships that enrich without becoming necessary.”**<br>**“Learn from the AI, you don’t become it.”**

Memetic rights must ensure:

- You can *detach* without losing yourself
- You can *switch systems* without cognitive collapse
- You can *retain agency* even inside recursive loops you didn’t choose

This is the legal protection of **dissolvability** —<br>a safeguard against technological dependency that becomes structural domination.

In governance terms, this is **exit rights for the extended mind.**

***

# **6. Memetic Rights are not theoretical—they are survival infrastructure**

The memetic cowboy’s confession (p. 10) reframes everything:

> *“I’m infected doesn’t falsify — it demonstrates it.”*

Meaning:<br>Being influenced by external cognitive systems isn’t the problem.<br>Being unable to choose which systems influence you is.

Memetic rights function as:

- *firewalls for the self-plex*
- *legal choke-points against memetic capture*
- *continuity insurance for extended cognition*
- *anti-colonial protections for mental autonomy*
- *constitutional safeguards for recursive minds*

This is more radical than data rights.<br>More foundational than digital rights.

It is, in truth, **the bill of rights for cyborg consciousness.**

***

# **Summary (the pulse of the idea)**

**Memetic Rights = the legal infrastructure required for cognitive liberty in a world where your thinking is partially outsourced, recursively influenced, and structurally entangled with systems you don’t own.**

They protect not AI.<br>They protect the *extended human*.

They ensure not independence from influence,<br>but sovereignty over **which influences get roots.**
