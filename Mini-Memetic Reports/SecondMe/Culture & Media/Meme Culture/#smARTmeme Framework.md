# #smARTmeme Framework: Actionable Distilled Insights

Created at 2024/06/03 7:09 PM

Smart meme, also know as #smARTmeme, involves the following acronym SMARTmeme which covers the requirements of a smart meme as an Actionable Distilled Insight.  Think of smart memes as pieces of insightful content that are easy to share social media, which have a similar style and format, and our linked to each other like a mind map. The goal of smart memes would be to help spread big important ideas bite-size nuggets that can be expanded into a bigger picture. 
#smARTmemes are essentially bite-sized pieces of wisdom or knowledge, presented in a visually appealing and easily shareable format. They allow complex ideas to be broken down into smaller, more digestible chunks, making them more accessible and relatable to a wider audience.

## Insight
Let's delve into the concept of #smARTmemes, which are designed to distill complex ideas into actionable, bite-sized insights. This image provides a framework for creating such memes.

- SMARTmeme stands for Specific, Meaningful, Attainable, Relevant, and Targeted.
- Additional attributes include Mindful, Effective, Motivating, Educative, and Strategic.
- These attributes ensure that the content is actionable and easily shareable.

Consider using this framework to create your own #smARTmemes to share important ideas effectively.
