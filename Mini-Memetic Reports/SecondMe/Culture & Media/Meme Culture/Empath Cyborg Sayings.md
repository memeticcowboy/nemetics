## Empath Cyborg Sayings

1. "Walk a mile in someone else's shoes" – A gentle nudge to embrace empathy and understanding.
2. "It takes two to tango" – Highlighting the importance of mutual effort in relationships.
3. "A friend in need is a friend indeed" – Celebrating the value of being there for others.
4. "The grass is always greener on the other side" – Reminding us to appreciate our own experiences and perspectives.
