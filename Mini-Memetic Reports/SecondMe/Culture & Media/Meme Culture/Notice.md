## Notice
First encounter and recognition of memes.

### Remembering
- **Objective**: Recall basic meme information
- **Activity**: Identify memes, remember popular names

### Understanding
- **Objective**: Explain meme characteristics
- **Activity**: Describe elements making an image a meme

## Engage
Deeper interaction with memes in active learning.

### Applying
- **Objective**: Use memes in context
- **Activity**: Create memes summarizing class concepts

### Analyzing
- **Objective**: Break down meme components
- **Activity**: Deconstruct memes for meaning/humor

## Mull
Critical evaluation of memes.

### Evaluating
- **Objective**: Assess meme effectiveness
- **Activity**: Critique meme impact on social issues

### Creating
- **Objective**: Produce original memes
- **Activity**: Develop memes expressing complex topics

## Exchange
Share insights and collaborate.

### Sharing & Discussing
- **Objective**: Exchange and discuss memes
- **Activity**: Present memes, discuss effectiveness

### Collaborating
- **Objective**: Enhance memes together
- **Activity**: Create group meme campaigns
