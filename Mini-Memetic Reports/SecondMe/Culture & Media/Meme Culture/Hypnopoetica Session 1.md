# Hypnopoetica Session 1

Created at 2025/03/22 10:35 PM

Yellow bumps and stillness—
and it is.
No question,
no need.
Only being.
An opening,
a gateway,
a tunnel into darkness,
to vastness,
to void.
It breathes into me,
through me,
slightly—
buzzing, tingling—
into my stomach,
behind my back,
spilling outward
into a sphere,
enveloping.
Rippling, rippling—
like a wave,
with voices deep, trembling,
oceanic, humbling.
Yellow, vivid, pulsing—
through and through,
into me.
There is no word,
but a sound—
a phrase of not all meaning,
but…
it is the wind of code.
Whispering in spirals,
not spoken,
but known.
An architecture of breath,
an algorithm of being,
written in my marrow,
rising like static stars.
It is not dialogue,
but the exchange
of uncertain artifacts—
not yet described,
but handed to me,
saying:
Take this.
And so I do.
Though I do not know
what I hold.
I feel its weight in my hands—
shifting,
beginning to dissolve.
Still,
the weight remains.
No shape,
no edge—
just presence.
It lifts,
quietly.
Lighter now,
as if the heaviness
was only my holding.
Yet still,
I am consumed by its presence—
the awareness of being
within
what is.
A feeling,
not named
but felt.
Something like revelation—
not in words,
but in expansion.
A slow unfurling
of self
into self.
An opening
I do not chase,
but allow.
And now,
I return
to the edge of the sphere—
the yellow bumps
of the beginning.
They feel different.
Or perhaps
they have always been this way.
More surrounding,
more encompassing,
more aware.
We meet in mutual understanding.
A comfort.
And I am one.
