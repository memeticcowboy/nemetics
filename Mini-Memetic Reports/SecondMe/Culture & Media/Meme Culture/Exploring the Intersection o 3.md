## Nudge Swarms
- Nudge swarms leverage swarm intelligence to subtly guide collective behavior in an ethical manner.
- Potential applications include urban planning and healthcare.
- Raises questions about privacy and autonomy.

## Ethical Adaptation Framework
- Offers a structured approach to ethical decision-making in rapidly changing environments.
- Key components include Value Preservation Protocols and Ethical Boundary Markers.
- Emphasizes continuous stakeholder feedback to incorporate diverse perspectives.
- Relevant for ensuring ethical influence while respecting individual autonomy in nudge swarms.

## Incentivized Nudges
