The phrase "small bananas" could be seen as a way to describe minor issues or problems that are easy to handle. Just like smaller bananas are simpler to hold, peel, and eat, small problems are easier to solve than bigger ones.

For example, if someone says, "These setbacks are just small bananas," they mean the setbacks are not a big deal and can be easily managed. Similarly, "small bananas" can also refer to unimportant matters. So if someone says, "Don't worry about those small bananas, let's focus on the bigger picture," they are suggesting that those issues are not significant and that the focus should be on more important things.
