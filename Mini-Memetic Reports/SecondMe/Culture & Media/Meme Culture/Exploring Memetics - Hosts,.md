### Puppet or partner?

The posthuman angle suggests we are not sovereign selves *possessing* memes, but rather emergent selves *composed of* memes. A shifting constellation of patterns. Dennett’s “self as center of narrative gravity” aligns here—our identities are not fixed but dynamically stabilized stories told through memetic threads.

### A better metaphor? *Symbiotic cyborgs.*

We are hybrid entities—wetware and language, instinct and ideology, sensation and signifier. Memes aren’t just agents; they are **co-authors of our cognition**, scripting the interfaces of perception and behavior. We navigate the world shaped by inherited and innovated memeplexes: religions, ideologies, aesthetics, rituals, slang, archetypes.

So: yes, we are their hosts. But also their gardeners, their battlegrounds, and sometimes their jailbreak tools.

The deeper question becomes: **how do we reclaim navigational agency within the memetic ecology we inhabit?**
