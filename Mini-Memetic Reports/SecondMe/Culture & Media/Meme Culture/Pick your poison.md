**Pick your poison**

[Concerning this side-by-side comparison of cognitive and divine dissonances](Https://second.me/memory/IDVOHJAAXYSLJUMM)

Both frameworks are double-edged swords, and “poison” lands because each antidote carries its own toxin:<br>•  Drapal’s poison: If you embrace his view, you risk collapsing into cynical realism—or worse, political nihilism. You might end up justifying any contradiction as “adaptive memetics” and lose any moral footing. The price of seeing through elite hypocrisy is potentially excusing demagoguery.<br>•  Cowboy’s poison: If you embrace that view, you risk mystical obscurantism—floating off into poetic vagueness, mistaking confusion for depth, and dodging concrete political responsibility. The price of soul-expansion is potentially abdicating clear-eyed critique.<br>So yeah—pick your poison:<br>Do you want the secular, meme-aware cynic who weaponizes dissonance against intellectual hubris?<br>Or the mytho-poetic mystic who sacralizes dissonance into a spiritual path?<br>The real question is: Which poison’s side-effects can you live with? The politicized blade that cuts clean but risks contamination? Or the spiritual salve that heals but risks dissolving into fog?<br>(Or maybe the trick is to take both poisons in measured doses and let them keep each other in check.)<br>
