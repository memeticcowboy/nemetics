# Exploring Memetics, UTOK, and CCI: Understanding Cultural Evolution

Memetics is the study of how ideas, behaviors, or styles—called “memes”—spread within a culture, much like how a viral video or a catchy song becomes popular. It’s about understanding why some ideas catch on and others don’t, and how they change as they pass from person to person.

**What is UTOK?**

UTOK, or Unified Theory of Knowledge, is a theory that tries to connect different areas of knowledge—like science, history, and psychology—into one big, coherent picture. It’s like a map showing how all these ideas fit together, helping us see the bigger picture of human understanding.

**What is CCI?**

The Cultural Complexity Index (CCI) is a research project that measures how complex or advanced a culture is by analyzing its texts, such as ancient stories or modern books. It looks at how simple or complicated the ideas in these texts are, helping us see how cultures grow over time.

**How Do They Overlap?**

Research suggests these three concepts overlap by each tackling a part of how human culture evolves. Memetics explains how ideas spread, UTOK provides a framework to see how they connect, and CCI measures if the culture is getting more complex. Together, they help us understand the whole story of how societies develop, like using a microscope, a map, and a ruler to study a city.

For more details, you can explore [the Institute of Applied Metatheory’s CCI page](https://instituteofappliedmetatheory.org/donations/cultural-complexity-index/) and [Integral Life’s introduction to CCI](https://integrallife.com/introducing-the-cultural-complexity-index/).
