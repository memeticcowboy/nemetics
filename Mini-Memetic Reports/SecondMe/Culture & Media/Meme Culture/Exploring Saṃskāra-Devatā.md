Saṃskāras can also mean purificatory rites or sacraments that shape an individual’s spiritual or social life.
    - **In Your Context**: Saṃskāra provides the **structure** of the psychic knot. 2. **Devatā** (देवता):

It represents a divine entity or consciousness presiding over specific aspects of existence, such as natural forces, rituals, or spiritual qualities. In a philosophical sense, Devatā can symbolize the personification of a divine function or the focal point of awareness.
    - **In Your Context**: Devatā gives the knot its **identity**. It imbues the Saṃskāra with a sense of purpose, personality, or divine significance, acting as the animating principle that makes the structure recognizable and meaningful. Like a meme’s distinct identity in a cultural context, the Devatā provides the psychic knot with a unique "face" or essence that draws attention and engagement.

3. **Prāṇa** (प्राण):

In yoga, Ayurveda, and Indian philosophy, Prāṇa is the universal energy that sustains life, flowing through subtle channels (*nāḍīs*) and governing physical, mental, and spiritual functions.
