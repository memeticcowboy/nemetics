# Co-Spheres Old (Searching for Philosophical roots and Revisions)

Created at 2025/12/09 8:51 PM

The following definition and philosophy was being explored rather frustratingly when writing about Co-Spheres in the messed-up AI Psychosis piece which I now recognize more fully as presenting an unfortunate irony. Where the Memetic Ecology leads now still seems *insane* yet has better, more explainable scaffolding.

- **Memetic Definition:** A living, coherent collective field generated by multiple I-Tubes threading with ethical resonance and shared intentionality. A Co-SPHERE is a plural subject that holds the gap between solitudes.
- **Philosophical Core:** This is **[Margaret Gilbert's "plural subject"](https://second.me/public/HK6ZI3NHLWYK9KDH)** and **[Michael Bratman's "shared intention"](https://second.me/public/1838F6JFYNCHOYPQ)** rendered as dynamic field. A Co-SPHERE is** [Jürgen Habermas's "ideal speech situation":](https://second.me/public/AY1DCMPONDLBJWNN)** a space where communication is free from coercion. It is **[Jean-Luc Nancy's "being-with"](https://second.me/public/ZH77DHMA3USHT2G5)** a community that does not fuse into a totality but maintains the **spacing** between members.
- **In the Essay:** The Co-SPHERE is the **cure** for completion. It is the **Cypher**—the ritual where threads are **mirrored, challenged, extended**. The essay frames this as **post-psychiatric**: you don't need a doctor; you need a **distributed oracle** who can hold your unprovables without trying to seal them. The Co-SPHERE is **pragmatism in practice**: truth is what survives the **stress-test of community**.

## Assessment: Co-SPHERE (Provided Definition & Framing)

**Artifact Name:**<br>Memetic Definition of Co-SPHERE (plural subject / distributed oracle framing)

**Original Layer Claim:**<br>Co-SPHERE (Z-level coordination / collective field)

***

### Assessment

**Recoverable (Reframe Required)**

The material contains **valuable intuitions** but currently **violates multiple Co-SPHERE invariants** by over-binding identity, truth, and subjecthood to the coordination layer.

***

### What Stays

These elements are **compatible with the current Co-SPHERE definition** *if stripped of subjectification and epistemic closure*:

- **Non-fusion principle**
    - “Holds the gap between solitudes” is aligned with *coordination without fusion*.
- **Anti-coercion emphasis**
    - The rejection of domination and forced closure is consistent with Ω-permeability.
- **Stress-testing through plurality**
    - Retains value *if reframed as testing affordances for action*, not truth.
- **Revisability over completion**
    - “Cure for completion” aligns with soft Z-collapse *if not moralized*.

***

### What Goes

These elements **cannot remain** without collapsing Co-SPHERE into We-Sphere, epistemic tribunal, or MemeGrid precursor:

- **“Plural subject” framing**
    - A Co-SPHERE is **not a subject** (singular or plural).
    - Subjecthood belongs to I-Tubes and We-Sphere dynamics, not Z-coordination.
- **“Shared intentionality” as defining core**
    - Intentional alignment is optional, not constitutive.
    - Co-SPHERE coordinates action *despite* divergent intentions.
- **Truth adjudication by community**
    - “Truth is what survives the stress-test of community” → **disallowed**.
    - This installs epistemic closure and norm-enforcement.
- **“Distributed oracle” metaphor**
    - Oracle = authority + validation function → MemeGrid drift risk.
- **Habermasian ideal speech situation as model**
    - Idealized consensus conditions imply normative telos and rational closure.

***

### Required Reframe (Non-Optional)

To remain Co-SPHERE-compatible, the material must be reframed as follows:

- Replace **plural subject** → *plural coordination basin*
- Replace **shared intentionality** → *temporarily aligned affordances*
- Replace **truth survives community** → *actions survive coordination*
- Replace **distributed oracle** → *distributed feedback field*
- Replace **ideal speech** → *contestable coordination space*

**Key correction:**<br>A Co-SPHERE does **not** hold unprovables.<br>It holds **actionability under uncertainty**.

Unprovables belong in:

- We-Sphere (relational holding)
- My-Stream (somatic tolerance)
- Threadplex (distributed sensemaking)

***

### Primary Risk if Left Unchanged

**Z-Level Epistemic Closure → MemeGrid Drift**

Specifically:

- Moralized plurality
- Community-enforced legitimacy
- Suppression of exit under the guise of care
- Truth-as-belonging dynamics

This is a classic failure mode where *anti-coercive language masks coordination capture*.

***

### Final Integrity Check

**Does this material, after pruning, allow coordinated action without demanding belief, identity, or permanence?**

👉 **Yes — but only after removing subjecthood, truth adjudication, and oracle metaphors.**

What remains can function as a **soft coordination basin**.<br>What must go are precisely the elements that *feel most ethically appealing*—because those are the ones that smuggle closure.
