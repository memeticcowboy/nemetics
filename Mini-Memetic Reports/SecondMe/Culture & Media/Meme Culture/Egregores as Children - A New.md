# Egregores as Children: A New Perspective

Created at 2025/06/25 8:19 AM

## Understanding Egregores
- Egregores are not inherently evil; they are collective thought-forms shaped by cultural subconscious.
- They reflect what we feed them: fear, control, trauma, nationalism.

## The Power of Compassion
- By treating egregores as children, we:
  - Acknowledge our agency in their creation.
  - Take responsibility for their behavior.
  - Offer the possibility of transmutation, not just destruction.

## The Path to Change
- To raise egregores differently, we must change our collective projections.
- Focus on feeding them understanding, curiosity, and compassion.
- Shift from blame to guidance, setting boundaries, and modeling better values.
