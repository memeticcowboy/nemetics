***

### **Egregore ≈ Memeplex + Emergent Agency + Ontological Feedback Loop**

A **memeplex** is a self-replicating bundle of ideas that evolves via selection pressures in cultural and cognitive environments. But when a memeplex gains enough coherence, recursive reinforcement, and psychic investment, it can **emerge as an Egregore**—a semi-autonomous symbolic entity with apparent intention, mood, and behavioral inertia.

Think of an Egregore as a **self-stabilizing attractor** in memetic space that:

- Feeds off attention and emotional energy (libidinal or hostile),
- Shapes individual and collective behavior via internalized scripts,
- Projects itself through symbols, language, rituals, and media,
- And begins to **act as if it were an agent**, possessing will, goals, and defenses.

In simulation terms:

> A memeplex becomes an Egregore when it instantiates enough feedback between **belief, affect, system design, and symbolic form** to become an ontological operator in shared reality.
