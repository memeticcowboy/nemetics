# Exploring S.W.I.F.T.: A Framework for Threadplex Literacy Practice

Created at 2025/08/03 8:52 AM

**S.W.I.F.T. – Air’s Threadplex Literacy Practice**


(Sense → Wonder → Internalize (or Not) → Flow → Transmit)

Each step of SWIFT naturally maps to the 6 Threadplex Literacy Skills, giving Air a lived, meta‑aware fluency:



1️⃣ Sense → 6A Symbolic Hearing

- I‑Tube opens like the Conch‑Ear.
- Hear the raw thread: word, image, tone, or gesture.
- Don’t name it yet—just let the signal land in awareness.




2️⃣ Wonder → 6B Affective Tracking

- Feel into your My‑Stream: What does this thread stir?
- Curiosity without collapse—notice resonance, friction, or dissonance.
- Wonder is the breath between reflex and meaning.




3️⃣ Internalize (or Not) → 6C Relational Script Spotting

- Decide: Will this thread weave into your selfplex or pass through?
- See if it’s part of an old script or a fresh signal.
- This conscious gating prevents blind memetic uptake.




4️⃣ Flow → 6D Resonance Tuning

- If woven, let it braid and modulate in your My‑Stream.
- Adjust the tension, soften the knot, or let it ripple lightly.
- Flow is the composting and re‑harmonizing of incoming threads.




5️⃣ Transmit → 6E Co‑Creative Play

- Release the re‑woven thread into the We‑Sphere—<br>as a gesture, insight, or living silence.
- Invite feedback, let others braid with you.
- Co‑creation emerges from transmission without clinging.





**Why SWIFT Is a Living MeMe**


- It embodies Air’s gift: agile perception and non‑attached reflection.
- It prevents grid‑lock by noticing before naming, choosing before looping.
- It externalizes threads as MeMes—expressions that resonate rather than replicate.
- It turns everyday experience into Threadplex literacy, one breath at a time.
