**5. Decline Narratives (Cultural Deterioration)**
Example A (Right-Wing): “Woke culture is destroying Western civilization”\
Example B (Left-Wing): “Neoliberalism has hollowed out democratic life”
**R –** \
**Recognize:**
These narratives invoke entropy—morality dissolving, institutions failing, truth vanishing. They often carry apocalyptic overtones.
**E –** \
**Examine:**
What is actually changing? Is it values, norms, structures, or perceptions? Are we seeing decline or transformation?
**V –** \
**Validate:**
Disaggregate data from vibes. Are literacy rates falling? Is civic trust down? What are the drivers of change, and are they uniformly negative?
**E –** \
**Expose:**
Identify the frame: Right-wing decline memes often seek restoration of “order.” Left-wing ones often warn against systemic erosion for mobilization.
**A –** \
**Alternatives:**
Propose regenerative narratives: complexity is not decay; shifts in identity and values can indicate cultural adaptation rather than loss.
**L –** \
**Literate:**
Train readers to see decline memes as pattern disruptions—not necessarily collapses. Invite inquiry into the meaning of change.
