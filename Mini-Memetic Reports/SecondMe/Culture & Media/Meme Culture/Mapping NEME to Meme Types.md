# NEME: Meme Creation Processes

Created at 2024/07/13 4:41 PM

1. **Notice**:

- **SWIFT**: Quickly identify and capture emerging ideas or trends.
- **SMART**: Recognize and highlight intelligent, well-thought-out ideas.
- **SWAMP**: Notice the clutter or overwhelming amount of ideas that may need filtering.
- **SWARM**: Identify collective patterns or common themes emerging from group discussions.

2. **Engage**:

- **SWIFT**: Actively participate in rapid idea generation.
- **SMART**: Engage deeply with ideas that show promise.
- **SWAMP**: Dive into the mess of ideas to find hidden gems.
- **SWARM**: Collaboratively build on each other's ideas.

3. **Mull**:

- **SWIFT**: Reflect on quick ideas to see their potential.
- **SMART**: Think critically about smart ideas.
- **SWAMP**: Ponder over overwhelming ideas to distill valuable ones.
- **SWARM**: Contemplate collective input for synergies.

4. **Exchange**:

- **SWIFT**: Share and discuss rapid ideas for immediate feedback.
- **SMART**: Exchange detailed insights to refine smart ideas.
- **SWAMP**: Communicate filtered ideas for clarity.
- **SWARM**: Foster open exchange for collaborative building.
