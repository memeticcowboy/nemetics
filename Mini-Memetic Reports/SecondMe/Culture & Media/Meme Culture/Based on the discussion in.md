### What is Swarm Intelligence?

**Swarm Intelligence** is a collective behavior of decentralized, self-organized systems, natural or artificial. It is inspired by the behavior of social animals like ants, bees, birds, and fish, where simple rules followed by individuals lead to complex, intelligent behavior at the group level.
