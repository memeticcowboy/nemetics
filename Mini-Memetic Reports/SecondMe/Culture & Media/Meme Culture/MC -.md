**MC:** In memetics, we’re swift to point out how much of behavior don’t serve the one doing it. Daniel Dennett, in[ Breaking the Spell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_the_Spell_(Dennett_book)), tells of ants who climb to the tops of grass blades—not for sun or sight, but 'cause[ a parasite’s riding their nervous system like reins](https://app.me.bot/public/OZZBDFAFZMHZGWH3). The lancet fluke needs to get eaten by a grazing animal to complete its cycle, so it hijacks the ant, sends her up high, and waits for a cow to do the rest. From the outside, looks like the ant chose that perch. From the inside, agency’s been rerouted.
