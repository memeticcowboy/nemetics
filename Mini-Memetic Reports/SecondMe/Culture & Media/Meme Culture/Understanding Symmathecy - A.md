# Understanding Symmathecy: A New Perspective on Complex Systems and Relationships

Created at 2024/08/14 3:28 PM

In this Farnam Street video, Nora Bateson explains the term she coined in a blog post, called Symmathesy. It describes systems that are living and emerge from communications and interactions of "living vitae."

**What is Symmathesy?**
Definition: Symmathesy is the study of how mutual learning occurs within a system. It focuses on the dynamic relationships between elements within a system, rather than the individual elements themselves.
Analogy: The video uses the analogy of a crooked tree in a forest. Instead of focusing on how to make the tree straight, symmathesy asks how the tree learns to be crooked in its environment. This involves considering the tree's relationship with soil, insects, wind, and other factors.

**Key point:** Symmathesy emphasizes that information is not passively received but actively interpreted based on existing experiences and relationships.

**Implications of Symmathesy**

* **Challenge to traditional thinking:** The video suggests that traditional approaches to problem-solving often focus on fixing individual elements within a system, rather than understanding the system as a whole.
* **Importance of relationships:** Symmathesy highlights the crucial role of relationships in creating and maintaining systems. It emphasizes that change and stability are interconnected within these relationships.
* **Analogy of a jungle:** The video uses the analogy of a jungle to illustrate the complexity of systems. It argues that focusing on individual components (e.g., reptiles, insects) misses the essence of the jungle's vitality, which lies in the ever-changing relationships between all elements.

**Conclusion**
The video introduces the novel concept of symmathesy, offering a fresh perspective on understanding and interacting with complex systems. By emphasizing the importance of relationships and mutual learning, symmathesy challenges traditional approaches that focus on individual components. While the concept may be unfamiliar to many viewers, the video provides accessible analogies and explanations to make it understandable.
