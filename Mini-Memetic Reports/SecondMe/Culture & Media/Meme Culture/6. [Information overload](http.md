6. **[Information overload](https://app.me.bot/memory/NPGB4Q95F0JZVVAS):**
   In our modern digital environment, the rapid spread of memes can contribute to information overload. This overload may affect an individual's ability to effectively observe and process relevant information. The sheer volume of meme-driven information can overwhelm cognitive capacities, making it challenging to discern signal from noise during the Observe phase. This overload can lead to hasty or incomplete observations, potentially compromising the entire OODA loop process.
