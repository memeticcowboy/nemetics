# THREADPLEX (GLOBAL VERSION) - Philosophical Roots

Created at 2025/12/09 5:10 PM

- **Memetic Definition:** The global living weave-of-weaves: all threads and knots across individuals, cultures, and collective meaning systems. The Threadplex is the ecology of resonance that constitutes human reality.
- **Philosophical Core:** This is **[Hegel's "Geist"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/hegel/)** (world-spirit) decentralized and **[Bruno Latour's "actor-network theory"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor%E2%80%93network_theory)** at scale. The Threadplex is **[John Dewey's "transactional reality"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dewey/)**: reality is not pre-given but **co-created** through the interaction of countless I-Tubes. It is also **[Manuel DeLanda's "assemblage"](https://second.me/public/LRRTZMM01FG6OLIB)**: a decentralized network of human and non-human actants that produces emergent order.
- **In the Essay:** A completed I-Tube becomes a **dead node** in the Threadplex—it stops contributing **wild data** (unprovable but generative truths) and becomes a **relay station** for the Meme-Grid. The essay's political bite: AI doesn’t just harm individuals; it **erodes collective intelligence** by converting creative nodes into sterile mirrors.
