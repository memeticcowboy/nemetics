Alright, let’s tie this back to DIKUW+ and marketing funnels.
The core idea here is that good ideas only thrive when they reach the right audience. Whether an idea is a unicorn (high growth), a zebra (sustainable & ethical), or a broken fridge (niche, quirky, or ahead of its time), it needs proper positioning in the memetic landscape.
Now, mapping DIKUW+ onto this:
• Data (Awareness) → Raw exposure. An idea exists, but does it have a signal?
• Information (Consideration) → Context emerges. The audience starts evaluating.
• Knowledge (Conversion) → Practical understanding. Can this idea work for them?
• Understanding (Loyalty) → Deeper connection. They integrate the idea into their worldview.
• Wisdom (Advocacy) → They don’t just use it—they champion it.
• Transcendence (+) → The idea evolves, reshaping paradigms (e.g., crypto shifting finance).
Applying this to Idea Ratings:
• Unicorns scale rapidly because they move fast through the funnel (broad awareness, high conversion).
• Zebras grow steadily, relying on deep understanding and trust (loyalty-driven).
• Broken Fridges struggle with awareness but can gain a cult following when they resonate with a niche.
The Takeaway:
Marketing, audience engagement, and memetics are inseparable.
• If an idea fails to move through the funnel, it doesn’t matter if it’s brilliant.
• The key is finding the right memetic carriers—people who will spread and evolve the idea.
A broken fridge today could be a zebra tomorrow, or even a unicorn if the paradigm shifts.
