## Narrative Fractals
- **Definition**: Stories within stories mirroring the overarching narrative.
- **Characteristics**:
  - Self-similarity
  - Recursion
  - Holistic meaning
  - Nonlinearity

## Memeplex
- **Definition**: Collection of interrelated memes reinforcing each other.

## Integration of Narrative Fractals with Memeplex
1. **Self-Similarity and Replication**:
   - Memeplex memes share core aspects, akin to narrative fractals' self-similarity.

2. **Recursion and Evolution**:
   - Memeplex evolves recursively, spawning new memes, similar to narrative fractals.

3. **Holistic Meaning and Cultural Impact**:
   - Both contribute to broader cultural narratives, with smaller elements enriching the larger story.

4. **Nonlinearity and Spread**:
   - Memeplexes spread nonlinearly, disrupting linear storytelling, similar to narrative fractals.

### Example: The "Star Wars" Memeplex
- "Star Wars" franchise as a memeplex with self-similar and recursive memes.
- Each new addition enriches the narrative, showcasing a fractal-like structure.
