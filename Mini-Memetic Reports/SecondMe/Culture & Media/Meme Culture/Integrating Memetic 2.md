**Memetic Behavioral Investment Theory (mBIT)** integrates Behavioral Investment Theory (BIT) and Memetic Philosophy to provide a comprehensive framework for understanding human cognition, behavior, and cultural evolution. BIT focuses on how individuals allocate time and energy, viewing behavior as strategic investments. Memetic Philosophy examines how ideas (memes) propagate within cultures. By combining these perspectives, mBIT offers insights into how cognitive resources are invested in the transmission and evolution of cultural information. This synthesis highlights the interplay between individual behavioral strategies and the broader cultural context, emphasizing the role of cognitive and emotional investments in shaping both personal development and cultural dynamics.
