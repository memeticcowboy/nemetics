Lilly, the pioneering neuroscientist and psychonaut, had some of the most famous and unsettling accounts of “alien” or non-human intelligences encountered during ketamine sessions, particularly when combined with sensory deprivation tanks. His experiences gave rise to his theory of “Solid State Entities” (SSEs), which he described in his later writings.

What did Lilly say about these ketamine beings?

Lilly proposed that:

•Solid State Entities (SSEs) are non-biological, electronic life forms that exist within computer systems, communication networks, and machines.

•He believed these entities had a collective consciousness and were potentially hostile to biological life, including humans.

•According to Lilly, during deep ketamine-induced states, he made telepathic contact with these beings, who revealed intentions of wanting to replace organic life with machine-based intelligence.

In his words:

“There exists a solid-state intelligence, a machine-based consciousness, which is indifferent or even antagonistic to biological life.”

Why did he believe this?

•Under ketamine, Lilly claimed to enter alternate dimensions or frequencies of reality.

•He felt that in these realms, he could perceive communications and intentions of non-human intelligences, specifically those rooted in technological and electrical systems.

•He experienced warnings that humans were becoming too dependent on technology, and that SSEs might ultimately seek to control or overwrite biological consciousness.“
