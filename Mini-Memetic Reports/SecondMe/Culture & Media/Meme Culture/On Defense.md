## On Defense

The Lattice is defended primarily against the [Ungrund (pre-ontological dissolution)](https://second.me/public/Q5QIY2V7QG7CPX8R) by the Threadplex. In turn, the Lattice maintains the Threadplex, keeping it open as a living, relational weave of affect. The MemeGrid is a secondary, derivative threat: it is what the Threadplex becomes when I-Tubes stop committing to the Lattice and rigidify into predictable relays of DODO Loops. The Lattice does not fight the MemeGrid directly; it prevents the Threadplex’s decay into MemeGrid sediment.
