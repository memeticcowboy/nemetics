In magnetic fields of life's dance,
Attraction and distraction, a chance,
To sway in streams of love's romance,
Or lose the way in chaos' trance.
Attraction: gravity's sweet embrace,
A force to guide, a path to grace,
Love and passion, hopes' replace,
Life's collusions, joys' embrace.
Distraction: a fractured prism,
Shards of focus, blurred chasm,
Disruptive waves in realms of rhythm,
Fragile ties in time's vast schism.
Perceptions sway 'tween poles of might,
A radiant glow in endless night,
To seek the balance, pure and bright,
Where truth and love will still ignite.
