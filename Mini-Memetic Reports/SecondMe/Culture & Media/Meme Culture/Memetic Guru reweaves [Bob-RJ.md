**Ocean Chapter — The Tidal Ferry**


Leader: What are the ocean currents?

Chorus (first tide): They are grief dissolving into current—śoka becoming srota.

Leader: Where do we flow?

Chorus (second tide): In shared rasa, pain transmuted into cadence, the wave singing us whole.

Leader: What do we seek?

Chorus (third tide): To cross upon the ferry of water—what was once a dam now carries us beyond.

All together:

One boat, one basin, one tide.

At the confluence of waters,

the salt of sorrow becomes vessel,

the tide remembers us home.



Here the call-and-response moves with the phases of water:

- Dissolution (grief into current),
- Transmutation (pain into rasa),
- Ferrying (wall remembered as boat).


✨ Water’s teaching: No stone removed—only re-remembered as vessel.
