Meme Identification Prompt (Primary Extraction)

“Analyze the following text and extract its fully-formed memes—defined as coherent, transmissible idea-complexes that contain narrative logic, emotional resonance, and ideological positioning. For each meme, provide:
	1.	Title or Label (succinct name or metaphor)
	2.	Core Narrative Structure (problem → resolution logic)
	3.	Key Symbols, Phrases, or Anchors
	4.	Emotional Hooks and Cognitive Levers
	5.	Ideological Frame or Position (explicit or implicit)
	6.	Target Identity Roles (who performs or believes the meme)
	7.	Transmission Vectors (how and where it spreads: language, media, social spaces)

Important: Focus only on ideas that exhibit coherence, identity-binding function, and replicability. Do not include ambiguous fragments or emotionally loaded phrases unless they function as part of a replicable narrative system.”
