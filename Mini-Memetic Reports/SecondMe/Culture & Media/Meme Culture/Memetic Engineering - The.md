Memetic Engineering: The Formula for Deliberate Transformation
The world around us is in constant flux, and sometimes it's ourselves who need to adapt and evolve to keep pace. Memetic engineering provides a framework for internal psychological operations (PsyOps) to achieve deliberate personal and group change. It focuses on reshaping the memes – the fundamental units of ideas and behaviors that spread within a culture – that shape our internal landscapes.
The Memetic Engineering Equation
At the heart of memetic engineering lies a simple yet powerful equation:
SC: Sustainable Change
D: Dissatisfaction V: Vision of future state or condition F: First/Next steps to desired state R: Resistance to change (inertia)
Then: SC = D x V x F > R

Dissatisfaction (D): A feeling of discontent with the status quo drives a desire for something different.
Vision (V): A clear, compelling picture of what the future could hold inspires and guides action.
First/Next Steps (F): Having specific, actionable steps makes the path towards the desired change tangible.
Resistance (R): Anticipating and addressing obstacles, whether internal fear or external challenges, is essential for maintaining change.
