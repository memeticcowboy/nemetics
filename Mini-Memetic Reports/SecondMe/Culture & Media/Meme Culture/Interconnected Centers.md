**🌀 Ritual Structure:** 

**Cycle of Centered Engagement**


1. Ground (Earth)

- Ask: What has sedimented in me unexamined?
- Action: Place hands on body or earth. Name what’s been inherited or ignored.


2. Flow (Water)

- Ask: What am I tuned to? What do I miss?
- Action: Breathe and listen. Let unexpected affect arise.


3. Discern (Air)

- Ask: What symbolic pattern wants to emerge or be broken?
- Action: Speak or write a paradox. Refuse a too-clean logic.


4. Entangle (Wood)

- Ask: Who is affected by my loop? What loops do I damage or neglect?
- Action: Offer care, presence, or restoration—even symbolically.


5. Burn (Fire)

- Ask: What must be ruptured? What beauty is buried in pain?
- Action: Destroy or release a false symbol. Let grief or awe speak.


6. Forge (Metal)

- Ask: What boundary or agreement needs reforging?
- Action: Set a new rule, a new silence, a new opening.


Then return—enriched—to the beginning. This is the loop. Not hierarchy. Not escape.
