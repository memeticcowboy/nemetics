# Exploring the Fire Kasina: Benefits, Risks, and Meditative Practices

Created at 2025/03/14 11:59 AM

Welcome to the site dedicated to exploring the ancient practice of the Fire Kasina, described in detail in places such as the *[Visuddhimagga](http://www.accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/nanamoli/PathofPurification2011.pdf "Online PDF version of this text")* and *[Vimuttimagga](https://archive.org/details/ArahantUpatossa-Vimuttimagga-PathOfFreedom.pdf "Online PDF version of this text")*, commentaries on the Pali Canon. It is a meditative practice in which, in its simplest form, you stare at a candle flame (or other source of flame, such as a lamp, or even the LED from a phone), close your eyes, and pay attention to what you actually see. It is the basis of deep concentration states, fascinating visual and other magickal experiences, and can also serve as a basis for insight and awakening. It can also be done eyes open, as well as standing, walking, laying down, etc.

This practice has a wide range of remarkable benefits, potentially producing insights and awakenings, blissful and peaceful states (jhanas), experiences of magic and “the powers”, realms, entities, energetic experiences, healings, improvements in the visual system, and a wide range of other remarkable and surprising effects.

**It can also produce challenges of a wide variety, including states of mind that are confusing, disorienting, agitated, fearful, dark, heavy, hard to process, and profoundly altered in ways that may mildly, moderately, or severely impair function for at least the short to medium term, and potentially longer.**
