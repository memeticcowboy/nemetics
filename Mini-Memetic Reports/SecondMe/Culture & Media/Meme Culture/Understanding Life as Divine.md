- **Līlā as Divine Play:**<br>Līlā, a Sanskrit term meaning "play" or "sport," is used by Alan Watts to describe the universe and life as a form of divine play. He refers to the Hindu concept of *Vishnu Līlā*, where Vishnu (a name of God) engages in the cosmic sport or play of existence. This playfulness implies that the universe is not a serious, rigid structure but a dynamic, creative game being enacted by the divine. The ultimate player, or the "big Joker," is Brahman, the ground of all being, and each individual is a "little Joker" participating in this cosmic game [(00:10:00)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4vv93azMkDE%3Fsi%3DJquO3GPIKhFsw7DE&t=600)
- **The Paradox of Līlā:**<br>The art of the Joker, or the divine player, is paradoxical: to reveal that life is a game while simultaneously keeping the game going. This means that while the universe is fundamentally playful and not to be taken too seriously, the experience within the game feels real and serious to the players (us). Recognizing Līlā helps one to awaken to the illusion of separateness and seriousness, seeing life as a joyful, creative dance rather than a grim struggle [(00:10:00)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4vv93azMkDE%3Fsi%3DJquO3GPIKhFsw7DE&t=600)
- **Līlā and Awakening:**<br>Understanding life as Līlā leads to a spiritual awakening where one realizes that the self and the universe are not separate. This awakening reveals that one was always part of the divine play, and the apparent barriers or struggles are part of the game itself. It brings a sense of harmony and acceptance of all aspects of life, including suffering and joy, as integral parts of the cosmic dance [(00:10:00)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4vv93azMkDE%3Fsi%3DJquO3GPIKhFsw7DE&t=600)
### Summary
Līlā in Alan Watts' philosophy represents the universe as a divine, playful game enacted by the ultimate reality (Brahman). It emphasizes the non-serious, creative, and interconnected nature of existence, inviting a perspective of life as joyful play rather than a tragic struggle. Recognizing this playfulness is key to spiritual awakening and understanding one's true nature within the cosmos.
