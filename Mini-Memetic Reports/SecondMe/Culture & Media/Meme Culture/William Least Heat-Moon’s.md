William Least Heat-Moon’s Blue Highways is a reflective travel narrative blending physical and emotional landscapes. Influenced by the Beat Generation, it explores self-discovery, connection, and spiritual truths through lesser-known American roads, echoing Kerouac, Thoreau, and Emerson.
