**🧠 DSRP Mapping onto Playable Epistemology**


Distinctions

- Function: Reveal the hidden boundaries between ideas and their opposites.
- In Playable Epistemology:<br>
    - Surface the binary contrasts embedded in memes (“truth vs. fiction,” “expert vs. amateur,” “fact vs. story”).
    - Encourage participants to break and redraw these distinctions (e.g., What if this “fact” is actually a narrative?).


Systems

- Function: Show how parts relate to wholes and nested structures.
- In Playable Epistemology:<br>
    - Make explicit the nested scaffolds of knowledge (e.g., a meme belongs to a narrative, which belongs to an ideology).
    - Let users model and remix these systems as interactive maps.


Relationships

- Function: Highlight interdependencies and causal loops.
- In Playable Epistemology:<br>
    - Expose how memes reinforce or undermine each other.
    - Enable players to see how emotional resonance and factual claims entangle.


Perspectives

- Function: Shift vantage points and reframe meaning.
- In Playable Epistemology:<br>
    - Provide mechanisms to step into different interpretive positions (e.g., observer, critic, participant).
    - Scaffold perspective-switching rituals and tools.

DSRP Benefits for Playable Epistemology:

✅ Strengthens meta-cognitive literacy.

✅ Offers a simple grammar to navigate complex memetic terrains.

✅ Provides a recursive tool for sensemaking.
