* "Memetics" is a theoretical framework that applies evolutionary principles to the study of cultural information. The term was coined by Richard Dawkins in his 1976 book "The Selfish Gene," where he introduced the concept of memes as units of cultural transmission analogous to genes in biology.
* Dawkins proposed that memes, like genes, are replicators that compete for survival and propagation. Successful memes are those that are effectively transmitted and adopted by individuals within a culture. Examples of memes include ideas, beliefs, behaviors, styles, and technologies.
