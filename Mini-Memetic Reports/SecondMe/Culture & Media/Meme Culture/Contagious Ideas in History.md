# Contagious Ideas in History

Created at 2025/08/08 7:10 PM

## Democracy in Ancient Greece
- The concept of ordinary citizens governing themselves originated in Athens.
- This idea inspired future revolutions and democratic movements.

## The Printing Press & Reformation
- Gutenberg’s invention allowed Martin Luther’s 95 Theses to spread widely.
- This led to the Protestant Reformation, significantly altering European society.

## The Scientific Method
- The approach to inquiry developed by Bacon and Galileo became a global standard.
- It replaced older dogmas and transformed the pursuit of knowledge.

## Nonviolent Resistance
- Gandhi’s philosophy of nonviolent protest influenced various global movements.
- It inspired the U.S. Civil Rights Movement and continues to impact modern protests.

## Viral Internet Memes
- Campaigns like the “Ice Bucket Challenge” and movements like “Black Lives Matter” demonstrate how ideas can mobilize large groups quickly in the digital age.
