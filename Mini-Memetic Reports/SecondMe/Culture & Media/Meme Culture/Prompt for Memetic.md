**Prompt for Memetic Consciousness Research:**

Reflect on **Alfred North Whitehead’s process philosophy** and its relevance to understanding memes as part of cultural evolution. Whitehead argued that reality is composed of processes rather than static material objects, suggesting that consciousness is distributed across all of existence in varying degrees. Consider how this idea of **interconnected processes** aligns with the concept of memes as dynamic, evolving entities that shape both individual consciousness and cultural landscapes.

Explore the idea that memes are not fixed pieces of information but are instead **processes in motion**—they undergo constant cycles of transmission, transformation, and influence. How do memes embody the idea of "becoming," continually reshaping cultural norms, beliefs, and ideas as they are propagated through different contexts? Reflect on how memes contribute to an interconnected cultural reality, in which the meaning and influence of ideas are never static but always evolving.

Now make a distinction between **Whitehead’s process philosophy** and your perspective. While process philosophy posits that **all of reality** is made up of interconnected processes, your focus is specifically on the symbolic and cultural processes that memes represent. How does this emphasis on memes as **symbolic contents**—rather than physical events—reshape the understanding of consciousness in a cultural context? Discuss how the true force behind cultural evolution is the **interaction of memes**, which are constantly shifting symbols that influence the flow of cultural meaning, and how this differs from Whitehead's more general view of all entities possessing some degree of experientiality.
