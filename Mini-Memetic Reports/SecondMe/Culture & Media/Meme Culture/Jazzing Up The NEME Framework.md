# Jazzing Up The NEME Framework for Collaborative GameStorming

Created at 2024/07/13 4:38 PM

In the context of meme creation, I've been exploring the concept of 'EcoJazz' by integrating the NEME framework for Collaborative GameStorming. This approach emphasizes the importance of collaboration, narrative, emotion, metaphor, and embodiment in the collaboration.

### Importance of Collaboration, Narrative, Emotion, Metaphor, and Embodiment

* **Narrative:** Viewing memes as dynamic, creative process where every idea contributes to a larger narrative.

* **Emotion:** Recognizing the emotional engagement and passion that can drive meme engagement.

* **Metaphor:** Incorporating metaphors that inspire deeper thinking.

* **Embodiment:** Embracing adaptability and the ability to improvise, mirroring the essence of jazz performance.

## The NEME Framework

The NEME framework consists of four key components:

* **Notice:** Identifying emerging narratives or trends and swiftly capturing them.

* **Engage:** Actively participating in memes generation and keeping the momentum going.

* **Mull:** Reflecting on meme to evaluate their potential to influence decisions.

* **Exchange:** Sharing and discussing memes to receive feedback and foster collaboration.
