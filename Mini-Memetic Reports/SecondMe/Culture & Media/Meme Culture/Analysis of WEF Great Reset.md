Title: Meme Identification of WEF “Great Reset” Narratives on X

Brief Description:

This analysis extracts and structures the dominant memes circulating on X (formerly Twitter) about the World Economic Forum’s (WEF) “Great Reset.” The memes present the WEF as a global elite engineering a dystopian future through social, political, and economic control. Two main memeplexes emerge: (1) the general “WEF Great Reset Conspiracy,” portraying the WEF as dismantling sovereignty and freedoms, and (2) “Great Reset: Currency Collapse and Digital Control,” emphasizing financial surveillance and the imposition of CBDCs and social credit systems.

**Meme 1: WEF Great Reset Conspiracy**


1. Title or Label:

“WEF Great Reset Conspiracy”

2. Core Narrative Structure (Problem → Resolution):

- Problem: Global elites (WEF) are using crises (COVID-19, climate change) to dismantle national sovereignty and enforce a totalitarian world order.
- Resolution: Awareness, public resistance, and defense of individual freedoms and national identity are required to stop the plan.


3. Key Symbols, Phrases, or Anchors:

- “Great Reset”
- “You will own nothing and be happy”
- “Build Back Better”
- “New World Order”
- “Global communism”


4. Emotional Hooks and Cognitive Levers:

- Fear of losing freedom, property, and national identity.
- Outrage at elite manipulation and hidden agendas.
- Cognitive shortcut: framing complex global issues as a coordinated conspiracy.
- Solidarity via anti-globalist sentiment.


5. Ideological Frame or Position:

- Anti-globalist and nationalist, opposed to centralization of power.
- Libertarian, emphasizing individual rights.
- Conspiracy-oriented worldview, interpreting global events as elite orchestration.


6. Target Identity Roles:

- Nationalist and sovereignty-minded citizens.
- Skeptics of mainstream narratives and government authority.
- Anti-establishment and anti-globalist communities.


7. Transmission Vectors:

- Social media (X/Twitter) posts, videos, and viral threads (e.g., @wideawake_media).
- Right-wing and libertarian news outlets.
- Online conspiracy forums and alternative media spaces.





**Meme 2: Great Reset — Currency Collapse and Digital Control**


1. Title or Label:

“Great Reset: Currency Collapse and Digital Control”

2. Core Narrative Structure (Problem → Resolution):

- Problem: WEF and global elites plan to collapse traditional currencies and impose CBDCs and social credit systems, erasing personal financial freedom.
- Resolution: Recognizing the threat and resisting adoption of digital currencies or globalized financial control.


3. Key Symbols, Phrases, or Anchors:

- “CBDCs” (Central Bank Digital Currencies)
- “Social Credit Score” (linked to China)
- “Collapse of currencies”
- “You will own nothing and be happy”


4. Emotional Hooks and Cognitive Levers:

- Fear of financial surveillance and loss of privacy.
- Anxiety about digital authoritarianism.
- Cognitive framing: familiar dystopian comparisons (China’s system, Orwellian control).


5. Ideological Frame or Position:

- Anti-authoritarian and libertarian, defending financial sovereignty.
- Anti-globalist, opposing centralized economic governance.
- Privacy-centric, hostile to digital surveillance.


6. Target Identity Roles:

- Individuals invested in personal financial autonomy.
- Privacy and digital rights advocates.
- Libertarians and cryptocurrency enthusiasts wary of centralized banking.


7. Transmission Vectors:

- Viral X posts (e.g., @liz_churchill10 on CBDCs and social credit).
- Financial and crypto forums amplifying the narrative.
- Alternative economic blogs and libertarian digital spaces.





**Summary of Memetic Landscape**


Both memes are components of a broader anti-globalist memeplex that frames the WEF as a sinister elite cabal. They operate through fear, distrust, and simplified cause-effect narratives that bind group identity and drive engagement. These memes replicate effectively because they provide clear villains (WEF/elites), high-stakes narratives (loss of freedom and sovereignty), and emotional urgency.
