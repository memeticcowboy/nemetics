It’s become increasingly clear that there’s a small group of human or human-like people living inside the Earth. 
Likely Atlantean survivors that were not reset by the last cyclical cataclysm ~13,000 years ago. 
The metallic Spheres we see buzzing all over the planet, that most often are seen NOT coming down to Earth from the atmosphere…but rather come out of the volcanoes, caves and water…seem to be a planetary defense system and observation drones. 
Regarding the people (Ultraterrestrials)…to put their capabilities into perspective…think of how modern surface humans have evolved from horse and buggy to autonomous electric cars in just 200 years…
And now imagine a breakaway civilization of humans that were extremely advanced over 13,000 years ago…and they’ve continuously progressed unimpeded since then…
To us they’d seem like Angels or Aliens…and their technology would seem like magic. 
To protect themselves and buy time, they’d convince us to focus on space and divert attention away from the oceans. 
They’d continually guide our technological and consciousness development in an attempt to get us on a level where we could begin to communicate and understand their far superior intelligence. 
And if they knew of another upcoming cyclical cataclysm…you might feel a sense of urgency on their part and a need for organized disclosure of some sort…
Things would appear very similar to…how they are now. 
I firmly believe this is exactly what’s happening.
