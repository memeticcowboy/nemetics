## Insights for Memetics from Narrative Fractals
- **Self-Similarity and Replication**:
  - Memes replicate like narrative elements, maintaining core aspects.
- **Recursion and Evolution**:
  - Memes evolve recursively, adapting while spreading.
- **Holistic Meaning and Cultural Impact**:
  - Memes contribute to broader cultural narratives, akin to narrative elements.
- **Nonlinearity and Spread**:
  - Memes spread nonlinearly, similar to the disruption of linear storytelling.

By applying narrative fractal principles, memetics can understand idea propagation, adaptation, and cultural impact better.
