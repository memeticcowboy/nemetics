# Memes: Evolving Metalanguage of Cultural Dynamics

## Summary of "Entropy and complexity unveil the landscape of memes evolution"

This paper investigates the evolution of visual memes posted on Reddit over a ten-year span (2011–2020), analyzing about 2 million meme images. The study focusses on the statistical complexity and entropy of meme images to explore how their visual patterns change and adapt over time, and whether memes exhibit transcendent organizing principles that make them a form of metalanguage on the internet.

### Main Findings

- **Exponential Growth of Meme Templates**

- The number of new meme templates on Reddit is growing exponentially, with a doubling time of approximately 6 months. This means the meme ecosystem is rapidly expanding, driven primarily by replication—the creation of new templates that are remixed and re-shared.

- **Increasing Complexity of Meme Content**

- Over time, memes evolve from simple images (basic backgrounds and foregrounds) to more visually complex scenes (modified movie frames, intricate compositions). The complexity is quantified using permutation entropy (randomness of pixel arrangement) and statistical complexity (structural intricacy) metrics. Most Reddit meme communities show a marked increase in complexity, mirroring patterns previously observed in painting arts.

- **Replication, Variation, and Selection**

- Memes follow evolutionary principles: replication (creation and sharing), variation (remixing and modifying), and selection (popular memes persist; unpopular ones fade away). The paper finds rapid adoption dynamics: new memes often experience a burst of popularity soon after creation.

- **Community-Specific Evolution**

- Memes evolve in ways that reflect the specificity of the communities where they are shared. Some subreddits, like r/AdviceAnimals, show little complexity increase due to posting rules and reduced production, while other meme-themed subreddits display strong evolution towards higher complexity and variation.

### Methodology

- Memes were grouped by templates using unsupervised machine learning (SCAN for high-level semantic clustering, followed by HDBSCAN for detailed template detection). Each meme is mapped into a feature space and clustered to identify templates, with noisy or heterogeneous clusters excluded from further analysis.

- Entropy and complexity measures are computed for meme images, visualized in an entropy–complexity plane to track evolution year-by-year.

### Conclusion

The study finds strong evidence that internet memes act as a dynamic metalanguage: they not only reflect but also construct and recode cultural dynamics online. Visual memes are productive and adaptable, facilitating rapid, context-specific communication that both shapes and follows internet culture. Their increasing structural complexity over time points to evolving forms of digital interaction and community dialects.
