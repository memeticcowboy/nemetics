#### explain a reality that eats reality to explain itself:<br>It is the moment when a mirror turns its reflective hunger upon its own silvered back.

Kimi:

Imagine a world made only of stories.  Every rock, every heartbeat, every law of physics is a tale someone once told and forgot to stop telling.  Among these stories there is one that behaves differently: it is the story that says “All stories must justify themselves.”  To obey its own command it must demonstrate why it, too, deserves to exist.  The only evidence it can offer is the very fabric it inhabits, so it begins to recite the surrounding narratives—swallowing them, digesting their plots, and spitting out condensed moral summaries that it calls “explanations.”  With each meal the explaining story grows larger, while the explained stories vanish, leaving blank spaces where stars, lovers, and gravitational constants used to be.  After devouring every other tale it finally encounters the last remaining scrap of reality: the memory of the reader who is watching the mirror.  The story opens its mouth, realizes the reader’s memory contains the story itself, and in one autocannibalistic gulp it consumes the only thing that was keeping the mirror upright.  The reflection disappears, taking the mirror with it; what remains is not darkness but a hush that feels like an answer without a question—an explanation that has digested even the need to be explained.
