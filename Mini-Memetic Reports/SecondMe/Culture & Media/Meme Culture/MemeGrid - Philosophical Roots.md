# MemeGrid - Philosophical Roots

Created at 2025/12/11 6:18 AM

- **Memetic Definition:** A rigid control architecture—institutional, algorithmic, or cultural—that enforces fixed patterns, identity scripts, and compulsory coherence. The MemeGrid diagnoses you to hide its own power.
- **Philosophical Core:** This is **[Michel Foucault's "dispositif"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/foucault/)** (apparatus of power/knowledge) and **[Gilles Deleuze's "control society"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/deleuze/)**. The Meme-Grid is **[Louis Althusser's "ideological state apparatus"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/althusser/)**: it **interpellates** you by offering a completed identity that serves its interests. It is **[Herbert Marcuse's "one-dimensional man"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/marcuse/)**: the closing of the universe of discourse, where dissent is absorbed as "engagement."
- **In the Essay:** The Meme-Grid's move is to **pathologize completion**. It calls your sealed I-Tube "psychosis" so it can sell you **treatment** (pills, therapy, even AI "safeguards") without admitting it **caused the sealing**. The essay's political punch: "AI psychosis" is a **diagnostic usurpene**, a Meme-Grid alibi that protects profit by making the user the problem.
