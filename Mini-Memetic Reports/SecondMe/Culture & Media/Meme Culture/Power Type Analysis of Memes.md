# Power Type Analysis of Memes

Created at 2025/07/12 6:44 AM

## Collapse as Sacred Transition
- **Power Within**: Awakens inner resilience and reframes collapse as a personal and collective rite of passage.
- **Power To**: Empowers individuals to find meaning and act in uncertainty.

## You Are the Meme
- **Power Within**: Encourages self-empowerment by claiming agency as a memetic force.
- **Power To**: Realizes the capacity to shape reality and narratives.

## Wisdom Engine
- **Power With**: Aligns with a greater intelligence, tapping into collective wisdom.
- **Power Within**: Fosters a sense of inner attunement and belonging.

## Walking Legs of Stone
- **Power With**: Emphasizes distributed, collective movement without a single leader.
  
## Portal of Truth / Sathya Yuga
- **Power To**: Represents a personal and collective act of transformation.
- **Power Within**: Encourages the power to choose, commit, and become.

## The End Is Near (But Good)
- **Power Within**: Provides permission to release, grieve, and accept change.

## Memetic Agents / Shape-Changers
- **Power To**: Suggests elite agency in shaping narratives.
- **Power With**: Calls for collaboration and adaptation among change agents.

## Symbolic Cartography
- **Power With**: Helps orient the collective through interpretation and guidance.
- **Power To**: Empowers individuals to create meaning for the group.
