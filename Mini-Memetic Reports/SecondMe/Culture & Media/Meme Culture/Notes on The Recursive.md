# Notes on The Recursive Futures Engine and Strategic Temporal Trichotomy

Created at 2025/06/10 9:45 PM

## Recursive Futures Engine
- A dynamic toolkit for strategic foresight.
- Combines futures thinking, memetics, systems theory, and recursive consciousness.
- Helps navigate the complex web of possible futures.
- Focuses on actively shaping futures rather than just predicting them.

## Strategic Temporal Trichotomy
- Breaks down futures into three categories:
  1. **Possible Futures**: All imaginable futures, including wild scenarios.
  2. **Probable Futures**: Most likely futures based on current trends and data.
  3. **Preferable Futures**: Futures aligned with personal values and vision (memetic north star).
- Interaction between categories creates a recursive feedback loop.
- Uses backcasting to design steps from Preferable to Probable, influencing the Possible space through meme injection and cultural reorientation.
- Engages actively with futures, steering towards desired outcomes.
