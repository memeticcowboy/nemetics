### Meet the Man Who Solved Going Viral (Paddy Galloway Interview)

This video is an interview with YouTube strategist Paddy Galloway by Jay Clouse.

Key Points

 * Niche is Not Destined to Fail

   * There are no niches destined to fail.

   * Every niche has a market.

   * Some niches are naturally smaller than others.

   * Use the term "Tam" (Totally Addressable Market) to determine the size of the market for your niche.

   * Search for your niche on YouTube and filter by most viewed videos to get an idea of the Tam.

 * How to Identify Your Niche's Total Addressable Market (Tam)

   * Search for your niche on YouTube.

   * Filter by longer videos.

   * Sort by most viewed.

   * Look at the viewership of the top channels in your niche to get an idea of your Tam.

 * Tips for Creating Viral Content

   * Simplicity: Keep your thumbnails and videos simple.

   * Exaggeration: Exaggerate your content slightly to make it more interesting.

   * Feasibility: Make sure your ideas are feasible and can be executed.

   * Passion: Create content that you are passionate about.

   * Collaboration: Ask for feedback from others on your ideas.

 * Pre-Production is Key

   * Pre-production is essential for creating successful content.

   * Spend more time planning your content before you start filming or editing.

   * Develop a one-page idea development document for each idea.

   * Get feedback from others on your ideas before moving forward.

 * There is No One-Size-Fits-All Meta for YouTube

   * There is no one-size-fits-all meta for YouTube success.

   * Different types of content can be successful on YouTube.

   * Don't get caught up in trying to follow the latest trends.

   * Focus on creating high-quality content that your audience will enjoy.
