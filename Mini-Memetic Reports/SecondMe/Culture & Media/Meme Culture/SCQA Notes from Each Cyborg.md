- Mental loops can be modeled as feedback loops within a cognitive system.
- Thoughts are signals in a semi-open system, expressions of an adaptive process.

- Individual agency exists within larger networks that co-shape each other.
- Identity is a moving node in a shifting semantic field.
- The observer-observed loop is a Möbius strip, emphasizing flows and recursions.

## Creator (Visionary Impulse)
