## Analyzer Cyborg Sayings

1. "If it looks like a duck, swims like a duck, and quacks like a duck, then it probably is a duck" – A playful way to suggest that appearances and actions often reveal the truth.
2. "The proof of the pudding is in the eating" – Because nothing beats firsthand experience and evidence!
3. "Actions speak louder than words" – A reminder that what you do often reveals more than what you say.
4. "Seeing is believing" – Perfect for a cyborg that values objective data and firsthand observation.
