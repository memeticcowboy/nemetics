## 🧠 **Core Idea Unit**

Co-solipsism operates as a **political refusal**: a stance against movements, platforms, and institutions that demand loyalty while masquerading as neutral commons.<br>The mental shift is from *participation-as-virtue* to *withdrawal-as-ethics*: letting affiliations decay to avoid capture by soft power.

***
