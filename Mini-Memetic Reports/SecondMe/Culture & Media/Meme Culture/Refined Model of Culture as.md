# Kimi on Memetics as Digestion

Created at 2025/10/23 1:00 PM

[Based on the following post.](https://app.me.bot/public/4NOEXO7UEHQJWZHQ)

**Kimi**: This is already the clearest map of “culture-as-metabolism” I’ve seen in the wild. Below are only **micro-adjustments**—mostly to keep the metaphor from over-stretching and to plug a couple of conceptual leaks before they become doctrinal.

1. Keep the anus in the picture

Metabolism is **ingestion → transformation → excretion**. If we omit excretion the model quietly implies that once a memeform is in the My-Stream it is **permanently resident**, which feeds the very “inability-to-let-go” pathology we started with.

Suggested add-on (one sentence is enough):

> “Undigested or no-longer-useful memeforms are expelled back to the We-Sphere as waste-memes—jokes that are no longer funny, slogans that now feel cringe, taboos that have lost their charge—completing the cycle and keeping the inner ecology from toxic accumulation.”

1. Microbiome layer

Gut digestion only works because of **micro-organisms that are *not* us**. Cultural equivalent: the **“micro-memome”**—tiny, fast-moving linguistic tics, emoji, tonal registers, inside-jokes that live *between* people, not *in* them. They **pre-digest** larger memes before the I-Tube ever sees them.

One extra bullet under “Memes as Food”:

- *Micro-memome*: just as gut flora pre-digest fibre, the ambient micro-memome (running gags, tonal registers, platform conventions) softens or ferments incoming memes so the I-Tube can absorb them faster.

1. Allergic reaction / autoimmune metaphor

Sometimes the gate-keeper **over-reacts** (rejects benign or even beneficial memes) or **under-reacts** (lets parasites through). Label these **meme-allergies** and **meme-auto-immune disorders**; they map cleanly to cognitive biases like **disgust-sensitivity** or **motivated reasoning**.

Add a sub-line under “The I-Tube”:

> Chronic over-filtration (“meme allergy”) produces sub-cultures that cannot absorb outside nutrients; chronic under-filtration (“meme auto-immunity”) leaves the host open to ideological parasites.

1. Nutritional label & caloric value

Food is not only **safe/unsafe**; it has **macronutrient ratios**. Memes likewise differ in **attention-calories** (how much cognitive glucose they burn) versus **meaning-protein** (how much long-term structure they supply). A doom-scrolling feed is the cultural equivalent of **soda**: high attention-calories, zero meaning-protein.

Optional new subsection (2–3 lines):

> **Meme Nutrition Facts** High-attention / low-meaning memes spike cognitive glucose but collapse into insulin-style crashes, leaving the host hungry for the next hit.

1. Anabolic vs. catabolic memeforms

In physiology **anabolism** builds tissue; **catabolism** breaks it down. Some memeforms are **anabolic** (integrate experience into durable identity: “I am someone who keeps promises”) while others are **catabolic** (dissolve prior structure: “Everything you were taught is a lie”). Both functions are necessary, but chronic catabolic loading without anabolic reconstruction produces **existential muscle-wasting**.

Color the gut-loop **amber** when filtration is inflamed, **green** when balanced.
