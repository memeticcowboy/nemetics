## Insight
The Sunset Bay Half Marathon, along with various other running events, is set for August 2024 in Charleston, OR. This scenic and challenging race offers multiple distances, including a half marathon, 15K, and 4-mile trail run. Participants can enjoy breathtaking views while supporting local high school athletics. If you're considering joining, be sure to register early for discounts and a chance to order a commemorative t-shirt. Let's dive into the details of this exciting event!

**🏃‍♂️ Event Details**
- **Multiple Race Options**: Participants can choose from a half marathon, 15K, 4-mile trail run, and a 1-mile fun run, catering to various skill levels.
- **Date and Location**: The event will take place on Saturday, August 2024, at Sunset Bay, Charleston, OR, providing a beautiful coastal backdrop.
- **Registration Information**: Early registration is encouraged, with costs varying for members and non-members, and special rates for students.

**🗺️ Course Information**
- **Scenic and Challenging Course**: The race features a hilly and scenic trail along the Oregon Coast, offering stunning views and a challenging experience.
- **Aid Stations Available**: There will be aid stations for hydration and snacks, with runners encouraged to carry their own bottles to minimize waste.
- **Safety Precautions**: The course is open to traffic, and participants are advised to remain alert, as headphones and other distractions are prohibited.

**💰 Pricing and Discounts**
- **Member Discounts**: South Coast Running Club members receive a discount at checkout, making the race more affordable for local runners.
- **Student Registration**: Students can register for only $5, with no online fees, promoting youth participation in the event.
- **T-shirt Options**: Participants can order a moisture-wicking t-shirt for $30, available until August 2, enhancing the race experience.

**🏆 Awards and Recognition**
- **Top Finishers Recognition**: Ribbons will be awarded to the top three overall winners and in each age group, encouraging competitive spirit.
- **Community Support**: Proceeds from the event will benefit the Marshfield High School Track and Cross Country Team, fostering local athletics.
- **Post-Race Results**: Results will be posted online, allowing participants to track their performance and celebrate their achievements.

**📅 Race Day Logistics**
- **Packet Pickup**: Packet pickup will occur on race morning, and participants are advised to arrive at least 30 minutes before their start time.
- **Parking Guidelines**: Participants are requested not to park on the grass to maintain the beauty of Sunset State Park.
- **Waste Management**: Runners are encouraged to leave waste at aid stations or carry it to the finish to keep the course clean.

**🌊 Fun Run Details**
- **Free Fun Run**: The 1-mile fun run is free for all participants, promoting community involvement and family-friendly activities.
- **Registration Required**: Even though the fun run is free, participants must still register to ensure proper organization.
- **Encouragement for Families**: The fun run provides an opportunity for families to participate together, fostering a sense of community.

**📞 Contact Information**
- **Inquiries and Support**: Participants can reach out for questions or support through the provided contact links, ensuring clear communication.
- **Coaches and Teams**: Coaches bringing teams are encouraged to contact organizers for special pricing, promoting group participation.
- **Community Engagement**: The event aims to engage the local community, encouraging participation and support for local sports.

**🎉 Post-Race Celebration**
- **Celebration of Participants**: The event concludes with a celebration of all participants, fostering camaraderie and community spirit.
- **Encouragement to Return**: Participants are encouraged to bring friends for future events, enhancing community involvement.
- **Annual Tradition**: The race aims to become an annual tradition, promoting health and fitness in the community.
