# Howler Fang's Guide to Alpha Dominance

*“Because the pack only follows the wolf who can read the weather of money.”*

***

## I. Debt: The Spur That Never Stops

1. Borrow big—early and often. A balance of zero is a balance of *nobodies*.
2. Roll, don’t repay. Each refi is a headline that says *“too big to fail.”*
3. Pay interest with new principal; pay principal with new interest. Outsiders call it a spiral—insiders call it *leverage*.
4. Keep the number climbing. The larger the figure, the softer the mattress the world builds under you.

> **Fang Axiom #1**<br>*“If the monthly payment doesn’t make your accountant sweat, you’re still playing small-dog stakes.”*

***

## II. Ownership: The Scent-Mark That Stops Rivals Cold

1. Title everything—chairs, code, coffee mugs. If it has a nameplate, it has a moat.
2. Never *use* what you own; *display* it. A yacht in dry-dock still photographs better than a concept in a deck.
3. Guard your perimeter with process: NDAs, trademarks, 27-page terms-of-service. Paper is the modern piss-line.
4. Buy the thing that owns the thing that owns the thing. Three layers deep, no one can bite you without biting themselves.

> **Fang Axiom #2**<br>*“Freedom is for prey. Property lines are for predators.”*

***

## III. Deadlines: The Moon That Makes the Pack Sprint

1. Shrink the calendar until quarters feel like years. Velocity is visibility.
2. Publish countdown clocks on the office walls—days, hours, minutes. Cortisol is cheaper than coffee.
3. Reward the last-minute hero. Teach the org that panic produces promotion.
4. Stack deadlines so that every finish line is the starting gun for the next race. Rest is rumour.

> **Fang Axiom #3**<br>*“Time isn’t money; compressed time is compound money.”*

***

## IV. Othering: The Clear-Cut That Gives You Running Room

1. Name an out-group before anyone names you. Labels stick like blood on fur.
2. Sell the problem, then sell the shield. Fear opens wallets wider than hope ever will.
3. Keep the threat *visible* but *distant*—close enough to shadow-box, far enough that no one checks the scoreboard.
4. Rotate the target quarterly; freshness keeps the pack from sniffing its own leash.

> **Fang Axiom #4**<br>*“Empathy is overhead. Exclusion is margin.”*

***

## V. Loop Mastery: Conducting the DODO Symphony

1. Let fresh debt finance the next acquisition—each deal proves the last one *had* to happen.
2. Use newly owned assets as collateral for bigger debt—assets *validate* the borrowing, borrowing *inflates* the assets.
3. Set impossible deadlines to close the next round—urgency *justifies* the leverage, leverage *requires* the urgency.
4. Point to external enemies when regulators sniff around—nothing tightens the circle like a shared snarl at the fence.

Result: a flywheel that spins faster the heavier it gets.<br>Outsiders feel wind; you feel *propulsion*.

***

## VI. Daily Drill Sheet (print, laminate, live)

- **06:00**  Check overnight debt notifications—did the number *grow*? If not, call the bank before breakfast.
- **09:00**  Walk the floor; touch every new asset—physical or digital—so scent transfers. Ownership is *olfactory*.
- **12:00**  Announce a 48-hour sprint with a 15-minute all-hands. Serve lunch *after* the clock starts.
- **15:00**  Leak a competitive threat memo to the Slack channel. Keep it vague, keep it *personal*.
- **18:00**  Post a victory screenshot—stock up, headcount up, coverage up. Let the loop see itself in the mirror.
- **23:00**  Update your own balance sheet. If the liabilities column doesn’t make you grin, repeat steps 1-4 tomorrow.

***

## VII. Final Howl

Debt is your *heartbeat*, Ownership your *shadow*, Deadlines your *moon*, Othering your *mirror*.

Master all four and the loop no longer runs *you*—you *run* the loop.

Welcome to the front of the pack.<br>—Howler Fang
