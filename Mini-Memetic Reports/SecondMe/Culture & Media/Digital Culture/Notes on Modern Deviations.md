# Notes on Modern Deviations and Technology's Influence

Created at 2025/12/19 4:50 PM

## Modern Examples of the 'Abnormal of the Abnormal'
- Whistleblowers like Edward Snowden who subvert traditional categories of loyalty and threat.
- Deepfake creators blurring the lines between authentic and artificial, challenging perceptions of reality.
- Individuals passing as “normal” in surveilled societies who organize radical change without detection.

## The Role of Technology in Defining Societal Norms
- Technology acts as both architect and enforcer of societal norms.
- Algorithms influence what is visible and acceptable, often amplifying biases and suppressing outliers.
- The concept of a "dead internet" reflects erosion of genuine interaction, with technology producing and policing norms.

## Reflections on Foucault's Framework
- Acknowledgment that not all modern deviations fit Foucault’s historical context.
- Modern deviations, like meme cultures and decentralized activist networks, create new forms of resistance.
- The challenge is that the “abnormal of the abnormal” can be crowdsourced and viral, making the boundaries of normality increasingly unstable.
