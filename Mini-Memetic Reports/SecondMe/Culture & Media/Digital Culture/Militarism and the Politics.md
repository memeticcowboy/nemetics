# Militarism and the Politics of Seriousness

## **Perpetual War as Seriousness**

***

## 🧠 Core Idea Unit

Militarism doesn’t win primarily by argument, but by **tone**. “Seriousness” becomes a status credential that frames permanent war-readiness as maturity, while imagination, diplomacy, and moral grief are filtered out as naïve or unserious.

**Mental shift:** from *policy disagreement* to *affective gatekeeping*—recognizing seriousness itself as a control surface.

***

## 🎭 Identity Play & Roles

- **Cast Roles:**
    - *The Adult in the Room* (policy elites, pundits, senior officials)
    - *The Serious Nation* (identity-level deterrence)
- **Repositioning:**<br>Viewers are invited to perform adulthood by accepting force as baseline reality rather than a contingent choice.

***

## 💥 Emotional Triggers / Cognitive Levers

😨 Fear (of adversaries *and* reputational exile)<br>🧑‍💼 Maturity signaling (“hard truths”)<br>🤐 Conformity pressure (avoid being coded activist / idealist)

These emotions normalize escalation by making dissent socially costly.

***

## 📖 Narrative Engine

**Serious tone → Urgency → Suspension of imagination**

1. Tone gatekeeping (“Adults know the world is dangerous”)
2. Threat inflation as maturity (“competitive threat environment”)
3. Off-ramps vanish (diplomacy as charity, not strategy)
4. Rearmament reads as prudence (budgets and weapons as the only “serious” verbs)

***
