# C Thi Nguyen's 'The Score': Gamification's Hidden Costs

Created at 2026/01/07 6:38 AM

<br><br>Concise summary<br><br>- Philosopher C Thi Nguyen's book, "The Score", warns about the gamification of everyday life, where people prioritize metrics and points over their original goals.<br>- Nguyen argues that "value capture" occurs when the lines between what you care about and how you measure progress become blurred, leading to a focus on the wrong things.<br>- The book highlights how this phenomenon can lead to wasted lives and a fixation on metrics such as GDP, employment figures, and exam grades, at the expense of context, nuance, and human flourishing.<br><br><br>

## Insight


**📊 The Dangers of Gamifying Our Lives: A Review of "The Score" by C Thi Nguyen**
- **The Perils of Metrics Over Meaning**: C Thi Nguyen warns that when people start prioritizing numerical metrics—like points and scores—over their intrinsic goals, it can lead to a distorted sense of achievement and fulfillment. This shift encourages people to focus on superficial measures, which can diminish the quality of their life experiences and personal growth.
- **Understanding Value Capture**: The phenomenon of "value capture" occurs when there's a blurring of boundaries between genuine interests and the metrics used to evaluate them. Nguyen illustrates how individuals inadvertently allow their objectives to be redefined by whatever is easily measurable, resulting in a loss of context and importance.
- **Impact on Education and Society**: Nguyen highlights how the gamification trend infiltrates educational systems, where ranks and scores replace nuanced educational values. This has led to law schools and other institutions compromising their missions to chase higher rankings, ultimately detracting from authentic learning and progress.

**📉 Metrics and the Illusion of Objectivity**
- **Misleading Clarity**: Metrics may seem simple and clear but often strip away important context and nuance. Nguyen argues that this "delicious clarity" offered by statistics leads society to oversimplify complex issues like GDP and employment figures, affecting how these metrics influence public perception and policy.
- **The Dangers of Objectivity Laundering**: The concept of "objectivity laundering" describes how administrators may hide subjective biases behind quantitative measures. By relying on metrics, decision-makers can disguise their accountability, legitimizing choices driven by selected data that may distort the truth.
- **Societal Obsession with Numbers**: Nguyen articulates that societal fixation on numerical data can lead to detrimental outcomes, prioritizing statistics over the qualities of life that truly matter, such as well-being and personal fulfillment.

**🏆 The Shift from Player to Scorekeeper**
- **Gaming Behavior in Learning**: The review shares personal anecdotes, such as competing for points in language learning apps like Duolingo. This reflects a broader cultural trend where the journey of learning is overshadowed by achievements measured solely in numerical terms, leading to frustration and disengagement.
- **Lessons from Law School Rankings**: Law schools previously distinguished themselves based on mission statements and philosophies, but the introduction of league tables led to a focus on arbitrary metrics that devalue educational integrity. Schools began diverting resources from genuine pedagogical efforts to artificially inflate their standings.
- **Rejection as a Game Tactic**: The mechanics of law school admissions have evolved into a game where schools attempt to maximize their rejection rates to bolster perceived exclusivity, often at the expense of genuine educational outreach and integrity.

**🌱 Human Flourishing Contrasted with Metric Fixation**
- **Intrinsic Joy vs. External Validation**: Nguyen emphasizes the importance of retaining intrinsic enjoyment and authenticity in pursuits rather than succumbing to pressures of achievement dictated by external metrics. Engaging in activities for the sake of enjoyment can lead to a more fulfilling life.
- **Experiential Focus**: Real-life examples, such as joy in activities like rock climbing or fly-fishing, underscore that these experiences should not be reduced to mere outcomes. The essence of these activities lies in personal enjoyment and engagement, not just tangible results.
- **Rediscovering Fulfillment**: The text urges a return to prioritizing experiences that genuinely enrich our lives, suggesting that society must recalibrate its values away from quantitative measures towards qualitative experiences that enhance human flourishing.

**🔄 Changing Perspectives on Value Assessment**
- **Evaluating What Matters**: Nguyen calls for individuals and society to critically assess the values that matter most, advocating for a more nuanced understanding of success that transcends traditional metrics.
- **Encouraging Reflective Engagement**: Engaging thoughtfully with what we care about empowers individuals to pursue passions that enrich their lives rather than simply chasing points or scores. This reflection can lead to a more authentic and meaningful existence.
- **A Future of Meaningful Metrics**: Nguyen’s insights invite readers to re-evaluate what it means to measure success and fulfillment, suggesting that metrics should support our goals rather than dictate them.

**📖 Conclusion: Embracing a Balance Between Metrics and Meaning**
- **Urgent Call to Action**: "The Score" emphasizes the critical need to balance the use of metrics with the appreciation of qualitative values in life. Nguyen advocates for a societal shift where the quality of experiences takes precedence over mere numeric evaluation.
- **Hope Amidst Challenges**: Despite the heavy themes explored in the book, Nguyen offers a sense of hope, encouraging readers to find rejuvenation and joy in their pursuits, free from the constraints of external validation.
- **A Thought-Provoking Read**: This book serves as a timely reminder of the complexities of modern life, compelling readers to reconsider their relationships with goals, metrics, and personal satisfaction in a gamified world.
