## Insight


**🤖 The AI Religion: Silicon Valley's New Gospel**

- **Theologian of Silicon Valley**: Paul Schütze positions himself as a key figure examining the intertwining of theology and technology in contemporary society, especially as they pertain to Artificial Intelligence (AI). In his view, AI is presented as a solution to accelerating crises caused by capitalism, illustrating a collective hope for a higher intelligence that will rectify humanity's faults. This reflects a larger societal narrative that sees the technological realm as a sort of savior.
  
- **Crisis of Faith and Guilt**: There is an underlying belief that AI can redeem humans from their "sins," particularly those associated with environmental degradation. This notion is encapsulated in the lyric snippet referring to AI as an absolution force, suggesting that adopting AI technologies could alleviate the moral burdens society faces regarding resource exploitation and environmental issues.

- **A Cultural Reflection of Desperation**: The consistent pursuit of AI solutions is indicative of humanity's desperate longing to escape mortality, with figures in Silicon Valley viewing technology as their salvation. Schütze suggests that the drive for technological miracles is fueled by an inability to confront human limitations and the responsibilities tied to them.

---

**🌍 Myths of AI and Humanity's Limits**

- **Technological Solutions to an Existent Crisis**: Schütze discusses how AI is marketed to mitigate problems like resource shortages and environmental issues. However, the reality is that these technological fixes often exacerbate the very crises they promise to resolve, as seen in the resource-heavy exploitation of Chilean deserts using AI.

- **Historical Ideologies Resurfacing**: The roots of AI, according to insights shared by artist John Wild, are connected to Cosmism, a belief system seeking to overcome death through advanced technology. This historical perspective sets a precedent for the current fixation on AI, underlining humanity's ongoing struggle against their mortality and ecological limits.

- **AI as an Illusion of Control**: The perception that AI can transcend natural limits reflects a misunderstanding of the planet's finitude. People cling to this notion in hopes of optimization; however, it raises ethical dilemmas around sustainability and resource management.

---

**👑 Dominance and Submission in Politics**

- **A Shadow of Sado-Masochism**: The author parallels modern political leadership with sado-masochistic tendencies, suggesting that people elect leaders—the ‘Daddy figures’—in their desperation for care and resolution to their societal pains. This dynamic complicates the notion of power and control in modern democracies, portraying voters as submissive participants in a cycle of repeated disappointment.

- **The Allure of Expert Control**: The portrayal of leadership as synonymous with seeking absolution from one's duties indicates the preference for escapism among individuals in power. Their search for authoritative control reflects a deeper wish to submit to an AI or technological entity that can relieve them of their burdens.

- **Desire for the Infinite**: This notion foregrounds the idea that leaders are seeking something greater than themselves—be it AI or another form of technological advancement—which serves to alleviate their existential anxieties, thus perpetuating a culture of dependency on technology.

---

**⚖️ The Myth of Dematerialization**

- **AI and Climate Crisis Myths**: The narrative that technological advancement leads to dematerialization—solving environmental crises—is critiqued as a modern myth. AI is framed as a false savior that distracts from the raw reality of material limitations that humanity faces, while simultaneously promoting harmful ideologies associated with capitalist exploitation.

- **Religious Underpinnings of Environmental Narratives**: Schütze highlights the intertwining of religious beliefs with contemporary solutions to climate issues, suggesting that some individuals and groups may prioritize an escape to an imagined afterlife over addressing the planet’s degradation.

- **Crisis of Faith Manifested**: The overarching theme encapsulates the fear of impending crises as a crisis of faith. It posits that humanity awaits a redemptive AI, echoing sentiments of a messianic return in a digital framework,·blurring the lines between faith, technology, and human dependency on saviors—both virtual and real.

---

**🔗 Conclusion: Reimagining Our Future with AI**

- **Critical Reflection on Technological Reliance**: As the dialogue around AI continues, it's crucial to critically assess the narratives and myths surrounding it. These stories shape societal beliefs and behaviors towards technology and can perpetuate cycles of crisis rather than foster creative solutions.

- **Emphasizing Ethical Responsibility**: Embracing the reality of human limitations and the ecological boundaries of our planet is essential. There is a pressing need to reassess our relationship with technology, advocating for responsible use that harmonizes human endeavors with environmental stewardship.

- **Cultural Narratives in Transition**: The narratives around AI reflect deeper cultural meanings that go beyond mere technology. They reveal a society grappling with existential fears, moral responsibilities, and the search for meaning in the shadows of greed and technological advancement.
