# Transformative Shifts in Social Media Strategies: Embracing Innovation and AI Influencers

Created at 2025/07/03 4:08 PM

[Study Notes: Social Media Is About to Change Forever](https://youtu.be/Vcqgw5zWtDQ?si=jctOjmwlDIiv5nYA)

Main Ideas and Shifts

1. Shift from Art Exhibits to TV Networks

	•	Old Strategy: Brands viewed social media as an art exhibit, focusing on curated, aesthetic content primarily on one main account.

	•	New Strategy: Brands should adopt a TV network approach, where they have:

	•	A primary aesthetic account for updates.

	•	An R&D experimental account to test content formats.

	•	Successful formats spun off into separate accounts (e.g., mini media startups).

2. AI Influencers

	•	Definition: Humanoid digital characters created using AI technology that can influence culture and market products.

	•	Types:

	•	Category Evangelist: Represents the brand (e.g., Brian the Tax Accountant).

	•	Buyer Lookalike: Represents the target customer (e.g., Megan the Small Business Owner).

3. The Great Divergence

	•	Concept: As AI content begins to converge on popular formats, human content creators will diverge with more experimental and unique approaches.

	•	Example: Comedian Andrew Schultz’s innovative format of turning phone orientation for better sketch delivery.

4. Creator in Residence

	•	Definition: Brands are starting to hire young creators internally to innovate and execute their social strategies.

	•	Examples:

	•	Starbucks: Hiring a global coffee creator.

	•	John Deere: Appointing a Chief Tractor Officer.

5. Long-form Content and Content Funnels

	•	Strategy: Use short-form content to funnel audiences into long-form engagement (e.g., YouTube, podcasts, live streams).

	•	Importance: The more time audiences spend with content, the better the conversion to loyal customers.

6. Future of Devices

	•	Emerging Technologies:

	•	AR Glasses: Will create an immersive content experience.

	•	Screenless AI Devices: Like a voice-responsive puck, which could transform content consumption dynamics.

7. Future of Product Development

	•	New Process: Companies will use AI to create product prototypes with content before physical products are made.

	•	Benefits: Reduces risks and costs by validating demand through content engagement first.

8. Worldbuilding

	•	Concept: Transitioning to immersive and interactive digital worlds for branding.

	•	Opportunity for Creators/Brands: Collaborate to create engaging and interactive experiences for consumers.

Tactical Opportunities

Earned Media Strategies

	•	Launch for Coverage: Brands should create events or stories that attract media attention and organic content coverage (e.g., Dr. Squatch’s collaboration with Sydney Sweeney).

Affiliate Training Boot Camp

	•	Innovative Program: Brands can offer training to affiliates, enhancing their content skills to drive better sales and happier affiliate partnerships.

Important Terms and Definitions

	•	R&D Account: A testing account for experimenting with content formats before wider release.

	•	Social Architect: A strategic role responsible for managing a brand’s social media strategy and content portfolio.

	•	Social Scientist: A role focused on analyzing social content trends and testing new formats.

Examples

	•	R&D Example: A men’s personal care brand (Axel) using a secret testing account (Lexa Test) to validate content formats before launching them into a new brand account (Axel Routines).

	•	Earned Media Example: Dr. Squatch launching limited edition soap bars featuring Sydney Sweeney’s bathwater to generate buzz and free media coverage.

Summary

The lecture outlines transformative shifts in social media strategies, focusing on innovative roles, AI influencers, and evolving content formats. Brands are encouraged to adopt a more experimental, network-like approach to engage with audiences more effectively, leverage new technologies, and build immersive experiences for their consumers.
