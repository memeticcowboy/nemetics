# Choosing Your Elemental Orientation

Created at 2025/12/21 3:36 PM

**A Simple Orientation Guide**

**Before you 'play' NEMA SWARM, you must select your elemental orientation for the session.**

You are not choosing a character.<br>You are choosing **how attention is oriented right now**.

Each Element highlights a different aspect of what is already present.<br>None is better. None is required.<br>You can always change.

***

## Start Here (One Question)

Ask yourself:

> **What feels most unclear right now?**

Then look for the matching orientation below.

***

## 🔥 Choose **FIRE** if…

Something feels **pulled**, **pressured**, or **ready to move**.

- There is momentum, tension, or direction
- You feel drawn somewhere but aren’t acting yet
- The question is about *where this wants to go*, not what it means

**Fire helps you notice:**

- direction
- pull
- pressure
- readiness (or resistance)

**Fire does *not*:**

- force action
- tell you what to do
- rush you

If it feels like energy is gathering → **[Fire](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6945daa59cd88191a673ba5bbf16ffdc-fire)**

***

## 🌬 Choose **AIR** if…

Things feel **confusing**, **blurred**, or **undifferentiated**.

- You can sense “something is off” but can’t name it
- You want clarity without commitment
- You need to see *what’s different from what*

**Air helps you notice:**

- contrasts
- differences
- signal vs. noise

**Air does *not*:**

- decide meaning
- interpret motives
- explain anything away

If it feels like you need a clean cut, not an answer → **[Air](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-69459eaf0f94819184704a5da2d2c933-air)**

***

## 🌱 Choose **WOOD** if…

You feel **stuck**, **narrowed**, or **boxed in**.

- You want more options
- You sense potential but don’t want to choose yet
- You’re curious what *else* might be possible

**Wood helps you notice:**

- branching possibilities
- safe vs. wild options
- what could grow

**Wood does *not*:**

- make decisions
- converge on one path
- push commitment

If it feels like there should be more room → **[Wood](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6945c5ca80608191a88b82c365342f9f-wood)**

***

## 💧 Choose **WATER** if…

Something feels **charged**, **subtle**, or **relational**.

- There is emotion, mood, or tone without clear words
- You want to sense without being overwhelmed
- You want to feel *without fixing*

**Water helps you notice:**

- felt tone
- resonance
- relational charge

**Water does *not*:**

- soothe or therapize
- absorb or merge
- demand sharing

If something is felt but not spoken → **[Water](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6945d5a97570819191173ba622f7dad5-water)**

***

## 🛡 Choose **METAL** if…

Boundaries feel **unclear**, **strained**, or **too tight**.

- You’re unsure what’s allowed, protected, or required
- Something needs structure, not emotion
- You want to sense limits without enforcing them

**Metal helps you notice:**

- boundaries
- rules
- gates
- pressure points

**Metal does *not*:**

- dominate
- police
- enforce compliance

If the question is “where are the edges?” → **[Metal](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6945d80679488191ae01c674a88d58ae-metal)**

***

## 🌍 Choose **EARTH** if…

You feel **tired**, **strained**, or unsure what can last.

- You’re wondering about capacity or cost
- You want sustainability, not optimization
- You need to know what’s realistic

**Earth helps you notice:**

- weight
- strain
- nourishment
- sustainability

**Earth does *not*:**

- moralize effort
- demand sacrifice
- push endurance

If the question is “what can be sustained?” → **[Earth](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6945d7402b288191a2c57d49174c5a6a-earth)**
