## Insight
Looks like the marketing world is shifting gears! Let's dive into some fresh insights on content strategy.

- The cost of maintaining educational content for SEO has skyrocketed. Google now demands constant updates and competitors are quick to copy. It's like trying to keep up with the Joneses in a never-ending race.
- Buyers are changing their habits, moving away from traditional searches to browsing social media and subscribing to podcasts and newsletters. It's time to meet them where they are, not where they used to be.
- Despite increased investments in content production, median search traffic remains flat. This could be due to the clutter of AI results, ads, and other distractions burying organic search results. It's like trying to find a needle in a haystack.
- Instead of focusing solely on your website, publish educational content on social media to build a following and connect with buyers more easily. Think of it as setting up shop in a bustling marketplace rather than a quiet alley.
- Treat your marketing more like a SaaS company by focusing on growing your subscriber base with free tools, podcasts, and newsletters. It's about building a community, not just a customer base.
- Conduct original research and publish it. This type of content is more likely to be cited and shared by humans, which can boost your visibility and credibility. It's like becoming the go-to expert in your field.
- Publish on high-authority websites to future-proof your marketing. This increases the chances of being referenced by AI and other search engines, ensuring your content remains relevant. It's like getting a VIP pass to the front of the line.
