The video uses the following key concepts to explain the network society:

- **Network Society:** A society where the key social structures and activities are organized around electronically processed information networks. 1 <br>[1. en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_society)<br>[en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_society)
- **Hyperconnectivity:** The pervasive and non-linear flow of information in the network society.
- **Information Hierarchy:** The functional relationship between data, information, knowledge, and actionable insight.
- **Network Labor:** Labor that serves the goals of the network and is valued for its ability to process information.
- **Switched-off Labor:** Labor that has nothing to offer the network and is excluded from its benefits.

- The network society is characterized by hyperconnectivity and the information hierarchy.
- Social and economic inequality in the network society is based on access to and the ability to process information.
- The transition to a network society is creating both opportunities and challenges.
