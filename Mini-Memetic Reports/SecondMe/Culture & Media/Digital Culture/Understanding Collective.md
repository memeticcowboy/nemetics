## Insight
This content explores the concept of Collective Intelligence (CI), its evolution, and its implications in the digital age. It highlights how the internet facilitates CI through digital collaboration and swarm intelligence, while also discussing its applications in team dynamics and global problem-solving. The findings suggest that CI can significantly enhance knowledge sharing and collaboration on a global scale. For anyone looking to leverage CI, understanding its foundations and applications is crucial.

**📖 Definition of Collective Intelligence**
- **Collective Intelligence Defined**: Collective Intelligence refers to the shared or group intelligence that emerges from the collaboration and competition of many individuals. It contrasts with individual intelligence, emphasizing the power of collective efforts.
- **Evolution of the Term**: The concept of CI has evolved significantly, particularly through the work of Pierre Lévy, who has contributed to its understanding in the context of digital culture and knowledge sharing.

**🌐 Internet as an Enabler of CI**
- **Digital Collaboration Platforms**: The internet serves as a crucial enabler of CI by providing platforms that facilitate collaboration among individuals, allowing for the pooling of knowledge and resources.
- **Swarm Intelligence Online**: Swarm intelligence, a form of CI, manifests in online spaces where groups can solve problems collectively, drawing on the diverse inputs of many participants.

**🔍 Applications of Collective Intelligence**
- **Small Team Dynamics**: CI can enhance small team dynamics by fostering collaboration and leveraging diverse perspectives, leading to more innovative solutions.
- **Large-Scale Problem Solving**: On a larger scale, CI enables effective online problem-solving, where vast numbers of individuals contribute to addressing complex challenges.

**⚖️ Implications of CI**
- **Technological Impact**: The rise of CI has significant technological implications, particularly in how knowledge is shared and disseminated across various platforms.
- **Global Collaboration Consequences**: CI has the potential to reshape global collaboration, creating new opportunities for collective action and problem-solving across borders.
