# McLuhan and Transhumanism: A Connection

Created at 2025/06/03 7:40 PM

## McLuhan's Insights on Identity
- McLuhan noted that in digital communication (telephone, radio, TV, internet), individuals become "discarnate beings" without a physical body.
- This lack of physicality leads to a different relationship with the world, merging identities at "the speed of light."
- He suggested that the electric age has deprived people of their private identity.

## Malleability of Digital Identity
- The malleability of digital identity is not matched in physical reality.
- This fluidity contributes to the concept of transhumanism, where human limitations are transcended through technology.

## McLuhan's Influence on Transhumanism
- Although McLuhan did not directly address transhumanism, his ideas foreshadowed its emergence.
- His thoughts on media altering perception and identity align with transhumanism's goals of enhancing human experience through technology.
- McLuhan's insights into media extending our senses and changing reality resonate with the aspirations of transhumanism.
