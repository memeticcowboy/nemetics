# Exploring Contextual Inquiry Techniques

Created at 2025/11/14 1:19 PM

## Starting with Contextual Inquiry
I feel a bit of anxiety about where to start with contextual inquiry techniques. It can be daunting, but I want to apply it effectively. 

## Suggested Approach
- Begin with a familiar digital tool or community.
- Observe a couple of users interacting with it.
- Conduct a short “think aloud” session to gain insights.
- Focus on comfort and curiosity over perfection.
