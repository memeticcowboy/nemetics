# Implementing Storytelling and Anonymous Feedback in Team Meetings

Created at 2025/08/18 8:59 AM

## Storytelling in Team Meetings
- Consider using "micro-stories" for sharing challenges or wins.
- Frame sharing as “learning moments” or “what surprised me this week” to lower defenses.
- Aim to create a space for emotion and context, not just facts.

## Anonymous Feedback Tools
- Utilize anonymous tools to encourage honest input from all team members.
- Combine real-time polls or digital sticky notes with storytelling to enhance feedback culture.

## Addressing Concerns About Storytelling
- Acknowledge that storytelling may not be comfortable for everyone.
- Offer storytelling as one option among several, such as role-play or anonymous sharing.
- Create multiple “entry points” for expression to accommodate different preferences.
