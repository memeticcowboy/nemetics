## Insight
This document outlines a comprehensive strategy for improving multimedia content engagement through innovative measurement, mobile enhancements, and video evolution. Key findings indicate that transitioning from Direct Page Views (DPVs) to Multimedia Content Views (MCVs) can provide a more accurate picture of client engagement. Additionally, a focus on mobile and podcast strategies has shown a significant increase in client retention. For best results, consider implementing these strategies to enhance your multimedia offerings.

**📊 Client Engagement Metrics**
- **Shift to Multimedia Content Views (MCVs)**: Replacing DPVs with MCVs allows for a more accurate measurement of client engagement by capturing every instance a client clicks play across various platforms and experiences.
- **User Testing Insights**: Results from user testing show that 41 out of 49 participants are likely to engage with multimedia content for business purposes, indicating a strong interest in enhanced multimedia offerings.
- **Retention Correlation**: Clients who engaged with at least one video retained their contracts more significantly than those who did not, highlighting the importance of multimedia in client retention.

**📱 Mobile and Podcast Strategy**
- **Mobile App Enhancements**: Upgrading the podcast experience on the mobile app to industry standards has led to a 21% increase in month-over-month repeat client usage.
- **Strategic Podcast Development**: Developing best practices for podcast content and enhancing discovery features can significantly improve client engagement and retention.
- **Teaser Content Strategy**: Testing teaser content on third-party platforms aims to drive clients to the Gartner mobile app, increasing engagement with full episodes.

**🎥 Video Content Evolution**
- **Structured Video Programming Stages**: Implementing a structured approach to video production, from planning to execution, has shown to improve content quality and viewer engagement.
- **Batch Recording Efficiency**: Leveraging recordings from conferences for video content creation is an efficient way to produce high-performing explainer videos.
- **New Video Playbook**: A comprehensive playbook for video content creators will provide essential tools and guidelines to enhance video quality and effectiveness.

**📈 Content Value Measurement**
- **Comprehensive Engagement Metrics**: Shifting from traditional metrics to more comprehensive methods allows for a better understanding of true client engagement and content value.
- **Improved Cost Comparability**: Enhancing the comparability of cost estimates by better accounting for the cost of intellectual property generation can lead to more informed decision-making.
- **Data Cleanup and Dashboard Creation**: Ongoing efforts to clean up data and create dashboards will facilitate better tracking and analysis of multimedia content performance.

**🚀 Innovative Content Delivery**
- **Repurposing Content Across Media**: Innovative strategies for content delivery, such as repurposing conference recordings, can maximize reach and impact across different media types.
- **Client-Exclusive Experiences**: Creating client-exclusive experiences through tailored content can enhance engagement and foster loyalty.
- **Leveraging High-Performing Content**: Identifying and leveraging evergreen content with extreme viewership can lead to the production of new, impactful video content.

**🔍 Understanding Demand & Economics**
- **Client Behavior Insights**: Supplementing engagement metrics with unmoderated user testing can provide valuable insights into what clients value from multimedia content.
- **Retention Impact Analysis**: Understanding the viewership and retention impact of multimedia content is critical for evolving video strategy and programming.
- **Client Journey Mapping**: Mapping the client journey to video content can reveal how clients access and interact with multimedia offerings.

**📅 Action Steps and Future Directions**
- **Client Surveys and Interviews**: Identifying and surveying clients will provide insights into their multimedia preferences and needs.
- **Podcast Experience Upgrades**: Upgrading the podcast experience on the mobile app is essential for meeting industry standards and enhancing client engagement.
- **Continuous Improvement of Content Quality**: Establishing proven best practices for content planning and quality will ensure ongoing improvements in multimedia offerings.
