## Insight
Let's delve into the digital marketing funnel and understand its various stages and strategies.

- The funnel is divided into five stages: Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Loyalty, and Advocacy.
- Awareness (Top of Funnel - TOFU) involves attracting viewers/visitors through online ads, storytelling posts, and SEO-optimized websites.
- Consideration (Middle of Funnel - MOFU) focuses on engaging website visitors and content readers with blogs, video tutorials, and customer stories.
- Conversion (Bottom of Funnel - BOFU) aims to turn leads into customers through lead magnets, free mini-courses, and sales offers.
- Loyalty and Advocacy stages involve retaining customers and turning them into brand advocates through loyalty offers, customer FAQs, and influencer marketing.

Consider tailoring your content and strategies to each stage of the funnel to maximize conversions and customer retention.
