### 🔟 Interesting Points about the View in Valletta, Malta:

1. **St. Paul's Pro-Cathedral (Right, with Spire)**<br>The tall spire belongs to St. Paul's Anglican Cathedral, built in the 19th century. Its spire dominates Valletta’s skyline and is often mistaken for a Catholic church.
2. **Basilica of Our Lady of Mount Carmel (Center-Left, with Dome)**<br>The large dome belongs to the Carmelite Church, one of Valletta’s most recognizable landmarks. It’s a modern reconstruction (1950s) of the original church destroyed in WWII.
3. **UNESCO World Heritage City**<br>Valletta is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It’s one of the most concentrated historic areas in the world, packed with palaces, churches, and fortifications.
4. **Baroque Architecture**<br>The city is a showcase of Baroque architecture, with the skyline lit at night enhancing the grandeur of buildings like these two churches.
5. **Harbor Fortifications (Foreground)**<br>The illuminated stone structures at the bottom are part of Valletta’s impressive harbor defenses built by the Knights of St. John in the 16th century.
6. **Strategic Location**<br>This view faces the Grand Harbour, one of the world’s finest natural harbors and a vital naval base for centuries.
7. **WWII History**<br>Valletta was heavily bombed during World War II. The dome of Our Lady of Mount Carmel was rebuilt after being destroyed during the air raids.
8. **Contrast of Faiths**<br>The juxtaposition of the Anglican cathedral and Catholic basilica in one skyline reflects Malta’s complex religious and colonial history.
9. **Nighttime Ambience**<br>The soft golden lighting is part of Valletta’s preservation efforts, ensuring historical buildings can be appreciated even after dark.
10. **Filming Location**<br>Valletta and its harbor have appeared in numerous films and shows, including *Gladiator*, *Assassin’s Creed*, and *Game of Thrones*.
