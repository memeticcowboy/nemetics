# Jazzing Up Your Brainstorming: The NEME Framework for Collaborative GameStorming

Created at 2024/07/13 4:27 PM

In the context of brainstorming, I've been exploring the concept of 'Jazzing Up Your Brainstorming' by integrating the NEME framework for Collaborative GameStorming. This approach emphasizes the importance of collaboration, narrative, emotion, metaphor, and embodiment in the brainstorming process.

## Importance of Collaboration, Narrative, Emotion, Metaphor, and Embodiment

- **Collaboration**: Encouraging teamwork and collective creativity to generate diverse ideas.
- **Narrative**: Viewing brainstorming as a dynamic, creative process where every idea contributes to a larger narrative.
- **Emotion**: Recognizing the emotional engagement and passion that can drive innovative thinking.
- **Metaphor**: Incorporating jazz-themed visuals and storytelling techniques to inspire innovative ideas.
- **Embodiment**: Embracing adaptability and the ability to improvise, mirroring the essence of jazz performance.

## The NEME Framework

The NEME framework consists of four key components:

1. **Notice**: Identifying emerging ideas or trends and swiftly capturing them.
2. **Engage**: Actively participating in idea generation and keeping the momentum going.
3. **Mull**: Reflecting on ideas to evaluate their potential for further development.
4. **Exchange**: Sharing and discussing ideas with the group to receive feedback and foster collaboration.
