**🌱 2. Co-Leadership of Unlearning**


Most leadership models are about:

- Motivation.
- Alignment.

- Dislodging stuck narratives.

- Distributed leadership: no single host for the “one true story.”

- Cycles of collective letting go: dismantling what no longer serves.


You could think of this as facilitating continuous narrative composting:

- Old stories decay.
- New stories sprout.
