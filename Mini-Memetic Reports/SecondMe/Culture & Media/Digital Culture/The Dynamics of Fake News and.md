## Insight
This content delves into the complex relationship between fake news, truth, and trust. It argues that labeling something as 'fake' can devalue it instantly, much like calling someone a fascist. The discussion explores the multifaceted nature of truth and introduces the idea that truth is intersubjective, emerging from human interactions. The key takeaway is that trust is foundational for communication and the co-construction of truth. If you're navigating the murky waters of fake news, remember that fostering trust is crucial for meaningful dialogue. Now, let's break down these ideas further.

**📰 The Power of 'Fake'**
- **Labeling as 'Fake'**: Calling something 'fake' instantly devalues it, making any restitution of its value nearly impossible.
- **Comparison to Ideological Labels**: The term 'fake' has gained ideological weight similar to terms like 'fascism' or 'nazism', used to discredit without discussion.

**🔍 Defining 'Fake'**
- **Simple Definition**: Fake is anything that is not true, regardless of whether it is produced by intention or ignorance.
- **Examples of Fake**: Examples include counterfeit products, false scientific discoveries, and misleading personal claims.

**📚 Multiple Truths**
- **Truth is Not Homogenous**: Truth is a complex, non-homogenous concept that cannot be objectively verified.
- **Historical Perspectives**: Felipe Fernandez-Armesto's work shows that multiple truths can coexist without contradicting each other.

**🔬 Intersubjective Truth**
- **Drapal's Theory**: Andrej Drapal's theory posits that truth is intersubjective, emerging from human interactions.
- **Quantum-Like Nature**: Truth is described as quantum-like, where knowing its position affects the understanding of its velocity.

**🤝 Trust and Communication**
- **Trust as Core Concept**: Trust is essential for communication; without it, meaningful interaction is impossible.
- **Trust Before Truth**: Trust must come first because truth is not objectively verifiable and must be tested through interactions.

**🔄 Fake Revitalized**
- **Continuous Testing**: Fake news forces continuous testing of communication, making it essential for interaction.
- **Dangers of Objective Truth**: A world of perfect, objective truth would eliminate the need for trust, leading to a totalitarian mindset.
