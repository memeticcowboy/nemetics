# NVIDIA's Fugatto: Revolutionizing Audio Creation with AI

Created at 2024/11/29 11:52 AM

NVIDIA's Fugatto is a versatile generative AI model that creates and transforms music, voices, and sounds based on text and audio prompts. It enables users to generate unique audio combinations, modify existing tracks by adding or removing instruments, and alter vocal attributes like accent and emotion. This tool is beneficial for music producers, advertisers, language educators, and game developers, offering innovative ways to enhance audio content.
