# Applying the EAST Framework in My Project

Created at 2025/05/13 6:33 PM

## Easy
- Identify friction points in the project.
- Streamline processes to enhance user experience.
- Simplify sign-up processes or improve navigation.

## Attractive
- Make the project visually appealing with vibrant colors and catchy graphics.
- Consider gamifying elements to increase engagement.
- Create an enjoyable experience for users.

## Social
- Leverage community power to foster a sense of belonging.
- Incorporate social proof, such as testimonials or user stories.
- Highlight that others are already engaged with the project.

## Timely
- Consider the best timing for audience engagement.
- Identify specific moments or life transitions relevant to the project.
- Use timely nudges or reminders to prompt action.
