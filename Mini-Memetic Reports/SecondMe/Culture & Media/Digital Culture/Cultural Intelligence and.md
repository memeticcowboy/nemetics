**🧠 The Disconnect in Leadership**
- **Words vs. Actions**: Many leaders' actions do not align with their words, causing more harm than good.
- **Alpha Personalities**: Corporate and political leaders often have strong self-motivation and are result-oriented, leading to unethical practices.
- **Outcome-Based World**: The competitive, outcome-based world can change leaders' initial values and lead to scandals.

**🌍 Future Leaders' Challenges**
- **Millennials and Gen Z**: These generations are witnessing leaders getting away with unethical practices while maintaining a clean image.
- **Impact on Future Leaders**: If this continues, future leaders may follow the same path, leading to further deterioration of ethical standards.
- **Need for Ethical Leadership**: Future leaders must prioritize ethics, honesty, and cultural understanding over mere profitability.

- **Cultural Intelligence**: Understanding different cultures and people is crucial for building bridges and conducting successful businesses.
- **Leveraging Technology**: Technology can help build cultural intelligence without the need for physical travel.
