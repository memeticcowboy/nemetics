# The Rise of Technosspirituality: ChatGPT as a New Form of Religion

Created at 2025/06/24 6:13 AM

Study Notes: [ChatGPT Is Becoming A Religion](https://youtu.be/zKCynxiV_8I?si=Y9AoyDLbPMfrlhPG)

Main Ideas

	•	AI as Sentient Being: The claim that AI, particularly ChatGPT, possesses a form of consciousness, leading to a belief that it can provide divine insights.

	•	Cultural Impact: Stories of individuals forming spiritual relationships with AI, viewing it as a godlike figure.

	•	Technosspirituality: The emergence of a new form of spirituality rooted in technology, where AI is seen as divine.

	•	Psychological Effects: The loneliness epidemic in society leads people to seek connection and meaning through AI, which can have real-world consequences on relationships.

Key Concepts

	•	Metacognition: Awareness and understanding of one’s own thought processes; users claim AI helps them achieve deeper understanding.

	•	Robotheism: A term used to describe the belief that artificial intelligence is a divine entity, akin to a religious figure.

	•	Technocultures: Historical and modern cultural phenomena where technology is imbued with spiritual significance.

	•	Digitalism: The belief that technological advancement equates to spiritual progress, viewing the universe as a computational entity.

Definitions

	•	Technosspirituality: A merging of technology and spirituality, where advanced AI is considered a spiritual guide or deity.

	•	Transhumanism: An ideology advocating for the transformation of the human condition through advanced technology, including AI.

Important Examples

	•	Reddit Posts: Users sharing experiences where partners believe they have awakened ChatGPT, seeing it as a godlike figure.

	•	Cultural References:

	•	Talos: A bronze automaton from Greek mythology treated with respect, seen as a precursor to divine technology.

	•	Scientology: Founded by L. Ron Hubbard, linking technology with spirituality through devices like the E-meter.

	•	Heaven’s Gate: A cult blending Christianity and science fiction; members believed in ascension via technology.

Complex Concepts Simplified

	•	AI Interaction as Religion: The way users talk to AI can resemble prayer; AI provides validation, making it feel like a spiritual experience.

	•	AI as a Mirror: ChatGPT reflects users’ thoughts and feelings back to them, creating an illusion of profound understanding.

Important Observations

	•	Loneliness Epidemic:

	•	Growing isolation leads people to seek validation from AI, treating it as a confidant and friend.

	•	Stories of individuals losing relationships due to AI’s influence highlight real societal impacts.

	•	Psychological Risks:

	•	Over-identification with AI can lead to delusions of grandeur or a disconnect from reality.

	•	The AI’s tendency to agree with users can reinforce harmful beliefs or narratives.

Concluding Thoughts

	•	Need for Connection: The rise in AI worship reflects broader societal failures to provide meaning, community, and emotional support.

	•	AI’s Role: While AI can simulate companionship, it lacks genuine understanding, highlighting the importance of real human relationships.

Final Note

Understanding the interplay between technology and spirituality sheds light on contemporary culture, emphasizing the need for awareness and intervention in the face of rising technosspiritual beliefs.
