**📜 Understanding the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)**

- **Overview of DMCA Provisions**: The DMCA was enacted to protect copyright holders in the digital environment, making it illegal to remove or alter copyright management information. The lawsuit against Meta centers on allegations that the company failed to comply with these provisions, raising concerns about accountability in the tech industry.

- **Legal Ramifications of DMCA Violations**: If the court finds that Meta did indeed violate the DMCA, the company could face significant penalties, including monetary damages and injunctions against further use of the infringing materials.
