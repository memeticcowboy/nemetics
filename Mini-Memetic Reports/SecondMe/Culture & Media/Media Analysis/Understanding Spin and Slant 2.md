**Operational Definition:**


Spin and slant are not just rhetorical tactics—they are memetic levers in the battle for public perception.

- Spin operates at the narrative level, restructuring reality through timing, framing, and diversion. It’s the art of narrative misdirection.
- Slant works at the sentence level, biasing detail without altering structure. It’s the craft of linguistic tilting.


Both embed affective-symbolic codes into communication—encoding ethics, urgency, and identity into transmissible patterns. Their power lies not in facts, but in the feeling of credibility they generate.
