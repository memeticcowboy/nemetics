The phrase is a quote by Paul Valéry, and its most common versions are "The trouble with our times is that the future is not what it used to be" and "The future, like everything else, is no longer quite what it used to be". The quote reflects a sense that rapid societal changes make it impossible to predict what is coming, breaking the traditional link between past experience and future expectation.
