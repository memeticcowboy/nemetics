PPP FORECASTING

[SUMMARY]
The text provides a quick introduction to futures thinking, discussing the concept of the Cone of Plausibility and different types of futures (plausible, possible, and preferable) that futurists consider.

[KEY POINTS]
•  Cone of Plausibility: 
  ◦  It is not a foresight method but a teaching tool.
  ◦  Describes the idea that the future is diverse, not singular.
  
•  Types of Futures:
  ◦  Plausible Futures:
    ▪︎  These are likely scenarios within the bounds of uncertainty and change.
  ◦  Possible Futures:
    ▪︎  Any outcome is conceivable, even if unlikely.
  ◦  Preferable Futures:
    ▪︎  Rooted in values and aspirations, indicating what is desired for the future.
    ▪︎  Serve as a guiding vision or a north star.
    ▪︎  Focus on how to adapt plans and create new solutions to move towards the envisioned future
