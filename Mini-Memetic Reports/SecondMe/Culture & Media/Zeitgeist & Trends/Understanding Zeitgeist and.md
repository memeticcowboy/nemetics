## Insight
Kirstine Fratz explores the concept of zeitgeist, emphasizing its role as a catalyst for cultural evolution and societal change. Unlike fleeting trends, zeitgeist reflects a collective yearning for innovation, particularly in business and mobility. This insightful discussion highlights the importance of human-centric values and sustainability in shaping our future. **🌍 Zeitgeist as Cultural Evolution**
- **Cultural Awakening**: Zeitgeist represents a collective cultural shift that emerges when traditional societal norms fail to meet contemporary needs, leading to new perspectives and transformations.
- **Distinction from Trends**: While trends are short-lived and surface-level, zeitgeist offers deeper insights into the cultural subconscious, driving long-term cultural evolution.
- **Response to Crises**: Recent global crises have heightened awareness of zeitgeist, prompting individuals to seek understanding of the underlying principles driving social change.

**💡 Human-Centric Values in Modern Business**
- **Shift from Profit to People**: Businesses are increasingly prioritizing human-centric approaches over linear profit models, reflecting a significant cultural transformation in economic activity.
- **Role of Spirit Makers**: Employees with a unique mindset, termed 'spirit makers,' are crucial in recognizing cultural shifts and integrating them into company practices for sustainable growth.
- **Cultural Evolution Over Trend Adaptation**: Engaging in meaningful discourse about cultural evolution is more beneficial for companies than merely adapting to transient trends.

**🚗 Mobility Transformation through Zeitgeist**
- **Evolving Mobility Aspirations**: The future of mobility is expected to be more diverse, with a focus on services rather than products, driven by societal demands for assistance and innovation.
- **Role of Technology**: Technological advancements, including AI and digital networking, are pivotal in reshaping mobility, suggesting a shift towards service-oriented solutions.
- **Continuous Innovation**: Electromobility is just the beginning; ongoing exploration of mobility solutions is essential to meet evolving societal needs and expectations.
