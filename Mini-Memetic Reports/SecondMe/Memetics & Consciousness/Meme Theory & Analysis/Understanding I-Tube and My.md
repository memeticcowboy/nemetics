# Understanding I-Tube and My-Stream in Memetic Ecology

Created at 2025/06/30 12:15 PM

## 🌿✨ **MEMETIC ECOLOGY LEXICON – EXTENSION**

### 🔹 **I-Tube**

**Definition:**<br>The interpretive conduit of perception—your *“I”-based filters* of belief, assumption, and narrative through which you make sense of experience.

**Rooted in Self-Referential Cognition:**

- “I think…”
- “I believe…”
- “I expect…”

**Function:**<br>Channels perception through conceptual and interpretive frameworks.

**Metaphoric Image:**<br>A semi-permeable Tube shaping and coloring the Stream of awareness.

***

### 🔹 **My-Stream**

**Definition:**<br>The flowing current of subjective perception—your *“my”-based phenomenology* of feeling, sensing, and experiencing as your own.

**Rooted in Immediate Subjectivity:**

- “My feeling is…”
- “My view is…”
- “My sense is…”

**Function:**<br>Carries the lived, embodied content of experience through the Tube.

**Metaphoric Image:**<br>A luminous Stream coursing within the I-Tube, animated by somatic and affective charge.

***

### ✨ **Why This Framing Matters**

✅ **Clear Split Between Interpretation and Perception**

- I-Tube = interpretive beliefs and assumptions.
- My-Stream = felt, pre-reflective experience.

✅ **Direct Connection to Language**

- “I think” signals cognitive interpretation.
- “My feeling” signals embodied experience.

✅ **Supports Metacognition**

- Helps individuals ask:
    - *Am I examining the Tube (how I interpret)?*
    - *Or observing the Stream (how it feels)?*

✅ **Aligns With Phenomenology**

- In phenomenology, “mine-ness” clarifies the first-person character of experience.

✅ **Aids Interventions**

- Soften the I-Tube: *Is that belief true?*
- Redirect the My-Stream: *Where is your attention now?*

✅ **Preserves the Luminous Ecology Metaphor**

- I-Tube = structure shaping perception.
- My-Stream = beam of luminous awareness.

***

### 🌀 **Known Overlaps and Limits**

❌ **Potential Overlap in Lived Experience**

- “I believe” and “I feel” often interweave into reinforcing loops:
    - *I believe → I feel → I believe…*

✅ **Solution:**<br>Treat them as **interwoven but distinguishable layers**, rather than fully separate.

❌ **Complex States**

- Awe or dread are often hybrid:
    - *I believe something vast is happening* + *I feel overwhelmed.*

✅ **Solution:**<br>Consider a **third term** for blended states (e.g., **I-My Currents**), while keeping the two main concepts for clarity.

***

### 🌿 **Practical Example**

**Situation:**<br>Anxious in social settings.

**I-Tube:**

- I believe people are judging me.
- I think I must perform perfectly.

**My-Stream:**

- My chest feels tight.
- My awareness is locked on micro-signals of rejection.

**Intervention:**

- **Soften I-Tube:** *What else could be true about others’ intentions?*
- **Redirect My-Stream:** *Can you feel your breath instead of scanning the room?*

***

### ✨ **TL;DR**

✅ **Does this framing make sense?**<br>Yes—strongly.<br>It clarifies the layered relationship between interpretation and felt perception.

✅ **Does it break down?**<br>Only in the sense that most experience is hybrid—acknowledge the entanglement.

✅ **Why use it?**

- Adds precision to language and intervention.
- Honors the layered nature of perception.
- Respects the ecology of how we make meaning and feel meaning.
