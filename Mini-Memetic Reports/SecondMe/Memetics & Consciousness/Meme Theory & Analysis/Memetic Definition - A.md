- **Memetic Definition:** A liberatory meme—a thread that increases volitional coherence, agency, curiosity, and relational openness.

- **Philosophical Core:** This is **[Friedrich Nietzsche's "will to power"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche/)** rendered constructive. A lumeme is an **affirmation of life** (Deleuze) that expands the **field of possible action** (William James). It is **[Michel Foucault's "technologies of the self"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/foucault/)** used **against** the Meme-Grid: practices that increase **cognitive liberty** rather than capture.

- **In the Essay:** Lumemes are what the Cowboy's protocol produces. They are the threads that **survive friction** in the Co-Sphere, that **reopen the gap** in a completed I-Tube. The essay's entire therapeutic arc is a **lumemic hygiene** practice: defragment the I-Tube, stress-test in the We-Sphere, archive the gap. The goal is **resilience, not certainty**.
