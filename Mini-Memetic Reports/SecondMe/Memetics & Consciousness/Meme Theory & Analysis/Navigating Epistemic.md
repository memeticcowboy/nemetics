The choice of habitats indicates an obligation to preserve ecological balance in knowledge production and dissemination.

* The inclusion of different natural layers (soil, water, tree canopies) represents the interplay of various epistemological frameworks, emphasizing that knowledge is not a static entity. This metaphor resonates with the METAL perspective on the structures that support reliable understanding while remaining flexible.

* Overall, the representation invites contemplation on how various epistemologies can enrich each other while avoiding the pitfalls of a single narrative claiming supremacy, which could lead to epistemic rigidity or collapse under the pressure of exclusive validation.
