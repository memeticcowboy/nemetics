# From Knowledge Management (KM) to Knowledge Wrangling (KW)

Created at 2025/06/11 6:53 AM

This is essentially the shift from a mechanistic to an organismic or ecological view of knowledge within organizations. Let’s map this concept through several interpretive lenses, including #SystemsThinking, #MemeticPhilosophy, and the #SixCyborgs framework, with an eye toward applications in living organizations and interdisciplinary learning ecosystems.

**🔄** 

**From Static to Dynamic: Knowledge as a Living Entity**

**📚 Knowledge Management (KM)**

- Ontology: Knowledge as artifact
- Mechanism: Capture, categorize, distribute
- Epistemology: Objective, explicit, context-independent
- Metaphor: Library or database
- Toolset: Taxonomies, intranets, document management systems

**🌀 Knowledge Wrangling (KW)**

- Ontology: Knowledge as a living, evolving organism
- Mechanism: Dialogue, feedback loops, emergent collaboration
- Epistemology: Situated, tacit, context-rich
- Metaphor: Ecosystem or neural network
- Toolset: Sensemaking practices, storytelling, memetic mapping

**🧠 #SixCyborgs Application**

**1.**  **Analyzer (Data→Insight)**

KW treats data as fluid. It encourages pattern recognition over rigid categorization. Example: Using social network analysis to identify idea propagation.

**2.** **Strategist (System View)**

KW aligns with complex adaptive systems—where knowledge behaves more like weather than like a ledger. It considers nodes of intelligence (people, platforms) and how they co-evolve.

**3.**  **Creator (Ideation)**

KW enables cross-pollination and creative tension. It values idea mutation over preservation. Think: meme incubation spaces, digital salons, collaborative whiteboards.

**4.**  **Protector (Risk Management)**

KM defends against loss; KW guards against stagnation. Knowledge that doesn’t move risks becoming brittle. Thus, KW enables epistemic resilience.

**5.** **Optimizer (Improvement Loops)**

KW fosters continuous reinvention. Rather than “managing” knowledge for efficiency, it optimizes the system’s capacity to learn and unlearn.

**6.**  **Empath (Culture & Meaning)**

KW centers humans-in-the-loop, valuing emotional resonance, cultural nuance, and psychological safety. It builds cognitive intimacy, not just information accessibility.

**🔁 Knowledge as a Memetic Organism**

Drawing on Memetics and SMART memes principles :

- Knowledge wrangling thrives on memeplex evolution: clusters of ideas that adapt, recombine, and compete in an organization’s cognitive environment.
- Ideas must meet thresholds of persistence, propagation, and impact to stay alive .
- Memes don’t just live in documents—they live in culture, rituals, linguistic shorthand, and design affordances.

This approach transforms an organization from a “knowledge warehouse” into a knowledge ecosystem.

**🏢 Application to Organizations as Living Systems**

**📈 Organizational Implications**

- From storage to flow: Prioritize conversational infrastructures over repositories.
- From control to curation: Equip teams to tend and cultivate knowledge.
- From expertise to learning agility: Reward idea evolution, not just idea ownership.
- From silos to synapses: Enable horizontal dialogue and interdisciplinary interfaces.

**🧰 Tactical Moves**

- Design feedback-rich digital workspaces (like Miro + Notion + Slack integrations).
- Facilitate cross-functional idea collisions via Open Space or Cynefin-inspired sessions.
- Implement Knowledge Flow Mapping using meme-tracking or attention heatmaps.
- Use AI-assisted pattern detection to identify and amplify emergent memes.

**🏛️ Recursive Ontology: The Hearth Metaphor**

Organizations as living entities are hearths—recursive nodes of cognitive warmth where ideas are kindled, exchanged, and reshaped. From this lens:

- Knowledge is not a noun (a thing) but a verb (an action).
- We aren’t just knowledge managers; we’re cognitive stewards and idea gardeners.
- The hearth thrives on meaningful tension and shared rituals.
