# The Role of DSRP and Cyborgs in Problem-Solving

Created at 2024/09/24 9:12 PM

## DSRP Framework
- **Distinctions**: Helps define the problem by identifying boundaries and differences.
- **Systems**: Focuses on understanding the big picture and how different parts fit together.
- **Relationships**: Examines connections and underlying causes, revealing how changes impact other areas.
- **Perspectives**: Encourages looking at the problem from multiple angles to uncover biases and consider alternative solutions.

## Cyborgs and Their Roles
- **Analyzer Cyborg**: Excels in the Distinctions stage by gathering data and clarifying issues.
- **Strategist Cyborg**: Leads in the Systems stage, developing frameworks for understanding complex interactions.
- **Empath Cyborg**: Shines in the Relationships stage, using emotional intelligence to foster understanding among stakeholders.
- **Optimizer Cyborg**: Dominates the Perspectives stage, promoting open dialogue and ethical communication to explore various viewpoints.

Each cyborg enhances different aspects of the DSRP framework, contributing to effective problem-solving.
