## Best Practices for Organizing Notes in Google Docs
- Use clear, consistent headings for structure.
- Add a table of contents for easy navigation.
- Color-code or highlight key sections to emphasize important information.
- Include brief context or purpose notes at the top of each section.
- Regularly revisit and update notes to keep them relevant and actionable.
- Maintain a simple, clean layout to enhance readability and focus.
