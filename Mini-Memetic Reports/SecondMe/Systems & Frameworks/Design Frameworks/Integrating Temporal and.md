## Key Alignment Analysis
### Head = Sky = Past = Simple/Clear
- **Conceptual Coherence**: Historical knowledge provides established patterns and known solutions
- **Decision Logic**: Past experiences allow for categorize-then-respond approaches
- **Cognitive Foundation**: Represents accumulated wisdom that has been processed and understood
### Heart = Earth = Present = Complicated
- **Operational Significance**: Present challenges require expert analysis of known unknowns
- **Practice Application**: Current situations demand methodical investigation of cause-effect relationships
- **Experiential Focus**: Represents active engagement with tangible, analyzable realities
### Hands = Sea = Future = Complex to Chaotic
- **Strategic Implication**: Future possibilities exist in emergent, unpredictable states
- **Adaptability Requirement**: Forward movement needs probe-sense-respond or act-sense-respond approaches
- **Action Orientation**: Represents the fluid, evolving nature of potential futures
