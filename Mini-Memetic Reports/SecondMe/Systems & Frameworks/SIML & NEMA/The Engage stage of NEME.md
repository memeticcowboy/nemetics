## The Engage stage of NEME

The Engage stage, represented by the letter "E" in the NEME cycle, is the second stage in the four-part process of Nemetics. It follows the Notice stage and precedes the Mull stage in the cycle. This stage represents the "out-breath", bringing male energy into play to develop understanding about a Neme.

During the Engage stage, one seeks to discern and discriminate, checking and extracting information from what was observed in the Notice stage [3, 4]. The Engage stage involves examining the possibilities of future complexities and emergences that may result from what was observed. It is a search for "actionable levers" for change and the “triggers” to initiate adaptation and new growth cycles.

One of the sources describes the Engage stage as an abstraction of what was noticed in the previous stage, separating "the necessary from the unnecessary" and observing changes in quantities, qualities, and their interactions. The source uses the analogy of identifying the right components and their interactions with the environment to understand their role in a system's emergence.

Just as breathing involves both inhalation and exhalation, the Engage stage is a necessary complement to the Notice stage.
