## The Mull stage of NEME

The Mull stage, represented by the letter "M" in the NEME cycle, is the third stage in the four-part process of Nemetics. The Mull stage follows the Notice and Engage stages, and precedes the Exchange stage. The Mill stage is characterized by a reflective awareness of experiences and sensations derived from the Notice and Engage stages. During the Mull stage, one listens to and feels the feedback from those earlier stages. Mull can be understood as the feminine force of reflection that connects patterns in preparation for the Exchange stage.

In the Mull stage, one apprehends the meaning of relationships between memes and lumenes by listening to their echoes in the body, mind, and spirit. Mull may constrain or expand the NemiTube to either allow or resist an exchange. Mull desires to articulate the Exchange using the signs of reality within the mind. 

The Mull stage is also associated with feelings and the expression of intention and will. It brings into play all elements of creation: new relationships where new feelings replace the old, new insights and thoughts along with the necessary will and intention. This leads to the communication of a new set of thoughts and feelings that replace old, entangled NemeStrings (relationships), thereby bringing about changes in the collective consciousness.
