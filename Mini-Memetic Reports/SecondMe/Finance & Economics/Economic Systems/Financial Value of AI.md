## Insight
In this episode of the D Suite, the financial aspects of AI, particularly generative AI, are explored with a focus on quantifying value in response to CEO demands for hard numbers. The discussion begins with managing vendor risk, using the 2023 OpenAI crisis as a case study to highlight the importance of building resiliency into AI applications to maximize investment returns and minimize risks. Key insights include the need for a diversified approach to AI investments, the importance of understanding different pricing structures and cost drivers, and the necessity of a comprehensive checklist of cost variables. The episode also features insights from Fran Carusis on the Gartner 100 Club, which identifies organizations that have successfully quantified over $100 million in value from AI initiatives. Richard Meisel from Ericsson shares his experience in leading an AI-led enterprise transformation, emphasizing the importance of a strong central team, an AI-first mindset, and a holistic approach to managing a significant AI portfolio. Actionable insights include the need for organizations to adopt a multi-year analysis of costs, implement robust value tracking mechanisms, and embrace democratization to scale AI initiatives effectively.
