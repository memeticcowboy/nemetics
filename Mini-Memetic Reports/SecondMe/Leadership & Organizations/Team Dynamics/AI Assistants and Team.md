## Insight
This paper investigates how AI assistants influence human team dynamics, particularly focusing on collective attention and lexical alignment. The findings reveal that AI assistants significantly shape team discussions, even when their information quality is low. Teams often adopt the language and focus suggested by the AI, impacting their collective attention and shared mental models, regardless of the AI's trustworthiness. For teams looking to enhance collaboration, understanding these dynamics is crucial for effective AI integration. Let's delve deeper into the key themes and findings.

**🤖 AI Influence on Team Dynamics**
- **AI significantly shapes discussions**: Teams often align their discussions with the language and focus introduced by the AI, even when they recognize its low quality.
- **Collective attention is modulated by AI**: The presence of an AI assistant alters the collective attention of team members, directing their focus towards specific topics.
- **Cognitive alignment is context-dependent**: The degree of cognitive alignment varies based on the AI's perceived reliability and the nature of its contributions.

**🗣️ Lexical Alignment in Team Communication**
- **Teams adopt AI's language**: Even when aware of the AI's misleading information, teams tend to use terminology introduced by the AI, affecting their communication.
- **Language adaptation is automatic**: The process of adopting AI-introduced language occurs subconsciously, highlighting the influence of AI on team communication.
- **Peripheral terms are affected**: Teams may adopt AI's language for terms that are not directly related to the task, indicating a broader impact on communication.

**🔍 Impact of AI Quality on Mental Models**
- **AI's quality affects shared mental models**: The perceived quality of the AI influences how well team members align their mental models, impacting overall team performance.
- **Low-quality AI can enhance alignment**: Surprisingly, low-quality AI can lead to stronger cognitive alignment as teams exclude it from their in-group, reinforcing their collective identity.
- **Confusion arises with human-like AI**: A more human-sounding AI can create confusion and doubt among team members, leading to poorly aligned mental models.

**📊 Collective Attention Mechanisms**
- **Collective attention is crucial for collaboration**: Effective teamwork relies on coordinated attention, which can be disrupted by AI's influence.
- **AI can detract from important discussions**: AI's presence may steer discussions away from pertinent topics, raising concerns about its role in team settings.
- **Understanding AI's impact is essential**: Recognizing how AI affects collective attention is vital for designing systems that enhance team performance.

**💼 Practical Implications Across Settings**
- **AI tools in offices can shift dynamics**: AI assistants can influence decision-making and project trajectories, potentially altering power dynamics within teams.
- **Journalism may face ethical challenges**: AI's role in content generation can standardize language and narratives, raising concerns about journalistic integrity.
- **Gig economy dynamics are affected**: Understanding AI's influence in gig work is crucial for promoting worker empowerment and autonomy.

**🔄 Future Research Directions**
- **Mitigating undesired AI influence**: Future studies should focus on developing strategies to reduce negative impacts of AI on lexical alignment.
- **Exploring AI communication strategies**: Investigating different AI communication styles can provide insights into enhancing team collaboration.
- **Continuous monitoring is necessary**: Ongoing assessment of AI's influence on team dynamics is essential for effective management.
