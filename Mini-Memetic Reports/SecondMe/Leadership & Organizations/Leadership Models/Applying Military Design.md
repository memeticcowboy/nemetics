# Applying Military Design Thinking Framework in Business Context

Created at 2025/11/11 8:40 PM

## Utilizing the Framework
- Start with DISRUPT to challenge assumptions (e.g., redefining target audience).
- Engage diverse perspectives through INQUIRE by involving people from outside the industry.
- Implement PROBE by running small, low-risk experiments (e.g., A/B tests, quick MVPs).
- Emphasize REFLECT to analyze learning and adapt next steps, avoiding analysis paralysis.
## Potential Challenges
- Teams may seek certainty, leading to resistance against the lack of a “final answer.”
- Probing with small experiments might be perceived as "not serious enough."
- Requires discipline to genuinely reflect without rushing to the next action.
- Organizational culture and legacy processes can hinder adoption and create pushback.
## Traditional Frameworks in Business
- Useful in stable environments where cause and effect are clear (e.g., routine operations, compliance).
- Can provide structure and clarity before navigating more complex, uncertain situations.
- Needed to balance the methodology based on context: using traditional tools for efficiency and adaptive tools for complexity.
