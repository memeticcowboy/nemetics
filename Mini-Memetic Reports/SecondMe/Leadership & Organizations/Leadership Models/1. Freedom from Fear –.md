### 1. **Freedom from Fear** – Protector Cyborg

- **Maslow’s Level**: **Safety Needs**
    - Protects individuals from violence, oppression, and threats, ensuring personal security and stability.

### 2. **Freedom of Expression** – Optimizer Cyborg

- **Maslow’s Level**: **Esteem Needs**
    - Supports individuals in expressing their thoughts, opinions, and creativity, leading to respect, recognition, and self-esteem.

### 3. **Freedom of Peace** – Strategist Cyborg

- **Maslow’s Level**: **Safety Needs**
    - Ensures societal and collective peace, fostering stability and long-term safety, providing the necessary foundation for growth.

### 4. **Freedom of Community** – Empath Cyborg

- **Maslow’s Levels**: **Belongingness and Love Needs**
    - Ensures the right to form and participate in meaningful communities, fostering emotional connections, belonging, and collective well-being.

### 5. **Freedom from Want** – Analyzer Cyborg

- **Maslow’s Levels**: **Physiological Needs** and **Safety Needs**
    - Addresses material security, ensuring access to basic necessities like food, shelter, and healthcare, which are foundational for survival.

### 6. **Freedom to Grow** – Creator Cyborg

- **Maslow’s Levels**: **Esteem Needs** and **Self-Actualization**
    - Encourages continuous learning and creative expression, helping individuals reach their full potential and pursue personal growth, mastery, and fulfillment.
