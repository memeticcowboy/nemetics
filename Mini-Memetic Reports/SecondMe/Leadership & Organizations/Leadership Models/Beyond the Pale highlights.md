"Beyond the Pale" highlights the **importance of considering a range of possible outcomes when developing strategies, particularly in complex and chaotic systems.** The author argues that **traditional military planning methods, such as NATO-OPP and JPP, focus on single-state future strategies, which are inadequate for dealing with the unpredictability of complex systems.**12 Instead, the author advocates for a shift toward a **systemic approach that generates and maintains multiple futures.**3

Several reasons are given for why strategies should include a range of possible outcomes:

●

**Complex and chaotic systems are inherently unpredictable.**2 Attempting to predict a single outcome in such systems is likely to be futile.

●

**Systematic planning that relies on linear, cause-and-effect reasoning is ineffective in complex systems.**2 These systems are characterized by emergence and nonlinearity, where small causes can have large effects and outcomes are often unpredictable.

●

**Focusing on a single desired outcome can lead to confirmation bias and a failure to consider alternative possibilities.**4

●

**Generating multiple futures creates cognitive "maneuver space" for decision-makers, allowing them to consider a wider range of potential actions and responses.**4

●

**Scenario Planning:** This method involves developing multiple, divergent future scenarios that explore a range of possibilities.5 The author highlights the work of Pierre Wack and his team at Shell, who pioneered the use of scenario planning to prepare for the unpredictable future of the oil industry.678

**The Semiotic Square:** This heuristic device can be used to generate multiple futures by considering the opposite and negation of an initial concept.9 The author describes a "Z-method" that uses the semiotic square to help design teams break free from their traditional, single-future thinking.9

●

**Deleuzian Folds:** This concept can be used to visualize the interconnectedness and complexity of systems.10 The author suggests that Deleuzian Folds can help strategists move away from traditional, linear models and embrace a more fluid and dynamic understanding of reality.11
