AI job disruption:

Generative AI offers pre-made solutions (like cookies) instead of recipes
This reduces control over the process and quality of the output ⚠️
Users (like businesses) need to test generative AI outputs themselves
Some tasks (repetitive, digitized) are more susceptible to automation by AI ➡️
Difficult skills like creativity and problem-solving are still valuable
A challenge exists in training future leaders because their initial tasks might be automated
We need to develop talent with skills that are not easily automated (judgment, expertise) ‍
Businesses automating with generative AI need to focus on data quality, testing, and developing talent
