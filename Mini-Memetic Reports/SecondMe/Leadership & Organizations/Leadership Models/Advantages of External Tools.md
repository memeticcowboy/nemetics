# Advantages of External Tools Over Implants

Created at 2025/05/02 3:46 PM

## Flexibility
- External tools can be upgraded or swapped out without surgery.
- Less hassle compared to permanent implants.

## Safety
- No surgical risks associated with external devices.
- Easier to replace or turn off malfunctioning tools.

## Adaptability
- External tools can evolve quickly with tech advancements.
- Allows for trying new gadgets without permanent changes.

## Identity and Autonomy
- Maintains a clearer sense of self by keeping tech external.
- Users can choose when to engage with technology.

## Cost and Accessibility
- Generally more affordable and accessible than implants.
- Wider availability of smartphones and wearable tech compared to high-tech implants.
