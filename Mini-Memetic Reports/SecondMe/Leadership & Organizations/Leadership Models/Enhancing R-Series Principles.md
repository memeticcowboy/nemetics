# Enhancing R-Series Principles with Purpose, Principles, and Priorities for Strategic Leadership

Created at 2025/12/22 1:02 PM

The best 3P triad to extend and enhance the relevance of your R-series principles (Respect, Roles, Responsibilities, Relationships, Reliability, Resonance) is Purpose, Principles, Priorities.<br>Why This 3P Fits Best<br>This triad, popularized in strategic leadership models (e.g., Robbie Bach’s framework for corporate and personal renewal), provides a foundational, actionable layer that elevates the operational and interpersonal focus of the Rs into a more strategic, meaningful, and sustainable structure.<br>	•	Purpose — Gives overarching “why” and direction, infusing Respect and Relationships with deeper meaning. It ensures Roles and Responsibilities align with a larger vision, creating emotional Resonance and long-term Reliability.<br>	•	Principles — Serves as the ethical and value-based guardrails that directly reinforce and expand the core idea of Respect. Principles guide consistent behavior across Roles, Responsibilities, Relationships, and foster trustworthy Reliability.<br>	•	Priorities — Focuses effort and resources, preventing dilution of the Rs. It ensures Resonance by concentrating on what truly matters, making Relationships more intentional and Responsibilities more impactful.<br>Together, this 3P turns the descriptive Rs into a dynamic, integrated system: The Rs describe how we interact and operate day-to-day, while Purpose-Principles-Priorities provide the strategic intent that makes them enduring and relevant in personal development, leadership, teams, or organizations.<br>How It Integrates with RE5P3CT<br>The stylized “RE5P3CT” cleverly highlights ~5-6 core Rs while nodding to the 3P layer—blending them into a memorable acronym for a holistic framework (e.g., the Rs for relational execution + 3P for strategic foundation).<br>Strong Alternatives (If Context Shifts)<br>	•	People, Process, Purpose → Emphasizes human-centered execution (strong tie to Relationships and Respect), but less directly elevates the Rs strategically.<br>	•	Personalization, Permanence, Pervasiveness (Seligman’s resilience 3Ps) → Great for adding psychological depth to Resilience (a natural R-extension), sustaining Reliability and Resonance under stress.<br>Purpose, Principles, Priorities stands out as the most empowering extension—making the R-principles not just admirable, but practically transformative.<br>If your framework has a specific domain (e.g., indigenous values, positive psychology, corporate leadership), another 3P might edge it out—share more for finer tuning!<br>
