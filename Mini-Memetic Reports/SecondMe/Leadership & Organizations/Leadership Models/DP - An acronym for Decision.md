DP: An acronym for Decision Intelligence (DI) principles could be PRISM, symbolizing clarity, focus, and multifaceted decision-making. Here’s how it breaks down:

PRISM Framework

	1.	P - Parsimony

	•	Keep decision models as simple as possible without losing their effectiveness.

	•	Focus on clarity, avoiding overengineering, and streamlining workflows.

	2.	R - Responsibility

	•	Empower those closest to the decision to own and manage decision models while ensuring accountability.

	•	Emphasize ethical AI practices and regulatory compliance.

	3.	I - Iteration

	•	Continuously refine decision processes through feedback loops and monitoring.

	•	Use observability to track performance, enabling constant improvement.

	4.	S - Scalability

	•	Design decision frameworks to adapt and grow with organizational needs.

	•	Incorporate composable architectures and scalable tools to handle increasing complexity.

	5.	M - Measurability

	•	Ensure that decisions and their outcomes can be monitored, quantified, and evaluated.

	•	Leverage KPIs and metrics to assess impact and improve future decisions.

Why PRISM Works:

	•	Metaphorically, a prism takes in light and refracts it into its component parts, similar to how DI breaks down complexity to deliver clarity.

	•	Practically, the acronym emphasizes core principles critical for building and sustaining effective decision-making systems.
