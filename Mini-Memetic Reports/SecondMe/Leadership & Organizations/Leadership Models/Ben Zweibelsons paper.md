Ben Zweibelson's paper highlights the need for new paradigms in space warfare, breaking free from terrestrial strategies like land, air, and sea. The space domain presents unique challenges requiring systemic thinking, blending technologies like AI and autonomous systems with ethical considerations for security.

### Top Three Themes:
1. **Reconceptualizing Space Warfare:** The inadequacy of terrestrial war frameworks for the space domain.
2. **Systemic and Ethical Challenges:** Integrating AI, human-machine teaming, and moral questions into space security.
3. **Unique Properties of Space:** Space’s supra-global nature demands innovative strategies distinct from historical precedents.

### Top Eight Keywords:
- Space warfare  
- Systemic thinking  
- Supra-global domain  
- Autonomous systems  
- AI integration  
- Ethical considerations  
- Space security  
- Human-machine teaming
