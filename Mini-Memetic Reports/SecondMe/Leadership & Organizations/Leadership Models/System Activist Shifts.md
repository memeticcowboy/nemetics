# System Activist Shifts

Created at 2024/06/03 5:42 PM

The key shifts associated with the system activist role - around collaboration, learning, diversity, mindset and balance - are all highly relevant considerations when designing and implementing collaborative decision making systems with AI. The core challenge is often not just technical, but human and organizational - transforming the way we think, behave and interact to fully harness the potential of human-machine collaboration.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​

## Insight
The concept of a 'system activist' is about mobilizing change within an organization by shifting from traditional leadership roles to more activist-oriented approaches. The key outcomes include improved collaboration, learning, and diversity, which are crucial for implementing AI-driven decision-making systems. The main challenge lies in transforming human and organizational behaviors to fully leverage human-machine collaboration. As an assistant, I recommend focusing on fostering a collaborative mindset and being open to learning and adapting. Now, let's delve into the seven key shifts that define a system activist.

**🌐 From Organizations to Outcomes**
- **Collaborating beyond roles**: System activists work beyond their formal roles to improve overall outcomes, emphasizing the importance of collaboration across different parts of the organization.

**🚀 From Management to Mobilization**
- **Building informal authority**: Instead of relying on formal power, system activists build informal authority to influence and drive action within the organization.

**🤝 From Me to We**
- **Recognizing limited perspectives**: System activists understand that their own perspective is partial and limited, and they value the input and collaboration of others.

**🔍 From Expert to Explorer**
- **Adopting a learning approach**: System activists guide others through uncertainty by adopting a learning mindset, exploring new possibilities and solutions.

**🤲 From Delivery to Co-creation**
- **Harnessing disagreement productively**: They use disagreements as opportunities for productive discussions and distribute power to co-create solutions.

**🔄 From Expectations to Agency**
- **Changing old patterns**: System activists recognize and change patterns and behaviors that have sustained outdated ways of working.

**❤️ From Head to Heart**
- **Personal transformation**: They acknowledge that they are part of the system they are trying to change and must also change themselves.
