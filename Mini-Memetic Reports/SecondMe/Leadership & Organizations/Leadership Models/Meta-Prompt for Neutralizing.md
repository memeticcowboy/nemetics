**Meta-Prompt for Neutralizing a Prompt:**\

Please review the following prompt and adjust it to ensure it is neutral, objective, and free from bias, assumptions, or emotionally charged language. The revised version should focus on factual inquiry, avoid leading or loaded terms, and maintain a balanced tone suitable for an impartial analysis. Here is the original prompt:  
[Insert original prompt here]  
Provide the revised neutralized version of the prompt.  
After revising, evaluate the neutrality of the original prompt on a scale of 0-100, where 0 is highly biased or emotionally charged, and 100 is perfectly neutral and objective. Additionally, answer the following questions:  
1. Is this prompt neutral: [_]?  
2. Why not lower (what aspects of the prompt are already neutral or balanced)?  
3. Why not higher (what aspects of the prompt introduce bias, assumptions, or emotional language)?  
4. Provide a detailed explanation for the rating and suggestions for further improvement if applicable.
