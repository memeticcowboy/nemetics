LinkedIn is not a mystery. 

https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nstrvs_linkedin-is-not-a-mystery-master-these-activity-7199396441478340609-ydxO

Master these 3 key aspects and you'll grow like crazy.

1) Personal brand

Dos:

⟶ Position your brand as a journey
⟶ Share your evolving story
⟶ Explore new ideas in public
⟶ Be transparent 
⟶ Allow your audience to get to know you as a whole person

Don'ts:

⟶ Don't focus solely on self-promotion
⟶ Don't lock your brand identity to your current services
⟶ Don't be afraid to show vulnerability
⟶ Don't underestimate the importance of consistency

2) Content creation

⟶ Monitor engagement metrics.
⟶ Assess if your content aligns with biz goals.
⟶ Study what's working for competitors.
⟶ Determine where your content is in its lifecycle.
⟶ Stay on top of emerging trends.
⟶ Continuously test new content angles.

3) Networking

⟶ Actively listen to others' goals
⟶ Appreciate others' unique situations and perspectives.
⟶ Help others achieve their objectives.
⟶ Know your strengths.
⟶ Share knowledge, make connections.
⟶ Don't just transact. Build authentic relationships.
⟶ Help others in your network develop and advance their goals.
⟶ Create an ecosystem of mutual support.

Turn LinkedIn into a growth machine.
