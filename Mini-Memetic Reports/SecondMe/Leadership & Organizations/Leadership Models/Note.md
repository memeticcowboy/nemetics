**Title:** Labor-Assisting Technologies: Opportunities, Creativity, and Ethics

**1. Overview**  
- Labor-assisting tech enhances human abilities instead of replacing workers.  
- This approach contrasts with labor-automation, which often focuses on cost-cutting and workforce reduction.

**2. Examples**  
- Healthcare: AI decision support helps doctors interpret data, improving accuracy while keeping human judgment central.  
- Education: AI tools personalize learning, freeing teachers to focus on creativity and connection.  
- Manufacturing: Collaborative robots (“cobots”) handle repetitive tasks, letting humans focus on problem-solving.

**3. Creativity**  
- AI as a creative partner sparks new ideas and perspectives.  
- Human-AI collaboration in writing and brainstorming leads to richer, more diverse results.

**4. Ethics**  
- Raises questions about power, privacy, accountability, and what counts as creativity or skill.  
- Ethical frameworks are needed to ensure these tools empower people, not just optimize efficiency.

**5. Reflection**  
- The most promising tech fosters partnership, creativity, and empowerment.  
- Ongoing ethical exploration is essential for responsible development.
