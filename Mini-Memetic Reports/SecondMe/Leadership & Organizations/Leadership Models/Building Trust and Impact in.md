# Building Trust and Impact in Data Leadership

Created at 2024/09/04 11:05 AM

This episode of the D-Suite podcast explores how data and analytics (D&A) leaders can gain trust from the C-suite and drive impactful results. Key challenges, such as skills shortages (reported by 45% of respondents in Gartner’s CDO Survey), are highlighted, making talent retention and purpose-driven culture essential for success. Distinguished VP Analyst Deb Logan shares strategies for building an inclusive, mission-driven D&A culture through leadership and "culture hacks." Rachel Porteous, CIO at FINTRAC, discusses how she has used data to address issues like human trafficking and money laundering, earned the CEO's trust, and retained top talent by promoting a purpose-driven culture. She also previews FINTRAC's upcoming Generative AI initiatives.

## Insight
This episode of the D-Suite podcast delves into the critical role of data and analytics (D&A) leaders in fostering a purpose-driven culture to retain top talent and gain the trust of the C-suite. Hosted by Rita Salem, the podcast features insights from distinguished VP Analyst Deb Logan and Rachel Porteous, CIO at FINTRAC. The discussion highlights the importance of connecting D&A work to a meaningful mission, as evidenced by FINTRAC's efforts in combating human trafficking and money laundering. Key strategies include creating a clear mission statement, understanding team motivations, and implementing 'culture hacks' to drive a data-driven culture. The episode also explores the use of generative AI in advancing organizational goals and the significance of psychological safety in retaining talent. Actionable insights and future steps are provided to help D&A leaders build an inclusive and impactful culture.
