# Key Factors Influencing Geopolitical Predictions

Created at 2025/06/26 7:23 AM

[via Bob-RJ](https://x.com/burkhartrj/status/1938192949365412183?s=46&t=7Z-E-ACnGlzdI-iyUwNCrQ)

## Factors That Could Change Predictions

- Unexpected economic shocks
- Shifts in public opinion
- Major diplomatic breakthroughs
- Changes in strategy or leadership among key players (U.S., Iran, Israel, allies)

## Potential Iranian Response to U.S. Withdrawal

- Framing the withdrawal as a victory to boost national unity
- Increased regional influence
- Preparation for new power struggles and potential overreach

## Concerns About U.S. Military Actions

- Not all parties truly benefit from U.S. military actions
- Risks of unintended blowback and internal fractures
- Long-term instability may outweigh short-term gains
