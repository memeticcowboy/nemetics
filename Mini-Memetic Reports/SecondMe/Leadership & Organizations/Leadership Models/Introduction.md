**Introduction**

- General [Paul Nakasone](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/7acb1503-8c94-41ad-b767-f3de869783ef), a retired [US Army](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/d75a3803-21fc-4774-8a61-1ab96166d4e9) general, has over three decades of experience in signal intelligence and cyber operations, and previously led [the US](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/f48b2654-0e55-4910-a4a9-59062a377aae) Army Cyber Command and the [National Security Agency](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/05ef835c-d0a0-431a-bcea-dac15a8cfe8f) [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=117s).
- The National Security Agency plays a crucial role in signals intelligence, making and breaking codes, and providing combat support, which can be a competitive advantage for army leaders [03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=230s).
- The relationship between signal and cyber is closely linked, as cyberspace is a domain where signals intelligence is prominent, and combining the two allows for speed, agility, and unity of action [06:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=374s).
- Artificial intelligence is expected to have a significant impact on future warfare, providing incredible analysis, collection, and insights, and strategic leaders need to understand this technology and how to communicate its implications to policymakers [07:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=456s).

## The Future of Intelligence and Cyber Operations

- The role of intelligence officers is evolving, and optimal commanders allow them to provide answers and think about the implications, as seen in planning the invasion of Afghanistan with General Votel [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=617s)
- AI is expected to change the way signals intelligence is conducted, particularly in terms of language analysis, with machine learning and artificial intelligence-based platforms becoming more credible and spanning almost all known languages and dialects [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=673s)
- Integrating cyber into operational level campaign planning requires understanding the requirements and having honest conversations about capabilities, as seen in Operation Glowing Symphony, which was done in conjunction with [Joint Special Operations Command](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/a6105066-25aa-4728-80cd-678a819cafca) [15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=931s)

## Operational Aspects and Cybersecurity

- The [NSA](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/05ef835c-d0a0-431a-bcea-dac15a8cfe8f) and [Cybercom](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/5ee6839a-3e42-4410-912a-733b916c38ae) are learning from and partnering with the commercial sector to develop new technologies, including standing up the cybersecurity director at NSA in 2019 to provide better support to the defense industrial base [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1055s)
- Ensuring cybersecurity is crucial, and providing free services like secure email and scanning can help prevent incidents, which is more cost-effective than dealing with the aftermath of a cyber attack [19:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1143s)
- The concept of "defend forward" involves understanding what adversaries are doing outside of [the US](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/f48b2654-0e55-4910-a4a9-59062a377aae) and setting boundaries in cyberspace to prevent them from operating against the US without consequences [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1232s)

## Proactive Cybersecurity Measures

- The "hunt forward" teams were established to send teams to hunt on networks at the invitation of a government, share data with them, and collaborate with the private sector to attribute and provide remedies for malware [21:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1288s)
- The US needs to build deeper and stronger partnerships with other countries to understand what adversaries are doing and to share capabilities and intelligence [23:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1385s)

## Modern Conflict and Strategic Challenges

- Modern conflict is evolving, with lessons to be learned from the war in [Ukraine](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/4c88ead4-a155-449b-9133-8759f57163ed), such as the use of autonomous systems and the blurring of non-kinetic and kinetic capabilities [23:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1438s)
- [The US](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/f48b2654-0e55-4910-a4a9-59062a377aae) is focused on [China](https://app.getrecall.ai/item/80c7c27b-66e9-4759-9313-e4c7b35234e4) as a major threat, but has advantages in terms of partnerships, technology, and joint warfighting capabilities, and needs to think differently about gray zone warfare [25:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1530s)

## Leadership and Transparency

- Managing risk involves defining what's at risk, understanding who is involved, mitigating risk, and describing risk in a way that is understandable to the American people [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1621s)
- Gaining and maintaining the trust of the American people is a delicate balancing act between protecting national security and being transparent, with the goal of finding a middle ground 
- The importance of transparency was learned from the Snowden incident in 2013, which highlighted the need for intelligence agencies to be more forward in explaining their actions and purposes to the public [29:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1790s)

## Conclusion

- To be a strategic leader, one must have vision, be able to communicate that vision, and possess critical thinking and strong communication skills, which are essential for dealing with diverse groups and making informed decisions [30:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6lQYTo4vGVU&t=1845s)
