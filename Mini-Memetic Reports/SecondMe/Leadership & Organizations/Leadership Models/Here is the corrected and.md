**SO WHAT? ANALYSIS — A NEW ACRONYM, A NEW OUTCOME**


SO WHAT? builds on the familiar pillars of SWOT by adding Actions (A), How (H), and Hypothesis (?). The goal is simple: go beyond observation and move toward decisive execution. Each element plays a distinct role:



S — Strengths

The assets, capabilities, or advantages that set you apart in the market.

O — Opportunities

External openings or trends you can seize to grow or differentiate.

W — Weaknesses

The internal gaps or shortfalls that could undermine your progress.

H — How

The operational roadmap: who’s involved, which resources matter, and which milestones mark success.

A — Actions

The tangible steps that transform insight into concrete, strategic initiatives.

T — Threats

External forces that might erode a key advantage or amplify a lingering vulnerability.

? — Hypothesis

A testable assumption, clarifying your expected outcome and defining how you’ll measure results.



By infusing Actions, How, and Hypothesis, SO WHAT? shifts the conversation from

“We’ve cataloged the issues” to “Here’s exactly how we’ll tackle them.”
