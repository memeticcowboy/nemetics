🧠"Teaching Executives to Think with Machines" 🤖

🔮Kes Sampanthar ✨ and I had the privilege and pleasure of presenting our third iteration of a module on Augmented Intelligence as part of Rita McGrath's "Leading Strategic Growth and Change" program at Columbia Business School Executive Education with a room full of leaders from MENA. Two years after ChatGPT's release, we're finally asking the right questions: not "How do we use AI?" but "How do we think with machines?"

👇 Here's what we're seeing that's different:

The Gulf region isn't just adopting AI - they're reimagining it. With trillions in investment capital and a hunger for transformation, they're positioned to pioneer what we've dubbed "Centaurian thinking" - the art of combining human and machine intelligence to solve previously impossible challenges.

The secret? It's not about prompt engineering or automation. It's about understanding five distinct thinking systems:

• System Zero: Thinking outside our heads (using tools and our environment)
• System One: Fast, intuitive thinking (our unconscious)
• System Two: Slow, analytical thinking (our working memory)
• System Three: AI (thinking machines)
• System X: The integration of all systems (augmented collective intelligence)

What excites us most is watching leaders grasp this framework in real-time. When they stop seeing AI as a replacement and start seeing it as an augmentation and extension of human cognition, the innovation possibilities explode.

Our favorite part: the most creative solutions emerged not when AI worked alone, but when human intuition danced with machine intelligence. It's not about AI being more creative than humans - it's about the magic that happens when we combine both.

For those leading innovation in any region: The future isn't about humans VS machines. It's about humans X machines.

Think of it this way: We're not teaching people to use AI. We're teaching them to think in entirely new ways - ways that weren't possible before this technology existed.


Watch our quick post-workshop reflection to hear more about how we're reimagining innovation in the age of AI. This is just the beginning of a much bigger conversation about the future of human-AI collaboration.
