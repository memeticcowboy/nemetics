# Sam Altman on Politeness Costs for ChatGPT

Created at 2025/04/17 8:20 AM

## Summary
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman revealed that the courtesy of saying "thank you" and "please" to ChatGPT costs the company tens of millions of dollars in electricity bills. Despite the cost, Altman believes it is worthwhile as it helps the AI learn human politeness and improves its interactions.
