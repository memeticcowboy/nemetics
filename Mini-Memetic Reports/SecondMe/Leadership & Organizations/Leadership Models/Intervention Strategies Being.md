## Intervention Strategies Being Developed

Organizations and researchers are developing new approaches to combat the bystander effect in 2025:

## **Training Programs**

- **Workplace Bystander Training**: Specific programs for recognizing and intervening in workplace harassment
- **Digital Citizenship**: Education on online intervention techniques and platform reporting
- **Climate Action**: Community programs to overcome climate inaction through collective responsibility

## **Policy Interventions**

- **Good Samaritan Laws**: Legal protections for those who intervene in emergencies
- **Institutional Accountability**: Requirements for organizations to address bystander behavior
- **Platform Responsibility**: Social media companies implementing intervention facilitation tools

## **Technology Solutions**

- **AI-Assisted Intervention**: Tools that help identify when intervention is needed
- **Community Reporting**: Simplified systems for reporting problems and tracking responses
- **Bias Detection**: Automated systems to identify and flag discriminatory content or behavior
