## Insight
You’ll find Ivan Zhao's story of Notion particularly insightful as it aligns with your analytical mindset. The article explores how Zhao initially struggled to change consumer behavior but ultimately succeeded by understanding the invariables of human needs. His philosophical approach to business, likening it to a beetle's ecosystem, emphasizes the importance of integrating customer feedback into a cohesive system. This resonates with your commitment to interdisciplinary studies and innovative solutions, offering actionable insights on how to navigate complex societal challenges through a deep understanding of user behavior.

**🚀 Building a Business: Understanding Behavior**
- **Changing Behavior is Key**: To build a successful business, understanding and changing consumer behavior is essential. Entrepreneurs often misjudge the market, believing their product will be immediately embraced, but many fail to recognize that consumers may not see the need for their offerings.
- **The Importance of Adaptation**: Ivan Zhao's journey with Notion illustrates the necessity of adapting to market needs. Initially, his product failed because it did not align with what users wanted. The shift to a flexible collaboration tool was a direct response to understanding user behavior.
- **Intuition Over Assumptions**: Zhao emphasizes the role of intuition in recognizing market needs. His initial failure taught him that understanding user behavior is more critical than simply having a great product idea.

**🌍 The Philosopher Founder**
- **A Unique Approach to Business**: Zhao's philosophical approach to business sets him apart from typical founders. He views business as a thought experiment, focusing on user-centric design and the importance of community feedback.
- **Customer Service as a System**: Notion's customer service is integrated into its development process. Zhao receives notifications for every customer inquiry, allowing him to identify trends and ensure that user feedback directly informs product development.
- **Learning from Nature**: Zhao draws parallels between business systems and natural systems, suggesting that a company should function like a living organism, where every part learns from customer interactions and contributes to overall growth.

**🔍 Understanding Invariables**
- **Recognizing Limitations**: Invariables are the unchangeable aspects of human behavior that entrepreneurs must acknowledge. Zhao learned that most people are not builders; they prefer ready-made solutions to their problems.
- **The Need for Simplicity**: Zhao identified a core human desire for simplicity in tools. Notion aims to consolidate various functions into one platform, addressing the fragmentation of software tools that complicate users' lives.
- **Adapting to New Invariables**: As a company evolves, it creates new invariables that must be respected. Zhao's experience with Notion's project management tool taught him the importance of staying true to the company's established identity.

**🌊 Riding the Wave**
- **Behavioral Shifts Create Opportunities**: Zhao describes the concept of 'waves' in consumer behavior, where shifts create opportunities for businesses to adapt and thrive. Notion capitalizes on the growing demand for simplicity in a fragmented software market.
- **Learning from Competitors**: Zhao acknowledges that successful companies like Airbnb did not create new behaviors but rather rode existing waves of change. Notion follows a similar path by addressing the frustrations of users with multiple software tools.
- **Generational Waves Matter**: Zhao focuses on younger generations who are more open to adopting new tools like Notion. He recognizes that older generations may resist change, allowing Notion to carve out its niche among college students and young professionals.

**💊 Painkillers vs. Vitamins**
- **Addressing Real Needs**: Zhao differentiates between 'painkillers' and 'vitamins' in product offerings. Notion serves as a painkiller by alleviating the frustrations of users overwhelmed by multiple tools, thus driving behavioral change.
- **Creating Value Through Solutions**: The success of Notion lies in its ability to provide real solutions to users' problems. By focusing on pain points, Zhao ensures that the product remains relevant and necessary for its users.
- **Understanding User Behavior is Crucial**: Zhao concludes that to build a great business, one must understand user behavior deeply. This understanding informs product development and helps in creating solutions that resonate with users.

**🔄 The Cycle of Learning and Adapting**
- **Iterative Learning Process**: Zhao emphasizes the importance of trial and error in understanding market needs. Entrepreneurs must be willing to experiment, fail, and learn to refine their offerings.
- **Feedback Loops Enhance Development**: By integrating customer feedback into the development process, Notion creates a continuous loop of improvement, ensuring that the product evolves in line with user expectations.
- **Philosophical Foundations Drive Success**: Zhao's philosophical approach to business encourages a deeper understanding of the market and user needs, which ultimately leads to more successful and sustainable business practices.
