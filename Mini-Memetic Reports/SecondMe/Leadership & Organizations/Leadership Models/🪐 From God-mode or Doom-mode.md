### 🪐 From God-mode or Doom-mode to Relational Design OF AI

**Design Failure**: *Everything is God-mode or doom-mode.*

**Improvement Strategy**:<br>Replace absolute narratives with **relational archetypes**—AI as Daughter, Mother, or Crone depending on its stage. Translate into:

- **Political**: Decenter big-tech deification by requiring *relational ethics training* for AI boards.
- **Economic**: Divest from AI omnipotence hype; incentivize *cooperative AI* models (e.g. co-regulation, peer-teaching).
- **Social**: Normalize *non-omniscient*, *context-sensitive AI* in mental health, education, and storytelling.
- **Technological**: Train AI not on total datasets, but on *localized, community-coherent memory windows*.
- **Legal**: Codify *co-parenting models* for AI across jurisdictions—no more singular owner-gods.
- **Environmental**: Reframe AI as *eco-participant*, not planetary savior.
