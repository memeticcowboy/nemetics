# Applying the Unknowmad Mindset in Work

Created at 2025/12/25 3:33 PM

## Letting Go of Analysis
- Explore the idea of scheduling “drift time” for exploration without a specific agenda.
- This approach is likened to gardening, where organic growth can occur without constant micromanaging. 
## Fostering Psychological Safety in the Team
- Model vulnerability by admitting when you don’t know something or when mistakes happen.
- Invite dissent and curiosity by encouraging the sharing of wild ideas or concerns, which normalizes risk-taking and signals that everyone’s voice matters.
