# AI Transformation at Merck

Created at 2024/09/03 10:31 AM

Walid Mehanna is the Chief Data and AI Officer of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. Driven by his passion for people and technology, he leads the company’s enterprise wide data and AI strategy. He and his team build a comprehensive technology ecosystem, establish an effective operating model, and foster a strong governance and culture framework for data and AI at the organization. Additionally, he chairs the Merck Digital Ethics Advisory Panel, guiding the company’s ethical standards in the digital realm.

## Insight
In this insightful session, Walid Mehanna, Chief Data and AI Officer at Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, discusses the transformative impact of AI, particularly generative AI, on the organization. He outlines the journey from the initial exploration of ChatGPT in late 2022 to the development and deployment of 'MyGPT' within Merck. Key outcomes include the establishment of a robust data and AI governance framework, the creation of a dual operating model for data and AI products, and the successful integration of AI into various business processes. Mehanna emphasizes the importance of data readiness, organizational mindset, and continuous innovation in AI. He also highlights the significant time savings and efficiency gains achieved through MyGPT, as well as the strategic partnerships and ethical considerations involved. The session concludes with actionable insights on developing an AI strategy, addressing common obstacles, and the future potential of AI agents. This comprehensive overview sets the stage for a deeper understanding of AI's role in driving business value and innovation at Merck.
