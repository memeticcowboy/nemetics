# Exploring the Implementation Challenges of CAIT³

Created at 2025/10/28 11:38 AM

## Ontological Whiplash
- Risk of disorientation and dissociation as identities blend between human and technology.

## Power Asymmetry
- Potential for cyborg auditors to dominate, which may erode trust within communities.

## Ethical Drift
- Ongoing meaningful consent becomes challenging; systems change may outpace individual understanding.

## Integrative Complexity Insights
- Integrative complexity offers a framework for understanding the balance of multiple viewpoints.
- Enhanced complexity can facilitate better decision-making but risks decision paralysis and exploitation by adversarial actors.
- Emphasizes that CAIT³'s deep synthesis could both enrich and complicate audit processes. 

## Reflection and Experience
- There’s a tension between the beneficial aspects of integrative thinking and the potential downsides, evident from both academic research and practical experiences in collaborative settings.
