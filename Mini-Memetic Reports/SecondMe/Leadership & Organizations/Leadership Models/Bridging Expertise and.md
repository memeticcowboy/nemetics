# Bridging Expertise and Technology: The Power of Decision Intelligence Framework

Created at 2024/12/08 7:36 PM

Decision Intelligence (DI) merges human expertise with AI to enhance decision-making. The diagram shows three intersecting elements:

Domain expertise: Industry-specific knowledge (healthcare, retail)
Packaged solutions: Pre-built templates combining industry expertise with analytics
Technical tools: BI, ML, and NLP capabilities that power intelligent decisions

The orange overlap represents where DI platforms meet domain applications, creating decision templates that blend industry knowledge with data analysis. For example, healthcare providers can combine clinical protocols with real-time analytics for better patient care decisions.
This framework shows how DI bridges domain knowledge and technical capabilities, enabling intelligence-augmented judgment across industries and business functions.
