# Impact of Brain Research on the Six Cyborgs Concept

Created at 2025/09/05 8:05 AM

## Connection to Distributed Decision-Making
- The brain's decision-making is distributed across various regions, similar to the Six Cyborgs model.
- Each cyborg represents a unique perspective, akin to different brain regions contributing to a holistic understanding.

## Integrative Approach
- True intelligence emerges from the interplay of the cyborgs, not their isolation.
- This suggests a focus on designing interactions and overlaps between the cyborgs rather than perfecting each individual part.

## Clarifying Cyborg Philosophies
- The distributed model may help clarify the philosophies of each cyborg.
- Emphasizes the importance of collaboration and integration in achieving a comprehensive understanding of consciousness and cognition.
