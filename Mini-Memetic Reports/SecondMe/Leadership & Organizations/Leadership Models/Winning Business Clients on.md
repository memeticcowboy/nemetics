# Winning Business Clients on LinkedIn: Strategies for Effective Content and Lead Generation

Created at 2024/05/19 1:53 PM

Win business clients on LinkedIn

🔗 LinkedIn prioritizes content based on your connections and interactions (social graph).

🎯 Select your top-performing piece of content aimed directly at your ideal client.
🪝 Craft a call to action (CTA) at the END of your post with these elements:
1️⃣ Directly address your ideal client and a pain point they face.
2️⃣ Empower them to avoid wasted effort due to that problem.
3️⃣ Offer a low-commitment, high-value solution (e.g., free audit, blueprint).
4️⃣ Close with a friendly pitch for further work (but no pressure if they decline).
💡 This strategy combines value with a smart lead generation opportunity.
⏱️ This generates inbound leads quickly because of the direct CTA.
🤝 It builds trust by giving potential clients something helpful upfront.
💫 Not every post needs a CTA like this, but it's a powerful tool to use strategically.

⚖️ Don't overuse the direct CTA strategy – once a week is a good guideline.
💎 Value-driven content is your most powerful long-term "call to action".
🚫 Avoid asking for business without first earning trust through quality content.
🤏 Subtle CTAs can work well within your posts for a softer approach.
💪 To make direct CTAs work, your content itself needs to be exceptional.
🚪 "Permission to reach out" is a key element of a successful CTA.
🔎 Make sure your LinkedIn profile is optimized to convert if people click through.
📈 Initial success on LinkedIn is measured mainly through engagement, not just views.

💬 Early on, focus on qualitative feedback: Are people engaging positively with your content?
🧐 Measure the quality of reactions (comments, shares) to gauge if you're hitting the mark.
📈 Quantitative metrics (views, likes) will naturally improve as your content quality does.
🎯 Successful LinkedIn strategy is measured ultimately by leads, clients, and revenue.
👂 Track how people discover you (forms, sales calls) to understand what's working.
👤 Personal LinkedIn profiles get significantly more reach than company pages.
🏢 Companies should still consider a YouTube channel or podcast for broader reach.
🔄 Encourage employees to share company content from those bigger platforms onto their personal LinkedIn feeds.

🏢 Focus company efforts on a strong YouTube channel or podcast for broader content creation.
♻️ Repurpose larger content (podcast/video) into smaller formats for both company and personal LinkedIn pages.
🤝 Building personal brands within a company amplifies reach far beyond a company page alone.
📹 Video is the dominant content format, so it should be a priority in your strategy.
🔎 If you're not seeing results, the most likely culprit is the quality of your content.
🪝 Focus on strong hooks, embedding value throughout the post, and using both subtle and direct CTAs.
💪 Compare content creation to fitness: if you want results, you have to put in the work (or hire someone who can!).
💡 Knowing how to create high-performing content is non-negotiable for LinkedIn success
