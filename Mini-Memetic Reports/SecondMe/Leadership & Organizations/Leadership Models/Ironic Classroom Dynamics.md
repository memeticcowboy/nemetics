# Ironic Classroom Dynamics: Disinterest in Leadership Development Inspired by Confucius

**Title: Navigating Leadership Development with Confucius (Ironic Version)**

**Setting:** A classroom or training room with a large screen displaying the image. The students are slouched in their chairs, scrolling on their phones, or staring blankly into space.

**Characters:**

- **Instructor**: An overly enthusiastic trainer or teacher.

- **Learners**: A group of students or participants who couldn’t care less.

---

**Scene 1: Introduction to the Framework**

*Instructor stands by the screen, pointing at the title with exaggerated energy.*

**Instructor:** Welcome, everyone! Today, we're diving into a *super exciting* framework for leadership development inspired by Confucius' saying, "When I see, I remember. When I do, I learn." Let's start by understanding the stages of this process!

*Points to the top left of the image. The learners don’t even glance up.*

**Learner 1:** (muttering) Yeah, because Confucius was totally thinking about leadership frameworks when he said that.

**Learner 2:** (yawning) Can we just skip to the part where this is over?

---

**Scene 2: Exploring the Stages**

*Instructor moves to the central part of the diagram, still overly animated.*

**Instructor:** Let's look at the stages: See, Remember, Do, and Review. Each stage has *specific actions* associated with it!

*Points to each stage as they describe. The learners are either texting or doodling.*

**Instructor:** 

- **See**: This is where we observe and gather knowledge. Here, we have Knowledge Management and Coaching.

- **Remember**: This involves consolidating what we've seen. Activities include Teaching, Showing, and Mentoring.

- **Do**: This is the action stage where we apply what we've learned. It involves actions like Listening, Doing, and Practicing.

- **Review**: Finally, we reflect and refine our learning. This includes Reviewing and Tutoring.

**Learner 3:** (sarcastically) Wow, groundbreaking. See, remember, do, review. Never heard that before.

**Learner 4:** (whispering) I’m just here for the certificate.

---

**Scene 3: Understanding the Actions**

*Instructor points to the detailed actions under each stage, still trying to engage the class.*

**Instructor:** Each stage has *specific actions*:

- **Knowledge Management**: Before we start, we ask "What NEXT?"

- **Coaching**: We start with looking, asking "What NOW?"

- **Teaching**: Here, we show what we know, asking "What-IF?"

- **Mentoring**: We listen and learn, reflecting on "What-IF? & NOT - IF-Only!"

- **Doing**: We take action, responding to "What NOW?"

- **Listening**: We pay attention to learn from experiences.

- **Reviewing**: We review to understand and cope, asking "What-IF?"

- **Tutoring**: We help others understand, resolve, and investigate.

**Learner 5:** (mockingly) "What-IF? & NOT - IF-Only!" Sounds like a bad motivational poster.

**Learner 6:** (to Learner 5) I’m already coping—by not paying attention.

---

**Scene 4: Leadership Development**

*Instructor moves to the right side of the diagram, still trying to salvage the session.*

**Instructor:** Now, let's connect this to leadership development. We see elements like:

- **The Ascent of a Leader**: A course or resource for leadership growth.

- **Competency Learning**: Developing skills through C-E-R-T & M-W-A.

- **Outcomes**: The ultimate goal of all-win/win outcomes.

**Learner 7:** (sarcastically) All-win/win outcomes? Sounds like corporate buzzword bingo.

**Learner 8:** (leaning back) I’m winning by not participating.

---

**Scene 5: Practical Application**

*Instructor steps back to overview the entire diagram, still smiling despite the obvious disinterest.*

**Instructor:** To apply this in real life, consider events like EcoTrekUSA, where participants engage in GeoVenturing, which involves Tell-Show-Do-Review. This practical application helps solidify the learning process!

**Learner 9:** (mockingly) Oh no, not GeoVenturing. My worst nightmare.

**Learner 10:** (to Learner 9) Don’t worry, I’ll just tell, show, and do... nothing.

---

**Scene 6: Conclusion**

*Instructor smiles at the learners, who are now packing up early.*

**Instructor:** I hope this framework gives you a structured yet flexible approach to learning and leadership. Remember, like Confucius said, the real learning happens when we do. Let's keep this cycle alive in our personal and professional lives!

**Learner 11:** (under their breath) Or we could just... not.

**Learner 12:** (grabbing their bag) Yeah, I’ll reflect on this never.

*The learners file out of the room, leaving the Instructor standing alone with the diagram.*
