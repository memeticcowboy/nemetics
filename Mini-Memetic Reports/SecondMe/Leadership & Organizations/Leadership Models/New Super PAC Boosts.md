# New Super PAC Boosts Bipartisan AI Regulation Candidates

Created at 2025/11/14 8:15 AM

The White House is upset with a new $100 million super PAC called Leading the Future, which aims to support candidates who back a national framework for artificial intelligence regulations.


The super PAC, led by former Schumer staffer Josh Vlasto and Republican consultant Zac Moffatt, plans to support AI-friendly candidates from both parties, potentially helping Democrats win back control of Congress.


The White House is monitoring the group's donors, including prominent Silicon Valley figures such as Marc Andreesseen, Greg Brockman, and Ron Conway, warning them not to support the effort.
