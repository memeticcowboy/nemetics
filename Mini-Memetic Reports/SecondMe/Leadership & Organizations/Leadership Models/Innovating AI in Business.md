## Insight
This episode of 'The D Suite' delves into the intricacies of managing innovation within data and analytics, particularly in AI projects. The discussion highlights the importance of aligning innovation efforts with business priorities, focusing on value over tools, and exploring creative funding methods for high-return initiatives. Key insights include the need for Chief Data and Analytics Officers (CDAOs) to balance immediate results with long-term innovation and the unique approach of running a data and analytics team like a startup to foster value creation and stakeholder engagement. The episode features insights from Michael Gabbard on overcoming challenges in scaling innovation and Constantine Chitzius, Chief Digital Officer at Westlake Corporation, who shares his experience of managing AI and digital programs in a large corporation. Actionable recommendations include understanding business priorities, focusing on value, and building a data-driven culture to support AI initiatives.
