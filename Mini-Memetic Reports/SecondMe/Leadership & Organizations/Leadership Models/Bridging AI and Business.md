**🚫 Mistakes to Avoid in Decision Intelligence**

- **Poor AI Technique Choice**: Avoid choosing inappropriate AI techniques for business decisions, as availability bias can lead to suboptimal outcomes. Composite AI offers a toolbox of techniques, emphasizing the importance of selecting the right one for each use case. [_TIMESTAMP_](1624-1701)
- **Ignoring Mature Technologies**: Rushing towards emerging technologies while neglecting mature capabilities can hinder decision intelligence implementation. Optimization engines and solvers are recommended over generative models for decision intelligence. [_TIMESTAMP_](1641-1747)
- **Neglecting Decision Quality**: Ensuring decision quality through governance is crucial. Decisions should be timely, effective, compliant, and trusted, with a focus on cost-effectiveness. [_TIMESTAMP_](1672-1733)
