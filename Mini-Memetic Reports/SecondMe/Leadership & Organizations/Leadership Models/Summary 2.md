## Summary
Gen Z's career strategy, which emphasizes continuous learning, flexibility, and purpose-driven work, is facing challenges as the job-hopping economy hits a wall. Despite valuing autonomy and meaningful work, Gen Z faces economic realities that may limit their job-hopping freedoms.
## Top 3 Overarching Themes
- **Career Flexibility and Autonomy**: Gen Z's preference for flexible work arrangements and the ability to choose their work environment.
- **Meaningful and Purpose-Driven Work**: The importance of work that aligns with personal values and contributes to societal impact.
- **Continuous Learning and Development**: The necessity for ongoing skill development and career growth opportunities.
