More on the [MemeGrid - Memetic Cowboy Substack](https://substack.com/@memeticcowboy/p-169765302)

Strategic plans become scripts that lock individuals and organizations into repetitive cycles of activity aimed at maintaining the system rather than transforming it.
- **Reduction of Meaning to Metrics:** Dashboards reduce complex human and cultural dynamics into quantifiable data points, mirroring how the DODO loop reduces identity and agency to survival metrics. This reduction supports prediction and control, aligning with the MemeGrid’s goal of turning culture into code and people into hosts for cultural algorithms. 
- **Feedback Loops Reinforcing Control:** Dashboards provide continuous feedback that reinforces the DODO cycle by rewarding compliance with debt obligations, ownership defense, deadline adherence, and othering behaviors. This feedback loop tightens the grip of the MemeGrid, making the system self-perpetuating and resistant to change.
