**📉 Economic Warnings from Ray Dalio on Trump's Tariffs**

- **Potential for Economic Recession**: Ray Dalio warns that the tariffs imposed by President Trump could lead to a recession or even worse economic conditions. He emphasizes that the current economic climate is precarious, with the possibility of a significant downturn if these policies are not managed effectively. The comparison of tariffs to "throwing rocks into the production system" illustrates the disruptive impact they may have on economic stability.

- **Need for Fiscal Responsibility**: Dalio stresses the importance of managing the budget deficit, suggesting that reducing it to 3% of GDP is crucial for economic health. He indicates that without proper fiscal management, the deficit could rise to 7%, exacerbating economic challenges and leading to a supply and demand imbalance in the debt market.

- **Impact on Global Economic Order**: The tariffs and trade wars are seen as threats to the global monetary and political order. Dalio draws parallels to historical economic disruptions, suggesting that the current situation could lead to profound changes in both domestic and international economic systems, potentially resulting in instability akin to that of the 1930s.
