# Exploring Different Types of Power: Definitions and Implications

Created at 2025/06/21 9:49 AM

Based on your notes, here are the definitions of the different types of power:

1. **Power To**: The capacity to act in the world. Framed as generative volition rather than domination. It represents the ability to create change or accomplish goals.
2. **Power For**: Acting in service of others with ethical or compassionate intent. Associated with mutual aid, caregiving, and solidarity. This is supportive, generative power focused on tending and enabling, operating through stability and practical alignment.
3. **Power Against**: Coordinated resistance to harm, domination, or injustice. Associated with protest, whistleblowing, sabotage, and refusal. Adjacent to concepts of civil disobedience and moral courage.
4. **Power From**: Drawing strength from ancestral, cultural, or ecological roots. Associated with ancestral resilience, cultural revival, and land-based knowledge. Connected to deep time, spiritual grounding, and intergenerational wisdom.
5. **Power Beneath**: Power exercised from marginalized or hidden positions. Associated with subversion, invisibility, liminality, and dual codes. Connected to underground networks and symbolic resistance.
6. **Power As**: Symbolic embodiment of a role, narrative, or archetype. Associated with masks, icons, and performative politics. This involves becoming a narrative node through persona or representation.
7. **Power Over**: Imposed symbolic control or dominance. Involves the coercive structuring of meaning to control interpretation through saturation, suppression, or standardization.
8. [Power With:](https://substack.com/home/post/p-164938029) Coordinated semiotic meaning-making across collectives. Involves shared rituals, oral histories, or grassroots media practices. Emphasizes collaboration and mutual support.
9. **Power Through**: Power that emerges through spontaneous symbolic amplification across distributed networks, creating coherent patterns without centralized control. Characterized by emergent coherence, decentralized amplification, and self-organizing patterns.
10. [Power Within](https://substack.com/home/post/p-162805193) refers to an individual's sense of self-worth, self-knowledge, and inner strength. It's about personal consciousness, confidence, and the capacity for self-determination. This type of power centers on recognizing one's own intrinsic value and agency.
