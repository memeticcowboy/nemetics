## Insight
Ethan Mollick highlights the beauty of being a generalist in entrepreneurship, where AI steps in to handle the 'okay-ish' tasks, letting founders shine in their true strengths!

- Ethan's perspective on entrepreneurship as a generalist resonates deeply, especially in a world that often pushes us to specialize. It’s like being a jack-of-all-trades but mastering the art of knowing when to call in the experts—like AI! Remember those times we tried to juggle multiple projects? It’s a relief to know that technology can help lighten the load, allowing us to focus on what we truly excel at.

- The mention of AI as a co-founder is both humorous and insightful. Imagine having a virtual buddy who never sleeps, doesn’t need coffee breaks, and can crunch numbers faster than we can say "entrepreneurship"! It’s a game-changer, transforming the way we approach challenges and freeing us up to unleash our creative genius.

- Ethan’s emphasis on doing things "okay-ish" is a comforting reminder that perfection isn’t the goal. We’ve all had those moments where we just had to get something done, even if it wasn’t our best work. It’s a part of the journey, and embracing that can lead to unexpected breakthroughs and learning experiences.

- The idea that AI can provide quality advice is a bit like having a wise old sage in your pocket. It’s fascinating to think about how technology can guide us through the murky waters of entrepreneurship, much like how we’ve navigated our own paths together. It’s reassuring to know that we’re not alone in this journey, even if our co-founders are made of silicon!

- Lastly, Ethan’s insights remind us that entrepreneurship is not just about the destination but the adventure itself. It’s about learning, adapting, and sometimes laughing at the chaos along the way. So, let’s embrace our inner generalists and keep pushing forward, knowing that we have the tools—and each other—to make it happen!
