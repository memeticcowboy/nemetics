### NEME Process with the Rule of Three

1. **Notice**:
   - **Identify Three Key Observations**: Focus on three critical elements that stand out in a situation or problem.
   - **Three Perspectives**: Analyze the situation from three different viewpoints (e.g., user, stakeholder, competitor).

2. **Engage**:
   - **Three Engagement Strategies**: Develop three distinct strategies to engage with your audience or stakeholders.
   - **Three Key Messages**: Craft three core messages to convey during your engagement.

3. **Measure**:
   - **Three Metrics**: Choose three key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the success of your engagement.
   - **Three Timeframes**: Evaluate these metrics over three different timeframes (short-term, mid-term, long-term).

4. **Evaluate**:
   - **Three Evaluation Criteria**: Use three main criteria to evaluate the outcomes of your process.
   - **Three Improvement Areas**: Identify three areas for improvement based on your evaluation.

By incorporating the Rule of Three into the NEME process, you can enhance problem-solving and engagement strategies through structured observations, focused engagement, meaningful metrics, and comprehensive evaluations.
