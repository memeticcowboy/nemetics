# Using ChatGPT Prompts to Personalize Math Tutoring for Improved Learning

## How AI Can Help Mitigate Cognitive Biases in Personalized Tutoring

- **Objective Data Analysis**:
  - AI algorithms can analyze student performance data without human biases.
  
- **Adaptive Learning**:
  - AI can adapt the learning material to the student's needs.
  
- **Diverse Perspectives**:
  - AI can incorporate diverse teaching methods and perspectives.
  
- **Continuous Monitoring**:
  - AI systems can provide real-time feedback and correct biases.
  
- **Decision Support**:
  - AI can offer data-driven insights and recommendations for tutoring strategies.
