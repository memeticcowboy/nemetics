# Mapping the Rose of Leary to Power Types

Created at 2025/08/10 7:02 AM

## Above Dominance
- **Leading**: 
  - Power To (initiating transformation)
  - Power Over (directing/coercing)
  
- **Competitive**: 
  - Power Against (resisting/opposing)
  - Power Over (asserting dominance)
  
- **Helping**: 
  - Power For (serving/healing)
  - Power With (mutual support)
  
- **Cooperative**: 
  - Power With (co-creation)
  - Power Through (emergent group flow)

## Under Submission
- **Dependent**: 
  - Power From (drawing on roots/others)
  - Pseudo Power (simulated reliance)
  
- **Withdrawn**: 
  - Null Power (collapse/withdrawal)
  - Power Beneath (hidden, subversive)
  
- **Rebellious**: 
  - Power Against (resisting authority)
  - Power Beneath (subversive challenge)
  
- **Offensive**: 
  - Power As (role-playing, sometimes hollow)
  - Power Against (provocation)

## Cross-cutting
- **Power Within**: Can manifest in any style as self-sovereignty, especially visible when someone shifts styles consciously.
- **Power As**: Overlays any style when someone is performing a role rather than acting authentically.

This mapping highlights the dynamic nature of power and interpersonal styles, emphasizing that styles and power types can blend or shift depending on context.
