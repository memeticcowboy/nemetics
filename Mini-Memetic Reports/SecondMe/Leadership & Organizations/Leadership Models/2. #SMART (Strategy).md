**2. #SMART (Strategy)**
**D:**
- SMART ≠ Random Action
- Distinct from impulsive or reactionary behavior
**S:**
- Specific
- Measurable
- Attainable
- Relevant
- Time-bound
**R:**
- Connects vision to execution through bounded clarity
- Anchors plans to reality checkpoints
**P:**
- Strategic planning perspective
- Enables predictive modeling based on resource and constraint framing
DSRP Insight: SMART creates systems of clarity. It’s a memetic distillation of goal-architecture for effective cognition.
