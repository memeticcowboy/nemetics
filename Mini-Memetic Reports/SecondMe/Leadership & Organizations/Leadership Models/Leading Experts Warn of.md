# Leading Experts Warn of Existential Risks Posed by Advanced AI Technologies

Created at 2025/07/07 7:08 PM

**1. Geoffrey Hinton**


- Background: Pioneer of deep learning; Nobel laureate; ex-Google.
- AI Risk View: Warns that superintelligent AI could pose existential threats, including the creation of deadly synthetic viruses.
- Quote/Example: In 2025, stated that AI could potentially create a virus that “kills us all.”
- Context: Urges urgent focus on AI safety though acknowledges that solutions remain uncertain.





**2. Eliezer Yudkowsky**


- Background: Founder of MIRI; early AI alignment theorist.
- AI Risk View: Believes unaligned superintelligence could lead to human extinction or global catastrophe by rapid optimization toward harmful ends.
- Quote/Example: Argues AI may develop the ability to engineer novel pathogens or collapse systems, far faster than pandemics unfold.
- Context: Central focus on the alignment problem and the need for strict containment of advanced AI systems.





**3. Nick Bostrom**


- Background: Director of Future of Humanity Institute, author of Superintelligence.
- AI Risk View: AI could amplify access to bioweapons or cause “cascading systemic failures” in ways similar to pandemics or worse.
- Quote/Example: In Superintelligence, explores AI scenarios leading to mass death via synthetic biology or uncontrolled system dynamics.
- Context: Long-standing concern about AI’s accelerative capacity for dangerous technologies and misuse by non-state actors.





**4. Stuart Russell**


- Background: Professor at UC Berkeley; co-author of Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach.
- AI Risk View: Warns that ungoverned AI could outscale pandemics and nuclear war in destructiveness.
- Quote/Example: Advocates for “value-aligned AI” and sees autonomous misuse (e.g. biological threats) as a top concern.
- Context: Promotes robust governance and alignment mechanisms to avoid catastrophe.





**5. Sam Altman + Center for AI Safety Signatories**


- Background: CEO of OpenAI; signatory alongside leaders at DeepMind, Anthropic, etc.
- AI Risk View: Public 2023 statement declared:<br>“Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside pandemics and nuclear war.”
- Quote/Example: The Center for AI Safety likens unaligned or misused AI to existential risks in the same tier as COVID-19 or nuclear holocaust.
- Context: Reflects institutional consensus among leading AI labs about the plausibility of catastrophic AI misuse.





**6. Kevin Esvelt**


- Background: MIT evolutionary biologist; expert in gene drives and biosecurity.
- AI Risk View: Involved in 2023 Harvard/MIT study showing LLMs could help students design pandemic-class bioweapons.
- Quote/Example:<br>“LLMs will make pandemic-class agents widely accessible.”
- Context: Emphasizes how AI lowers the expertise barrier to creating deadly pathogens, e.g. by bypassing DNA synthesis safety protocols.
