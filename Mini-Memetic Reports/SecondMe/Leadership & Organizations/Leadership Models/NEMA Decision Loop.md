# NEMA Decision Loop

Created at 2025/01/16 1:18 PM

To frame a decision using **Notice, Engage, Muse, Act**, (NEMAtics) you can follow these steps:

### 1. **Notice**

- Recognize the need for a decision and identify the goals you aim to achieve.
- Observe the available resources and alternative actions at your disposal.
- Example: "I notice that we need to allocate resources to meet our quarterly objectives and there are multiple strategies to consider."

### 2. **Engage**

- Actively gather relevant information and involve key stakeholders or perspectives.
- Evaluate the potential impact of alternative actions on your resources and goals.
- Example: "I engage with my team to analyze the pros and cons of each option and assess how they align with our objectives."

### 3. **Muse**

- Reflect deeply on the alternatives and deliberate over the consequences of each choice.
- Weigh the trade-offs and ensure the decision aligns with your overarching purpose.
- Example: "I muse on the potential outcomes, considering which choice best fulfills our priorities while using resources effectively."

### 4. **Act**

- Make the deliberate choice and commit resources irrevocably to the selected course of action.
- Implement the decision confidently, knowing it was thoughtfully considered.
- Example: "I act by committing resources to the chosen strategy, ensuring we stay focused on achieving our goals."

This framework ensures a thoughtful and deliberate process when making a decision that involves resource allocation and goal alignment.
