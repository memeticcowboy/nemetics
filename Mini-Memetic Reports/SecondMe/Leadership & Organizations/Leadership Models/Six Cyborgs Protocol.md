**Six Cyborgs | Independence Before Synthesis**

Principle:
Each cyborg must first observe, interpret, and reflect independently—free from the memetic influence of the others—before converging to synthesize a coherent whole.
This mirrors the collective intelligence paradigm:
- Diversity of perspective
- Independence of cognition
- Decentralized access to info
- Delayed consensus for maximum emergence

**Synthesis Phase: After Independence**

Once each cyborg has produced an independent memo or model, a synthesis ritual occurs:
1. Roundtable of Shadows – Each cyborg names the potential bias or ghost haunting their view.
2. Emergent Consensus Map – Overlapping truths and contradictions are visualized.
3. Meta-Memetic Synthesis – New understanding arises not through averaging, but through reflexive tension and symbolic reweaving.
4. Action Thread – Only then is action taken, woven from integrated awareness rather than reactive consensus.


**Applied Wisdom-of-Cyborg-Crowds**

Just like crowd wisdom, this only works when:
- Each cyborg maintains cognitive independence
- Cross-contamination of worldviews is minimized until the synthesis phase
- Shadows are named as part of the process—not hidden

Six distinct perceptual algorithms forming a collective conscience
without collapsing into groupthink or echo chambers.
