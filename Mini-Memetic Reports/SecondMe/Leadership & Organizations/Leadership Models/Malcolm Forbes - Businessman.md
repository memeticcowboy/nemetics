# Malcolm Forbes: Businessman and Balloonist

Created at 2024/08/24 6:51 PM

Malcolm Forbes (1919-1990) was the publisher of Forbes magazine, a prominent businessman, and an avid hot air balloonist. He set world records in ballooning, flew across America, and received numerous awards for his contributions to the sport. Forbes also served in WWII and briefly pursued a political career.

**💼 Forbes' Business Leadership**
- **Publisher of Forbes Magazine**: Malcolm Forbes became the Editor-in-Chief and Publisher of Forbes magazine after his father's death in 1954, significantly shaping the publication into a leading business magazine.
- **Political Career**: Forbes briefly ventured into politics, serving in the New Jersey State Senate and running for Governor, showcasing his leadership skills beyond the business realm.
- **Awards and Recognition**: Under his leadership, Forbes magazine gained numerous accolades, solidifying its reputation in the business community.

**🎈 Achievements in Ballooning**
- **World Records Holder**: Forbes set six official world records in hot air ballooning in 1973, including being the first to fly across America in a hot air balloon.
- **Awards for Ballooning**: He received the Harmon Trophy from President Gerald Ford in 1975 for his aeronautical achievements, highlighting his impact on the sport.
- **Founding a Balloon Museum**: In 1975, he established the world's first balloon museum in Normandy, France, promoting the sport and its history.

**🌍 Diverse Interests and Accomplishments**
- **Art Collector and Author**: Forbes was not only a businessman but also an art collector and author, writing books that reflected his passions and experiences.
- **Military Service**: He served in WWII, earning a Bronze Star and a Purple Heart for his bravery, which added to his diverse life experiences.
- **Motorcycle Enthusiast**: His love for motorcycles further exemplified his adventurous spirit and willingness to embrace various hobbies.

**🏅 Awards and Honors**
- **Induction into Halls of Fame**: Forbes was inducted into the Ballooning Hall of Fame in 2007 and the Aviation Hall of Fame of New Jersey in 1985, recognizing his contributions to ballooning.
- **International Recognition**: He received multiple international awards, including the Diplome Montgolfier, for his outstanding contributions to ballooning.
- **Board Memberships**: Forbes held significant positions in various aeronautical organizations, influencing the development of ballooning in the U.S.

**📚 Publications and Writings**
- **Authored Notable Books**: Forbes wrote 'Around the World on Hot Air' and 'More Than I Dreamed,' sharing his experiences and insights into ballooning and life.
- **Contributions to Literature**: His writings reflect his adventurous spirit and passion for ballooning, inspiring others to explore their interests.
- **Promotion of Ballooning**: Through his books, Forbes aimed to promote the sport of ballooning and its unique challenges and joys.

**🎖️ Military Service and Valor**
- **Service in WWII**: Forbes served as a staff sergeant in the Army, demonstrating leadership and bravery during critical battles.
- **Awards for Valor**: His military service earned him prestigious awards, showcasing his commitment and courage.
- **Impact on Leadership Skills**: The experiences gained during his military service likely influenced his leadership style in business and politics.
