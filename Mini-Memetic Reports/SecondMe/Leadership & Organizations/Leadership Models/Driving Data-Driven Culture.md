## Insight
In this episode of the D-Suite podcast, we delve into the insights from Gartner's Data and Analytics Summit in London, focusing on the pivotal role of the Chief Data and Analytics Officer (CDAO) in fostering culture change and creating value within organizations. JP Bhamu, CDAO at GEMS Education, shares his strategies for integrating data-driven culture into business strategy, emphasizing the importance of regular CEO communication, domain knowledge, empathy, and collaboration. The discussion highlights the significant impact of these efforts on business outcomes at GEMS Education, including financial savings, strategic business decisions, and improved academic results. Key takeaways include the necessity of understanding organizational culture, building a strong team of evangelists, and delivering tangible results to drive meaningful change. This episode offers actionable insights for CDAOs and other leaders aiming to leverage data for strategic advantage.
