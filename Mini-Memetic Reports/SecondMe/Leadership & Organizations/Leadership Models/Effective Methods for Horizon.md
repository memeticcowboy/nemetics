## Methods of Conducting Horizon Scanning

Horizon scanning is a strategic foresight method used to identify early signals of change, emerging trends, and potential future developments.

### **1. Defining the Scope and Objectives**
- **Define the Topic:** Clearly articulate the topic or scope of interest to guide the scanning process..
- **Set Objectives:** Determine what you aim to achieve with the horizon scanning exercise, such as identifying emerging risks, opportunities, or research questions.

### **2. Expanding Perspectives**
- **Broaden Your View:** Use frameworks like STEEPLE (Societal, Technological, Economic, Environmental, Political, Legal, Ethical) or PESTLE (Political, Economic, Societal, Technological, Legal, Environmental) to consider a wide range of potential sources of future risk.
- **Diversity of Perspectives:** Engage a diverse group of stakeholders with different disciplines, professions, and backgrounds to ensure a comprehensive view.

### **3. Data Collection and Analysis**
- **Scan the Environment:** Identify trends, weak signals, and wild cards by reviewing industry reports, market research studies, scientific publications, news media, and other relevant sources.
- **Crowd-Sourcing Information:** Utilize communities of practice to gather and verify information, ensuring a broad and inclusive data collection process.
- **Text Mining:** Employ tools for text mining of available literature, patents, and funding datasets to identify emerging trends and innovations.

### **4. Collaborative Review and Synthesis**
- **Collaborate and Combine:** Collate and synthesize the collected data, presenting it back to the group for discussion and further analysis.
- **Structured Expert Elicitation:** Use structured methods like the Delphi technique to aggregate expert judgments and improve the reliability of the findings.

### **5. Visualization and Communication**
- **Visualize Results:** Use tools like foresight radars or risk radars to categorize and visualize the identified trends and phenomena.
- **Document the Process:** Keep a detailed record of the scanning process, findings, and methodologies used to ensure transparency and reproducibility.

### **6. Formulating Action Steps**
- **Develop Proactive Strategies:** Use the insights gained to develop strategies that leverage upcoming opportunities or mitigate potential challenges.
- **Scenario Planning:** Build scenarios based on the identified trends to explore possible future states and plan accordingly.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** Make horizon scanning a continuous activity to keep the findings relevant and updated.

### **7. Application and Feedback**
- **Inform Strategy:** Use the results to inform strategic planning and decision-making processes.
- **Engage Stakeholders:** Mobilize stakeholders to take action based on the identified trends and insights.
