**🏡 Prefigurative Community Building: A Path to Housing Justice**

- **🌱 Embodiment of Future Values**: Prefigurative community building emphasizes living out the ideals of a future society in the present. This approach encourages communities to adopt values such as inclusivity, sustainability, and mutual care, which are essential for fostering social change. By embodying these values now, communities can create a foundation for a more equitable future.

- **🏘️ Intentional Community Building**: The focus on creating intentional communities allows individuals to collectively own and manage their housing. This model promotes democratic participation and shared stewardship, enabling residents to resist exploitation and prioritize their needs. Housing cooperatives serve as a powerful alternative to traditional ownership models, emphasizing community over profit.

- **✊ Grassroots Activism**: Grassroots activism is central to prefigurative community building, as it encourages local, participatory actions that drive social change. By fostering relationships of accountability and care, communities can empower marginalized groups, ensuring that everyone has a voice in the decision-making process.

**🛠️ Steps to Start a Housing Cooperative**

- **👥 Forming a Founding Circle**: The first step involves gathering a diverse group of individuals who share a common need for housing justice. This foundational circle should prioritize trust and shared experiences, ensuring that every voice is heard and valued. Listening circles can help articulate collective needs and aspirations.

- **🏗️ Deciding on Housing Project Type**: Groups must collaboratively determine the type of housing project they wish to create, such as limited-equity cooperatives or community land trusts. This decision should reflect the group's values and the specific needs of vulnerable populations, ensuring inclusivity in the housing model.

- **🏞️ Securing Land or Property**: Identifying and securing land is often the most challenging aspect of starting a housing cooperative. Strategies may include partnering with land trusts, pooling resources, or advocating for municipal support. Creative solutions like leaseholds or building takeovers can also be explored to overcome barriers to ownership.

**📜 Governance and Accessibility**

- **⚖️ Creating a Governance Structure**: Establishing a governance structure based on the principle of "Power With" is crucial. This involves using consensus or sociocracy for decision-making, distributing roles equitably, and fostering transparency. Such structures help avoid hierarchical models that can perpetuate power imbalances.

- **🚪 Ensuring Accessibility for All**: A key tenet of a "Power With" housing cooperative is to ensure that housing is accessible to those most at risk, including the unhoused and low-income individuals. This can be achieved by reserving spaces for marginalized groups and avoiding exclusionary practices like background checks.

**💰 Funding and Sustainability**

- **💵 Securing Funding Autonomously**: Funding should be pursued in a way that maintains the cooperative's autonomy. Options include grassroots fundraising, cooperative banks, and grants from aligned foundations. It is essential to ensure that external funding does not compromise the cooperative's decision-making power.

- **📚 Documenting and Celebrating Success**: Documenting the cooperative's journey is vital for sharing knowledge and replicating success. Celebrating milestones through communal events fosters a sense of belonging and joy, reinforcing the cooperative's role as a supportive community space.

**🌍 Real-World Examples of Power With Housing Projects**

- **🏡 East Bay Permanent Real Estate Cooperative**: This Black-led cooperative in California focuses on removing land from the speculative market and redistributing power to working-class residents of color. It employs collective governance and equity structures to promote housing justice.

- **🇬🇧 Sun Housing Co-op**: Located in London, this member-led initiative operates under sociocratic principles, allowing members to pay a flat rate of their income as rent. The co-op emphasizes equality and diversity, ensuring long-term, affordable housing solutions.

**🤝 Repairing Home, Together**

- **🏠 Housing as Belonging**: The concept of housing extends beyond mere shelter; it encompasses belonging and community. By organizing housing cooperatives as "Power With" systems, communities can reweave the social fabric that supports safety and connection, shifting the focus from ownership to collective care. This approach lays the groundwork for a future rooted in dignity and interdependence.
