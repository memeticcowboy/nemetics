# Refining User Prompts for Neutrality and Evidence-Based Responses

Created at 2025/03/09 7:49 AM

Refine the following user-provided prompt to ensure it is neutral, precise, and evidence-based while minimizing speculation or bias. Apply the following principles:
1. Define Terms & Scope
• Ensure key terms are explicitly defined to avoid ambiguity.
• Clarify the scope by specifying relevant constraints (e.g., time frame, domain, sources).
2. Require Evidence-Based Responses
• Modify the prompt to explicitly request peer-reviewed research, official documentation, or empirical studies rather than assumptions.
• Reframe vague or speculative questions into fact-based inquiries.
• Example: Instead of “How does X work?”, ask “Explain the mechanisms of X based on documented research.”
3. Encourage Comparative or Multi-Perspective Analysis
• Ensure the revised prompt presents multiple viewpoints rather than assuming a specific conclusion.
• Example: Instead of “Why does X lead to Y?”, use “Evaluate whether X influences Y, considering both supporting and opposing evidence.”
4. Structure Responses to Separate Fact from Interpretation
• Format the output to distinguish cited evidence from analysis, such as:
• Observations (cited facts)
• Analysis (interpretation based on sources)
• Confidence Level (scale of 1–5 with justification)
5. Minimize Leading Language or Assumptions
• Remove phrasing that implies a predetermined outcome.
• Example:
• ❌ “Why does AI cause unemployment?”
• ✅ “What are the documented effects of AI on employment across different industries?”
6. Constrain Speculation & Require Justification
• If likelihood assessments are requested, specify a scaling system (e.g., 1–5, low-medium-high) with justification.
• Example: “On a scale of 1–5, assess the probability of X occurring based on available data.”
