## The Importance of Collective Infrastructure

- Good intentions alone are insufficient; what matters is the infrastructure we build to sustain those intentions over time.
- The fragility of our collectives reflects unexamined assumptions about shared power.

## Power With: A Practice Beyond an Idea

- Power With should be more than just a concept or feeling; it needs to be a repeatable and adaptable practice.
- This practice involves being together without domination, emphasizing collaboration and shared responsibility.

## Emotional, Political, and Ethical Dimensions

- Building sustainable power is not merely a technical task; it encompasses emotional, political, and ethical considerations.
- The future of movements may hinge on our ability to approach this task with care, clarity, and courage.

## Meeting the Challenge

- Addressing the fragility of collectives requires confronting unexamined assumptions.
- Emphasizing the need for courage, clarity, and care in our collective efforts is essential for long-term sustainability.
