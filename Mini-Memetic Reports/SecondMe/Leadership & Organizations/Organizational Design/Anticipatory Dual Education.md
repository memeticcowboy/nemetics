## Insight
This paper explores anticipatory dual education in Kazakhstan, focusing on a systemic approach to enhance students' competencies for industrial-innovative transformations. It highlights the importance of aligning education with industry needs and emphasizes the development of systemic thinking as a crucial skill for future professionals. The findings suggest that integrating education and industry can significantly improve workforce readiness. For those involved in educational reform, consider how to implement systemic thinking in curricula to better prepare students for the evolving job market.
