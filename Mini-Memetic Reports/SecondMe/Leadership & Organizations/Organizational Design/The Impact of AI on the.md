# The Impact of AI on the Consulting Industry: Opportunities and Challenges

Created at 2025/07/03 5:05 PM

[Summary of the YouTube Video: “AI Daily Brief - The Disruption of Consulting by AI”](https://youtu.be/3DPxhKgsKA8?si=uCGyVetxoAR73PLf)

Introduction

The video explores the transformative impact of AI on the consulting industry, highlighting how consulting firms like Accenture and McKinsey are benefitting from the AI boom while simultaneously facing existential threats from AI technologies. The discussion focuses on the rise of consulting services provided by AI companies such as OpenAI, the evolution of roles like Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), and the implications for traditional consulting firms.

The Current State of Consulting in AI

	•	Growth in Consulting Revenue: Consulting firms have seen significant revenue growth from AI-related engagements, with AI being a pivotal component of their new business strategies.

	•	Existential Threat: Despite short-term gains, consulting firms face a potential existential threat as AI and large language models (LLMs) can perform tasks traditionally done by human experts. The shift in demand for services represents a challenge to the knowledge-based business model of consulting.

The Rise of AI Consulting Services

	•	OpenAI’s Strategy: OpenAI is expanding into consulting-like services, offering fine-tuning for its models based on company-specific data. This move positions them in competition with traditional consulting firms by providing tailored AI solutions directly to enterprises.

	•	Fine-Tuning Explained: Fine-tuning involves modifying AI models to better fit the unique data and operational needs of specific companies, enhancing the relevance and effectiveness of AI solutions.

	•	Investment Required: OpenAI has set a high financial threshold for its consulting services, often requiring clients to invest at least $10 million.

The Role of Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)

	•	Definition and Responsibilities: FDEs are engineers who work closely with clients to integrate and customize AI solutions rapidly. They differ from traditional consultants by focusing on specific software platforms and delivering tailored solutions efficiently.

	•	Palantir’s Model: The concept of FDEs originated with Palantir, where engineers embed with clients to leverage existing software tools to solve complex problems. This model allows for rapid deployment and customization without reinventing the wheel.

	•	Comparative Advantages: FDEs can quickly implement software solutions compared to traditional consulting approaches, which are often slower and more cumbersome due to extensive scoping and customization processes.

Market Trends and Shifts

	•	Changing Dynamics: The consulting market is experiencing a shift where technology providers are becoming service companies, creating a blend of software and consulting services.

	•	Historic Context: The rise of product-led growth (PLG) strategies in software has led to a reevaluation of traditional consulting models. Companies like Salesforce and ServiceNow have succeeded with complex implementations requiring significant service and support.

	•	Impact of AI: AI is changing how integration and implementation work are performed, automating many of the tasks previously done manually, which could lead to lower margins but enhanced customer retention due to deeper integration.

OpenAI’s Business Strategy

	•	Partnerships: OpenAI’s business strategy includes forming partnerships with various implementation firms (like Tribe AAI and Fractional) to support customers from ideation to production.

	•	Competition with Traditional Firms: While OpenAI’s expansion into consulting services does create competition with firms like Accenture and PwC, the presenter argues that OpenAI’s goal is not solely to compete but to capture the customer relationship by providing robust AI solutions.

Price Pressure in Consulting

	•	Reduced Costs: As AI technologies improve efficiencies, consulting firms are under pressure to reduce their prices. For instance, PwC’s AI chief mentioned that their firm has begun to lower prices due to the efficiencies gained from AI.

	•	Impact on Revenue: The downward pressure on pricing could significantly impact revenue streams for consulting firms that rely on traditional hourly billing models and extensive discovery processes.

Future of Consulting

	•	Evolution of the Industry: Consulting as a profession is expected to evolve significantly in the coming years. Firms that can adapt quickly to incorporate AI-driven solutions will likely thrive, while others may struggle to remain relevant.

	•	The Role of Human Expertise: Despite the rise of AI, there remains a need for human expertise in high-value consulting tasks that produce tangible results. The ability to integrate AI into these tasks will be crucial for consultants moving forward.

Conclusion

The video concludes by reiterating the substantial changes AI is bringing to the consulting industry. Firms will need to adapt to survive, leveraging new technologies and models to meet the evolving demands of their clients. The adoption of roles like Forward Deployed Engineers and the incorporation of AI consulting services signify a shift in how consulting will operate in the future, marking a potential renaissance or disruption for traditional consulting firms.

This summary captures the key points and arguments presented in the video, providing a comprehensive overview of the implications of AI on the consulting industry.
