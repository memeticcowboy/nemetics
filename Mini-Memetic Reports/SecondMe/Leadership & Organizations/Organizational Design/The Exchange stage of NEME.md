## The Exchange stage of NEME

Nemetic Exchange is the final stage of the NEME cycle, where the value created in the previous Mull stage is offered for implementation, exchange, feedback, and learning. The exchange is consistent with the will and intention expressed in the creation state, making it 'authentically aligned.' 

The value is exchanged through resonance of specific NemeStrings both in the inner and outer 'worlds' that then get entangled with the individual and collective Neme cycles to change the collective consciousness or collective representation in order to drive new behaviors, actions resulting in either successes or failures triggering new Neme cycles of cognition, emotions, thoughts and learning through insights and reflection starting off new cycles of reflection, exploration and exploitation to effect quantum leaps in collective consciousness though the engagement would be more complex and possibly chaotic than the previous cycles.

Essentially, Nemetic Exchange is the process of sharing and receiving nemes, or quanta of energy, with others. This exchange can happen between people, places, or things, and it creates a web of interconnectedness and meaning.

When we engage in Nemetic Exchange, we are not only sharing information or ideas; we are also sharing our energy and our intentions. This can be a powerful experience, as it allows us to connect with others on a deeper level and to create something new and meaningful together.

The NEME cycle is a dynamic process that is constantly evolving. As we learn and grow, our understanding of Nemetics and Nemetic Exchange will also deepen. This is an exciting journey, and we are grateful to be a part of it.
