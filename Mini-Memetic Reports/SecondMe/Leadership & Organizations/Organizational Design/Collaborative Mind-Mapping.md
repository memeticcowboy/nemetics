# Collaborative Mind-Mapping Process: Proposal to Implementation

Created at 2024/07/06 2:17 PM

## 1. Introduction
- Setting up the Project Context
  - Brief project outcomes and rationale in shared Google Drive document
- Introducing the Characters and Setting
  - Introduce team members and establish shared Google Drive Hub

## 2. Proposal and Planning
- Proposing Prompts and Timeline
  - Draft checkpoint review timeline for the project
- Collaboration and Review
  - Team collaborates to review and refine proposed structure and timeline

## 3. Review and Feedback
- Providing Assessment and Suggestions
  - Offer revision suggestions on the proposed structure and timeline
- Iterative Refinement
  - Collaborate iteratively to refine the structure based on feedback

## 4. Finalization
- Finalizing the Plan
  - Finalize the knowledge management structure and timeline
- Confirmation and Agreement
  - Confirm agreement on finalized plan and prepare for implementation

## 5. Implementation
- Executing the Project Plan
  - Begin implementing the knowledge management system
- Monitoring Progress
  - Conduct regular check-ins and updates to monitor progress

## 6. Evaluation
- Assessing Project Outcomes
  - Evaluate effectiveness of the knowledge management structure
- Gathering Feedback
  - Collect feedback to identify areas for improvement

## 7. Reflection
- Reflecting on the Experience
  - Team reflects on working together and lessons learned
- Identifying Key Takeaways
  - Document key insights for future reference

## 8. Documentation
- Documenting Project Documentation
  - Compile and store finalized structure, timeline, and outcomes
- Archiving Project Materials
  - Archive all project-related materials for future reference

## 9. Closing
- Wrapping Up the Project
  - Officially close the project and complete any remaining tasks
- Expressing Gratitude
  - Team expresses gratitude for contributions and collaboration
