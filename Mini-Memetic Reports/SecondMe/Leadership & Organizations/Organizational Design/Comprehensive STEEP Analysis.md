# Comprehensive STEEP Analysis Guide for Business Proposals

Created at 2024/07/19 10:28 PM

Prompt:
Conduct a comprehensive STEEP (Social, Technological, Economic, Environmental, Political) analysis for [your proposal or business idea] following these steps:

1. Introduction:
   - Briefly describe your proposal
   - State the goal of balancing comprehensiveness with usability in your analysis

2. Process Overview:
   - List factors for each STEEP category
   - Rate importance (High, Medium, Low) and impact (Positive, Neutral, Negative) for each factor
   - Focus on high-importance factors for deeper analysis
   - Summarize key findings for each category

3. STEEP Categories and Factors:
   Analyze each category in detail:

   3.1 Social Factors:
   - Demographics and consumer behavior
   - Cultural trends and values
   - Education and health trends

   3.2 Technological Factors:
   - Relevant technological advancements
   - Digital transformation in your industry
   - R&D and innovation landscape

   3.3 Economic Factors:
   - Economic growth and market conditions
   - Inflation and interest rates
   - Labor market and costs

   3.4 Environmental Factors:
   - Sustainability trends and regulations
   - Climate change impacts
   - Resource availability and management

   3.5 Political Factors:
   - Regulatory environment
   - Political stability and policies
   - International relations and trade policies

4. Analysis Template:
   Use a table format with columns for Factor, Importance (H/M/L), Impact (+/0/-), and Notes

5. Conducting the Analysis:
   - List relevant factors for each STEEP category
   - Rate importance and impact
   - Add notes on potential implications
   - Identify high-importance factors for deeper analysis
   - Research and analyze high-importance factors in detail

6. Summarizing Findings:
   - Review completed analysis for each category
   - Identify key findings, focusing on high-importance factors
   - Summarize implications for the proposal
   - Highlight potential opportunities and challenges

7. Decision Making:
   - Review summary of findings
   - Assess overall viability of the proposal based on STEEP analysis
   - Identify areas requiring further investigation or mitigation strategies
   - Make an informed decision on proceeding with, modifying, or rejecting the proposal

Present your analysis in a clear, structured format that allows for easy review and decision-making.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
