# Implementing Humanocracy Principles

Created at 2024/08/20 10:47 AM

## Establishing Micro-Enterprises
- Break large departments into small, autonomous teams of 10-15 people.
- Each team should have its own P&L responsibility.
- Grant teams the authority to make decisions regarding budgets, hiring, and strategy for their area.

## Internal Market System
- Create a system where teams can bid on projects and resources.
- Allow employees to allocate a portion of their time (e.g., 20%) to projects outside their core role that they are passionate about.

## Peer-Based Reputation System
- Replace traditional performance reviews with a peer-based rating system.
- Employees rate each other on skills, collaboration, and impact.
- Use this data for compensation and promotion decisions instead of relying solely on manager assessments.

## Challenge Process
- Establish a process where any employee can contest decisions made by leadership.
- Create a panel of randomly selected employees to hear challenges and make binding rulings.

## Eliminating Job Titles and Formal Hierarchies
- Remove job titles and formal hierarchies to foster a more egalitarian work environment.
