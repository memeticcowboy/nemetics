This is the Great Decoupling! It's time for a divorce! 

Phase 1: Coming to terms with the end of this relationship. It's toxic and codependent. Business no longer wants employees, and employees don't want to work. Let's call it quits ASAP. 

Phase 2: Divorce proceedings. Who gets the house? Who gets the dogs? What about alimony? The division of assets as well as a "soft landing" for both parties is the next phase. 

Phase 3: The "new normal" of life after separation. Some alimony (UBI) might be necessary for a while, perhaps indefinitely (the jury is literally and figuratively still out). But we'll find new paradigms. 

One key consideration is what I call the economic agency paradox. If, indeed, we humans lose access to capital (money) then we also lose the ability to demand goods and services. The very machines that have replaced us will be producing goods and services that no one can afford! 

Markets tend to adjust. 

But also, people are going to be sitting around with a whole lot of time (and brain cycles) to spend. This is called cognitive surplus, and I have a great way to allocate this! 

Consider the institutions of democracy, rule of law, and free market capitalism. People are going to want to do something get more material support, even if they have UBI or "UHI" as proposed by Elon Musk. The fundamental social contract of "you have a right to exchange labor for wages" will break down because no one will want to purchase your labor soon!

However, civil society has many more institutions, such as property rights, taxes, and entitlements. For instance, if you own a house, you still have a right to rent that house out to people for rent. If you have a farm, you can still sell produce. 

This leads to a paradigm I call hyper-capitalism, but not like the Ferengi in Star Trek. What I mean by hyper-capitalism is that capital is pretty much all that matters in this future. Everything comes down to markets. X is the "marketplace of ideas" and you actually get paid for contributing here! 

The ongoing need to allocate assets (money, energy, resources, and even attention) can all be marketized in what I call Post-Labor Economics, and technologies such as AI and blockchain will be critical to securitizing everything. Some people will still engage in rent-seeking behaviors, like selling NFTs (why???) but the vast majority of IP will flow freely and we'll have a sort of "back to basics" once we solve the crypto/reality divide--tokenizing real economic assets and distributing ownership and management. 

Think of it this way: if AI is smart enough to displace every worker, and even crack CEOs, don't you think it's smart enough to run the economy without human intervention? We simply delegate our preferences and values into the nebula and away it goes. (Okay, I'm making it seem overly simple, but from a UX perspective, it literally will be that simple.
