# Microsoft-OpenAI Partnership Faces Strain Amid Rivalry

Created at 2025/01/23 6:10 PM

## Summary

Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff suggests that Microsoft's relationship with OpenAI has deteriorated, particularly after Microsoft hired Mustafa Suleyman, a rival of OpenAI's Sam Altman. This move, along with OpenAI's new partnerships and Microsoft's development of its own AI models, indicates a potential shift away from the exclusive partnership.
