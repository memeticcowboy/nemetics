# Reframed Proposal: Exercising Global Brain Ethics through EM Synthesis, CLA, and Cynefin

[via x/Bob-RJ](https://x.com/BurkhartRj/status/1896565864780230922)

This proposal synthesizes the **Ends-Means (EM)** framework to align ethical goals (ends) with actionable strategies (means) across four key futures wheel implications: **Extractive Economics, Energy Futures, Futures, and YOU/nity enJOYable Environment.** Each implication is analyzed using an augmented **Causal-Layered Analysis (CLA)** to explore its visible outcomes (litany), **systemic causes, worldview, and myths/metaphors**. <br><br>**The Cynefin Framework** contextualizes these implications into appropriate domains—clear, complicated, complex, chaotic, or confusion—to determine the best intervention strategies. At its core, **Global Brain Ethics (GBE)** ensures that all strategies uphold honor, respect, and trust, navigating the spectrum from **"The world of STRIFE shut out" (WAR) to "The world of LOVE shut in" (PEACE).**

## Framework Overview

For each futures wheel implication, the following steps are applied:

1. **Augmented Causal-Layered Analysis (CLA):**

    - Litany: Surface-level events or outcomes.
    - Systemic Causes: Underlying structures or systems.
    - Worldview: Beliefs or ideologies driving the system.
    - Myth/Metaphor: Deep narratives shaping perceptions.

2. **Global Brain Ethics (GBE) Alignment:**

    - Define ethical ends rooted in honor, respect, and trust.

3. **Ends-Means (EM) Synthesis:**

    - Identify means (strategies) that align with the ethical ends.

4. **Cynefin Contextualization:**

    - Categorize the implication into a Cynefin domain to guide action:
        - Clear: Apply best practices.
        - Complicated: Use expert analysis.
        - Complex: Experiment and adapt.
        - Chaotic: Take immediate stabilizing action.
        - Confusion: Diagnose further.

This framework ensures a holistic approach to ethical decision-making, balancing individual and collective well-being.

# Application to the Four Futures Wheel Implications

## 1. YOU/nity enJOYable Environment Implications Wheel

- **CLA Analysis:**
    - Litany: Sustainable well-being, ALL-WIN games, education.
    - Systemic Causes: Interdependent systems, competent caring communities.
    - Worldview: Collective success through mutual respect and trust.
    - Myth/Metaphor: "The world of LOVE shut in" – a narrative of harmony and connection.
- GBE-Aligned Ends: A sustainable, harmonious future where communities thrive collectively.
- Means (Strategies):
    - Develop education programs promoting collaboration and sustainability.
    - Support community networks that share resources equitably.
    - Design "ALL-WIN" initiatives that reward collective achievements.
- Cynefin Domain: Complex – Requires experimentation (e.g., pilot community projects) and adaptation to evolving social dynamics.
- Synthesis: Foster adaptive, community-led initiatives that align with GBE, such as local sustainability hubs, with continuous feedback to refine outcomes.

## 2. Futures Implications Wheel

- CLA Analysis:
    - Litany: Innovation, foresight, future-oriented outcomes.
    - Systemic Causes: Systems prioritizing adaptability and long-term thinking.
    - Worldview: Faith in collective intelligence and ethical progress.
    - Myth/Metaphor: "TRUE NORTH THRUST" – a guiding force toward ethical futures.
- GBE-Aligned Ends: An innovative future guided by ethical foresight and shared wisdom.
- Means (Strategies):
    - Implement futures literacy workshops for diverse stakeholders.
    - Facilitate participatory scenario planning to align innovation with GBE.
    - Embed ethical guidelines in technology development.
- Cynefin Domain: Complicated – Needs structured expert analysis to balance innovation with ethical constraints.
- Synthesis: Use expert-led foresight tools to create ethical innovation strategies, ensuring alignment with honor, respect, and trust.

## 3. Energy Futures Implications Wheel

- CLA Analysis:
    - Litany: Zero-sum games, manifest destiny, resource depletion.
    - Systemic Causes: Command-control systems, extractive energy policies.
    - Worldview: Dominance over nature for short-term gain.
    - Myth/Metaphor: "The world of STRIFE shut out" – a narrative of conflict and exploitation.
- GBE-Aligned Ends: Equitable, sustainable energy systems that respect planetary limits.
- Means (Strategies):
    - Advocate for renewable energy policies with ethical incentives.
    - Educate communities on sustainable energy benefits.
    - Fund community-owned energy projects to shift from extractive models.
- Cynefin Domain: Complicated/Complex – Requires expert policy design (complicated) and adaptive community engagement (complex).
- Synthesis: Combine technical expertise with grassroots pilots (e.g., solar co-ops) to transition energy systems ethically.

#### 4. Extractive Economics Implications Wheel

- CLA Analysis:
    - Litany: Wealth disparity, environmental harm, social inequity.
    - Systemic Causes: Profit-driven capitalist structures.
    - Worldview: Growth and individualism above all.
    - Myth/Metaphor: "Duality of INTENT VIOLENCE with LOVE" – exploitation masked as progress.
- GBE-Aligned Ends: An equitable economy prioritizing sustainability and collective well-being.
- Means (Strategies):
    - Promote circular economy models (e.g., recycling, regeneration).
    - Enact policies for ethical wealth redistribution.
    - Support social enterprises focused on community and environmental health.
- Cynefin Domain: Chaotic/Complex – Immediate action for crises (chaotic) and long-term systemic reform (complex).
- Synthesis: Address urgent inequities with stabilizing measures (e.g., aid), while testing systemic shifts like universal basic income.

# General Framework for Application

To apply this framework broadly:

1. CLA Analysis:

    - Map the implication across litany, systemic causes, worldview, and myth/metaphor.

2. GBE-Aligned Ends:

    - Set goals reflecting honor, respect, and trust.

3. EM Synthesis:

    - Choose strategies aligning means with ends, considering stakeholders and barriers.

4. Cynefin Contextualization:

    - Clear: Use proven solutions.
    - Complicated: Leverage expertise.
    - Complex: Experiment iteratively.
    - Chaotic: Stabilize first.
    - Confusion: Clarify through diagnosis.

5. Actionable Strategies:

    - Integrate insights into tailored plans, adjusting based on feedback.
