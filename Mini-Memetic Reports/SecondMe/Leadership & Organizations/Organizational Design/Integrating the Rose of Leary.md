## Integrating Rose of Leary with NEMAtics

Here's how I would approach integrating the Rose of Leary model with the NEMAtics framework:

### 1. Mapping Six Cyborgs to the Rose of Leary

Each Cyborg archetype might naturally align with certain positions on the Rose:

- **Analyzer** (🧠): Tends toward "Above" position (analytical, fact-oriented)
- **Protector** (🛡️): Often operates in "Above-Against" (protective, boundary-setting)
- **Optimizer** (⚙️): Functions in "Above-Together" (improving, guiding)
- **Empath** (💗): Naturally aligns with "Together" (cooperating, connecting)
- **Strategist** (🧭): Can shift between "Above" and "Together" depending on context
- **Creator** (🎨): Might move between "Above-Together" (inspirational) and "Against" (challenging norms)

### 2. NEME Cycle as a Tool for Shifting Positions

The NEME cycle could be used as a framework for mindfully shifting positions on the Rose:

- **Notice**: Become aware of your current position and others' positions
- **Engage**: Actively interact with others from your current position
- **Mull**: Reflect on the effectiveness of your position and consider alternatives
- **Exchange**: Implement a position shift or deepen your current position

### 3. Elemental Realms as Contextual Influence

The three elemental realms might influence how positions on the Rose manifest:

- **Sky** (past, pattern): How historical interactions influence current positioning
- **Earth** (present, possibility): How embodied, in-the-moment dynamics affect positioning
- **Sea** (future, preferability): How desired outcomes influence position choices

### 4. Triadic Rituals as Group Dynamics

The eight Triadic Rituals could be mapped to specific group interaction patterns on the Rose, showing how different combinations of behavioral styles create distinctive group dynamics.

### 5. Practical Application Framework

Here's a structured approach for applying this integrated model:

1. **Identify Current Cyborg Mode**: Which Cyborg archetype are you primarily operating from?
2. **Assess Rose Position**: Where on the Rose of Leary does this place you?
3. **Notice Complementary Responses**: How are others responding to your position?
4. **Apply NEME Cycle**: Use Notice-Engage-Mull-Exchange to assess effectiveness
5. **Consider Elemental Influences**: How are Sky (past), Earth (present), and Sea (future) factors affecting interactions?
6. **Shift Positioning if Needed**: Intentionally adopt a different Cyborg mode to shift your position on the Rose
