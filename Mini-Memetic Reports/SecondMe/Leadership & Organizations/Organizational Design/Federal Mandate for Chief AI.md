**🤖 Federal AI Governance: New Directives from OMB**

- **Chief AI Officers Appointment**: Federal agencies are mandated to appoint Chief AI Officers within 60 days. This requirement aims to establish a dedicated leadership role responsible for overseeing AI initiatives, ensuring accountability, and promoting strategic governance in AI deployments across federal operations.

- **Comprehensive AI Strategies Development**: Agencies must develop and publicly post comprehensive AI strategies within 180 days. This initiative is designed to standardize AI adoption and ensure that all federal operations utilize AI responsibly and effectively, aligning with ethical standards and public trust.

- **High-Impact AI Definition**: The introduction of "high-impact AI" signifies a focus on AI systems that significantly affect rights or safety. Agencies are required to implement specific considerations and requirements for these systems, ensuring that their deployment is both responsible and transparent.
