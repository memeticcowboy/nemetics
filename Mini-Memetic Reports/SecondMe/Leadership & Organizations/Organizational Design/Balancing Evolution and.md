# Balancing Evolution and Revolution in Community Building

Created at 2025/07/14 5:57 PM

## The Importance of Controlled Change
- Emphasizing the need for a balance between rapid change and gradual evolution.
- The metaphor of "controlled burns" highlights the idea of introducing change in small, intentional ways.
- Change should respect existing community roots rather than causing disruption.

## Active Patience in Change
- Patience is crucial in community building, but it should be "active patience."
- Engaging with the community while waiting for the right moment to act is essential.
- This approach keeps the momentum alive and prepares for timely interventions.

## Resources on Intentional Transformation Strategies
- **“Emergent Strategy” by adrienne maree brown**: Focuses on organic, adaptive change inspired by nature.
- **“Switch: How to Change Things When Change Is Hard” by Chip & Dan Heath**: Offers frameworks for guiding change through small wins and emotional engagement.
- **“The Art of Gathering” by Priya Parker**: Explores intentional design and facilitation for meaningful group experiences.
- **“Theory U” by Otto Scharmer**: Provides a process for leading profound change through deep listening and co-creation.
- **“Creative Destruction” by Richard Foster & Sarah Kaplan**: Discusses managing the balance between stability and innovation in organizations. 

These resources can help deepen understanding of intentional transformation strategies in community contexts.
