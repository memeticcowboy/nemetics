# Integrating RCISAction with COBIT5, Sensemaking, and the OODA Loop for Effective IT Governance

Created at 2024/09/08 12:57 PM

Relating **RCISAction** to **COBIT5**, **sensemaking**, and the **OODA loop**, we can draw several parallels based on their shared focus on **continuous improvement**, **coordination**, and **systematic governance**.

### 1. **Cyclical and Recursive Improvement** (RCISAction) vs. **OODA Loop** and **COBIT5**

- **RCISAction's cyclical approach** mirrors the iterative nature of the **OODA loop** (Observe-Orient-Decide-Act), where continuous observation and refinement are key to staying adaptive. In both frameworks, each cycle builds on previous learnings, leading to ongoing optimization.
- In **COBIT5**, this recursive improvement is evident in its focus on **continuous governance enhancements**—adapting policies and processes in response to new risks and opportunities, much like the iterative cycles in RCISAction.

### 2. **Incremental and Manageable Changes** (RCISAction) vs. **Sensemaking**

- **Sensemaking** in IT governance involves breaking down complex problems into manageable pieces, similar to RCISAction's emphasis on **incremental improvements**. This allows leaders to make sense of evolving situations (e.g., AI risks) in smaller, understandable segments, which helps reduce inconsistencies and enhance decision-making.
- **COBIT5** also promotes **incremental governance changes**, enabling organizations to adapt gradually rather than making disruptive, large-scale changes, which aligns with RCISAction’s strategy of **manageable progress**.

### 3. **Coordinated and Synchronous Advancements** (RCISAction) vs. **COBIT5 Governance and Risk Management**

- RCISAction’s focus on **coordinated and synchronized improvements** parallels **COBIT5’s holistic governance framework**, where risk management, IT alignment, and business objectives must be coordinated across the entire organization. Both frameworks emphasize that changes must be harmonized across departments to avoid conflicts and ensure sustainable progress.
- The **OODA loop** also requires that decisions and actions be synchronized with the changing environment. This synchronization ensures that all parts of the system—whether in governance, risk management, or innovation—move in concert to achieve optimal results.

### Conclusion:

Both **RCISAction** and **COBIT5** emphasize **systematic, incremental improvements** with a focus on **coordination and synchronization**, which align well with **sensemaking** and the **OODA loop’s cyclical and adaptive nature**. Together, these frameworks enable organizations to manage IT governance, adapt to innovation, and mitigate risks in a structured, manageable, and sustainable way.
