Business Strategy: What is Chaffee's linear, adaptive, and interpretive models?
Chaffee's model categorizes strategic models into three types: linear, adaptive, and interpretive. The Linear model views strategy as a rational process, involving systematic planning and goal-setting. The Adaptive model emphasizes flexibility and responsiveness to changing environments, suggesting strategies evolve through learning and adaptation. The Interpretive model focuses on the social construction of reality, highlighting the role of language and symbols in shaping strategic actions and organizational identity. Each model offers a different perspective on how strategies are formed and implemented within organizations.
