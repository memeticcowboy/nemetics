  - Participants were encouraged to share their experiences and insights, fostering a collaborative learning environment.

- **Identifying Root Causes**: The workshop emphasized the importance of discovering the 'Causes behind the apparent cause' of reliability issues.

  - Simplifying complex issues allows teams to focus on actionable steps that can be quickly implemented.
