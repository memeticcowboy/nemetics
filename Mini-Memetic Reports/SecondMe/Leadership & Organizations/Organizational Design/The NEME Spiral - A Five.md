# The NEME Spiral: A Five-Phase Process for Meaningful Change

Created at 2025/06/27 9:30 AM

[x/Change w/Bob-RJ and Raj](https://x.com/rajprasad3251/status/1938627859104649645?s=46&t=7Z-E-ACnGlzdI-iyUwNCrQ)

The NEME Spiral is a five-phase process for sensing, understanding, and transforming complex situations. Think of it as a cycle that guides a group or system through meaningful change. Here are the five phases in everyday terms:

- **NOTICE**<br>
    - You pay close attention to what’s happening.
    - This is where you spot early signals, notice patterns, and sense what might be emerging before it’s obvious.
    - It’s about listening deeply—not jumping to conclusions.
- **ENGAGE**<br>
    - You get involved and start to frame what matters.
    - This phase is about clarifying principles, aligning around shared values, and making sure everyone understands what’s at stake.
    - It’s more than agreeing intellectually—it’s about feeling the purpose together.
    - 
- **MULL**<br>
    - You reflect without rushing to fix.
    - Here, you hold complexity and contradictions long enough for deeper insights to emerge.
    - It’s like letting things compost until you see what needs to grow.
    - 
- **ENACT**<br>
    - You put your insights into action.
    - This isn’t about random doing—it’s about coordinated, meaningful performance that expresses what you’ve discovered.
    - Think of it as ritualized action that carries your intention into the world.
    - 
- **REVELATION**<br>
    - You step back to see what you’ve learned and how things have changed.
    - This phase reveals deeper patterns, uncovers hidden assumptions, and helps you reset or evolve your approach.
    - It often feels like a fresh perspective breaking through.

In short, the NEME Spiral helps any group or initiative:

- Notice what is emerging,
- Engage and align around shared meaning,
- Reflect and digest complexity,
- Enact coherent action, and
- Reveal new possibilities to begin the cycle again
