To identify TAM (Total Addressable Market), SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market), and SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market), consider the following key questions:

## TAM Questions

- What is the total size of the market for this particular product or service?[1]

- Who are all the potential customers for this product or service, regardless of current engagement?[1]

- What is the revenue opportunity if the company had 100% market share with no competition?[2]

## SAM Questions

- What portion of the TAM can our business realistically serve given our current capabilities?[1]

- What are the specific market segments we can target effectively?[4]

- What limitations (e.g., geography, pricing, distribution) affect our ability to serve the market?[1]

## SOM Questions

- What percentage of the SAM can we realistically capture given our resources and competitive landscape?[1]

- What is our current market share, and how might it grow in the near term?[2]

- What specific, achievable goals can we set for market capture?[4]

## General Market Analysis Questions

- What is the market doing now? Are there any trends or changes?[6]

- What do our competitors do well or poorly?[6]

- Are there any gaps in the market or underserved segments?[6]

- What is our unique value proposition in this market?[4]

- How can we develop effective marketing strategies to reach and engage our target segments?[1]

## Business Strategy Questions

- What resources and investments will be necessary to capture a larger market share?[1]

- How does our pricing strategy align with the market segments we're targeting?[7]

- What is our long-term business viability in this market?[1]

- How do factors like brand awareness and marketing budget affect our ability to capture market share?[7]

By answering these questions, you can gain a comprehensive understanding of your market potential, identify realistic targets, and develop strategies to achieve your business goals within the TAM, SAM, and SOM framework.
