## Insight
Hey there! The speech was all about exploring the transformative potential of decision intelligence in higher education, specifically at Western Governors University (WGU). The main objective was to discuss how decision intelligence can enhance student success by integrating people, processes, and technology. The panel, including Jenny Sanders, Fred Laliu, and David Morales, shared insights on how decision intelligence can optimize decision-making processes, improve student outcomes, and streamline faculty efforts. They highlighted the importance of using data-driven models to predict student success and provide timely interventions. The discussion also touched on the challenges and opportunities of implementing decision intelligence in higher ed, emphasizing the need for collaboration and innovation. Moving forward, the focus will be on leveraging decision intelligence to create a more personalized and efficient educational experience for students. It's an exciting journey that promises to reshape the future of education at WGU and beyond. Looking forward to diving deeper into the specifics of how this will be implemented!
