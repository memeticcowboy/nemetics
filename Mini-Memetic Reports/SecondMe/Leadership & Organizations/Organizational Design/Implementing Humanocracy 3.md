# Implementing Humanocracy Principles

Created at 2024/08/20 10:48 AM

## Establish Micro-Enterprises
- Break large departments into small, autonomous teams of 10-15 people.
- Each team has its own P&L responsibility and authority over budgets, hiring, and strategy.

## Internal Market System
- Teams can bid on projects and resources.
- Employees can allocate a portion of their time (e.g., 20%) to projects outside their core role that they are passionate about.

## Peer-Based Reputation System
- Replace traditional performance reviews with a system where employees rate each other on skills, collaboration, and impact.
- Use this data for compensation and promotion decisions instead of manager assessments.

## Challenge Process
- Any employee can contest a decision made by leadership.
- A panel of randomly selected employees hears challenges and makes binding rulings.

## Eliminate Job Titles and Formal Hierarchies
- Use a system of earned credibility where influence is based on expertise and track record rather than position.

## Radical Transparency
- Make all financial data, strategic plans, and executive communications accessible to all employees.
- Hold regular town halls where leaders answer unfiltered questions.

## Internal Venture Capital Fund
- Create a fund that any employee can pitch to for funding innovative ideas.
- Allocate 5-10% of profits to this fund and allow employees to invest a portion of their compensation in promising internal ventures.
