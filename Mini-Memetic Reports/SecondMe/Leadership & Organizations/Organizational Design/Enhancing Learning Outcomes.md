## Importance of Collaboration

- Incorporating more collaboration can enhance learning outcomes.
- Focus on the Socialization mode of the Nonaka and Takeuchi model to foster collaboration.
- Organize workshops or team-building exercises to encourage open dialogue and idea exchange.
- Utilize digital collaboration platforms to facilitate interactions, especially in remote teams.

## Impact of Knowledge Sharing

- Knowledge sharing boosts creativity in teams.
- Sharing unique insights and experiences creates a melting pot of ideas.
- Collaborative moments can lead to creative breakthroughs.
- Experimenting with different approaches can enhance collaborative magic.
