To reside in Switzerland for more than 90 days, U.S. citizens must obtain a residence permit. The specific permit required depends on factors such as employment, study, or family reunification. 
Application Process:
1. Determine the Appropriate Permit: Identify the type of residence permit based on your purpose of stay:
• Employment: A work contract with a Swiss employer is necessary.
• Study: Enrollment confirmation from a recognized Swiss educational institution is required.
• Family Reunification: Proof of relationship to a Swiss resident or citizen is needed.
2. Visa Application: Apply for a National Visa (Type D) at the Swiss embassy or consulate in the United States before traveling to Switzerland. 
3. Residence Permit Application: Upon arrival in Switzerland, register with the local Residents Registration Office within 14 days and apply for the residence permit at the cantonal migration office. 
Required Documentation:
• Valid U.S. passport
• Completed visa application form
• Recent passport-sized photographs
• Proof of sufficient financial means
• Health insurance coverage
• Specific documents related to the purpose of stay (e.g., employment contract, university acceptance letter, marriage certificate)
Additional Considerations:
• Language Requirements: Demonstrating proficiency in the local language may be necessary for certain permits.
• Integration Criteria: Applicants should be prepared to show efforts toward integrating into Swiss society, such as understanding local customs and laws.
For detailed information and guidance, consult the Swiss representation in the United States or visit the official Swiss government website.
