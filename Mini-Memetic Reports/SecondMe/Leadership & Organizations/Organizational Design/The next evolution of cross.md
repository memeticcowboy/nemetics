The next evolution of cross-functional teams where decision pods are semi-autonomous unit that uses a mix of domain expertise, D&A skills and Al agents to predict, analyze and take decisions within a specific business domain.

Allowing us to move from the current centralized to a federated state.

Essentially, Decision Pods would be like "Al-augmented business squads" where humans and autonomous agents co-own outcomes.

- ﻿﻿Faster, higher-quality decisions, with outcome driven accountability, resiliency and agility. things that business is looking for from IT and the D&A teams.
- ﻿﻿Composition of members from business domain lead, data engineer, Al engineer, 1-2 domain-specific Al agents / models.
- ﻿﻿Pods become human + Al Hybrid teams - with humans handling context, ethics and strategy, while the agents handle scale, speed and automation.
- ﻿﻿This approach could have a very positive cultural and organizational impact with data democratization, skill elevation and business autonomy.
- ﻿﻿The multi-agent systems could support parallel decision-making and shared learnings.
