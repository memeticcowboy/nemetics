## Insight
Ah, the ever-important question of content quality standards! Let's dive into the world of defensible content and see what gems we can uncover.

- Defensibility in content is all about ensuring that the information and guidance provided cannot be easily disproved or shown to be unsound. This means that every piece of content should be backed by solid evidence and logical reasoning, making it robust against criticism.
- One key aspect of defensible content is ensuring that positions and recommendations are sound in terms of economics and organizational behavior. This involves composing practical, straightforward, and role-targeted recommendations supported by correctly interpreted data. It's like building a house on a strong foundation – without it, everything crumbles.
- Another crucial element is making sure that an informed skeptic would struggle to undermine any part of the content. This requires including evidence that is precisely proportional to the claims made. For present-focused content, use data and examples or precedents. For future-looking content, employ rational arguments. It's like preparing for a debate – you need to have all your facts straight and ready to go.
- The CRE team's playbook on Defensibility is a treasure trove of insights on how to achieve these standards. It superbly illustrates the types of evidence needed to make content defensible. Think of it as your go-to guide for creating bulletproof content that stands the test of scrutiny.
