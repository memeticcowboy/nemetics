# The Role of TOSCA in Defining the Problem

Created at 2024/09/24 9:04 PM

## Trouble
- Identify the core issue.
- Use data and reports to highlight the significance of the problem.
- Focus on the real problem rather than symptoms.

## Owner
- Determine who is responsible for addressing the problem.
- Identify key stakeholders and their roles.
- Understand who needs to be involved in finding a solution.

## Success
- Set clear goals and benchmarks for success.
- Define what success looks like using best practices and case studies.
- Ensure everyone is aligned on the desired outcomes.

## Constraints
- Recognize limitations such as budget or regulatory hurdles.
- Plan realistically to avoid future roadblocks.

## Actors
- Analyze the dynamics among all involved parties.
- Understand the interests and influences of stakeholders.
- Navigate the complexities of stakeholder interactions.

## Core Problem Statement
- Synthesize insights from the TOSCA elements to craft a focused direction for resolving the issue.
