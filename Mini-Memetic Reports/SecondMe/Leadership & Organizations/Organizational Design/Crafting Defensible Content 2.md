# Crafting Defensible Content: A Guide to Quality Standards

Created at 2024/07/31 9:34 AM

## Ensuring Content Defensibility
- **Solid Evidence and Logical Reasoning**:
  - Back every claim with solid evidence (data, research studies, expert opinions).
  - Maintain a logical flow in arguments to avoid logical fallacies.

- **Sound Recommendations**:
  - Base recommendations on economic and organizational behavior principles.
  - Tailor advice to specific roles or audiences for relevance.

- **Proportional Evidence**:
  - Use data and examples for present-focused content.
  - Employ rational arguments for future-looking content.

- **Prepare for Scrutiny**:
  - Imagine an informed skeptic reading the content; ensure claims are robust.
  - Include evidence proportional to the claims made.

- **Utilize Resources**:
  - Refer to the CRE team’s playbook on defensibility for detailed insights on evidence types.
