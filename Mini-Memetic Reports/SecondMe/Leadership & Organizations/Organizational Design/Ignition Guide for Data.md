# Ignition Guide for Data Product Success

Created at 2024/11/06 1:52 PM

This Ignition Guide provides a step-by-step process for launching and managing data products within an organization. It aims to help data and analytics (D&A) leaders understand the roles and responsibilities involved, align data product visions with organizational goals, and establish a product development life cycle. The guide also highlights the importance of user engagement, pilot programs, and data contracts in ensuring successful data product launches. It further explores the shift from traditional project management to product management, emphasizing the value of a product-centric approach for data products.
