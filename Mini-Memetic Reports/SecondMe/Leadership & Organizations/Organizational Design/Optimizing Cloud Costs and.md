## Insight
In this episode of the D-Suite, hosts Rita and Afraz delve into the intricacies of FinOps and its role in optimizing spending on data, analytics, and AI cloud workloads. The discussion features insights from Gartner VP analyst Adam Ronthal, who explains the importance of measuring value and cost in generative AI investments. The episode also includes an interview with Michael Parris from Texas Health, who shares effective strategies for communicating the value of data, analytics, and AI within the healthcare sector. Key outcomes include the need for a decision framework to evaluate AI investments, the importance of active metadata in measuring business value, and the role of generative AI in augmenting FinOps practices. Actionable insights suggest that organizations should take stock of existing applications, identify gaps, and leverage AI to optimize their infrastructure. Future steps involve continuous evaluation and adjustment of FinOps practices to maximize value and minimize costs.
