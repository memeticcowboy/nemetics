#### Background on Gamification in the Workplace

Gamification involves integrating game mechanics, such as points, badges, leaderboards, and rewards, into non-game contexts to enhance engagement and motivation. In the workplace, it is commonly used for training, sales, customer loyalty, and employee engagement. The goal is to tap into intrinsic and extrinsic motivators, making routine tasks more enjoyable and encouraging desired behaviors. Research, such as a 2024 article from Harvard Business Review ([How Gamification Can Boost Employee Engagement](https://hbr.org/2024/03/how-gamification-can-boost-employee-engagement)), highlights its potential to address employee disengagement, a persistent issue with significant productivity implications.
