### 🌱 From Linear to Seasonal: Embed Temporal Wisdom IN AI

**Design Failure**: *Everything is linear. Nothing is seasonal.*

**Improvement Strategy**:<br>Adopt a **seasonal design cycle**, mirroring Maiden (emergence), Mother (maturity), and Crone (decline and compost). Apply this cycle to:

- **Political**: Mandate *sunset clauses* and *croning reviews* in AI regulation (Crone ethics).
- **Economic**: Prioritize *harvest pauses* in product timelines to assess alignment quality (Mother ethics).
- **Social**: Shift public narratives from perpetual growth to cyclical regeneration; include ritualized feedback phases.
- **Technological**: Schedule intentional *downtime builds* to simulate winter dormancy—reducing risks from runaway scale.
- **Legal**: Design contracts with *expiry triggers* and *user renegotiation windows*.
- **Environmental**: Link model retirements to resource audits—model death as ecological rebalancing.
