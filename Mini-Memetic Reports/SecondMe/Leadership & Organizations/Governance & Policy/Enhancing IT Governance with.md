# Enhancing IT Governance with SANITY Framework

Created at 2025/05/28 2:34 PM

#### 1. **Purposes and Goals**

To integrate the SANITY framework with the COBIT governance model, enhancing enterprise governance of information and technology (EGIT) by infusing it with dimensions of relational trust, ethical coherence, and adaptive resilience.

#### 2. **Central Questions or Problems**

- How can COBIT’s structured governance model incorporate more human-centric, relational, and ethical components?
- In what ways does the SANITY framework address gaps in conventional IT governance, especially concerning trust and adaptability?

#### 3. **Fundamental Concepts**

- **COBIT (Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies)**: A comprehensive framework for IT governance and management.
- **SANITY as a Trust Lens**: Six dimensions (Soundness, Alignment, Nurturance, Integrity, Transparency, Yielding) applied to governance design and assessment.
- **Integrative Governance**: Combining rational performance metrics with emotional, ethical, and relational dynamics.

#### 4. **Essential Information**

- COBIT excels at structure, accountability, and performance, but lacks in fostering relational trust, emotional safety, and adaptability.
- SANITY infuses COBIT with values-driven, co-regulatory design principles.
- Each SANITY dimension maps onto existing COBIT components, enhancing their human-alignment:
    - **Soundness** improves epistemic rigor in performance measurement.
    - **Alignment** bridges stakeholder values and system goals.
    - **Nurturance** supports psychological safety and care practices in governance culture.
    - **Integrity** ensures coherence across policies, ethics, and actions.
    - **Transparency** enhances legibility and decision traceability.
    - **Yielding** introduces feedback-responsiveness and adaptation over rigidity.

#### 5. **Points of View or Frames of Reference**

- **Governance as Trust Infrastructure**: Governance isn’t just control—it’s the architecture of shared trust.
- **Systemic Ethics**: Design and decision-making must account for lived experiences and moral coherence.
- **Sociotechnical Systems**: Trustworthiness is a result of both technical efficacy and social legitimacy.

#### 6. **Underlying Assumptions**

- Trust and legitimacy are prerequisites for effective governance—not afterthoughts.
- Emotional and relational dimensions are essential to sustainable digital systems.
- Procedural rigor without ethical grounding can create brittle, distrustful systems.
- Governance must evolve to reflect the needs of complex, adaptive ecosystems.

#### 7. **Inferences and Conclusions**

- SANITY complements and upgrades COBIT by embedding systemic sanity into IT governance.
- Without such augmentation, COBIT risks being too rigid, technocratic, or disconnected from human needs.
- The future of governance lies in hybrid models that merge structure with sensitivity.

#### 8. **Implications and Consequences**

- Organizations adopting SANITY-enhanced COBIT may experience more resilient, adaptive, and trusted governance systems.
- Leadership and IT functions may shift from control-based approaches to relationally attuned stewardship.
- This integration could serve as a model for transforming legacy governance frameworks to meet the demands of complexity and change.
