# The Critical Role of Trust Models in Data and Analytics Governance

Created at 2024/10/01 6:30 AM

### Key Insights on Trust Models in Data and Analytics Governance

- **Trust in Data-Driven Decision-Making is in Crisis**: Gartner predicts that by 2027, 80% of data and analytics (D&A) governance initiatives will fail without a real or manufactured crisis. Trust is a crucial factor in the success of data-driven decision-making.

- **The Role of Trust Models**: Organizations are adopting "trust models" in their D&A governance to enhance decision support. A trust model, as defined by Gartner, is "a dynamic inventory of enterprise information assets, categorized by materiality — the value and risk related to information assets — and trust rating — based on the lineage and curation of information assets."

- **Impact of Trust Models on Organizational Success**: Research shows a strong link between investment in trust models and the achievement of organizational objectives, particularly in initiatives like data quality, master data management, and decision science. Trust models significantly enhance compliance, financial risk management, and ethical use of D&A/AI.

- **Current Adoption Rates**: Only a small fraction of organizations have fully incorporated trust models, with about one-third partially doing so, and a quarter in development. Notably, one-third of respondents do not yet have a trust model.

- **Benefits of Incorporating Trust Models**: Organizations that have incorporated trust models into their governance initiatives report:
  - Improved compliance with laws
  - Enhanced financial and ethical management of D&A/AI
  - Greater success in leveraging D&A/AI as business differentiators
  - Simplified transformations of data, IT, and AI processes
  - Reduced operational costs

- **Lower Success for Organizations Without Trust Models**: Those planning but not yet incorporating trust models report significantly lower success rates, highlighting the importance of trust model integration in achieving effective governance.
