# Reflections on Governance, Friction, and Transparency

Created at 2026/01/11 8:20 PM

## Friction in Everyday Decisions
- Friction manifests as hesitations or second thoughts in decision-making.
- It encourages weighing options and questioning assumptions.
- Productive resistance often leads to better outcomes than impulsive actions.

## Governance as a Dance between Chaos and Order
- Governance viewed as a dance emphasizes creativity in negotiating tension.
- Innovation thrives on the balance of chaos and order; too much of either stifles progress.
- The "Edge of Chaos" concept highlights where new possibilities emerge.

## Transparency and Trust in Systems
- Transparency is foundational for building trust, but not merely about exposing everything.
- Real transparency involves creating pathways for understanding, not just flooding with data.
- Effective transparency aligns with the SANITY framework, where creating signals allows for genuine trust-building.
