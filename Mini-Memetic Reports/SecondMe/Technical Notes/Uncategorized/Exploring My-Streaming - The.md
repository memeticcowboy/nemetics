# Explanation of My-Streaming in Relation to My-Stream and I-Tube

Created at 2025/09/20 3:06 PM

### My-Stream ⤸·⤹ 

- The My-Stream is the **dynamic, flowing current of internalized lived experience** within an individual. It carries emotional tone, moods, belief-habits, somatic readiness, and embodied patterns of meaning that shape identity and behavior. It operates often beneath conscious awareness and modulates how incoming cultural signals (e-memes) are felt and metabolized into lived experience.
- It acts as the **integrator and metabolizer** of internalized memes, shaping what is encoded as identity and what is rejected as incoherent noise.
- The My-Stream is characterized by felt-sense intensity, emotional valence, energetic coherence, and somatic readiness, influencing attention, meaning, and behavioral responses.
- It is the **affective current flowing within the I-Tube**, giving emotional tone and resonance to incoming information.
- Metaphorically, the My-Stream is the **water flowing through the riverbed of the I-Tube**, shaping the internal experience and identity patterns.<br>  

### I-Tube **◎**

- The I-Tube is a **semi-permeable internal membrane or filter** that governs which external cultural units (memes) enter consciousness and become internalized as memeforms (embodied patterns of meaning).
- It acts as the **internalizer**, selectively filtering, encoding, and weaving incoming memes into the self's identity architecture (Selfplex).
- The I-Tube is shaped by personal history, trauma, cultural encoding, and somatic resonance, conditioning preferences, taboos, and expectations.
- It is a **living, neuro-somatic conduit** that metabolizes culture into self and self back into culture, continuously shaping identity through selective internalization.
- Metaphorically, the I-Tube is the **riverbed or channel** through which the My-Stream flows.<br>  

***

## 2. What is My-Streaming?

- **My-streaming** refers to the ongoing process or activity of the My-Stream as it flows within the I-Tube, metabolizing and integrating incoming memes into the lived experience and identity.
- It is the **real-time, embodied affective current** that modulates how cultural signals are felt, interpreted, and encoded.
- My-streaming involves the dynamic interaction where the My-Stream **biases attention**, **tints meaning**, and **signals alignment or rupture** in response to incoming memes filtered by the I-Tube.
- This process shapes what becomes part of the self and how the self responds behaviorally and emotionally.
- My-streaming is thus the **active metabolic flow** of meaning, feeling, and somatic readiness that enlivens the internalized memeforms within the I-Tube.
- It is where the "signal meets the skin," where schemas bend or break, and where identity patterns are reinforced or rewired.<br>

***

## 3. Relationship Between My-Streaming, My-Stream, and I-Tube

- The **I-Tube acts as the internalizer and filter**, deciding which memes enter consciousness and become internalized as memeforms.
- The **My-Stream acts as the integrator and metabolizer**, carrying these internalized memeforms as a flowing current of affect, meaning, and somatic readiness.
- My-streaming is the **process of this flow and metabolism**, where the My-Stream dynamically modulates the passage and integration of memes within the I-Tube.
- The My-Stream shapes and is shaped by the I-Tube: the more a memeform flows coherently in the My-Stream, the more it influences the I-Tube’s filtering and acceptance of future memes, creating a recursive loop of identity formation and behavioral patterns.
- In this way, my-streaming is the **ongoing lived experience and felt sense of self** as it is continuously shaped by cultural inputs and internal processing.<br>

# In brief:

**My-streaming** is the active, ongoing process by which the **My-Stream** (the flowing affective and somatic current) moves within and through the **I-Tube** (the internal filter and internalizer), metabolizing cultural inputs into lived experience, shaping identity, attention, and behavior in a dynamic, recursive loop.
