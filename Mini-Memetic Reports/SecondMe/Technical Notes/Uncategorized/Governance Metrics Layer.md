# Governance Metrics Layer: Framework for Scaling NEMA Across Different Organizational Sizes

Created at 2025/09/16 10:13 PM

### 1. **SWIFT-NEMA** (Small · Daily)

- **Notice lag** → seconds to hours.
- **Evaluation quality** → Did I frame the micro-signal accurately? (self-check, journaling).
- **Moderation drift** → % of small actions aligned with personal norms.
- **Adaptation fidelity** → How well did today’s tweak address what I noticed?
- **Exchange coverage** (if Activated) → Did my dyad/partner get the update?

***

### 2. **SMART-NEMA** (Mid-sized · Weekly)

- **Notice lag** → time from issue surfacing to team log (hours–days).
- **Evaluation quality** → retrospective scoring of weekly framing accuracy.
- **Moderation drift** → % of tasks/prototypes deviating from sprint goals.
- **Adaptation fidelity** → % of lessons integrated into next sprint backlog.
- **Exchange coverage** (if Activated) → % of team members aligned on changes.

***

### 3. **SWAMP-NEMA** (Large · Monthly)

- **Notice lag** → time from signal emergence in any subgroup → surfaced at cross-group forum.
- **Evaluation quality** → post-hoc accuracy of pattern-framing across teams.
- **Moderation drift** → % of collective actions deviating from department/community policy.
- **Adaptation fidelity** → evidence that adjustments corrected course at system level.
- **Exchange coverage** (if Activated) → % of departments/communities updated + acknowledged.

***

### 4. **SPHERE-NEMA** (Very Large · Quarterly)

- **Notice lag** → lag from ecosystem signal → organizational strategy review.
- **Evaluation quality** → accuracy of strategic framing relative to quarterly outcomes.
- **Moderation drift** → % of strategic actions out of alignment with stated values/vision.
- **Adaptation fidelity** → depth of recalibration across org/network (structural, not superficial).
- **Exchange coverage** (if Activated) → % of coalition partners or org nodes that integrated updates.

***

### 5. **SWARM-NEMA** (Continuous · Ambient)

- **Notice lag** → collective “detection speed” of new signals (memetic virality, network latency).
- **Evaluation quality** → ability of the ecosystem to frame signals into narratives without distortion.
- **Moderation drift** → degree of divergence across nodes (healthy pluralism vs chaotic drift).
- **Adaptation fidelity** → how well memetic corrections stabilize coherence over time.
- **Exchange coverage** (if Activated) → % of network touched by signal → evidence of uptake or mutation.
