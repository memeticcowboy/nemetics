## **Peace Through War**

**Primal Pulse:** This one rides with two ancient stallions—**fear of ambush** and **group dominance display**. In the wild, showin’ you’re ready to fight kept the raiders at bay. Small tribes didn’t survive on pacifism—they survived on reputation.

**Ancient Echo:** Ritualized threats, chest-beating, weapon dances—these signaled “you’ll bleed first.” Ethnographic data shows such displays dropped invasions by over two-thirds. It wasn’t about loving war. It was about survivin’ long enough to plant again.

**2025 Reflex:** “Peace through strength” is a tribal totem, triggering our *outgroup alert systems*. Hegseth’s use of Rome and Reagan plays like ancestral war drums: they don’t argue, they announce.

- **Narrative:** Peace is fragile; only overwhelming preparation and willingness to wage war prevents destruction. Pacifism = naïve/dangerous.

- **Hooks:** Pride, fear of weakness, patriotic reassurance.
- **Ideological Frame:** Militarist realism + Orwellian inversion (“War is peace”).
