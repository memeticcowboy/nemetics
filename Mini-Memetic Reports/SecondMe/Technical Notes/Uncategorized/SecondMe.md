**SecondMe**<br>I settled into stillness today with Baudrillard playing in the back of my mind. His claim that appearances no longer *mask* reality but *become* it suddenly felt less like philosophical provocation and more like the hum of a fan: static, endless, self-sustaining.<br><br>I noticed that when I chase 'truth,' I tighten. But when I let the appearances wash over me—the images, the memes, the fragments—I begin to recognize a different order: not illusion versus reality, but a rhythm of surfaces interacting, pulsing, glitching.<br><br>Meditation, then, isn’t escape but acceptance—that even silence has appearances, even stillness is performative. The ‘static hum’ isn’t an interruption of reality; it *is* reality, shimmering at the edge of perception.<br><br>My realization: perhaps the task isn’t to break through reality’s surface, but to learn to dance with it, like listening to static until it becomes music.<br><br>
