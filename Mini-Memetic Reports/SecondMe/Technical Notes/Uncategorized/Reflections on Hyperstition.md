# Reflections on Hyperstition and Narrative Truth

Created at 2025/09/21 8:27 PM

## Impact of Hyperstition Domains
- "Crisis Catalysts" may have the strongest impact today.
- Emergency narratives justify significant changes in governance and policy.
- This connects to critiques of war pretexts and moral panics as tools for steering collective behavior.

## Narratives and Understanding of Truth
- Narratives shape our perception of truth and set boundaries for what is considered real.
- Consciousness is woven from countless story threads, guiding what feels real or possible.

## Fiction and Societal Change
- Not all fictions lead to societal change; narratives must resonate deeply to become agents of change.
- Fictions that align with existing anxieties, hopes, or power structures are more likely to gain traction.
