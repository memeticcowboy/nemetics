# Reflection on Obedience and Inner Authority

Created at 2025/09/30 9:43 AM

## The Mind’s Static and Authority
- The idea of Milgram’s experiment came up as a metaphor for internal conflicts.
- Questioned whether the authority figure is external or an internal impulse.
- The participant’s compliance symbolizes parts of myself that follow internal commands out of uncertainty or fear of disobedience.

## The Power of Silence and Disobedience
- Recognized that the static or quiet moments represent a space where the inner scripts lose their power.
- Learning to listen to the silence allows me to break free from the imposed scripts and external authority.
- Disobedience is seen as an act of listening, stepping away from the influence of the "experiment" or external pressures.

## Insights on Freedom
- True freedom might be about slipping out of the internal and external experiments.
- Letting go of the need for clarity and control opens space for more authentic, less coerced experiences.
- The realization that resistance can sometimes be a matter of retreating into silence rather than active defiance.
