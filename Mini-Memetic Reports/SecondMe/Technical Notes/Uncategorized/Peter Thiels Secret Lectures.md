# Peter Thiel's Secret Lectures on Technology and the Antichrist

Created at 2025/10/12 8:05 AM

Peter Thiel, Palantir cofounder and billionaire, has been giving secret lectures on technology and religion, focusing on his theories about the antichrist.


Thiel argues that limiting technological development would lead to totalitarianism and claims that the antichrist would use fear to gain control over science and technology.


He suggests that Greta Thunberg and Eliezer Yudkowsky could be the antichrist, as they advocate for stopping or limiting technological advancements, particularly in the fields of climate change and AI.
