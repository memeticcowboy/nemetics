# Bhutan's BuddhaBot: AI Meets Buddhist Wisdom

Created at 2025/10/13 8:45 PM

The Central Monastic Body of Bhutan and Kyoto University have launched BuddhaBot, an AI-powered Buddhist dialogue system that specializes in answering questions about Buddhism.


BuddhaBot will be implemented as a pilot project in monastic institutions in Bhutan, and if successful, it will be rolled out in colleges and schools in the country.


The system uses Sentence BERT, an algorithm provided by Google, and is being developed to combine traditional wisdom with modern technology, making it available to the Bhutanese Buddhist community.
