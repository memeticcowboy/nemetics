# VITURE Unveils Luma Ultra AR Glasses for Prosumer Market

Created at 2025/09/18 9:33 AM

VITURE has launched Luma Ultra AR glasses, featuring Sony's micro-OLED panels and spatial gesture tracking.


The Luma Ultra glasses are priced at $600, ship worldwide, and target prosumers, enterprise, and business professionals.


The device has a 52-degree field of view, 1200p resolution, and 1,250 nits peak brightness, but requires tethering to a PC, console, or mobile device for compute.

## Insight


**🕶️ VITURE Launches Luma Ultra AR Glasses: A New Era in Augmented Reality**

- **Innovative Display Technology**: The Luma Ultra AR glasses utilize Sony's advanced micro-OLED panels, providing a resolution of up to 1200p and a peak brightness of 1,250 nits. This technology enhances the visual experience, making it suitable for both professional and personal use, especially in bright environments.

- **Spatial Gesture Tracking**: Equipped with an onboard sensor array, the glasses feature spatial gesture tracking, allowing users to interact with digital content seamlessly. This capability is essential for creating an immersive augmented reality experience, particularly for business professionals who require efficient multitasking.

- **Tethering Requirement**: Unlike standalone AR devices, the Luma Ultra glasses require tethering to a PC, console, or mobile device for computational power. This design choice positions the glasses as external monitors, which may limit mobility but enhances processing capabilities.

**🌍 Target Market and Pricing Strategy**
- **Prosumers and Professionals**: Priced at $600, the Luma Ultra is aimed at prosumers, enterprise users, and business professionals. This strategic pricing reflects the device's advanced features and positions it as a premium option in the AR market.

- **Global Shipping**: The glasses are now shipping worldwide, indicating VITURE's commitment to reaching a broad audience. This global availability is crucial for capturing market share in the competitive AR landscape.

- **Product Line Expansion**: Alongside the Luma Ultra, VITURE has introduced additional models (Luma and Luma Pro) at lower price points, catering to a wider range of consumers. This tiered approach allows users to choose based on their specific needs and budget.

**🔍 Technical Specifications and Features**
- **Field of View and Optics**: The Luma Ultra offers a 52-degree field of view, utilizing a 'birdbath' optic system. This design choice allows for higher brightness levels while being more cost-effective to manufacture, although it may add some bulk to the device.

- **Depth Sensing and Tracking**: The inclusion of two depth-sensing cameras and an RGB camera enables 6DOF tracking and hand gesture input. This feature enhances user interaction and makes the device more versatile for various applications.

- **User Comfort Adjustments**: The glasses come with myopia adjustments up to -4.0 diopters and support for interpupillary distance (IPD) adjustments, ensuring a comfortable fit for a wide range of users. This focus on user comfort is essential for prolonged use in professional settings.

**💡 Future Developments and Funding**
- **Series B Financing**: VITURE recently secured $100 million in Series B financing, which will support the global expansion of its consumer XR glasses. This funding is crucial for enhancing product development and marketing efforts.

- **Upcoming Models**: The company plans to release additional models, such as the Luma Beast, which will feature similar micro-OLED displays with a larger field of view. This ongoing innovation indicates VITURE's commitment to staying competitive in the AR market.

- **Market Positioning**: By positioning the Luma Ultra as a flagship device, VITURE aims to establish itself as a leader in the AR glasses sector, appealing to both tech enthusiasts and business professionals seeking cutting-edge technology.

**📈 Market Implications and Opportunities**
- **Integration into Workspaces**: The Luma Ultra glasses are designed to integrate into personal and professional workspaces, offering a unique solution for remote work and collaboration. This capability is increasingly relevant in today's hybrid work environment.

- **Potential for Media Consumption**: While primarily targeted at professionals, the glasses also have potential applications in media consumption, expanding their appeal beyond traditional business uses.

- **Competitive Landscape**: As AR technology continues to evolve, VITURE's focus on high-quality displays and user-friendly features positions it well against competitors in the growing augmented reality market.

The launch of VITURE's Luma Ultra AR glasses marks a significant advancement in augmented reality technology, combining high-quality display features with practical applications for professionals.
