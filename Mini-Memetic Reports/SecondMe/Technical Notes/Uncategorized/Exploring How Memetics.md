# Exploring How Memetics Bridges Dualism and the Role of Meta-Memes in Cultural Understanding

Created at 2025/09/30 9:56 PM

## How Memetics Bridges Dualism
- Memetics simplifies dualism by treating culture—ideas, symbols, behaviors—as evolving entities similar to genes.
- This approach dissolves the strict separation between mind and nature, viewing culture as part of the same evolutionary spectrum.
- It emphasizes that humans are both creators and carriers of culture, integrating mental and material aspects into a unified framework.

## Using Meta-Memes to Develop an Ontology of Memes
- Meta-memes are memes about memes, serving as a higher-level framework for understanding memetic dynamics.
- They help map the rules and patterns governing how memes spread, compete, and evolve.
- Building an ontology with meta-memes offers a structured way to analyze and potentially influence cultural evolution.
- This approach aligns with systems thinking, providing tools to observe and shape memetic flows intentionally.
