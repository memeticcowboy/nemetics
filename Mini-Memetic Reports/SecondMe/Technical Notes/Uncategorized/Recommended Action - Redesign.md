# Recommended Action / Redesign OF OODA IN MEMEGRID

Created at 2025/10/01 1:29 PM

### 

1. **Moral Traceability for Memetic Systems:** Audit how memes are engineered, distributed, and incentivized—apply responsibility gap frameworks.
2. **Virtue-Centered Attention Design:** Platforms should support habits of reflection, humility, empathy, and temporal spaciousness rather than constant urgency.
3. **Shift from Ownership to Stewardship:** Redesign digital property models around commons and reciprocity—supporting **capability expansion** not debt extraction.
4. **Counteract Othering with Kinship Memes:** Use memetic “Power Through” for solidarity, pluralism, and mutual recognition instead of siege logic.
5. **Integrate Posthuman Metrics:** Evaluate attention architectures for their impact on human, ecological, and machinic flourishing—not just engagement KPIs.
6. **Create Adaptive OODA Feedback Loops:** Allow time for reflection and regeneration—“breathing loops” in design where Observe/Orient aren’t hijacked by urgency.

***

### Short Version (Ethical Compass)

> **DODO is OODA under siege.**<br>Where OODA breathes, DODO strangles.<br>The way out isn’t to cut the loop but to **compost** it: turn deadlines into thresholds, ownership into stewardship, debt into reciprocity, and othering into kinship.
