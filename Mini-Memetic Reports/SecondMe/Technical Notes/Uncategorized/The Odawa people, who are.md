The Odawa people, who are intrinsically connected to the Great Lakes, exemplify this profound relationship. Their name, which translates to "trade," reflects their mastery of the lakes and rivers, which were the lifeblood of their extensive trade networks. This economic and political reliance on water is inseparable from their spiritual worldview. The Odawa view water as a home for powerful spirits, including the male and female Water Panthers depicted in their tribal logo, who represent a balance between opposing forces. Furthermore, the proximity of their burial sites to water, and their refusal to be removed from their homelands, is a testament to the spiritual and ancestral connection to their waterways.
