# My-Stream: Irrigating the Memetic Terrarium

Created at 2025/09/08 9:59 AM

**🌱 What Is a Memetic Terrarium?**


A terrarium is a bounded, self-sustaining ecological container where life—plants, microbes, moisture cycles—flourishes in microclimate loops.

A Memetic Terrarium, then, is:

A bounded internal ecosystem of patterned meaning, affect, and behavior—fed by symbolic, relational, and environmental “nutrients”—that loops and evolves recursively inside your I-Tube (your internal meaning-processing tube).


**So What Is the** **My-Stream?**

The My-Stream is the dynamic fluid of experience, memory, and sense-making that irrigates, circulates, and reconfigures the I-Tube’s Memetic Terrarium.

It delivers affective nutrients, redistributes meaning, and carries dissolved memeforms across time and thresholds of transformation.

Think of it as a semi-conscious stream of affect, attention, memory, and narrative interpretation that runs within and across the I-Tube’s internal ecosystem.


**🐌🧠** **RoboSnails of AI: Core Metaphor**


RoboSnails are slow-moving, semi-autonomous agents that traverse the Memetic Terrarium, cleaning up residue, composting outdated memeforms, and gently resurfacing forgotten or fragmented patterns for reintegration.

In short: they are memetic janitors + composters + archivists within your cognitive ecology.
