# Exploring Memory's Role in Global Narratives

Created at 2025/09/21 9:30 PM

The book "Critical Memory Studies" is a multilayered collection that brings together diverse thinkers, writers, artists, and activists to consider the importance of memory.


The volume features contributors from various locations, including India, Belgium, Australia, and the United States, and includes a range of voices, from senior scholars to independent writers and novelists.


The study of memory is crucial, as it touches every aspect of our lives, and the book explores how memory is represented, narrated, and interpreted through various forms, including art, literature, and music, in the context of significant global events such as the Covid-19 pandemic and social injustices.
