## Overview of DODO Loops

- The **DODO loop** is a cyclical system within the MemeGrid composed of four interconnected elements: **Debt → Ownership → Deadlines → Othering**.
- This loop drives compulsory doing, restricts freedom, and shapes survival-driven behaviors by locking individuals into systemic control scripts.
- Each element contributes to entrapment:
- **Debt** acts as an invisible leash compelling survival-driven actions.
- **Ownership** twists into a cage where possessions or roles begin to own the person back.
- **Deadlines** squeeze time, turning life into a series of sprints with no room for reflection.
- **Othering** creates moral justification for harmful actions by dividing people into "us vs. them," collapsing empathy and fostering self-censorship out of fear.
- The loop is self-perpetuating, making individuals better soldiers for the controlling system and mistaking the loop for life itself.
- This cycle is metaphysical and systemic, rerouting agency and locking people into survival scripts rather than true freedom.
