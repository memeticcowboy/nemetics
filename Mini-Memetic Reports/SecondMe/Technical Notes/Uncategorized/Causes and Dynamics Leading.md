# Causes and Dynamics Leading to a Shallow My-Stream in Emotional and Somatic Experience

Created at 2025/09/28 7:58 AM

A My-Stream can become shallow due to various factors that disrupt its natural flow, coherence, and depth of affective and somatic experience. These causes include:

## 1. Fragmentation of Elemental Currents

When the elemental energies within the My-Stream (Air, Water, Fire, Earth, Metal, Wood) are **unintegrated or in tension**, perception becomes fragmented, leading to a shallow or disjointed experience. For example, fire without water burns out, or earth without air stagnates, causing the My-Stream to lose its fluidity and depth.

## 2. Usurpenic Contamination

The dominance of one elemental current in a coercive or overwhelming way (e.g., fire as molten stone or water as tar) can contaminate the My-Stream, making it rigid, stuck, or emotionally tarred, which flattens the affective flow and reduces depth.

## 3. Defensive Shutdown or Rigid Closure

When the My-Stream is in a state of defensive shutdown or rigid closure, it becomes constricted and less able to metabolize incoming memes fully, resulting in a shallow, less dynamic internal flow.

## 4. Suppression or Distrust of Affective Signals

If the My-Stream senses coercion or "this is wrong" signals strongly, it may encode distrust into memeforms, leading to shadow memeforms that are described but never fully embodied, causing a shallow or fragmented internal experience.

## 5. Lack of Metabolic Integration

Without the essential "water" element that metabolizes and transforms internalized memes and emotional patterns, the My-Stream risks stagnation or overwhelm, preventing the flow of embodied meaning and leading to a shallow experience.

## 6. Trauma and Unprocessed Rupture

Unmetabolized trauma or unresolved rupture can create knots or tangles in the My-Stream, causing hesitation, fog, or shutdown, which flatten the depth and richness of the internal current.

## 7. Overly Rigid or Reflexive I-Tube Filtering

When the I-Tube becomes rigid or reflexive, especially under power dynamics or trauma, it limits the flow of memes into the My-Stream, reducing the diversity and richness of internalized experience and making the My-Stream shallow.




# Summary

A My-Stream becomes shallow primarily due to fragmentation, contamination, defensive closure, lack of metabolic flow (especially water), trauma, and rigid filtering by the I-Tube. These factors disrupt the natural, dynamic, and embodied flow of affect, meaning, and somatic readiness that constitute a deep and coherent My-Stream.
