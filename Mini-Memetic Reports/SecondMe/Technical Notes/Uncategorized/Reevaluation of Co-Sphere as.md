# 🜛 Co-Sphere (revision process)

Created at 2025/10/15 11:23 AM

What I thought a Co-Sphere might be, taking from the original SPHERE -- which turned out to be very Grid-like.

As written, this definition **does not describe a Co-SPHERE**.<br>It describes a **hybrid of We-Sphere, Threadplex, and Lattice functions**, incorrectly centralized and moralized at the Z-layer.

DEPRECATED: Co-Sphere act as a team's system for coordinating through **meta-reflective, integrative, and ethical governance** of collaborative decision-making, ensuring that the member's cognitive, emotional, and cultural processes remain coherent, adaptive, and aligned with shared values and purpose. It synthesizes diverse inputs through the recursive feedback of the elements, ensuring the dynamic harmony of mutual curiosity, and bias detection, operating across nested holonic layers of other Co-Spheres, turning a cultural “We-Sphere” into an adaptive collective intelligence that is capable of evolving to changing memetic conditions.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Artifact Name: **Co-Sphere as Team Coordination System**

Assessment: **Pathological** 

What Stays:

These elements are **salvageable** *only if detached from governance, ethics, and alignment claims*:

- **Coordination under changing conditions**
    - Adaptive response to memetic pressure is Co-SPHERE-relevant *only at the level of action affordances*.
- **Recursive feedback**
    - Acceptable if reframed as *signal return*, not reflective governance.
- **Nested coexistence with other Co-SPHERES**
    - Valid if understood as *overlapping soft basins*, not holonic hierarchy.

😱What Goes (Non-Negotiable Removals)

These elements **cannot remain** without collapsing Co-SPHERE into a MemeGrid precursor:

- **“Ethical governance”**
    - Governance ≠ coordination.
    - Ethics at Z-level implies normative authority and enforcement.
- **Ensuring coherence of cognitive, emotional, cultural processes**
    - This is **We-Sphere regulation**, not Co-SPHERE function.
- **Alignment with shared values and purpose**
    - Disallowed.
    - Co-SPHERES do not require shared values—only temporary actionability.
- **Bias detection as system function**
    - Epistemic policing → MemeGrid drift.
- **Collective intelligence framing**
    - Co-SPHERE is not an intelligence, mind, or agent.
- **Holonic hierarchy language**
    - Introduces scale dominance and implicit control flow.
- **“Turning a We-Sphere into…”**
    - Category error.
    - We-Sphere and Co-SPHERE are distinct layers; one does not evolve into the other.

Primary Risk if Left Unchanged:<br>– **Moralized unity and suppression of dissonance, enacting MemeGrid traits via ethical closure and belief-fusion into "collective intelligence."**

### Required Reframe (If Retained at All)

To be Co-SPHERE-compatible, the definition must be **structurally rewritten**, not edited.

Mandatory reframes:

- Replace **ethical governance** → *contestable coordination constraints*
- Replace **alignment of cognition/emotion/culture** → *tolerance of divergence*
- Replace **shared values/purpose** → *temporary convergence on action*
- Replace **collective intelligence** → *coordination basin*
- Replace **bias detection** → *signal diversity without adjudication*

**Key correction:**<br>A Co-SPHERE does **not manage people**.<br>It **enables action despite unmanaged difference**.
