# Guardian Protection's AI Deterrence: Automated Audio Response for Crime Prevention

Created at 2025/10/14 8:53 AM

Guardian Protection's AI Deterrence is **an automated crime prevention solution that uses AI-generated, neural-synthesized audio responses to create the impression of a live security operator**. This technology is designed to deter intruders by immediately responding to them with personalized verbal warnings based on real-time video analytics. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uL0O9eJX5Fk), [2](https://help.fluenthome.com/en_US/ai-deterrence/ai-deterrence-aid-autonomous-audio-response-service), [3](https://staging.guardianprotection.com/business-security/proactive-video-surveillance/#:~:text=AI%20Deterrance%20Our%20smart%20detection%20technology%20delivers,and%20location%2C%20providing%20a%20powerful%2C%20human%2Dlike%20deterrent.)]

Key elements of the AI deterrence response

- **Adaptive verbal warnings:** The system analyzes video data to provide a specific, contextual response. For example, the audio response can identify and announce details about the intruder, such as their clothing and location.
- **Immediate action:** The system provides an instant audio warning when an intruder is detected. This lower latency and cost is a key difference compared to traditional operator-initiated two-way talkdowns, where a human must first review the alert.
- **Clear instructions:** The automated voice commands the intruder to leave the property immediately and states that they are being recorded and that the police will be called.
- **Works with video surveillance:** The AI Deterrence system can function as a standalone feature or in conjunction with Guardian's Proactive Video Surveillance service. If the AI warning does not deter the intruder, a human monitoring professional can intervene and dispatch authorities.
