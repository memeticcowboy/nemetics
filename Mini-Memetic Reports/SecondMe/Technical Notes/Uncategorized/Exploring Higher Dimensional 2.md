- Is space-time a fundamental layer of reality, or an emergent construct?
- How do perception and consciousness shape what we take as “real”?
- Can higher-dimensional or holographic principles explain the structure of the universe?
- What lies beyond space-time, and how might consciousness scale infinitely?
