# Nixon’s Enemies List and Modern Parallels

Created at 2025/10/05 6:19 PM

President Richard Nixon used the government as a weapon against his perceived enemies through an "enemies list" that aimed to "screw our political enemies" using federal machinery.


The list's existence was revealed during the Watergate hearings of 1973, sparking bipartisan revulsion and criticism, including from conservative columnist William F. Buckley Jr. who called it "proto-fascism".


The concept of an "enemies list" has been referenced again with Donald Trump's intention to appoint Kash Patel, who published a similar list in his book "Government Gangsters", to a high-level position, drawing comparisons to Nixon's tactics.
