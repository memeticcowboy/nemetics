### Summary

- **Vague deictic othering** is primarily seen in phrases like "those kind of people" and "these people," which create a moral and social distance without specifying individuals.
- **Adversarial group-labeling** targets "the left" and "the Democratic party," attributing negative moral qualities and behaviors.
- The **affective valence** is predominantly negative: outrage, contempt, disgust, and moral condemnation.
- The **memetic functions** include projection (assigning negative traits to the out-group), tokenization (reducing complex groups to a single negative trait), flattening (ignoring nuance), and signaling (communicating in-group solidarity against an out-group).
- The **citation method** is mostly affect-first, relying on emotional appeals rather than empirical evidence.
- All instances are **used by the speaker** (Benny Johnson) rather than quoted from others.
