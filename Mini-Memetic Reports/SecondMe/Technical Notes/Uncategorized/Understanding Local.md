# Understanding Local Threadplexes: Embodied Symbolic Ecologies in Religious and Spiritual Contexts

## What Are Local Threadplexes?

- Local threadplexes are *dynamic, semi-stable weaves of memeforms*—clusters of symbolic patterns such as images, metaphors, beliefs, and narratives—that are co-created and embedded within specific social and cultural contexts called We-Spheres. They are affectively charged and embodied through ritual, somatic tracing, and emotional resonance, making them more than just cognitive ideas but lived symbolic ecologies within individuals and groups.

## Relation to Religious and Spiritual Beliefs

- Local threadplexes often incorporate *inherited mythologies, archetypes, and religious narratives* that provide symbolic frameworks for meaning-making. For example, archetypes like the hero, exile, mother, or judge, which are common in religious stories, become part of the symbolic fabric of a local threadplex, shaping how individuals interpret their experiences and identities.

- Religious and spiritual beliefs contribute *affectively charged symbolic content* that is embedded in local threadplexes through ritual, repetition, and somatic experience. This embodiment makes these beliefs deeply felt and resistant to change, as they are woven into the bodymind architecture via installation vectors such as authority, rhythm, and emotional intensity.
- Local threadplexes formed around religious themes often manifest as *mythopoetic loops* or narrative arcs that structure personal and collective identity. For instance, a religious narrative of sin and redemption may crystallize into a threadplex shaped by guilt, salvation, and sacrifice, influencing how individuals experience and enact their spiritual lives. - These threadplexes are not static; they *evolve through processes like ReThreading* (individual identity recomposition) and *ReThreadplexing* (collective re-patterning), allowing religious and spiritual beliefs to adapt and transform within local cultural contexts. This fluidity contrasts with rigid ideological memeplexes and supports participatory, affective engagement with spiritual meaning.

## Summary

- Local threadplexes are embodied, affectively charged symbolic ecologies that incorporate religious and spiritual beliefs as core narrative and metaphorical elements.
- They shape how individuals and communities experience, interpret, and live their spirituality through ritual, myth, and emotional resonance.
- These threadplexes provide coherence and identity but remain adaptable, allowing spiritual meanings to evolve in response to new experiences and cultural shifts.

This understanding highlights local threadplexes as living, participatory symbolic systems deeply intertwined with religious and spiritual life.
