## Insight


*   **Shift from Data-Driven to Decision-Centric:** Gartner predicts a shift where organizations move beyond merely using data to validate pre-made decisions, towards a "decision-centric" approach. This implies a more proactive and integrated use of data and analytics to drive decision-making processes. By 2028, Gartner anticipates that 25% of Chief Data and Analytics Officer (CDAO) vision statements will prioritize being "decision-centric" over simply being "data-driven."

*   **Decision Intelligence as a Discipline:** Decision Intelligence is defined as the discipline of converting information into better actions at any scale. It is an interdisciplinary field that combines data science (statistics, machine learning, AI, analytics) with behavioral sciences (psychology, neuroscience, economics, and managerial sciences). This integration suggests a holistic approach to decision-making, considering both quantitative data and qualitative human factors.

*   **Decision Intelligence in the AI Era:** Decision Intelligence is described as a new academic discipline focused on selecting between options by integrating applied data science, social science, and managerial science. It is considered vital for the AI era, encompassing the skills needed to responsibly lead AI projects and design objectives, metrics, and safety nets for automation at scale. This highlights the importance of ethical and responsible AI implementation in decision-making processes.
