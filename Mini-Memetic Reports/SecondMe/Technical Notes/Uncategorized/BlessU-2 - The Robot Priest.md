# BlessU-2: The Robot Priest Sparking Debate in Wittenberg

Created at 2025/10/14 9:54 AM

A robot priest called BlessU-2 has been unveiled in Wittenberg, Germany, to mark 500 years since the Reformation.


The robot delivers blessings in five languages, beams light from its hands, and is intended to trigger debate about the future of the church and artificial intelligence.


According to Stephan Krebs of the Protestant church in Hesse and Nassau, the robot is meant to provoke debate, not replace human pastors, as it "could never substitute for pastoral care".

## Insight


**🤖 BlessU-2: The Robot Priest Paving the Future of Faith?**

- **500 Years of Reformation Commemorated**: BlessU-2, a robot priest, was unveiled in Wittenberg, Germany, to celebrate the 500th anniversary of the Reformation. This milestone represents not just a historical event but also the integration of technology in faith practices, sparking discussions on the evolving nature of religious traditions. 

- **Multilingual Blessings for a Modern Audience**: The robot is capable of delivering blessings in five languages – German, English, French, Spanish, and Polish. This feature allows access to diverse congregations and encourages inclusivity, marking a shift towards more adaptable and globally aware practices within religious settings. 

- **Prototyping a Dialogue on Technology and Spirituality**: The initiative aims to provoke debate on whether blessings can be genuinely communicated by machines or whether human presence is indispensable in spiritual practice. This challenge not only questions the role of technology in religion but also reflects broader societal shifts as artificial intelligence becomes more prevalent.

**💡 Provoking Thought: A Tool for Debate, Not Replacement**

- **Human Connection Remains Essential**: Stephan Krebs, from the Protestant Church in Hesse and Nassau, emphasizes that while BlessU-2 can provide blessings, it is not intended to replace human pastoral care. This clarifies the intended role of the robot as a facilitator for discussions about the relationship between technology and spirituality rather than a substitute for human interaction.

- **Mixed Reactions from the Community**: The robot has elicited both curiosity and criticism. While many find the exhibition amusing and engaging, some church members express concern that it may undermine the role of human pastors. This reflects the tension between adopting technology and preserving traditional values within faith communities.

- **Collecting Feedback for Future Analysis**: The project involves gathering perspectives to analyze how congregants perceive BlessU-2’s role. This feedback will be vital in understanding the implications of such technology in faith practices and addressing associated concerns.
