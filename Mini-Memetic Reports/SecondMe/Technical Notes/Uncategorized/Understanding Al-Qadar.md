# Understanding Al-Qadar: Divine Decree and Human Free Will

Created at 2025/10/13 8:52 PM

Qadar means that Allah has decreed everything that happens in the universe according to His prior knowledge and the dictates of His wisdom.


Belief in al-Qadar includes four things: Allah's knowledge of all things, Allah's writing of decrees in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz, the occurrence of events only by Allah's will, and the creation of all things by Allah.


Belief in al-Qadar does not contradict the idea of human free will, as humans have the ability to make choices, but their will is subject to the will and decree of Allah.

## Insight


**🌌 Understanding the Meaning of Belief in al-Qadar (Divine Decree and Will)**

---

### 🧠 The Concept of al-Qadar
- **Key conclusion:** al-Qadar signifies that Allah has decreed everything in the universe based on His knowledge and wisdom.
- **Explanation & Details:** It emphasizes that Allah’s omniscience encompasses all that occurs, from eternity to eternity, with His decrees written in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz. Allah's knowledge predates creation and encompasses all events and actions, signifying divine omnipotence and foreknowledge.

### 📜 The Foundations of Belief in al-Qadar
- **Key conclusion:** Belief in al-Qadar comprises four core beliefs: Allah’s knowledge, divine writing, will, and creation.
- **Explanation & Details:** These pillars confirm that Allah knows all things, has written decrees in the celestial ledger, causes all events through His will, and is the creator of everything. This reinforces that nothing happens outside Allah's divine plan and power.

### ✍️ The Written Decree in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz
- **Key conclusion:** Allah has recorded all decrees in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz long before creation.
- **Explanation & Details:** Quranic references and prophetic sayings affirm that Allah inscribed all acts and events in this preserved tablet. The creation of the Pen by Allah signifies the importance of divine predestination, with decrees established 50,000 years prior to creation.

### 🕊️ The Will of Allah and Creation
- **Key conclusion:** All occurrences, whether actions of Allah or creations of Allah, happen solely by His will.
- **Explanation & Details:** Every event materializes because Allah wills it. This includes His actions, human actions, and natural phenomena. The universe operates under Allah's control, and nothing occurs without His permission.

### 🏗️ The Creation of All Things by Allah
- **Key conclusion:** Everything in existence was created by Allah with precise measurements and attributes.
- **Explanation & Details:** Quranic verses highlight Allah as the creator and disposer of all things. Ibrahim’s statement underscores that human creations and actions are ultimately under divine sovereignty, emphasizing Allah's role as the creator.

### ⚖️ The Compatibility of al-Qadar with Human Free Will
- **Key conclusion:** Belief in divine predestination does not negate human free will.
- **Explanation & Details:** Quranic evidence demonstrates humans have the capacity to choose and act freely, yet their choices occur within Allah’s overarching will and decree. Verses like "Whoever wills" and "You cannot will unless Allah wills" illustrate this balance.

### 🎯 Practical Implication in Life and Shari’ah
- **Key conclusion:** Humans are held accountable for their actions, which they can freely choose within Allah’s decree.
- **Explanation & Details:** Islamic teachings confirm that individuals possess both the ability and responsibility to choose right or wrong. While their will is subject to Allah’s will, they are accountable for their choices, emphasizing divine justice and human responsibility.

### 🔑 Final Takeaway
- **Key conclusion:** Belief in al-Qadar reinforces Allah’s omnipotence and knowledge without negating human free will, forming a fundamental pillar of Islamic faith.
- **Explanation & Details:** This balanced understanding ensures that believers trust in Allah’s perfect knowledge and decree while acknowledging personal responsibility, fostering trust, humility, and accountability in life and worship practices.
