# Swarm OODA Questioning

Created at 2024/09/05 8:20 PM

by Dr. Jamie Schwandt, (Ed.D.) [soure](https://www.teachthought.com/critical-thinking/four-dimensional-questioning/)

## Observe

**Location Questions** (street name)  

**Swarm Question**: "What Have" - What?  

*(Left-Right (X-axis))*

### 5 Questions:

1. What have we been doing?
2. What is the question/issue/problem I'm trying to resolve?
3. What can I see? What are the facts?
4. What happened? When and where? Who else was involved?
5. What is most important? What's causing it?

***

## Orient

**Orientation Questions** (cross street or avenue)  

**Swarm Question**: "What Should?"  

*(Back-Forth (Y-axis))*

### 5 Questions:

1. What should we be doing or what should have happened?
2. Why is this important? Why does this problem exist?
3. Where is the dog who isn’t barking, and who else might know the truth?
4. What am I missing or do I need to look at the issue from a new perspective (or differently)?
5. What information and/or resources do I need to solve this issue/problem?

***

## Decide

**Elevator Questions** (floor number)  

**Swarm Question**: "What If?" – The Right Why?  

*(Up-Down (Z-axis))*

### 5 Questions:

1. What do I believe happened, and do the indicators support my belief or say otherwise?
2. What must be true for this assumption to be correct?
3. Where are the bullet holes not located or what’s hiding in plain sight that we cannot see?
4. How do my observations relate to what I want to believe? Or better yet, what do they not support?
5. What evidence supports or fails to support my conclusions?

***

## Act

**Time Questions** (IF-Then) (time to arrive at destination)  

**Swarm Question**: "What If?" (Future)

### 5 Questions:

1. What if we were doing what we should be doing? Is that the right thing to do? And why?
2. How might we be wrong? And how would we know?
3. What are the unintended consequences?
4. If I do “X” then what will likely happen? Or if I fail to do “X” then what might happen?
5. If "X" person violates "Y" standard, then what should happen?
