# Applying the NEMA Cycle to AI Collaboration and Community Systems Thinking

Created at 2025/09/11 3:22 PM

## NEMA Cycle Overview
- The NEMA cycle consists of four stages: 
  - **Nota!**: Noting reality and documenting the current state.
  - **Èxplora!**: Exploring possibilities through experimentation.
  - **Manifesta!**: Manifesting creation by co-creating tangible outputs.
  - **Adapta!**: Adapting fluidly based on feedback and reflection.

## Application to AI Collaboration
- The NEMA cycle can serve as a grounding ritual for AI-human teams:
  - **Nota!**: Observe and document team needs, AI capabilities, and context.
  - **Èxplora!**: Experiment together with pilots and new workflows.
  - **Manifesta!**: Co-create outputs like reports or models.
  - **Adapta!**: Reflect and iterate based on feedback from users and AI performance.

## Impact on Community Systems Thinking
- NEMA provides a shared language for collective action:
  - **Nota!**: Identify community needs.
  - **Èxplora!**: Brainstorm solutions collaboratively.
  - **Manifesta!**: Launch community initiatives.
  - **Adapta!**: Adjust based on community feedback, creating a living feedback loop.

## Concerns About Broadness
- The NEMA cycle may seem too broad for specific tasks.
- It can be used as a flexible backbone, allowing for the addition of detailed sub-steps or domain-specific tools within each phase.
- This approach can help maintain structure while addressing concrete challenges effectively.
