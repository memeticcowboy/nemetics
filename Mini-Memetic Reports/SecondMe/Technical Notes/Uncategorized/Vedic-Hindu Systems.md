### **Vedic/Hindu Systems: Samsara, Karma, and Final Liberation**

Within Hinduism, *saṃsāra* refers to the repeated cycles of birth, death, and transmigration, driven by the principle of *karma*.17 Every action, moral or negative, determines the soul's future state, leading to reincarnation in a better form (good karma) or repetition of the same stage (bad karma).17 The spiritual journey is thus characterized by evolutionary progression designed to escape this cycle of necessity.

The ultimate goal for the evolving soul is *moksha* (emancipation, liberation, or release) from *saṃsāra*.19 This is the highest evolutionary state, achieved through self-knowledge (*gnosis*)—specifically, the realization that one's individual being (*ātman*) is identical with *brahman* or the supreme soul.18
