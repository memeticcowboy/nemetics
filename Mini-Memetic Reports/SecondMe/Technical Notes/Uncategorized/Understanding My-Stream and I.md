# Understanding My-Stream and I-Tube in Relation to the 'Old Soul' Memeform

Created at 2025/10/19 1:28 PM

## My-Stream

- The My-Stream is the **dynamic, flowing current of internalized lived experience** within an individual. It carries emotional tone, moods, belief-habits, somatic readiness, and embodied patterns of meaning that shape identity and behavior. It operates often beneath conscious awareness and modulates how incoming cultural signals (e-memes) are felt and metabolized into lived experience. It acts as the **integrator and metabolizer** of internalized memes, shaping what is encoded as identity and what is rejected as incoherent noise. It is the **affective current flowing within the I-Tube**, giving emotional tone and resonance to incoming information. Metaphorically, the My-Stream is the **water flowing through the riverbed of the I-Tube**, shaping the internal experience and identity patterns. ,
- In the context of the "Old Soul" memeform, the My-Stream carries this memeform as a **deep, flowing current** that shapes moods, sensitivities, and identity patterns. The "Old Soul" memeform flows through many My-Streams, running deep like a whisper of still waters amid algorithmic torrents, providing an identity template with emotional modularity that resonates with introspection, melancholy, and connectedness beyond mainstream interests. This emotional and somatic resonance within the My-Stream allows the memeform to feel real and alive in the individual's lived experience.

## I-Tube

- The I-Tube is a **semi-permeable internal membrane or filter** within an individual that governs the intake of memes from the external environment. It decides which memes enter consciousness and are metabolized into memeforms (internalized, embodied patterns of meaning). The I-Tube is shaped by an individual's history, trauma, resonance, gut instincts, and cultural encoding, conditioning preferences, taboos, and expectations. It forms a personal loom where incoming threads (memes) are woven into personal meanings and behaviors. The I-Tube can become rigid or reflexive, especially under power dynamics or trauma, influencing how new memes are accepted or rejected and shaping identity and behavior patterns. Metaphorically, the I-Tube is the **riverbed or channel** through which the My-Stream flows. ,
- Regarding the "Old Soul" memeform, the I-Tube acts as the **internalizer and filter** that selectively admits this memeform into the individual's identity architecture. The "Old Soul" memeform provides an identity template for the I-Tube with just enough curvature—its mystery, melancholy, and meaning—to feel authentic within the My-Stream. This selective internalization shapes the I-Tube's filtering preferences and narrative self, embedding the "Old Soul" as a cultural and emotional pattern that influences how the individual perceives and interacts with the world.

## Summary

- The **I-Tube** functions as the selective filter and internalizer that decides if and how the "Old Soul" memeform enters the individual's consciousness and identity structure.
- The **My-Stream** is the flowing affective current that carries and metabolizes the "Old Soul" memeform as lived experience, shaping moods, emotional tone, and embodied identity patterns.
- Together, they create a recursive loop where the "Old Soul" memeform is both filtered into the self and lived through the affective flow, enabling the memeform to persist and influence behavior and self-perception over time. ,
