Norse Mythology: Yggdrasil, an immense ash tree, is considered holy and central to all existence.10 Its branches reach the heavens, and its three roots extend into different realms, including the home of the gods (Ásgard), the realm of mortals (Midgard), and the underworld. The tree’s purpose is to uphold the cosmos, and its daily court gatherings for the Æsir underscore its central, unifying role.
