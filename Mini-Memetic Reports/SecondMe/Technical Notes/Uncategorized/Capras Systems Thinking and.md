## Capra's Systems Thinking and Network Boundaries

Fritjof Capra's integration of systems thinking with ecological awareness provides insights into how boundaries function within network organizations. His analysis of living systems reveals how boundaries emerge through autopoietic processes that maintain systemic identity while enabling environmental exchange. The network pattern, as Capra describes it, shows how boundaries operate as interfaces rather than barriers.

https://greattransition.org/publication/systems-thinking-and-system-change

Capra's ecological perspective demonstrates how boundary maintenance and boundary permeability must be balanced for systemic health. His critique of mechanistic thinking reveals how rigid boundaries can prevent the adaptive flexibility necessary for survival in changing environments. Metal, in this framework, represents both the structural integrity necessary for network coherence and the conductive properties that enable information flow.
