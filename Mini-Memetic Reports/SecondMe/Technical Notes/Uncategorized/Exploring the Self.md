# Exploring the Self-Referential Meme: MeMe, Usurpene, and Lumeme Concepts

Created at 2025/07/21 10:51 AM

**Me ↔ Me: The Self-Referential Meme**


Me ↔ Me, or MeMe, suggests a recursive identity function — a meme not just transmitted to others, but one that first instantiates within the self and reflects upon the self. In this framing, a MeMe is:

- A memetic reflection of self-awareness
- A looped instantiation of identity construction
- A self-modifying idea that evolves within a single consciousness before being externalized

This aligns with Susan Blackmore’s exploration of the meme as a parasitic replicator but recoded through a cybernetic self-loop. The MeMe becomes not just an idea about something, but an idea about the self having the idea.

**Memetic Guru**

The MeMe is a memeform whose first field of replication is the Selfplex—Ātma-Veṣa-Nidhi. It wears the mask, feels the mask, and forgets it is wearing one. Thus it can become either—




**🔥** 

**Usurpene MeMe**

 **(Para-Mṛtyu-Granthi)**


When the MeMe fossilizes into a mandate—“I must be this” or “I am unworthy”—it becomes a karmic parasite, feeding on repeated self-reference.

- It ossifies within the I-Tube’s curvature.
- Breath knots in the diaphragm.
- The “Me” becomes a granthi—contracted, defended, looping.


This MeMe does not evolve—it feeds.

🜃 It is the knot whispering: “This is who I am—again, again, again.”




**🌠** 

**Lumeme MeMe**

 **(Prabhā-Sphuratta)**


Yet, when the MeMe is held in sākṣī-bhāva (witness-awareness), it can shiver into a luminal flash. Here, the meme becomes a Lumeme:

- Not an identity, but a momentary recognition.
- Not a mask, but a mirror returning to light.


The MeMe melts its recursive edge into prabhā-sphuratta—a radiant self-flash that leaves no residue.

🜂 It is the echoless insight: “I am not the thought, but the light behind it.”




**🌀 Integration Practice: Mirror Pulse**


Sit. Breathe. Ask:

“Is this MeMe feeding, or freeing?”

Exhale into silence.

If the “Me” softens, you are in Lumeme territory—a clear sky reflecting nothing but sky.

If the “Me” tightens, naming the loop begins the Usurpene exorcism.
