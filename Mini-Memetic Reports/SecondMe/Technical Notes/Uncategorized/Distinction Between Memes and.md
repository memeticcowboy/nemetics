# Distinction Between Memes and Internet Memes: Cultural Units and Digital Variants

Created at 2025/09/28 9:42 AM

## 1. Definition and Scope

**Memes** are units of cultural information that encompass ideas, behaviors, symbols, rituals, and cognitive structures that propagate and evolve across individuals and societies. They function as carriers of cultural knowledge, shaping beliefs, values, and identity over time. Memes operate at multiple levels, including neural patterns inside the brain (i-memes), external cultural expressions (e-memes), and durable cognitive frameworks (memeforms) that influence perception and behavior.

**Internet memes**, by contrast, are a specific subset of memes that exist primarily in digital form. They are often humorous or ironic images, videos, phrases, or jokes that spread rapidly online but tend to have short lifespans and focus on entertainment or social commentary rather than deep cultural transmission. [[1]](https://memeticcowboy.substack.com/p/from-viral-to-vital-memeforms-and)

## 2. Depth and Function

Traditional memes are **complex cultural units** that can persist for years or generations, influencing social norms, ideologies, and collective consciousness. They serve as cognitive tools and cultural scaffolding that extend human mental capabilities and shape how societies evolve. 

Internet memes are often **lighthearted, ephemeral, and surface-level**, focusing on quick replication and viral spread rather than deep internalization or long-term cultural impact. They are more about immediate social bonding or humor than about shaping enduring cultural frameworks. [[1]](https://memeticcowboy.substack.com/p/from-viral-to-vital-memeforms-and)

## 3. Analytical Levels and Complexity

Memes are studied across multiple **levels of analysis**: micro (individual cognition), meso (groups and subcultures), and macro (societies and civilizations). They involve complex interactions between internal neural patterns (i-memes), external cultural expressions (e-memes), and cognitive architectures (memeforms). This multi-layered approach highlights their role in shaping both individual and collective realities.

Internet memes primarily occupy the **macro and digital cultural level**, often lacking the deeper cognitive and neural embedding that traditional memes possess. They are more easily observable but less integrated into the cognitive architecture of individuals.

## 4. Cultural and Psychological Impact

Memes influence **long-term cultural evolution**, social norms, and identity formation. They can act as vehicles for transmitting wisdom, knowledge, and ideological frameworks, often operating below conscious awareness to shape behavior and thought.

Internet memes tend to be **transient cultural artifacts** that reflect current events, trends, or social moods. While they can foster community and shared humor, their impact is generally more limited in scope and duration.

## Summary

| Aspect | Memes (General) | Internet Memes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | Units of cultural information, ideas, behaviors | Digital images, videos, phrases, jokes |
| Scope | Broad cultural transmission across time and societies | Specific to online culture and digital platforms |
| Function | Shape beliefs, norms, identity, and cultural evolution | Entertainment, social bonding, viral humor |
| Duration | Long-lasting, persistent cognitive and cultural effects | Often short-lived and rapidly changing |
| Levels of Analysis | Neural, cognitive, cultural (micro to macro) | Primarily cultural/digital (macro) |

This distinction clarifies that while internet memes are a popular and visible form of memes, the broader concept of memes encompasses a much richer, deeper, and more complex set of cultural phenomena.
