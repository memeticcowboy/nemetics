### Translation and Definition:

1. **Granthi** (ग्रन्थि):

    - **Translation**: Granthi means "knot," "blockage," or "bond."
    - **Definition**: In yogic and Tantric traditions, Granthi refers to psychic or energetic knots in the subtle body that obstruct the flow of spiritual energy (*prāṇa* or *kuṇḍalinī*) through the *nāḍīs* (energy channels). The three primary Granthis are:
        - *Brahma Granthi* (at the root chakra, *mūlādhāra*), associated with material attachments and survival instincts.
        - *Vishnu Granthi* (at the heart chakra, *anāhata*), linked to emotional attachments and ego.
        - *Rudra Granthi* (at the third eye chakra, *ājñā*), tied to intellectual pride and spiritual ignorance. These knots represent psychological or karmic blockages that bind one to *saṃsāra* (the cycle of birth and death), requiring purification through yoga, meditation, or spiritual practice to achieve liberation.

2. **Karma** (कर्म):

    - **Translation**: Karma means "action," "deed," or "work."
    - **Definition**: In Indian philosophy (e.g., Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism), Karma refers to the law of cause and effect, where actions (physical, mental, or verbal) create imprints that influence future experiences and rebirths. Karma can be *sanchita* (accumulated), *prārabdha* (currently active), or *āgāmi* (future), and it binds the soul to the cycle of *saṃsāra* unless neutralized through spiritual practice.

3. **Bandha** (बन्ध):

    - **Translation**: Bandha means "bond," "binding," or "lock."
    - **Definition**: In spiritual contexts, Bandha refers to that which binds or restricts, such as the bondage of the soul to *saṃsāra* due to ignorance, desire, or karma. In yoga, *Bandhas* are physical and energetic locks (e.g., *mūla bandha*, *uḍḍiyāna bandha*, *jālandhara bandha*) used to control and direct *prāṇa* for spiritual awakening. Philosophically, it denotes the fetters that keep one trapped in the cycle of existence.

4. **Granthi-Karma-Bandha Combined**:

    - **Translation**: The term Granthi-Karma-Bandha can be translated as "the knot of action and bondage" or "the binding knot of karma."
    - **Definition**: This compound likely refers to the complex interplay of psychic knots (*Granthi*), actions and their consequences (*Karma*), and the resulting bondage (*Bandha*) that ties the individual to the cycle of *saṃsāra*. It suggests a spiritual or philosophical concept where:
        - **Granthi** represents the energetic or psychological blockages in the subtle body, rooted in deep-seated patterns or attachments.
        - **Karma** indicates the actions (past and present) that create and reinforce these knots through their consequences.
        - **Bandha** signifies the resulting bondage that keeps the soul entangled in ignorance and the material world.
    - In a yogic or Tantric context, Granthi-Karma-Bandha could describe the challenge of overcoming karmic imprints and psychic blockages to achieve liberation (*mokṣa*). Practices like *kuṇḍalinī yoga*, *pranayama*, or meditation aim to untie these knots by purifying karma and releasing the bonds of ignorance. In a philosophical sense, it may highlight the interconnected nature of mental conditioning, action, and spiritual entrapment, emphasizing the need for discriminative knowledge (*viveka*) or divine grace to break free.
