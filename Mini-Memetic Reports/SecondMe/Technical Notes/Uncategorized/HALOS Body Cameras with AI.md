# HALOS Body Cameras with AI-Powered Features for Enhanced Security and Evidence Management

Created at 2025/10/13 9:02 PM

HALOS body cameras are **a product line with AI-powered features designed for public and private sector security, primarily focused on proactive safety and evidence management**. The company offers body-worn cameras with a cloud-based digital asset management platform to serve a wide range of customers, including law enforcement, retail, security, and healthcare. Key features

HALOS cameras and their software suite include several AI-powered and "proactive" features that go beyond simple recording.

- **AI threat and behavior detection:** Using AI analytics on live and recorded video, the system is engineered to detect threats such as visible weapons or signs of escalating aggression.
- **Proactive monitoring:** The term "proactive" refers to features that enable real-time response capabilities. For instance, the system can provide live-streaming video and real-time alerts to supervisors when a panic button is activated, allowing for quicker intervention in unfolding incidents.
- **GPS tracking:** Some models, like the HALOS Horizon, include built-in GPS to track the user's location. This helps supervisors monitor patrols and provides location data for recorded incidents, which is especially useful during pursuits.
- **Automated uploads:** 4G-enabled models automatically upload footage to the cloud, eliminating manual transfers and ensuring evidence is secured immediately.
- **De-escalation:** The visible presence of the cameras is marketed as a deterrent to aggressive behavior, with the recorded footage serving as a tool for accountability. Privacy and civil liberties concerns

The use of AI-enabled body cameras, including HALOS's products, has prompted concerns from civil liberties and privacy advocates.

- **Increased surveillance:** Proactive, real-time monitoring and AI analytics on body camera footage raise worries about continuous, widespread surveillance of the public.
- **Racial bias:** Studies and reports have highlighted the potential for racial bias in AI-powered security tools, including the risk of misidentifying individuals or disproportionately flagging the behavior of certain groups.
- **Lack of oversight:**<br>Critics argue that without strong policies and transparent oversight, AI features can erode civil liberties. The R Street Institute, for example, has called for stricter regulations, such as requiring warrants for facial recognition and mandating regular audits<br><br>.
- **Misuse of data:** There are concerns that body camera data, including metadata and AI-generated insights, could be misused or shared inappropriately.
- **Consent and transparency:** Legal and ethical issues exist around recording the public without their consent, particularly with AI features that collect and analyze sensitive biometric data. HALOS states its cameras adhere to GDPR and other privacy standards, but debates on the ethical use of such technology continue.
