## 🧠 1. Purposes and Goals of Baudrillard 
- **Diagnose the Era of Transparency:** Baudrillard aims to understand how contemporary culture dissolves moral, symbolic, and aesthetic boundaries, leading to a universe of endless visibility where distinctions blur.  
  *Details:* His focus is on how good vs. evil, real vs. simulated, and beauty vs. terror become indistinguishable, creating a nihilistic perfection.  
- **Expose the Collapse of Meaning:** He seeks to reveal how total circulation of signs and images results in the disappearance of genuine meaning and depth.  
  *Details:* The goal is to show that the world operates as a system devoid of symbolic richness, leading to nihilism and superficiality.  
- **Revalorize Illusion and Reversibility:** Baudrillard advocates for the reintroduction of illusion, irony, and symbolic reversibility as resistance against flattening experiences.  
  *Details:* The destruction of boundaries demands alternative playful and ironic strategies to preserve vitality.
