# Bodhi Tree

Created at 2025/09/11 4:31 PM

The Peepul tree, also known as the Bodhi Tree, is a primary example. In Hinduism, its roots are associated with Lord Brahma (the creator), the trunk with Lord Vishnu (the protector), and the leaves with Lord Shiva (the destroyer), embodying the entire Trimurti.[1] For Buddhists, it is the sacred site where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment.[1] The use of sacrificial wood in Hinduism is another powerful symbolic act. Pieces of wood are used in fire rituals, and their burning symbolizes the relinquishing of transient ties for higher spiritual understanding.[2] The smoke from the sacred fire is believed to be a conduit for communication with the gods, making wood not only a sacred entity but a medium for spiritual communication.
