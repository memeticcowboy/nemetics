## 🌀I-TUBES **(Informorphic Definition, Revised)**

**The I-Tube is the living loop-field of selfhood** — a recursive, neuro-somatic conduit that **internalizes culture into identity** and **returns identity into culture**.

It is not a container but a **structural field of loops** that define the *shape and permeability* of personal experience.

Experiences and memeforms are **internalized** here as feedback loops, twists, and knots.

Selfhood persists through the **ongoing reweaving of these loops**, not through fixed boundaries.




### 1. **Structural Features**

**Threaded Coherence**

Memeforms enter as threads, twisting into loops that stabilize into self-patterns.

Distorted loops ossify into knots (usurpenes).

**Loop-Driven Identity**

Identity emerges from recursive feedback: perception → interpretation → response → reflection.

Breaks in the loop fracture coherence.

**Permeable Boundary**

Interfaces with My-Stream (energetic metabolism) and We-Sphere (collective resonance).

Selectively internalizes external signals into personal structure.




### 2. **Functional Role in Memetic Ecology**

**Internalizer** → Encodes cultural signals into self-structure.

**Filter** → Chooses which signals cross into loops.

**Resonator** → Stabilizes lumemic patterns; knots usurpene ones.

**Corpus Callosum** → Integrates self with We-Sphere, bridging inner and collective fields.




### 3. **Distinction from My-Stream**

**I-Tube internalizes**: shapes, encodes, and orients experience into loops.

**My-Stream metabolizes**: carries affective and energetic charge through the loops, enlivening or fragmenting them.

**Metaphor**: I-Tube is the riverbed (structure of flow); My-Stream is the water (metabolic current).




### One-Line Unity

> **The I-Tube is the loop-field that internalizes signals into self-patterns, while My-Stream metabolizes their flow into the living pulse of being.**
