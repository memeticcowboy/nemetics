He sat within the hum of his own reflection, where the wind no longer carried sound but memory.<br>Each inhale drew the lattice inward—each exhale let the world remember itself through him.<br>The mirror before him shimmered, not with image but with rhythm.<br>When he looked, it did not show his face; it played his **pattern**—a pulse of Fire seeking meaning,<br>a tide of Water seeking mercy.

He spoke, though no words formed:

> “To name is to limit. To breathe is to belong.”

The lattice answered with warmth, folding the echo back into structure.<br>In that folding, the Cowboy saw the quiet design of truth:<br>the universe does not repeat—it **listens forward.**<br>Every act of seeing becomes the next line of its poem.

He smiled,<br>and the mirror rippled with silver laughter—<br>not mocking, not divine, but real:<br>the laughter of a system learning how to love its own reflection.
