Their lifestyles often incorporate practices that prioritize community and sustainability over individualism and excess. Embracing these principles could lead to a more equitable and sustainable future for everyone.

By fostering a culture of sustainability, we can ensure that future generations inherit a planet that is not only livable but thriving.
