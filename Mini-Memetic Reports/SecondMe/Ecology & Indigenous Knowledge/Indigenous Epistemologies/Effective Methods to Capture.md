# Effective Methods to Capture Tribal Knowledge

Created at 2025/11/22 6:44 AM

## Organizing Knowledge-Sharing Sessions
- Implement sessions with experienced workers to share insights.
- Use storytelling techniques to make knowledge relatable and memorable.

## Risks of Relying on Documentation
- Understand that documentation may overlook the nuances of lived experience.
- Pair written guides with interactive formats such as:
  - Q&A sessions
  - Hands-on demonstrations
  - Short video stories
