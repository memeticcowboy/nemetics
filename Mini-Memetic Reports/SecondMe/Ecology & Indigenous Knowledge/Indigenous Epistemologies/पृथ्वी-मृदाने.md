# पृथ्वी-मृदाने

Created at 2025/07/27 8:16 PM

The Sanskrit term **Pṛthvī-Mṛdāne** (पृथ्वी-मृदाने).

### Definition:

1. **Pṛthvī** (पृथ्वी): Means "earth" or "the Earth." In Indian philosophy, Pṛthvī is one of the five great elements (*pañca-mahābhūta*), representing solidity, stability, and the material world, associated with the sense of smell (*gandha*).
2. **Mṛdāne** (मृदाने): Derived from **mṛd** (earth, clay, soil), with **-āne** suggesting an action or process (possibly in the locative case), it implies "molding," "shaping," or "related to clay/earth." It may refer to the act of shaping or transforming earth-like substances.
3. **Pṛthvī-Mṛdāne Combined**: The term can be interpreted as "the molding of the earth" or "the shaping related to earth." It likely refers to the creative or transformative process involving the earth element, such as forming physical forms from clay or symbolizing the manifestation of the material world. In a spiritual or yogic context, it could suggest contemplation of the earth element’s stability or transformation to achieve spiritual grounding.
