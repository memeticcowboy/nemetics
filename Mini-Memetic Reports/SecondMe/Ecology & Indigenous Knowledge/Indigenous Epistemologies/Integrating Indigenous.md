# Integrating Indigenous Knowledge into STREAM Education

Created at 2024/07/04 12:28 PM

### Integrating Indigenous Knowledge into STREAM Classrooms

#### Science
- Incorporate indigenous ecological knowledge, such as traditional methods of land management, plant usage, and understanding of local ecosystems.

#### Technology
- Highlight indigenous innovations and tools, like traditional navigation techniques or sustainable building practices.

#### Engineering
- Study indigenous engineering feats, such as the construction of ancient irrigation systems or traditional architecture.

#### Arts
- Include indigenous art forms and storytelling in visual and performing arts projects.

#### Mathematics
- Explore indigenous mathematical concepts and systems, such as those used in navigation or architecture.

### Cross-Disciplinary Projects
- Develop projects that require students to apply STREAM principles to solve real-world problems using indigenous knowledge. For example, designing a sustainable garden using traditional agricultural practices.

### Creative Thinking and Problem Solving
- Encourage students to think creatively and solve problems by learning from indigenous practices that emphasize sustainability and community well-being.

### Cultural Practices
- Integrate cultural practices and ceremonies into the classroom to provide context and deepen understanding. This could include inviting indigenous elders to share their knowledge and stories.

### Physical Spaces
- Design classroom spaces that reflect indigenous cultures, such as using artwork or symbols that are meaningful to local indigenous communities.

### Virtual Spaces
- Utilize online platforms to connect with indigenous communities and access resources that can be integrated into the curriculum.

### Pedagogy
- Adopt teaching methods that are inclusive of indigenous ways of learning, such as storytelling, hands-on activities, and collaborative learning.
- Use assessment strategies that recognize and value the knowledge and skills students gain from indigenous perspectives.
