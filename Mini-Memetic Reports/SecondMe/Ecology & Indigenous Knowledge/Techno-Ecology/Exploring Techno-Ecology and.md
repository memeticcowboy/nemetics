## Real-World Examples of Techno-Ecology
- Smart forests using sensor networks to monitor deforestation and biodiversity.
- Drone swarms for conservation efforts, particularly in wildlife protection.
- Bio-logging technology to track animal movements and behaviors.
- Development of bio-batteries for sustainable energy solutions.
- IoT applications in agriculture to optimize resource use and improve crop yields.

## Environment as an Extension of Self
- Concept of the environment as part of oneself, enhanced by technology.
- Potential for tech interfaces to deepen sensory experiences and interactions with nature.
- Technology acting as an extension of human capabilities to foster a harmonious relationship with the environment.
