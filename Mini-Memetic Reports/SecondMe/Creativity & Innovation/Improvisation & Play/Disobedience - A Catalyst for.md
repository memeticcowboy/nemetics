* In contrasting educational models, Deutsch’s critique of rigid curricula aligns with progressive education theories, such as those proposed by John Dewey, which advocate for experiential learning that encourages students to question and explore rather than merely conforming to existing standards.

* Ultimately, both images exemplify a rich dialogue surrounding the interplay between disobedience, creativity, and progress in human civilization. They urge an examination of how societal structures can either stifle or nurture this essential dynamic.
