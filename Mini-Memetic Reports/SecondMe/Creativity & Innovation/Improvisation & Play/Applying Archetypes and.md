# Applying Archetypes and Improvisation in Projects

Created at 2025/06/30 7:57 PM

## Practical Application of Archetypes
- Start each project phase by asking, “Which archetype does this moment call for?”
- Consciously shift mindset or approach to match the required archetype.
- Think of it like picking the right instrument for the next song.

## Importance of Improvisation
- Improvisation enhances collaboration and invites real-time contributions.
- Creates a lively and connected atmosphere, similar to a jam session.

## Role of Tools in Structure and Clarity
- Tools provide necessary structure to maintain coherence in projects.
- Over-reliance on tools can hinder the organic flow of collaboration.
