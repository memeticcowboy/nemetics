What Napkin AI can do
 * Create visuals for webinar slides, presentations, email newsletters, social media, sales funnel diagrams, landing pages, infographics, blog posts, pitch decks, and onboarding material.
 * Generate onboarding flows for online membership communities.
 * Create visual representations of complex topics.
How to use Napkin AI
 * Go to the Napkin AI website and click on the "New Napkin" button.
 * Choose between "Draft with AI" and "Blank Napkin".
 * If you choose "Draft with AI", you will be prompted to enter a topic. Napkin AI will then generate a text-based draft of the topic, along with a visual representation of the draft.
 * If you choose "Blank Napkin", you can start with a blank canvas and add your own text and images.
 * Once you have added your text and images, click on the "Lightning Bolt" button to generate a visual representation of your content.
 * You can customize your visual representation by changing the text, alignment, font, colors, and images.
 * When you are finished, you can download your image or copy and paste it into another document.
Key features of Napkin AI
 * Easy to use
 * Fast
 * Versatile
 * Free
Is Napkin AI something novel or something well known?
Napkin AI is a relatively new tool, so it is not as well known as some of the other AI-powered graphic design tools on the market. However, it has the potential to be a game-changer for businesses and individuals who need to create high-quality visuals quickly and easily.
