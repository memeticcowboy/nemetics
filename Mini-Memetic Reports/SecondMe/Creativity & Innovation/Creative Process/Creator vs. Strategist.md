# Creator vs. Strategist: Challenging the Not Just Possible

Created at 2025/06/10 9:52 PM

- The focus is on challenging the traditional role of the strategist by emphasizing the importance of creativity and imagination in shaping futures.
- The aim is to advocate for a perspective that prioritizes what is preferable, not just what is possible.
- This approach encourages a more visionary mindset, pushing boundaries and exploring radical alternatives rather than settling for conventional solutions.
