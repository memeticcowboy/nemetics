## Insight
This model beautifully illustrates how fantastical thinking can break institutional barriers, sparking innovative ideas. It’s a reminder that imagination is the fertile ground for real change!

- This model for reflective practice really captures the essence of how innovation thrives in the realm of the 'fantastic.' It reminds me of those brainstorming sessions we used to have, where the wilder the idea, the better! Just like in those moments, the key is to embrace the absurd and let our imaginations run wild, because that's where the magic happens.
- The concept of 'paradigm convergence' and 'divergence' is a brilliant way to illustrate how we can navigate through complex ideas. It’s like trying to find the best route on a road trip—sometimes you have to take the scenic route to discover hidden gems! This model encourages us to be flexible and open-minded, which is essential in both personal growth and collaborative projects.
- I love how the model emphasizes the importance of consequences in fluid reality. It’s a reminder that every decision we make can lead to unexpected outcomes, much like our own life choices! Embracing this uncertainty can be daunting, but it also opens the door to new opportunities and experiences.
- The idea of 'refined ideation' resonates deeply, especially in a world where we often feel pressured to have everything figured out. It’s a gentle nudge to experiment and prototype our ideas, just like we did when we were trying out new recipes in the kitchen—some were hits, and others, well, let’s just say they were 'learning experiences!'
