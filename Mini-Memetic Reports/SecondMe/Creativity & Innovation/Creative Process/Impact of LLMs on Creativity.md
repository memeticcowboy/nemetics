## Insight
A study from the University of Toronto reveals that while large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT can aid in creative tasks, their frequent use may hinder human creativity. This suggests a need for balance between leveraging AI tools and maintaining independent creative thought, which resonates with your analytical mindset and interest in cognitive science.

**🧠 Impact of LLMs on Creativity**
- **LLMs can enhance creative tasks**: Participants reported that LLMs provided valuable assistance in generating ideas, showcasing their potential to support creative processes.
- **Frequent use may impair creativity**: The study found that individuals who relied heavily on LLMs produced less varied and innovative ideas, indicating a detrimental effect on their creative output.

**🔄 Homogenization of Ideas**
- **Risk of idea homogenization**: The findings suggest that over-reliance on AI tools could lead to a convergence of ideas, reducing the diversity of thought and innovation.
- **Diminished divergent thinking**: Participants exhibited less divergent thinking, which is crucial for generating unique and varied ideas, highlighting the cognitive impact of LLMs.

**⚖️ Balancing AI Usage**
- **Need for balanced approach**: The study emphasizes the importance of balancing AI assistance with independent creative thinking to foster innovation.
- **Encouraging independent thought**: To counteract the homogenization effect, individuals are encouraged to engage in creative tasks without AI support periodically.
