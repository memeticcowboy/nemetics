# Spiral Framework of SCAMPER: From Instrumental Knowmads to Radical Unknowing Unknowmads

Created at 2025/12/25 2:48 PM

Framing SCAMPER not as a linear tool but as a spiral mirrors the earlier philosophical tension: outward expansion (exploration, divergence) balanced with grounded return (humility, presence). The knowmad grips tightly—instrumentally optimizing each move for value—while the unknowmad grips loosely, allowing ideas to morph, dissolve, or evade utility. Applying SCAMPER to the knowmad concept itself spirals us toward unknowmad principles, questioning the grip of productivity.<br>Substitute – Loosening Instrumental Assumptions<br>Substitute the core driver: Replace “value creation” with “ambiguity preservation.”<br>Knowmad: Substitute rigid skills with adaptable ones to thrive in markets.<br>Unknowmad: Substitute the imperative to “add value” with reverent non-action—protect unknowing from enclosure. This loosens the tight grip on expertise, acknowledging that presumed “useful” knowledge often colonizes the unknown.<br>Combine – Outward Fusion Without Capture<br>Combine disparate elements, but refuse their instrumental merger.<br>Knowmad: Combine unrelated ideas (e.g., smartphone as phone + camera) for innovation and economic gain.<br>Unknowmad: Combine human/more-than-human relations in serendipitous drift, without forcing synthesis into “impact” or capital. Spiraling outward here means playful borrowing across domains, but loosely—ideas merge transiently, dissolving if they demand productivity.<br>Adapt – Borrowing from Flux, Not for Mastery<br>Adapt ideas from other contexts, grounded in humility.<br>Knowmad: Adapt biomimicry (Velcro from burrs) for efficient solutions in volatile economies.<br>Unknowmad: Adapt from natural impermanence or contemplative traditions (e.g., radical unknowing in mysticism) to linger in flux without extraction. Grip loosely: adaptation isn’t for resilience in capitalism but for harmony with change, echoing Heraclitus while refusing utilitarian backcasting.<br>Modify (Magnify/Minify) – Scaling Ambiguity<br>Magnify unknowing; minify the imperative to know.<br>Knowmad: Magnify skills/frequency of innovation for bigger impact.<br>Unknowmad: Magnify spaces of ambiguity (slow time, non-linear attention—vital for neurodivergent thriving); minify performative output. This spirals outward by amplifying reverence, grounded in subtracting the pride of “upskilling” to prevent burnout.<br>Put to Another Use – Defying Original Intent<br>Repurpose knowing beyond utility.<br>Knowmad: Repurpose tools/skills for new markets (shipping containers as housing).<br>Unknowmad: Repurpose nomadic mobility not for accumulation (cultural capital, networks) but for insurgency—drifting to evade metrics. Loose grip: the knowmad’s intentional “preferred futures” (StoryTech) becomes unknowmad’s open-ended wandering, where outcomes evade premeditated value.<br>Eliminate – Subtracting the Enclosure<br>Eliminate the relentless drive to innovate/productivize.<br>Knowmad: Eliminate inefficiencies to streamline value.<br>Unknowmad: Eliminate the enclosure of unknowing by productivity culture—strip away upskilling mandates, metrics, even “well-intentioned impact.” Grounded subtraction: less is liberation, countering overload and neurodivergent alienation from busyness.<br>Reverse/Rearrange – Inverting the Imperative<br>Reverse the question: From “How to create value in uncertainty?” to “Must uncertainty become value?”<br>Knowmad: Reverse traditional work (pay first in fast food) for efficiency.<br>Unknowmad: Reverse acceleration—slow down, start from unknowing rather than preferred outcomes. Rearrange sequence: linger in prior questions, flip agency from entrepreneurial to reverent. Loose grip inverts tight control, spiraling into post-instrumental freedom.<br>SCAMPER as Spiral: Grip Loose for Radical Unknowing<br>In knowmad mode, SCAMPER grips tightly—a grounded tool for instrumental divergence, always filtering for practical value in accelerationist contexts. Spiraling outward generates abundance, but returns to utility, risking pride in expertise and burnout from perpetual adaptation.<br>In unknowmad mode, SCAMPER spirals with loose grip: outward exploration without premature capture—ideas generated wildly, but allowed to evaporate if they demand extraction. Grounded in radical unknowing (letting inner/emergent wisdom arise without forcing outcomes), it becomes post-instrumental nomadism: a quiet refusal of the knowmad’s optimistic enclosure.<br>This divergence invites direct questions to the knowmad ecosystem (Moravec, Cobo, Harkins, Kubik, and inheritors): How might we expand the imaginary to include unknowmads—not as failure of adaptation, but as ethical protectors of ambiguity? In neurodivergent experiences of dissonance and burnout, might radical unknowing reveal limits of instrumental reason, spiraling toward futures where not everything must “add value”?<br>Ultimately, the unknowmad doesn’t reject the knowmad but loosens its grip, allowing a broader ecology: dance with flux reverently, spiraling outward while staying grounded in the unextractable unknown.<br>[x/change w/Bob-RJ](https://x.com/burkhartrj/status/2004313098757493062?s=46&t=7Z-E-ACnGlzdI-iyUwNCrQ)
