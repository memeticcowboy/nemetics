# Innovative Thinking Models for Various Fields

Created at 2024/07/06 1:30 PM

## Bloom's Category: Create

### Mechanistic Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Develop a novel mechanistic model to predict outcomes in an unexplored scientific field, encouraging innovative approaches to traditional problems.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Construct a detailed simulation based on mechanistic principles that can serve educational purposes or help in research.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Design a new tool or machine that incorporates mechanistic thinking to improve efficiency or functionality in specific applications.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Invent a product or process that utilizes mechanistic thinking to address a current industry challenge, focusing on practical deployment and user experience.

### Living Systems Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Create a sustainable urban development plan that utilizes living systems principles to enhance ecological balance and community well-being.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Develop a comprehensive organizational model that mimics a living system, designed to enhance adaptability and resilience.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Design a series of experiments to test the efficacy of living systems approaches in various environments, such as agricultural or corporate settings.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Launch a startup based on the principles of living systems, focusing on innovative solutions to environmental challenges.

### Journey Thinking/Mapping

- **Diverging (LSI)**: Create a personal development app that guides users through a journey thinking framework, helping them set and achieve meaningful life goals.
- **Assimilating (LSI)**: Write a novel or develop a screenplay that centers around the concept of journey thinking, exploring the protagonist's evolution over time.
- **Converging (LSI)**: Design a workshop or training program that helps participants apply journey thinking to their professional and personal lives.
- **Accommodating (LSI)**: Initiate a community project that uses journey thinking to bring diverse groups together, focusing on shared goals and collaborative growth.
