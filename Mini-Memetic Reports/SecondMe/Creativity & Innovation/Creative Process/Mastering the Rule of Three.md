# Mastering the Rule of Three in Marketing

Created at 2024/08/01 2:28 PM

## Crafting Slogans and Taglines
- **Memorability**: Slogans with three elements are easier to remember (e.g., Nike’s “Just Do It”).
- **Engagement**: Grouping ideas in threes makes messages more engaging (e.g., “Reduce, Reuse, Recycle”).

## Structuring Campaigns
- **Three-Part Campaigns**: Break campaigns into Awareness, Engagement, and Conversion phases.
- **Content Strategy**: Plan content around three key messages or themes.

## Creating Compelling Content
- **Storytelling**: Use the structure of problem, struggle, and solution to keep the audience engaged.
- **Bullet Points and Lists**: Present information in groups of three to maintain clarity.

## Design and Visuals
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Use three colors, fonts, or main sections for a balanced design.
- **Call to Action (CTA)**: Limit CTAs to three options to avoid decision fatigue.

## Email Marketing
- **Subject Lines**: Craft subject lines with three key points to increase open rates.
- **Content Segmentation**: Divide email content into Introduction, Main Message, and Conclusion.

## Real-World Examples
- **Coca-Cola**: Focuses on themes of Happiness, Refreshment, and Togetherness.
- **McDonald’s**: Uses the slogan “I’m Lovin’ It” to convey a powerful message.
