# Exploring the Dark Enlightenment

Created at 2024/11/07 11:41 AM

AI hosts of NotebookLM explore the "Dark Enlightenment" [(Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Enlightenment) - a reactionary, anti-democratic perspective that critiques modern Western society's progressive ideals and institutions. Nick Land, the author of the excerpts, argues that liberalism, multiculturalism, and egalitarianism, represented by a powerful "Cathedral" of academics, media, and political elites, have led to societal degeneration and a decline in liberty. He criticizes the Cathedral's reliance on social constructivism and its suppression of "uncomfortable truths" about human biological diversity, particularly intelligence, and the consequences of policies that incentivize welfare dependency. Land, along with other "neo-reactionaries" like Mencius Moldbug, advocate for a return to a "formalized" state that prioritizes individual autonomy, economic freedom, and a more realistic understanding of human nature.
