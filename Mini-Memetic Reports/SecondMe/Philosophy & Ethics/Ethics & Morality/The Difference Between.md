# The Difference Between Fairness and Justice in Economics

Created at 2025/06/12 8:35 PM

## Fairness
- Focuses on the process of economic interactions.
- Ensures that everyone plays by the same rules.
- Emphasizes agreements made without tricks or pressure.

## Justice
- Broader concept that encompasses fairness.
- Concerns the overall system and its outcomes.
- Questions whether the rules themselves are just.
- Highlights that justice is not just about income distribution but also about the processes leading to those outcomes.
