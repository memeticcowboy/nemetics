**Summary:**  

Personalized algorithmic decision-making poses unavoidable ethical threats to user autonomy by embedding bias, narrowing self-perception, and promoting cognitive deskilling. A human-in-the-loop approach is proposed to preserve agency and ethical integrity in human-AI interactions.

**Top 3 Themes:**  

1. **Erosion of Autonomy** – Algorithms distort users' self-conception, reduce decision-making capabilities, and simulate autonomy while subtly undermining it.  

2. **Value-Ladenness & Bias** – Personalized algorithms embed designers’ values and societal biases, influencing user decisions without transparency.  

3. **Human-Centered AI Design** – A human-in-the-loop approach can mitigate risks, emphasizing user control, critical awareness, and algorithmic literacy.
