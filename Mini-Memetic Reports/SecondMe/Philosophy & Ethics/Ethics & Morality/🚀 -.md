**3. Progress Myths (Techno-Social Determinism)**
Example A (Right-Wing): “Free markets and innovation will uplift all”\
Example B (Left-Wing): “Technological socialism and automation will liberate humanity”
**R –** \
**Recognize:**
Note the narrative arc: from struggle to utopia via innovation. These myths often invoke inevitability, mastery over nature, and human perfectibility.
**E –** \
**Examine:**
Ask: What does “progress” mean here? Who defines it? Does it mean GDP growth, social justice, AI alignment? What histories are simplified?
**V –** \
**Validate:**
Assess empirical outcomes: Has market innovation consistently reduced poverty? Has automation increased worker liberation—or just changed the game?
**E –** \
**Expose:**
Unveil the ideological engine: libertarian techno-optimism vs. technocratic socialism. Both assume human flourishing via control over systems—but define control differently.
**A –** \
**Alternatives:**
Propose slower, layered progress: emotional intelligence, cultural healing, ecological regeneration—beyond linear techno-futures.
**L –** \
**Literate:**
Cultivate critical awareness of who benefits from progress myths. Teach how to map the memetic structure behind futurist visions.
