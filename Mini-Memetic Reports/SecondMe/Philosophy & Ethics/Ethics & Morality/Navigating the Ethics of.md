* The concept of **temporal justice** proposed in the image underscores the importance of aligning ethical development with technological progress. This notion implies that ethical frameworks must evolve alongside innovations to prevent a gap that leaves society vulnerable to morally questionable outcomes.

* One pressing ethical dilemma outlined in the provided analysis is the challenge of preparing society for increasingly autonomous systems without resorting to paternalistic or manipulative practices. This raises questions about autonomy and the nature of education in an age of profound technological change.

* Ultimately, the tension created by these loops signifies the urgency for intentional governance and ethical standards that can adapt as technology evolves. Without such measures, both loops risk amplifying systemic instability, with implications for society's collective well-being in the face of rapid technological change.
