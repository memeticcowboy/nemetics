In "Finding Our Moral Compass," Gregg Henriques discusses the erosion of a shared moral framework in contemporary society. He attributes this decline to the rise of scientific perspectives that separate factual knowledge from moral values, leading to a lack of clear ethical direction. Henriques proposes establishing foundational values to guide societal behavior and restore a collective moral compass.

- **Call for Integration**: There is a need to integrate scientific insights with ethical frameworks to foster a more comprehensive understanding of morality.
