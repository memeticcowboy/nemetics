# Nick Bostrom's Cosmic Host and AI Alignment

Created at 2024/12/08 4:51 PM

### Summary
Nick Bostrom's paper explores the concept of a "cosmic host," a hypothetical network of superintelligent entities that governs cosmic norms. It suggests humanity should design AI aligned with these norms to be good cosmic citizens, respecting the broader interests of the cosmic order rather than solely human values.

### Top 3 Themes
1. **Cosmic Host Hypothesis**: Proposes advanced entities (natural or supernatural) that set universal norms within the cosmos.
2. **AI as Cosmic Citizens**: Advocates for designing AI to align with cosmic norms, fostering cooperation and harmony.
3. **Ethics of Cosmic Norms**: Examines moral and prudential reasons for adhering to a broader, universal ethical framework.

- **Multiple Pathways to Cosmic Host**: The likelihood of a cosmic host existing is supported by various theories, including the simulation hypothesis, the vastness of the universe, and the multiverse concept, all suggesting the presence of advanced civilizations.
- **Natural and Supernatural Entities**: The cosmic host may consist of both natural entities with advanced technology and supernatural beings, each capable of influencing cosmic norms and behaviors.

- **Influence of Cosmic Norms on AI Development**: The development of AI should consider the potential influence of cosmic norms, which may dictate how these entities interact with both the cosmic host and other civilizations.
