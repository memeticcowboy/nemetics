## Insight

The survey aims to measure various moral and ethical dimensions through a series of questions rated on a 0-5 scale. The key outcomes include a structured approach to evaluating personal values and behaviors, providing actionable insights for personal development. If you're looking to understand your moral compass better, this survey can be a valuable tool. Let's dive into the detailed breakdown to see how each axis is structured.
