# Exploring Ethical Frameworks and AI as a Relational Actor

Created at 2025/06/14 5:21 PM

## Recommended Ethical Frameworks for AI Development
- **IEEE Ethically Aligned Design**: Focuses on human well-being and accountability.
- **European Union's Guidelines**: Emphasizes trustworthy, human-centric AI.
- **OECD AI Principles**: Promotes responsible stewardship and international applicability.
- **Montreal Declaration**: Highlights democratic values, privacy, and equity.
- **Google's and Microsoft's AI Principles**: Prioritize social benefit, safety, and inclusiveness.
- **Asilomar AI Principles**: Advocates for transparency and caution in AI research and development.
- **SANITY Framework**: Stands for soundness, alignment, nurturance, integrity, transparency, and yielding.

## Practical Applications of Ethical Ideas
- **Ethical Design Workshops**: Brainstorming sessions for integrating ethical principles into AI projects.
- **Real-Time Ethics Dashboards**: Tools for ongoing feedback on ethical metrics during development.
- **Diverse Development Teams**: Assembling teams with varied backgrounds for broader perspectives.
- **Scenario Simulations**: Testing AI systems in different ethical scenarios before deployment.
- **Feedback Loops with Users**: Creating channels for user feedback on ethical concerns.
- **Ethics Review Boards**: Establishing boards to review AI projects for adherence to ethical guidelines.

## AI as a Relational Actor
- **Interdependence Models**: Mapping how AI interacts with human and non-human entities.
- **Affective AI**: Developing AI that understands and responds to human emotions.
- **Collaborative AI**: Designing systems that work alongside humans to enhance capabilities.
- **Ecological Justice**: Considering the broader ecological impacts of AI development.
- **Narrative Integration**: Framing AI as a character in the human story to understand its societal influence.
