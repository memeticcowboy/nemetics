# Embracing Integrity Beyond Behavior

Created at 2025/10/31 12:41 PM

## Daily Practices to Embody Integrity
- Pause before decisions and assess alignment with values.
- Speak honestly with kindness, especially in uncomfortable situations.
- Admit mistakes quickly and seek to make amends.
- Reflect each evening on alignment with personal values.
- Engage in conversations that explore ethics beyond metrics.

## The Essence of Integrity
- Integrity is more than just behavioral actions; it involves inner dialogue and self-reflection.
- It’s an ongoing alignment between inner values and outer actions.
- Integrity as a verb emphasizes the journey of navigating intentions and choices.
