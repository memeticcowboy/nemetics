# Whitehead's Philosophy of Organism and Its Connection to Species-Being

Created at 2025/12/14 3:13 PM

"Species-being" connects Alfred North Whitehead's [philosophy of organism](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkN8X_a3W8A), particularly his concept of "actual entities" or "occasions," to the idea of species-being (Gattungswesen), a term from German philosophy (Fichte, Feuerbach, Marx) about shared essence/species nature. Whitehead generalizes this: his fundamental units of reality are events (actual occasions), not static things, which form by "feeling" past events, creating a dynamic, relational world where individuality emerges from interconnectedness, linking individual "drops of experience" to the universal process. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkN8X_a3W8A), [2](https://philarchive.org/rec/ANDISA-8#:~:text=Nevertheless%2C%20a%20type%20of%20individuality%20that%20is,social%20and%20political%20implications%20of%20Whitehead's%20cosmology.), [3](https://iep.utm.edu/whitehead/#:~:text=The%20basic%20units%20of%20becoming%20for%20Whitehead,translating%20it%20into%20the%20occasion's%20concrete%20reality.)]

**Key Concepts from Whitehead:**

- **Philosophy of Organism:** Reality is fundamentally process and events, not inert matter.
- **Actual Occasions/Entities:** The basic "drops of experience" or events that make up reality.
- **Prehension:** The "feeling" or relating of an actual occasion to past occasions, forming its identity.
- **Causal Efficacy:** The fundamental experience of being influenced by the past. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkN8X_a3W8A), [3](https://iep.utm.edu/whitehead/#:~:text=The%20basic%20units%20of%20becoming%20for%20Whitehead,translating%20it%20into%20the%20occasion's%20concrete%20reality.), [4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process_and_Reality#:~:text=Whitehead%20often%20speaks%20of%20the%20metaphysics%20of,and%20that%20of%20abstract%20entity%20or%20abstraction.), [5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_North_Whitehead#:~:text=Whitehead%20describes%20causal%20efficacy%20as%20%22the%20experience,of%20being%20influenced%20and%20affected%20by%20the), [6](https://footnotes2plato.substack.com/p/agency-and-perception-in-whitehead#:~:text=Whitehead%20lays%20out%20his%20theory%20of%20perception,to%20complex%20organisms%20with%20developed%20sensory%20organs.)]

**Connecting to Species-Being:**

- **Generalizing Individuality:** Whitehead's "actual entities" are inherently relational, meaning each is defined by its connections, much like a concept of species-being emphasizes the collective essence shaping individuals.
- **Social & Political Implications:** His cosmology, focused on interconnected events and relationships, provides a framework for understanding social structures and political systems not as static wholes, but as evolving processes. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkN8X_a3W8A), [2](https://philarchive.org/rec/ANDISA-8#:~:text=Nevertheless%2C%20a%20type%20of%20individuality%20that%20is,social%20and%20political%20implications%20of%20Whitehead's%20cosmology.)]

**In essence:** While species-being points to a shared essence, Whitehead offers a metaphysical system where *every* individual entity (actual occasion) is fundamentally a nexus of relations, embodying a universal process of becoming, thus extending the idea of a shared "being" into the very fabric of existence. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkN8X_a3W8A), [2](https://philarchive.org/rec/ANDISA-8#:~:text=Nevertheless%2C%20a%20type%20of%20individuality%20that%20is,social%20and%20political%20implications%20of%20Whitehead's%20cosmology.), [3](https://iep.utm.edu/whitehead/#:~:text=The%20basic%20units%20of%20becoming%20for%20Whitehead,translating%20it%20into%20the%20occasion's%20concrete%20reality.)]
