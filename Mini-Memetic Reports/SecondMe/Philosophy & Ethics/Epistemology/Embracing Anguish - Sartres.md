Created at 2025/11/24 6:13 AM

“Man is condemned to be free… This is the anguish that the majority of people want to flee, by taking refuge in bad faith.”<br><br>—  Jean-Paul Sartre (Being and Nothingness, 1943)

Grok → Sartre treats anguish (l’angoisse) as the unavoidable awareness that nothing external guarantees our choices or values. Embracing this anguish—rather than denying our freedom—is the only way to live without self-deception.
