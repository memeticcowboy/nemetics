- Ludwig Andreas von Feuerbach was a thinker who made humanism and atheism serious philosophical concerns, emphasizing the human origins of religious belief.
- Feuerbach's key idea is that "the true sense of Theology is Anthropology", meaning that religious beliefs are a projection of human needs and desires.
- He argued that human capacities and ambitions should be understood as part of human nature, rather than being attributed to a divine entity, and proposed a "philosophy of the future" based on a thorough examination of human nature.
