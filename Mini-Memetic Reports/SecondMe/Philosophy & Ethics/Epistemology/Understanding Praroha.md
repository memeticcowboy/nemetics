# प्ररोह-तन्मात्र -  Emergence-only-ness (WOOD)

Created at 2025/08/19 4:50 PM

The Sanskrit term **Praroha-Tanmātra** can be broken down into its components: **Praroha** (प्ररोह) and **Tanmātra** (तन्मात्र). Below is the definition and the term written in Sanskrit.

### Definition:

1. **Praroha** (प्ररोह): This term is derived from the prefix **pra-** (indicating forward, forth, or excellence) and **roha** (from the root **ruh**, meaning "to grow," "to ascend," or "to sprout"). Praroha means "growth," "sprouting," "ascent," or "emergence." It can refer to the act of rising, blossoming, or developing, often in a literal (e.g., plant growth) or metaphorical sense (e.g., spiritual or intellectual growth).
2. **Tanmātra** (तन्मात्र): This term means "subtle element" or "essence of perception." In Sāṃkhya philosophy, Tanmātras are the five subtle elements (sound, touch, form, taste, and smell) that are the precursors to the gross elements (*pañca-mahābhūta*: space, air, fire, water, earth). They represent the subtlest form of sensory perception before manifestation in the physical world.
3. **Praroha-Tanmātra Combined**: The term Praroha-Tanmātra can be interpreted as "the subtle essence of growth" or "the subtle element of emergence." It likely refers to the primordial or subtle potential for growth, ascent, or blossoming, considered as a fundamental essence in a philosophical or spiritual context. In Sāṃkhya or yogic frameworks, it could denote the subtle vibratory essence that underlies the process of growth or development, whether physical, mental, or spiritual. In a Tantric or meditative context, it might suggest contemplation of the subtle energy of emergence to connect with the creative process of consciousness or the unfolding of reality.
