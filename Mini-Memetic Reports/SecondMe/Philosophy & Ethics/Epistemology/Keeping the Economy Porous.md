The phrase "keep the economy porous" is a core concept from Anne Carson's 1999 book, Economy of the Unlost. It refers to the idea of resisting the rigid, transactional nature of a money-based economy and maintaining a flow of value that is open, generous, and non-commercial, similar to a gift economy. He employed an extreme economy of words, using silence, negation, and absence to salvage meaning and truth from a broken world.
