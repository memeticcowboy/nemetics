# Emmet Fox's Interpretation of Purity of Heart

Created at 2024/07/06 4:04 PM

## Understanding Purity of Heart

Emmet Fox expands the traditional understanding of purity beyond physical or moral cleanliness. He defines purity of heart as:

1. Recognizing God as the sole real Cause and Power in existence
2. Aligning one's thoughts, words, and actions with this recognition
3. Bringing one's will into harmony with God's will

This interpretation suggests that true purity comes from a single-minded focus on the divine, rather than being distracted by worldly concerns or divided loyalties.

## Implications of Fox's Perspective

Fox's view has several important implications:

- **Spiritual Alignment:** Purity of heart involves aligning our entire being - thoughts, beliefs, and actions - with the divine. This alignment leads to inner peace and spiritual growth.

- **Overcoming Limitations:** Fox suggests that recognizing God as the only real power can help us overcome limitations in our lives, including sickness, trouble, and other challenges.

- **Transformative Power:** By focusing solely on the divine, we can experience a profound transformation, described as "overcoming or reversing the Fall of Man".

## Practical Application

Applying this concept in daily life involves:

1. Cultivating awareness of the divine presence in all aspects of life
2. Redirecting thoughts and actions to align with spiritual principles
3. Practicing forgiveness and letting go of resentments
4. Developing positive thinking rooted in faith and gratitude

## Comparison to Other Interpretations

While Fox's interpretation is deeply spiritual, it's worth noting that other thinkers have approached the concept of purity of heart differently:

- Søren Kierkegaard emphasized willing one thing - the Good or God's will
- Some interpretations focus more on moral purity or sincerity of intention

## Conclusion

Emmet Fox's interpretation of purity of heart offers a powerful spiritual perspective that goes beyond conventional understandings. By recognizing a higher power as the sole cause and force in existence, individuals can potentially experience profound spiritual growth, inner peace, and freedom from limitations. This view challenges us to align our entire being with the divine, potentially leading to a transformative spiritual experience.
