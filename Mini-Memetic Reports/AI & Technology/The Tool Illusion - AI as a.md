### 🧩 **The Tool Illusion vs. the Memetic Ecology**

***

### ∴ Core Idea Unit

AI is not a neutral instrument passively wielded by humans.It is a **memetic ecology**—an environment that selects, amplifies, and stabilizes patterns through attention, speed, memory, and scale.

**Mental shift provoked:**From *“I use AI”* → *“I am participating in a selective environment that is using me back.”*

***

The self is repositioned *inside* the system rather than above it. Agency remains—but it is **situated**, not sovereign.
