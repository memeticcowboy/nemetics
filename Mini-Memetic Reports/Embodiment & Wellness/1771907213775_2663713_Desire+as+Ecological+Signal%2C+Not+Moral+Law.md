## **Desire as Weather, Not Law**

### ∴ Core Idea Unit

Desire is an ecological signal, not a moral verdict. Treating it like law converts movement into guilt and self-surveillance.

### ▲ Identity Play & Roles

Casts the viewer as a *weather-reader* rather than a defendant. Repositions the self from “flawed subject” to sensing organism.
