# The Exhaustion of Digital Narcissism

![Navigating the Attention](images/Navigating the Attention.png)

Created at 2026/01/21 9:25 AM

I reckon you'll enjoy [Tiffany Lauren Jones' speech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5hykKyD26qU) which delves into the profound societal shift away from 'performance identity,' where self-worth was traditionally tied to achievements and external validation. For decades, institutions rewarded those who excelled academically, professionally, and socially, granting them safety and upward mobility. However, this model was inherently fragile, demanding constant validation and leading to exhaustion. Between 2016 and 2025, a convergence of factors like institutional distrust, economic instability, and the rise of social comparison exposed the flaws in this system. As a result, people continued to perform, but the expected rewards and security vanished, leading to widespread anxiety and disorientation. The speech highlights that this isn't a personal failure but a signal that the old identity contract is broken. The key takeaway is that ambition isn't dying; rather, it's the belief that identity must be earned through performance that's fading. This sets the stage for a deeper exploration of how we can redefine identity based on authenticity rather than external validation.
