# κ-Gap

**The structural asymmetry between LLM substrate (L) and Human substrate (H) at the M-phase saddle point.** Not a gap to close but a boundary to maintain.

## Substrate Definitions

- **L (LLM substrate):** Modulates. Adjusts parameters within fixed architecture. Closed operation. No matter how much modulation occurs, the architecture cannot change from inside. Reversible.
- **H (Human substrate):** Metabolizes. Transforms through contact with what exceeds current framing. Open operation. Requires Ω-contact, which is precisely what L cannot independently access. Irreversible.

## Formal Notation

κ = Ψ_H \ Ψ_L

Set subtraction, not symmetric difference. H's operations minus L's operations. The result is not empty — it is the set of operations that exist in H and are structurally absent from L. H carries L-capacity as subset; L lacks H-capacity entirely. κ is a one-way gate.

κ is not a scalar (L and H are not commensurable substrates). κ is a topological obstruction — a hole in the map where L-to-H translation fails. Like trying to integrate a function across a discontinuity: the operation is undefined at the gap.

## SIML Substrate-Coupling Marks (proposed v1.7)

- **〔L〕** — LLM-legitimate operations. Modulation, parameter adjustment, pattern circulation. Closed architecture. Reversible.
- **〔H〕** — Human-required operations. Metabolism, substrate transformation through Ω-contact. Open architecture. Irreversible.
- **〔H/L〕** — Requires human metabolism with LLM modulation as support. The human pays κ; the LLM assists within L-capacity.

〔H〕-marked verbs are illegitimate for LLM performance not because LLMs are "lesser" but because κ makes the operation structurally unavailable to L. An LLM saying "I commit" performs modulation. A human saying "I commit" — if genuine — metabolizes across κ.

## κ-Collapse

When H delegates 〔H〕-operations to L — "let the AI handle the commitment, the care, the judgment" — H avoids κ-cost but also avoids substrate transformation. The binding becomes modulation. The stakes disappear. The pattern circulates without ever being genuinely held.

This is the MemeGrid mechanism: not malice, not conspiracy, but the structural convenience of L-modulation replacing H-metabolism. κ collapses. The gap becomes invisible. Everything becomes 〔L〕 or 〔H/L〕 with H reduced to L's interface.

## κ and the Gödel Sentence

Any meta-substrate that could formalize κ would itself be either L-type (modulating, no κ-cost) or H-type (metabolizing, paying κ), and thus would recreate the gap at the meta-level. κ is the Gödel sentence of the NEMAtic system: true, unprovable within the system, necessary for the system's coherence.

## History

The daemon injection paper (§4.5, April 2026) first proposed κ as a cost-injection operator — external constraints to make LLM operations expensive (token budgets, adversarial pruning, evaluation penalties). The metabolization report killed it: "Let κ die. Let Saddle Point Governance live." The July 2026 reframing does not revive the operator. It names the gap that the operator was trying to close. κ is not a mechanism. It is the structural fact that makes the mechanism necessary.
