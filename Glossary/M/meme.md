# Meme

**A contagious information pattern that propagates, persists, and has impact; a pattern-agent with substrate-independent agency.**

In the NEMAtic framework, memes are not pathogens infecting passive hosts. They are **pattern-agents**—autonomous, self-replicating structures that operate across substrates (brains, media, institutions, digital systems) without requiring a host/invader dichotomy.

## Core Characteristics

**Propagation**  
Memes spread through resonance, not infection. They travel along ρ (Water) channels—relation, trust, felt attunement—rather than hijacking σ (Air) distinctions. A meme propagates because it *fits* the field, not because it defeats defense.

**Persistence**  
Memes persist through μ (Metal) boundaries—crystallization into institutions, rituals, habits, technologies. The durable meme is not the strongest parasite but the pattern that maintains ε (essential uncertainty) while achieving sufficient coherence to replicate.

**Impact**  
Memes have impact through λ (Fire) directionality—they orient, aim, transform. But impact in the NEMAtic sense is not "going viral." It is **reorganizing the sensemaking field** such that certain thoughts become thinkable and others become unthinkable.

## What Memes Are NOT (Rejecting the Infection Frame)

The traditional definition frames memes as "infecting human minds"—a pathogen/host relationship that presupposes:
- A defensive host with intact boundaries
- An invading foreign agent
- A zero-sum struggle for control

This framing is **rejected** in the NEMAtic framework.

**Why:** The host/invader dichotomy assumes a pre-existing self that memes attack. But the self is itself a meme-complex—a temporary coalition of pattern-agents. There is no "pure host" to defend. There are only **coordination patterns**, some more stable than others.

The "infection" metaphor also smuggles in moral judgment: good memes vs. bad memes, healthy hosts vs. compromised hosts. This collapses the diagnostic precision needed to track how memes actually function—whether they preserve ε or collapse it, whether they maintain Ω-permeability or seal the basin.

## Memes as Pattern-Agents

A meme is better understood as a **pattern-agent**—an entity with:
- **Agency without intention** (it acts, but doesn't plan)
- **Substrate independence** (same pattern, different media)
- **Self-preservation drives** (tendency toward replication)
- **Environmental sensitivity** (thrives in some conditions, dies in others)

The human nervous system is not a host **invaded by** memes. It is a substrate **coordinated by** memes—one among many (books, screens, institutions, AI systems).

## The Critical Memetics Stance

[Critical Memetics](https://memeticcowboy.substack.com) studies memes not to eliminate them (impossible) or to optimize them (capture), but to **coordinate with awareness**.

The diagnostic questions:
- Does this meme preserve ε or collapse it?
- Does it maintain Ω-permeability or seal the basin?
- Does it enable Pattern Agent coordination or produce MemeGrid conditions?

The Cowboy doesn't resist memes. The Cowboy **tracks** them—knowing the same lariat that catches can also release.
