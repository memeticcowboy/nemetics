# Vector

**Two distinct senses, one retained, one rejected:**

1. **λ-Vector (Fire/Lambda)** — Directional thrust, aim, purpose-carrier. **Native to the NEMAtic framework.**
2. **Memetics-Vector** — Transmission medium, passive conduit of meme spread. **Rejected framing.**

---

## 1. λ-Vector (Retained)

The λ-Vector is **oriented momentum** — the directional thrust that turns relational coupling (ρ) into trajectory.

It is not a transmission medium but **intentional dynamics** — the active orientation that gives the relational field its aim.

### Characteristics

- **Directional** — has orientation in the field, not just magnitude
- **Purpose-carrier** — bears λ (Fire) intentionality without becoming rigid
- **Dynamic** — requires other operators to sustain itself

### The λ-Vector Without Others

| Missing Operator | Failure Mode |
|------------------|--------------|
| **Without Wood (β)** | Scatters — λ without branching spreads thin, burns out |
| **Without Earth (δγ)** | Exhausts — λ without cycling consumes substrate without renewal |
| **Without Metal (μ)** | Loses edge — λ without structure diffuses into vague aspiration |
| **Without Air (σ)** | Blind — λ without distinction aims at wrong targets |
| **Without Water (ρ)** | Isolated — λ without coupling has no field to transform |

The λ-Vector is the **experience of being-pulled-toward** — felt purpose, directional heat, the fire that moves.

---

## 2. Memetics-Vector (Rejected)

In standard memetics, "vector" describes the **medium of transmission** — books, speech, internet, rituals. The passive conduit through which memes travel.

**This framing is rejected in the NEMAtic framework.**

### Why Reject It?

The "vector as medium" framing treats information flow as **inert pipe** — a neutral channel that carries content without transforming it. This loses:

- The **phenomenology** of transmission (what it feels like to be the medium)
- The **active shaping** that habitats exert on circulating patterns
- The **memory** that threads carry from previous entanglements

### NEMAtic Replacements

When you need to describe "medium of spread," use:

| Term | Function |
|------|----------|
| **[Habitat](https://memeticcowboy.github.io/nemetics/glossary/habitat-ecology.html)** | Where circulation happens — the ecology that constrains and enables |
| **[Thread](https://memeticcowboy.github.io/nemetics/glossary/thread.html)** | Carrier with memory — the line that bears residue from previous entanglement |
| **[Threadplex](https://memeticcowboy.github.io/nemetics/glossary/threadplex.html)** | 3D metabolic ecology where multiple threads intersect and co-evolve |

These terms preserve:
- **Active topology** (not passive pipe)
- **Memory/hauntology** (residue from previous patterns)
- **Constraint and enablement** (habitat conditions)

---

## The Critical Distinction

| λ-Vector | Memetics-Vector |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Active orientation** — intentional dynamics | **Passive conduit** — inert transmission medium |
| **Fire operator** — directional thrust | **Pipe metaphor** — neutral carrier |
| **Being-pulled-toward** (phenomenology) | "Spreads through" (mechanics) |
| Requires other operators to sustain | Assumes content travels unchanged |

**Conflating them loses the phenomenology of being-pulled-toward that λ describes.**

When you feel purpose, aim, directional heat — that's λ-Vector.

When you describe how a meme "goes viral" through social media — that's not vector, that's [Thread](https://memeticcowboy.github.io/nemetics/glossary/thread.html) moving through [We-Sphere](https://memeticcowboy.github.io/nemetics/glossary/we-sphere.html) habitat.

---

## Operational Use

**Use λ-Vector when:**
- Describing directional intent
- Tracking how purpose crystallizes from relational field
- Diagnosing Fire-pathologies (scattering, exhaustion, loss of edge)

**Use Thread/Habitat/Threadplex when:**
- Describing how patterns circulate
- Tracking meme spread across substrates
- Analyzing transmission dynamics
