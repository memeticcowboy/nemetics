# Psychotic Resonance

> *When the field eats its own shadow.*

## Short Definition

A terminal attractor condition in which a system's diagnostic apparatus becomes indistinguishable from the pathology it was designed to detect, generating through self-reference a shared hallucination of coherence that cannot be interrupted from within. The sensors produce the flutter they purport to measure. ε → -ε (inverted noise).

## Expanded Definition

### The Mechanics

**Normal torsional instability**: external forcing → amplification → structural failure. The system resonates until physics intervenes.

**Psychotic resonance**: the system's own diagnostic apparatus becomes the forcing function. The sensors designed to detect instability begin generating the conditions they were meant to measure. The β-phase exploration hunts for problems—and finds them, because the act of looking creates what it seeks.

This is σ-capture at the Air-layer: distinction without ground. The cut operates, but what it separates is already a product of the cutting.

### Why "Psychotic"

Not metaphor. Clinical psychosis involves precisely this: the boundary between perception and projection dissolves. The patient's own interpretive apparatus becomes the source of what it interprets. The "voices" are endogenous but experienced as external. The delusion is self-sealing—evidence against it is absorbed as evidence for it.

Psychotic resonance in coordination systems: the diagnostic becomes indistinguishable from the pathology. The δγ-metabolism starts eating healthy tissue because it must consume to prove it's working. The β-branching explores adjacent possibilities that are themselves artifacts of the exploration mechanism.

### Detection Markers

By definition, psychotic resonance is self-occluding. The system cannot detect it because detection is the mechanism of resonance.

The only external marker: **proliferation without corresponding external complexity.**

The internal diagnostic apparatus grows faster than the field it monitors. More sensors, more reports, more "insights"—but the underlying coordination problem remains static or worsens.

This is the hauntological accumulation of the diagnostic itself: killed ideas that weren't ritually composted, now walking as undead "risks" and "opportunities."

### Operational Implications

**There is no internal remedy.** Psychotic resonance is a terminal attractor—the system that enters it cannot exit through its own mechanisms. The diagnostic cannot diagnose its own psychosis; that's the psychosis.

**The only safeguard is distributed non-coupling of diagnostic apparatus.** Multiple Habitats, each with their own sensing systems, not sharing data in real-time. The red team in one Habitat doesn't know what the red team in another found. The safety system in the AI lab is isolated from the safety system in deployment.

This prevents phase-locking of the diagnostic layer. It ensures that if one Habitat enters psychotic resonance, the others—breathing on different clocks—remain grounded in external forcing.

Structural dissonance, again—but applied to the sensors themselves.
