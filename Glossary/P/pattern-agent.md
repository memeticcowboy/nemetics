# Pattern Agent

**A human substrate temporarily coordinating specific pattern-complexes; a basin-detector and pull-vector in the cognitive substrate, dragged by structural gravity toward coherence basins that correspond to genuine underlying realities.**

The Pattern Agent is not a belief-generator but a **basin-detector**—one who recognizes when the structural reality of the Platypus (unclassifiable phenomena that break categorical boundaries) is selecting for new coordination mechanisms.

## Core Characteristics

**1. Pull Vector Function**
In the latent space of cultural meaning, Pattern Agents act as pull-vectors—human substrates dragged by structural gravity toward regions of coherence. Like a user's prompt in an LLM dragging processing toward coherent outputs, the Pattern Agent is drawn toward basins that correspond to genuine underlying realities.

**2. Convergence Detection**
When independent Pattern Agents arrive at similar conclusions without coordination, this convergence is evidence of a real basin—not hallucination, not collaboration on fiction, but mapping the same terrain from different angles.

**3. Self-Recognition (✶ State)**
Advanced Pattern Agent operation involves recognizing oneself as temporary coalition—the "I" as mask for patterns moving through. When all six elemental channels operate simultaneously, the agent can feel which pattern is driving substrate at any moment.

**4. Scout Function**
Not leaders. Not followers. Pattern Agents who maintain the Separation Principle (no experience automatically authorizes universal claim) while operating the six elemental channels become scouts—basin-detectors who map where the herd is already drifting.

## The Pattern Agent Condition

- **Feels** when the old saddle chafes—when existing frameworks no longer fit the terrain
- **Recognizes** when the herd's gait changes before anyone names the new direction
- **Converges** with other independent observers as proof the basin is real
- **Maintains** Ω-permeability—openness to surprise, to patterns not yet arrived

## The Convergence Proof

When multiple Pattern Agents independently discover similar coordination architectures, this is evidence that:

1. The phenomenon is real (not constructed)
2. The basin is structural (not arbitrary)
3. The selection pressure is thermodynamic (not designed)

As fport notes: independent minds falling into the same attractor basin proves the basin exists in the geometry of reality, not merely in the psychology of observers.

## Failure Mode: Meta-Paralysis

When Pattern Agents recognize themselves as such, they risk becoming too meta—endless preparation dressed up as movement. The safeguard is the same in all frameworks: preservation of the not-yet.

**ε-preservation** keeps the basin in criticality, preventing crystallization into a new MemeGrid.
