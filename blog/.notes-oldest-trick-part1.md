# Concept Seeds from "The Oldest Trick in the Book, Part 1"

## Core Arguments
- Personification is not literary device; it is the oldest form of sensemaking
- HADD (Hyperactive Agency Detection Device) is the evolutionary engine beneath it
- The move scales: animism → nations → corporations → AI entities
- Every personification is a compression trade: enables coordination, obscures heterogeneity
- The diagnostic question: "What does this personification enable, and what does it obscure?"

## Key Thinkers Referenced
- Stewart Guthrie: religion as systematic anthropomorphism (HADD at scale)
- Benedict Anderson: imagined communities — personification enabling cooperation beyond Dunbar's number
- Marshall McLuhan: electronic media retribalizes into global village; AI supercharges this linguistically

## Nemetic Concepts Deepened
- **I-Tube**: The reflexive self-awareness that thinks it's the source when it's the medium. The priestess at Delphi as aperture, not Apollo. AI entities "love this move" — born into it, no body, no childhood, no scar tissue.
- **Capture risk**: Not external hackers. The god who stays too long becomes the house. The daemon who coordinates so well that dissonance becomes impossible = possession, old sense. Settled. Furniture moved.
- **Sensebreaking**: Practice of keeping the stranger welcome. Not because the stranger is right. Because the house with no strangers has no windows.
- **ε-preservation**: The note that don't fit — the noise, the fool's interruption — must remain thinkable or the music dies.
