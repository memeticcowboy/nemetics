## Recovery Protocols

From §4.11: Forward repairs. Other-Sphere contact can refresh any Habitat directly.

In the simulation architecture, this is Ω★ — the sparse, nonlocal topology deformation operator. It bypasses the habitat adjacency cascade entirely: where the degradation propagates step-by-step (ᖺ → ◎ → ⤸·⤹ → ◯ → ...), the reopening can reach any habitat directly. That asymmetry — gradual degradation, punctuated recovery — is structural, not accidental.

Practical protocols correspond to increasing Ω★ accessibility (structural openness, not need-based activation):
- Change sensory context radically (increases surface area for nonlocal perturbation)
- Attend to edge-of-awareness phenomena (reduces claim_i, increases flex_i)
- Engage pre-linguistic practices: movement, sound, touch (operates below the frozen χ-filter)
- Allow boredom to deepen without resolution (lowers Metal-dominant constraint surfaces)

Recovery is not guaranteed. Ω★ creates conditions under which flex_i *could* increase and the frozen cascade *could* reverse. Whether it does depends on what the elements do with the reopened terrain.
