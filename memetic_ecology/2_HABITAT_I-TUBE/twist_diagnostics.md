## The Phenomenon

In the I-Tube, Twists form when the reflexive loop catches on itself:

- "I am thinking about myself thinking"
- "I notice that I notice"
- "This is MY experience of having an experience"

Each loop adds torsion. Without ε-space, the torsion tightens into Knot.

## Diagnostic Tests

### Twist vs Knot Test
> Can this unwind *without explanation*?
- Yes → Twist (still torsional, still revisable)
- No → Knot (stabilized, requires work to dissolve)

In simulation terms: a Twist is torsion that has not yet been absorbed into a knot during compression. It can still discharge or transfer across habitat boundaries. A Knot is the result of bottleneck binding — torsion that has crystallized into stiff_m and perm_m. The Bow-Tie Process Layer is explicit that bottleneck ε determines which way this goes: ε > 0 at binding produces a revisable knot (still loosenable); ε → 0 at binding produces an absolute knot (the trace of what was lost is destroyed, and the binding becomes permanent).
